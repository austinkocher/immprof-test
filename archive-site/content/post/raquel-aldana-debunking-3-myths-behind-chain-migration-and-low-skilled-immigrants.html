On The Conversation ("Debunking 3 myths behind ‘chain migration’ and ‘low-skilled’ immigrants), Associate Vice Chancellor for Academic Diversity  and Professor Raquel Aldana evaluates President Trump's attacks on "chain migration" amd efforts to reduce the migration of "low-skilled" immigrants to the United States.  She identifies and discusses "three myths" underlying the attacks:

 Myth #1: Family immigration is unlimited

Myth #2: Family immigration is overwhelming

Myth #3: ‘Low-skilled’ immigrants don’t benefit the US
