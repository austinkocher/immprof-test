It is an election year.  And Democrats are on the run about immigration.  In seeking to spur passage of a Senate border compromise, President Joe Biden’s assertion this weekend that he wants the authority to “shut down” the border.   This NBC report looks at Biden's promise to close the border and the public reactions to it.  Mexican President Andrés Manuel López Obrador was skeptical about Biden’s statement that he would close the border and blamed it on the upcoming elections and urged that Mexico and the U.S. "act with tranquility."  “Why these propositions?" said López Obrador. "Because there are elections! Once the elections pass, there’s another agenda. But we, the Mexicans, need to recognize this background. Politically it’s time. We are in a special time, in Mexico and the United States."
