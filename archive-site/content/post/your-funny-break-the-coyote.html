The Coyote is a short comedy sketch starting Frankie Quiñones and Kirk Fox, written by Colton Dunn and Frankie Quiñones, and directed by Robb Boardman. The premise: a group of Mexicans are stopped by Border Patrol while looking to "bounce" from the US. Among their gripes, Twitter: "You know I was the only coyote with a blue checkmark for years man. Now anybody can get one for eight bucks a month."
