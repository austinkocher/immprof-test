A new paper highlights immigrants working in law enforcement, illustrating immigrants’ contributions to public safety in local communities across the country.  “Immigrants in Law Enforcement Contributing to Public Safety,” published by the Law Enforcement Immigration Task Force, profiles immigrants who are “using their life experiences, language skills, and drive to contribute to the communities where they live,” and includes the stories of ten immigrants across seven states who work in law enforcement.
