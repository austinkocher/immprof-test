I am doubtful that, although terrifying immigrant communities,  President Trump's mass deportation campaign in the end will reduce the overall undocumented population.  Here are some thoughts in The Conversation.  "Undocumented immigrants often come to the U.S. to work or seek safety from natural disasters and mass violence. . . . These issues have not been seriously addressed by any modern U.S. president. Until it is, we can expect the undocumented population to remain in the millions."
