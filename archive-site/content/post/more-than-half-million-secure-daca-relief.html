Some 550,000 young undocumented immigrants have received temporary protection from deportation and work authorization since President Barack Obama created the Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA) program in June 2012, according to new data released by US Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). The news comes as DACA approaches its two-year anniversary in August, when Dreamers who benefitted from it will have to begin applying for renewal.  Click here for more details.
