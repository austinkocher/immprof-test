The University of San Diego will host a live taping of the MSNBC's show, “The Last Word with Lawrence O’Donnell,” from 7-9 p.m. on Monday, Nov. 15.   MSNBC is producing a special live town hall meeting on immigration, featuring six expert panelists. For the program’s last segment, O’Donnell and his panelists will take questions from the live audience, comprised of University of San Diego students, alumni, staff and faculty.
