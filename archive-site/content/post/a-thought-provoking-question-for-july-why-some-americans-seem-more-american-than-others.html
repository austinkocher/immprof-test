It is July and time to celebrate our nation,  Unfortunately, not all Americans are treated equally.  Katherine Kinzler for The Conversation writes that many people who explicitly endorse egalitarian ideals, such as the notion that all Americans are equally deserving of the rights of citizenship regardless of race, still implicitly harbor prejudices over who’s “really” American.  People of color unfortunately are not always treated as full members in the national community.
