Check out this virtual panel discussion on judicial independence and the immigration courts being organized by the New York City Chapter of the Hispanic National Bar Association.  This panel will discuss these issues along with how we got here.  The goal of this discussion will be to educate the greater legal community and public about these issues and how we can move forward to change this. 
 
Panelists include:
 
Claudia Cubas - Litigation Director of Capital Area Immigrant Rights Coalition
 
Hon. Paul Schmidt (retired) - Former Immigration Judge, Adjunct Professor, and Founder of the Immigration Courtside Blog 
 
Professor Jill Family - Commonwealth Professor of Law and Government and Director of Law and Government Institute at Widener University School of Law
 
Elizabeth Gibson - Senior Staff Attorney, New York Legal Assistance Group
 
This discussion will take place Wednesday, April 7 at 6:00pm - 7:30pm EST.  The flyer for this event is attached.  You may RSVP here.
