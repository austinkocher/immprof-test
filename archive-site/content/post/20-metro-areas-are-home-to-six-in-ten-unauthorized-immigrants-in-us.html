--- title: "20 metro areas are home to six-in-ten unauthorized immigrants in U.S." date: 2017-02-10T05:15:03 url: "/post/20-metro-areas-are-home-to-six-in-ten-unauthorized-immigrants-in-us/" aliases: ["/immigration/2017/02/20-metro-areas-are-home-to-six-in-ten-unauthorized-immigrants-in-us.html"] type: post year: "2017" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

The Pew Research Center in a report by Jeffrey S. Passell and D'Vera Cohn found that Most of the United States’ 11.1 million unauthorized immigrants live in just 20 major metropolitan areas, with the largest populations in New York, Los Angeles and Houston.

The analysis shows that the nation’s unauthorized immigrant population is highly concentrated, more so than the U.S. population overall. In 2014, the 20 metro areas with most unauthorized immigrants were home to 6.8 million of them, or 61% of the estimated nationwide total. By contrast, only 36% of the total U.S. population lived in those metro areas.

But the analysis also shows that unauthorized immigrants tend to live where other immigrants live. Among lawful immigrants – including naturalized citizens and noncitizens – 65% lived in those top metros.
