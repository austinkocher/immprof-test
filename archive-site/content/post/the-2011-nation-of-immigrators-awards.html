Angelo Paparelli on Nation of Immigrators has named the winners of the 2011 Nation of Immigrators Awards - The IMMIs. Among the winners are

Nativist Enabler. Kris Kobach, Kansas Secretary of State, and ghostwriter for many of the state anti-immigration bills, dubbed by The Daily Beast as "the intellectual architect of the right’s fight against illegal immigration," wins this ignominious IMMI.

I'm Not Captain Bligh. Alejandro Majorkas, the well-intentioned lawyer's-lawyer who directs USCIS, earns an IMMI for bringing tangible improvements to immigration-benefits administration.
