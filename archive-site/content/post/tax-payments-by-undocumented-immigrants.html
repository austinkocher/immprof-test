The Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy released a report today on the tax payments by undocumented immigrants.  One key finding:  "Undocumented immigrants paid $96.7 billion in federal, state, and local taxes in 2022. Most of that amount, $59.4 billion, was paid to the federal government while the remaining $37.3 billion was paid to state and local governments."
