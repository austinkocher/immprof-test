We've already pointed out that the U.S. House Judiciary Committee released talking points on immigration in the form of GIFs. Like many, I was mortified to see lawmakers being so flip about federal legislation and people's lives.

But I want to focus for a minute on the irony of using The Little Mermaid to sell the line that "we need innovative solutions that guarantee our immigration laws will be enforced in the future." It's hard to imagine a greater poster child for immigration than Ariel. Come on, she wants to be part of your world so much that she trades her voice for legs in the effort to achieve maximum assimilation!
