Today's Border Update from Adam Isacson at the Washington Office on Latin America covers:

  1. The government's use of the Alien Enemies Act
  2. Renewed military presence at the border
  3. Travel ban news
  4. Border Wall Construction; and
  5. Notes on Congress

Discussion of the first issue -- the Alien Enemies Act -- includes profiles on 15 of the 137 Venezuelan men sent to El Salvador. The profiles are heartbreaking--asylum seekers with apparently strong claims who were legitimately pursuing relief in the US but who've been extraordinarily renditioned and imprisoned in a hellscape without the ability to contact their attorneys or families and subject to hard labor.
