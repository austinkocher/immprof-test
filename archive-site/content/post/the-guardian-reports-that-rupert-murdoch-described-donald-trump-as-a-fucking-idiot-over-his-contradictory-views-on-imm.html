The Michael Wolff book Fire and Fury has created nothing less than a firestorm.  Here is an interview with Wolff. 

I have not read the book yet but it appears that immigration comes up in the book.  The Guardian reports that Rupert Murdoch described Donald Trump as “a f------ idiot” over his views on immigration policy, the book reveals.

The book describes a Trump Tower meeting in December 2016 during the presidential transition involving Trump and executives from Silicon Valley companies. Among the issues at stake was a future of H-1B visas.
