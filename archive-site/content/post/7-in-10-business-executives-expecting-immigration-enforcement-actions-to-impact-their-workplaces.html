The Hill reports on a survey finding that 7 in 10 business executives expecting immigration enforcement actions to impact their workplaces.  An annual poll from Littler found that 70% of employers said they expect ICE and DHS immigration actions to have a “significant or moderate” impact on their workplaces in the next year.
