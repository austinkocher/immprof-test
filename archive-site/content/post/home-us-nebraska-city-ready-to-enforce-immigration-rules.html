U.S. Nebraska City Ready to Enforce Immigration Rules" date: 2014-04-01T12:00:00 url: "/post/home-us-nebraska-city-ready-to-enforce-immigration-rules/" aliases: ["/immigration/2014/04/home-us-nebraska-city-ready-to-enforce-immigration-rules.html"] type: post year: "2014" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" date_from_address: true ---

Josh Funk of the Associated Press reports that Fremont, Nebraska will start requiring renters to get a $5 permit on Thursday. Earlier this year, voters in the city of about 26,000 again backed the housing restrictions that require renters to swear that they have legal immigration status and prohibit landlords from renting to anyone without a city permit.
