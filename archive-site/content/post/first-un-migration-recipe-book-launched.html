To celebrate the benefits of migration in our daily lives, the International Organization for Migration has compiled a cookbook with traditional recipes from across much of Southeast and Eastern Europe and Central Asia.  The recipe book was launched this week at the Vienna International Centre under the umbrella of the UN’s “Together” campaign which aims to promote tolerance towards migrants and refugees.
