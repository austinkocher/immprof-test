--- title: "Armenians among fastest-growing groups of naturalized citizens in California" date: 2011-03-26T07:08:46 url: "/post/armenians-among-fastest-growing-groups-of-naturalized-citizens-in-california/" aliases: ["/immigration/2011/03/armenians-among-fastest-growing-groups-of-naturalized-citizens-in-california.html"] type: post year: "2011" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

 
Natives of Armenia are among the fastest-growing groups of naturalized citizens in Southern California, according to the Department of Homeland Security. On Thursday, March 24, more than 4,000 immigrants who live in Los Angeles and six other Southern California counties were sworn in as U.S. citizens. Their most common countries of origin, according to the Homeland Security Department, are Mexico, the Philippines, China, Iran, El Salvador and Armenia.
