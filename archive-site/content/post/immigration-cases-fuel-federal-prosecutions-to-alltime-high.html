--- title: "Immigration Cases Fuel Federal Prosecutions to All-Time High" date: 2009-12-21T06:17:25 url: "/post/immigration-cases-fuel-federal-prosecutions-to-alltime-high/" aliases: ["/immigration/2009/12/immigration-cases-fuel-federal-prosecutions-to-alltime-high.html"] type: post year: "2009" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Transactional Records Access Clearinghouse (TRAC) data shows that federal prosecutions reached an all time high in FY 2009. The surge was driven by a sharp increase in immigration filings. According to timely case-by-case data obtained and analyzed by the TRAC, overall federal prosecutions peaked at 169,612, up nearly 9 percent from the previous year. But the increase in immigration filings was much sharper -- 15.7 percent. This means that such prosecutions now make up well over half of all criminal cases brought by the government. Meanwhile, the prosecution of other major crime categories such as drugs, weapons and white collar crime was up only slightly or had actually declined.
