Photo courtesy of the U.S. Supreme Court

Amy Howe for SCOTUSBlog reports that the Supreme Court will not take up the question whether to reinstate the Trump administration's tightened public charge rule. The justices had agreed to review, but yesterday, the Biden administration requested that the suits be tossed. "The filings by Acting Solicitor General Elizabeth Prelogar were the latest in a series by the Biden administration to take controversial immigration issues that arose during the Trump administration off the Supreme Court’s docket."
