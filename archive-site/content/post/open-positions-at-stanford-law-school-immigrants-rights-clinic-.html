The Stanford Law School Immigrants' Rights Clinic is hiring for two positions. We are now looking for a Program Fellow, a recent law school graduate or experienced paralegal who can assist us with litigation and advocacy against recent federal enforcement actions. Applications are being considered on a rolling basis. Please see attached for a complete job description.
 
We are also continuing our search for a Staff Attorney, who will supervise law students and develop litigation and advocacy to challenge recent enforcement orders and practices. This position requires at least three years of experience working on immigration and civil rights issues (with 5-7 years strongly preferred). We have started to schedule interviews for the staff attorney position, and are considering applications on a rolling basis. A full job description for the staff attorney position is attached.
