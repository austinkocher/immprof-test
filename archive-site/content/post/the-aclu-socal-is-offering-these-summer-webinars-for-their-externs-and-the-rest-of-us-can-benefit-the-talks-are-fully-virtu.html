Thursday, June 29 (12:00pm – 1:30pm)
Bring Them Home: The Meaning of Deportation and Repatriation of Deported U.S. VeteransPresented by a Panel of Deported Veterans
Moderated by Jennie Pasquarella and Jon Contreras (ACLU of Southern California)

Thursday, July 6 (12:00pm – 1:00pm)
Civil Rights Litigation at a Private Public Interest Law FirmPresented by Brian Olney and Hanna Chandoo (Hadsell Stormer Renick & Dai)

Thursday, July 13 (12:00pm – 1:30pm)
Uncovering Racism in Our Immigration Law
Presented by Ahilan Arulanantham (UCLA School of Law, UCLA Center for Immigration Law &
Policy)

Thursday, July 27 (12:00pm – 1:00pm)
Supreme Court Roundup
Presented by Erwin Chemerinsky (UC Berkeley School of Law)
