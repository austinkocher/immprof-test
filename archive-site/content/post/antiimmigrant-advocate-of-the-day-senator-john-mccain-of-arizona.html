Earlier today, Bill Hing posted on an enforcement-only immigration reform proposal submitted by the Arizona twins, Senators Jon Kyl and John McCain.  Now, Senator McCain has endorsed the pending Arizona state law that should be named the Latino Racial Profiling Act of  2010:  “I think it’s a very important step forward,” McCain said Monday. “I can fully understand why the legislature would want to act.”  Read more at Politico.Com.

We understand that Senator McCain is facing a tough primary challenge and is veering to the right on many issues.  But the flip-flop of a Senator who once championed comprehensive immigration reform and his embrace of a thoroughly mean-spirited stance on immigration is not the kind of "maverick" that will earn him respect from ImmigrationProf..  Indeed, at this point, I would rather see Maverick from Top Gun or Lucas "The Rifleman" McCain in the U.S. Senate than John McCain
