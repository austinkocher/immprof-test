Reuters has this heartbreaking story about a long term resident removed from the United States:  "As U.S. President Joe Biden took the oath of office last Wednesday, Felipe Ortega sat in a van handcuffed and chained at the waist and feet, headed toward Mexico and the end of his 30-year life in the United States."

Ortega's family wept as he crossed the bored into Mexico.  From the story, it appears that the immigration court denied Ortega cancellation of removal..
