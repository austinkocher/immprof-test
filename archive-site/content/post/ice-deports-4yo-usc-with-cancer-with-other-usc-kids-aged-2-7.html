WaPo reports that ICE deported three kids aged 2, 4, and 7 on Friday. Did I mention all three are U.S. citizens? And the 4 year old has Stage 4 brain cancer?

The lack of humanity is truly astounding.

I should note that the government says the children's parents wanted the kids deported as the parents were slated for deportation themselves. Attorneys say that there was no opportunity for the adults slated for deportation to discuss options with their co-parents (not being deported) or their attorneys.
