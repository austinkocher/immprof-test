Davino Watson, a U.S. citizen, spent over three years in an Immigration and Customs Enforcement jail in Buffalo, New York, despite his citizenship. According to a lawsuit, authorities repeatedly failed to confirm his citizenship, letting him remain in detention for years.  Click here for more on this story. Watson was born in Jamaica and became a citizen after his father naturalized.
