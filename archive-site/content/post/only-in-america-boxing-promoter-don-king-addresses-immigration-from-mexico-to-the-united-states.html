Many Americans, even if not boxing fans, have heard of flamboyamt boxing promoter Don ("Only in America") KIng. Never one to avoid controversy in promoting a fight, King stepped in it in hyping a fight on Saturday night between two Mexican-American heavyweights, Chris Arreola and Eric Molina, in Texas.

Here is what King had to say:

“We even brought in a Mexican-American to give him a chance to get out and be at the front of the forefront. No more wetbacks running up and down picking the fruits and things. Now, we’re rolling, you know what I mean.”

Chris Arreola spoke in the ring by knocking out King's fighter, Molina, in the first round. And he also got in the last word in the post-fight interview in calling King a racist.
