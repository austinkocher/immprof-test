--- title: "Class action challenges DoD denials of military security clearance for naturalized soldiers" date: 2020-08-19T06:04:00 url: "/post/class-action-challenges-dod-denials-of-military-security-clearance-for-naturalized-soldiers/" aliases: ["/immigration/2020/08/class-action-challenges-dod-denials-of-military-security-clearance-for-naturalized-soldiers.html"] type: post year: "2020" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Law360 reports that a naturalized U.S. citizen who served in the Army has hit the U.S. Department of Defense with a proposed class action alleging the department is unlawfully denying foreign-born soldiers security clearance needed for higher-level military positions. A fuller discusson of this issue can be found in Citizenship Denied published this summer in the Denver U. L. Rev. (2020) and Ending Citizenship for Service in the Forever Wars published in the Yale Law Journal Forum.
