The Works in Progress (WIP) committee for the 2022 Immigration Law Teachers and Scholars’ Workshop (June 5th-7th) is now accepting proposals for works-in-progress or incubator ideas. The conference will be held at Loyola LA (UPDATE registration link here).

Submitting a WIP or Incubator Proposal: If you wish to be considered for a works-in-progress or incubator session, please submit your proposal via this google form. You can also indicate on this form whether or not you would like to be a discussant. Deadline for proposals is Friday April 29 (midnight PST); the deadline for the WIPs/Incubators is Friday May 20 (midnight PST).

  • For a WIP, please submit an abstract that is approximately 250-350 words in length.
  • For an incubator idea, please submit a paragraph description that is approximately 150-250 words in length.

Signing up as Potential Discussant: If you are not submitting a WIP/Incubator, but you would like to be a discussant, please use this link to sign up. 

Questions? Please email Liz ([email address removed]) or Deep (pgulasekaram@scu.edu) directly.
