UPDATE AND CORRECTION (Feb. 26):  I neglected to mention that that great source for immigration news, The Forum Daily from the National Immigration Forum, provided much of the following content:

All 67 county sheriffs in Florida have signed an agreement to work with Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officials, reports Nancy Guan of WUSF.   

Participants will be part of the 287(g) program, which broadens the immigration enforcement authority of state and local law enforcement.  

ICE is making similar deals across the country, reports Daniel Rivero of WLRN. Owyhee County, Idaho, the Douglas County Sheriff’s Office in Nevada, and the Kansas Bureau of Investigation are among the other entities agreeing to work with ICE.  

The National Immigration Forum has a resource on local and state cooperation with federal immigration authorities. In December, the Law Enforcement Immigration Task Force posted "Best Practices for Law Enforcement to Preserve Community Trust in the Context of Increased Immigration Enforcement." 

Image courtesy of Immigrant Legal Resource Center

With the uptick in immigration enforcement, "red cards" with information on rights and protections for immigrants are in high demand, reports Miriam Jordan of The New York Times.
