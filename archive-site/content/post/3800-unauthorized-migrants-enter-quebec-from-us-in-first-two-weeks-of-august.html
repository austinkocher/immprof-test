Photo by Artur Staszewski

As the BBC reports, some 3800 unauthorized migrants entered Quebec from the United States in the first two weeks of August. That's a fourfold increase over June numbers.

The majority of those fleeing the US for Canada are Haitian migrants. There is speculation that Haitians are fleeing North because of concerns that President Trump will end temporary protected status (TPS) for Haitians in January.

As we noted earlier this month, Quebec has already responded to the uptick by opening its Olympic Stadium to offer temporary housing.
