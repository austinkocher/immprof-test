The American Immigration Council's new report — Trump’s 2025 Travel Ban: Who Is Affected and What It Could Cost the U.S. Economy — details the economic and humanitarian costs of the Trump administration’s latest travel ban, which restricts immigration from 19 countries and may soon target 36 more.
Read the Travel Ban Report
 

According to the report, the nearly 300,000 people from the 19 impacted countries who came in 2022 contribute extensively to the U.S. economy. In 2023 alone, households led by these recent arrivals earned $3.2 billion in income, paid $715.6 million in taxes, and held $2.5 billion in spending power.  

They also fill jobs in industries facing labor constraints, such as healthcare, construction, and hospitality. As more Americans age out of the work force, the impacts of the travel ban will increase.
