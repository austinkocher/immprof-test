It is never a dull (immigration) moment with President Trump in office.  After threatening Mexico with tariffs, which was opposed by many farmers, the President has announced an agreement with Mexico on immigration enforcement and indefinitely putting tariffs on hold. 

As reported by CNN, President Trump said tariffs on Mexican goods are "indefinitely suspended" after negotiators from the US and Mexico were able to reach a deal on immigration enforcement.

"I am pleased to inform you that The United States of America has reached a signed agreement with Mexico," Trump tweeted Friday. "The Tariffs scheduled to be implemented by the U.S. on Monday, against Mexico, are hereby indefinitely suspended."

According to a joint statement between the U.S. and Mexican governments,
  • Mexico has agreed to deploy its national guard throughout the country with priority given to its southern border.
  • Asylum seekers will be returned to Mexico while their asylum claims are being adjudicated.
