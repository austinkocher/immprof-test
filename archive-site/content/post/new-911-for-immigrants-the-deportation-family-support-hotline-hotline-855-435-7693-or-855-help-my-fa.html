In response to the Obama administration's greatly expanded number of removals, immigrant advocates in Chicago have unveiled a legal aid and assistance hotline. The hotline — 855-435-7693 or 855-HELP-MY-F(amily) — is modeled after ones for homelessness or domestic violence, where volunteers take calls around the clock and guide callers to help.﻿  Created by the Illinois Coalition for Immigrant and Refugee Rights (ICIRR), the Deportation Family Support Hotline is run by volunteers.
