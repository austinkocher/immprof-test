CNN reports that Jeb Bush is turning conservative heads with recently surfaced comments at a conference hosted by the Hispanic Leadership Network in 2013 that are contrary to the tough anti-amnesty, anti-DREAMer sentiments of a segment of the Republican Party. 

Bush's 2013 book on immigration reform generated controversy in no small part because it proposed denial of a path to citizenship for undocumented immigrants.
