— Kevin Scott (@politiconyx) November 4, 2024

Although numerous studies show that noncitizen voting is incredibly rare, as well as illegal, Republican politicians, including former President Trump, repeatedly claim that it is rampant.  The Associated Press reports that a video circulating on social media that shows a man who says he is a Haitian immigrant claiming he is planning to vote in Georgia is fake. U.S. intelligence officials have confirmed that the video is the work of Russian influence creators.
