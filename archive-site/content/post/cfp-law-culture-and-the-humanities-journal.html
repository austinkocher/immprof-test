I'm absolutely intrigued by this year's theme from the Law Culture and the Humanities Journal--Absence, The Present and the Past. So many potential immigration angles!

If you're interested in this theme, consider applying to the upcoming 2023 Law Culture and the Humanities Journal conference. They're looking for topics related to theme of Absence that are rooted in law and legal studies.

The conference will take place from June 22-23, 2023 at the University of Toronto School of Law.

All proposals are due Friday, March 17, 2023 at 11:59 pm Eastern Standard Time.
