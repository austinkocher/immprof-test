Unfortunately, farmworkers, including many immigrant workers, continue to be subject to exploitation in the United States. Jorge Rivas on ColorLines reports that nearly 60 farmworkers from a rural Washington state community just outside of Seattle said they were stranded with no way to get home last week after refusing to work for less than minimum wage.
