At the New Voices of Immigration Law session at the AALS annual conference, immigration scholars gathered to provide input to junior colleagues on a wide range of works-in-progress. Look out for these excellent papers coming your way soon:

  • Jennifer Breen: Going Rogue: Administrative Agencies, the Rule of Law, and ICE
  • Kate Evans: Federal Immigration Detainers and the Discretion of the Sheriff
  • H. Marissa Montes: Preventing the Use of CalGang by Immigration Authorities
  • Jamie Abrams: Mobilizing Family Law to Reveal the Fracking of Immigrant Families
  • Pooja Dadhania: Paper Terrorists: Regime Change and the Terrorism Bar
  • Shalini Ray: The Law of Rescue

Thanks to all who attended and provided feedback!
