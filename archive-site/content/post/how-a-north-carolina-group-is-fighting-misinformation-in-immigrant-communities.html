President Trump has promised mass deportations. NPR reports that "[t]his has led to panic, rumors and misinformation within immigrant communities. Within the first week of Trump's second term, the hotline at Siembra NC, a North Carolina nonprofit, received about 600 calls, Most were false alarms. Many were related to social media posts about raids. Here's how Siembra NC's hotline is fact-checking and stopping those rumors. "
