--- title: "Record Number of Immigration-Related Bills and Resolutions Introduced in States During First Quarter of 2011" date: 2011-05-23T11:38:54 url: "/post/record-number-of-immigration-related-bills-and-resolutions-introduced-in-states-during-first-quarter-of-2011/" aliases: ["/immigration/2011/05/record-number-of-immigration-related-bills-and-resolutions-introduced-in-states-during-first-quarter-of-2011.html"] type: post year: "2011" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

According to a new immigration report from the National Conference of State Legislatures (NCSL), the 50 states and Puerto Rico have introduced a record 1,538 bills and resolutions relating to immigrants and refugees in the first quarter of 2011. This number surpasses the first quarter of 2010 by 358.
