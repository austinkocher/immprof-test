Political art from New Mexico:  A New Mexico artist is creating a sculpture to raise awareness about immigration policies.  Santa Fe sculptor L.C. Shank says his latest sculpture was inspired by the millions of migrants and refugees seeking a better life in America, creating a version of the Statue of Liberty that serves as a place of refuge for a migrant child.
