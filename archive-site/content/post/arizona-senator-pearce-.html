Think Progress reports that a group called Judicial Watch hosted a panel discussion on immigration enforcement featuring Arizona State Sen. Russell Pearce (R), the author of Arizona’s SB 1070.  Pearce joked that Obama may not want to come to Arizona:

"A little levity is okay, I hope cause I like to [inaudible] at most things. But I can tell you that the best thing about [SB]-1070 is that Obama may not be visiting Arizona because we actually require papers now."
