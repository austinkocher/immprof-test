Molly Ball in the Atlantic analyzes the "wrenching choice" faced by Latino voters in yesterday's midterm elections. While we have have to wait and see how (and whether) Latinos voted (although poll results suggest that Latino support for the President has fallen significantly), it is clear that a Republican tide has given the GOP full control of Congress for President Barack Obama's last two years as President.
