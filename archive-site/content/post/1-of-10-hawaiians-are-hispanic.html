Last May, ImmigrationProf reported on the increasing Hispanic population in that island paradise of Hawaii. Here is the latest on that development. According to Census 2010 data, nearly 1 in 10 Hawaii residents identifies as Hispanic, an increase of 38 percent from the last decade. Roughly 90 percent of Hawaii's Hispanics were born in the United States and are scattered throughout the islands. Many came to Hawaii for work and chose to settle there.
