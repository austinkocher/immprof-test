Associated Press reports that Donald Trump, until recently an adherent of the birthers, now is spreading the word that border patrol agents have been told to allow immigrants into the United States illegally "so they can vote in the election." An official of the National Border Patrol Council, Art Del Cueto said agents told him they had directives to ignore immigrants' criminal records so they can vote.  Neither Del Cueto nor Trump provided any evidence to support the claim. Newly admitted immigrants are not permitted to vote.
