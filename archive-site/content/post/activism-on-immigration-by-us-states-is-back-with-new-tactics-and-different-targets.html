Florida Governor Ron DeSantis has been leading the charge on state measures hostile to immigrants.  This report (Activism on Immigration by U.S. States Is Back, with New Tactics and Different Targets) looks at how U.S. states are using a novel array of strategies to advance their immigration agendas. "Florida and Texas have led the charge against unauthorized immigration with new laws, litigation, migrant buses and flights, and by sending the National Guard to the border. States such as Minnesota and Utah have gone the other way, expanding unauthorized immigrants’ access to benefits and services including driver’s licenses and in-state college tuition."
