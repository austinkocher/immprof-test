The Guardian is reporting that deportations from the UK have reached a five-year high. According to the report, "Between 5 July and 7 December 2024, a total of 13,460 returns were recorded, most of them voluntary." Human rights groups have raised concerns about deportations of asylum seekers whose lives could be at risk. The full article is available here.
