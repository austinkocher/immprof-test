Irreconcilable? Trump's EO and the INA by Cindy Galway Buys, Southern Illinois University School of Law, The Refuge, Vol. I, Issue 1.  Date Written: March 15, 2017

Abstract

This short article analyzes the consistency of President's Trump's first Executive Order on Immigration with the Immigration and Nationality Act and points out a potential conflict with the nondiscrimination provision of that Act.
