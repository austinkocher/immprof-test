As reported in several news outlets, CoreCivic has sued the New Jersey's Governor and Attorney General to challenge the state law ending detention contracts. Relying on preemption, CoreCivic claims that the state law that bans contracts with Immigration and Enforcement for immigration detention purposes.

CoreCivic currently runs the Elizabeth Detention Center in Elizabeth, New Jersey that both detains immigrants and houses an immigration court. Other detention facilities in the state, including in Bergen County, have already closed.

According to Sophie Nieto-Munoz of the New Jersey Monitor, CoreCivic filed its lawsuit on Friday.
