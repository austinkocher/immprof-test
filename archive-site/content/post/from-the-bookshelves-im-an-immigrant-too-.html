Mem Fox is one of those writers who just occupies an outsized portion of my brain space. Maybe it's the fact that I can still recite all the lines from Time for Bed by memory. ("It's time for bed little goose, little goose. The stars are out and on the loose.") Maybe it's her 2017 opinion piece about being detained by CBP ("In that moment, I loathed America."). I still use that piece when teaching--newly paired with a 2022 TikTok about secondary screening.

Today I found myself perusing her website, reading her absolutely fascinating biography--she was born in Australia, raised in Rhodesia (now Zimbabwe) by her missionary parents, attended drama school in London, and taught English in Rwanda, all before returning to Australia where she's stayed now for decades.

I also learned about Mem Fox's 2018 children's book: I'm an immigrant too! A fun picture book read for those immprofs with littles or grand-littles.

-KitJ
