The Harvard Crimson reports that 31 professors from Harvard, Tufts, MIT, Babson, and other Boston-area universities were arrested Thursday during a "planned act of civil disobedience."

The professors blocked traffic along Boston's busy Massachusetts Avenue in an effort to draw attention to their protest of Trump's rescinding of DACA.
