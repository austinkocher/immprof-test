CBS News Los Angeles reports that local organizations, including CHIRLA, SEIU 721, UTLA and CARCEN-LA, in the Los Angeles area are calling for boycotts and a "day of action" to protest the ongoing immigration raids.   "A coalition of organizations .  .  . says the federal enforcement is escalating, and they plan `stop the Trump terror through non-violent resistance such as rallying, car caravans, and marching.' . . . They are asking residents to participate in a 24-hour boycott, to attend rallies in MacArthur Park and downtown LA and support local businesses and street vendors." 

The organizations reportedly are asking people to avoid shopping at Home Depot, Target, Walmart and several other businesses, which have been targeted for immigration raids.   "A spokesperson for CHIRLA said they are asking people to boycott those specific corporations because they believe `they can do more to speak out against the ICE raids.'"
