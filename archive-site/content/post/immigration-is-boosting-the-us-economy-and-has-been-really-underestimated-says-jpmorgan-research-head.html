I have always been struck how so much of the political debate over immigration focuses on the alleged economic costs.  Little attention is paid to the economic benefits of immigration, which are highlighted in this short news article.  "The recent surge in immigration into the U.S. is helping to bolster the economy despite a raft of global challenges, according to Joyce Chang, chair of global research at JPMorgan."
