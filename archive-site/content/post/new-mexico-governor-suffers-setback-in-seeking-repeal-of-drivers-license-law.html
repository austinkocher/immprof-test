At least for now, New Mexico Governor Susana Martinez has lost the battle to eliminate driver's license eligibility for undocumented immigrants in New Mexico.  Martinez, a former prosecutor, supports repeal of the state law that allows people to obtain driver's licenses without proof of a legal immigration status. Three Democrats on the House Consumer and Public Affairs Committee blocked a bill that would have required a Social Security number or proper immigration documents to qualify for a driver's license.

Washington, Utah, and New Mexico are the only three states that permit undocumented immigrants to secure a driver's license.  Here is a fact sheet from the NATIONAL IMMIGRATION LAW CENTER NATIONAL on "Why Denying Driver’s Licenses to Undocumented Immigrants Harms Public Safety and Makes Our Communities Less Secure."
