As Kevin and Ming have both discussed, President Trump is looking to implement a significant ban on immigration to the United States. Here are the latest details from CNN:

  • The goal is to protect the American workers.
  • It will apply only to immigrants seeking green cards (permanent residency).
  • It will not apply to nonimmigrants coming to the United States for a limited time and a limited purpose.
  • This "pause" will last 60 days.
  • "The executive order is being written now as we speak," Trump said today, and will "probably" be signed tomorrow.
