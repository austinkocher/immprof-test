Immigration attorney Olsi Vrapi tells a diversity immigrant story very different from the one that President Trump tells in seeking to dismantle the diversity visa program:  "I often joke about having won the lottery with a prize of $0, but in reality my wealth, prosperity, and success largely stem from one crucial fork in the road of my life.  The diversity lottery allowed me to build a successful business through hard work, utilizing my ambition and talents.  What is more American than that?."
