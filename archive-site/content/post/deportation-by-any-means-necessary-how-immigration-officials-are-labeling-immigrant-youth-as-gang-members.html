President Trump has painted with a broad brush immigrants from El Salvador as MS-13 members (and Mexicans as criminals).  This report from the Immigrant Legal Resource Center details findings from a national survey of legal practitioners concerning the increased use of gang allegations against young immigrants as a means of driving up deportation numbers, at the encouragement of the Trump administration. The report suggests emerging best practices for immigration attorneys to employ in both fighting against unfounded gang allegations and working to mitigate the impact of prior gang involvement.  Click here for more on the report.
