Sharemoney supports the vision of foreign immigrants moving to the US to build better lives for themselves and their families. The work and educational opportunities in this country are often not available in other parts of the world and we understand the effort required to realize these dreams.  For that reason Sharemoney is proud to offer a $1,000 scholarship for students embarking on this journey.

Eligibility Requirements:  Enrollment:  The Sharemoney Immigrant Support Scholarship is open to first and second generation immigrants enrolled or in the process of enrolling in an undergraduate degree program at Rutgers University. Enrollment must occur by September 1, 2020.
