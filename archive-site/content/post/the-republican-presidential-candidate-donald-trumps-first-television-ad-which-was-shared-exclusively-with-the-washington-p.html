The Republican presidential candidate Donald Trump's first television ad, which was shared exclusively with The Washington Post, is set to launch today as part of a series that will air in the final month before the Iowa caucuses. Islamic terrorism and immigration from Mexico are the centerpieces of the ad.  Trump has vowed to spend at least $2 million a week on the television ads.
