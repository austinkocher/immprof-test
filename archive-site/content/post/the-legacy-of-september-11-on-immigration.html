It is September 11 and I was tempted not to post about the impacts on immigration of that fateful day.  However, I do think that this report dated September 11, 2011 from Penn State Law (with essays, remarks, and photographs) on the legacy of September 11 on immigration law is worth a plug.  The anthology of 9/11 reflections concentrates on the impact of the attacks on the lives of immigrants and immigration policy, providing both a report card and ideas for the future.  The report remains highly relevant.
