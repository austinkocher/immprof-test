Below is the Family Law Quarterly's Call for Papers on Race and Family Law. Abstracts are due September 17, 2021, and drafts of selected papers are due February 1, 2022. Submissions can range from 6,000 to 15,000 words. The issue will be published in Summer 2022. For additional information, please see the pdf below. For questions, please email Jessica Dixon Weaver at jdweaver@mail.smu.edu or Lisa Grumet at lisa.grumet@nyls.edu.
