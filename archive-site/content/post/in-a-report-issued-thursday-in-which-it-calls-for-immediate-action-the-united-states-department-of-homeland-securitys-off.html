In a report issued last week, the United States Department of Homeland Security’s Office of Inspector General detailed several striking observations about the overcrowded and unsanitary conditions at the El Paso Del Norte immigration processing center during unannounced inspections in early May.  The N.Y. Times and CNN reported on the IG report.
