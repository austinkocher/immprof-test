Lucas Guttentag's secret project to track every Trump immigration policy with Yale Law and Stanford Law students is now publicly reported on and available. This article in the New Yorker describes the project. The Immigration Policy Tracking Project a.k.a. Trump Tracker website provides access to data, upon registration and request. A real treasure trove and service to researchers, practitioners, and immigrants!
