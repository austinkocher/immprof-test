It is summer time.  It is hot.  And the risks facing migrants seeking to enter the United States without inspection are great.

The southern border crossing can be lethal for migrants. U.S. Border Patrol data shows that since October 1, nearly 55 migrants have lost their lives in El Paso and Southern New Mexico. With triple-digit heat, authorities are warning migrants of the danger the heat poses in their journey, reports Julian Resendiz for Border Report.
