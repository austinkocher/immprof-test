Grannies Respond/Abuelas Responden seeks secondhand winter coats to help legally processed migrants on the Texas-Mexico border stay warm, reports Sandra Sanchez of Border Report. Organization leaders launched the effort after a visit to the Rio Grande Valley in October where they heard the needs of Team Brownsville, a nonprofit that assists migrants.
