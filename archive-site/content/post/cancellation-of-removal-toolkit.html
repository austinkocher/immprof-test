As part of its mission to promote a modernized immigration system through representation of immigrant advocacy organizations, Penn State Law's Center for Immigrants' Rights has teamed up with the Pennsylvania Immigration Resource Center (PIRC) on various projects.  Today, the Center and PIRC released a toolkit for practitioners on LPR cancellation of removal. A link to the press release and public version of the toolkit can be found here.  Please note that sections of the public toolkit have been redacted. If you are a practitioner and would like to obtain the unabridged version of the toolkit, please send an email to centerforimmigrantsr@psu.edu or mbremer@pirclaw.org.
