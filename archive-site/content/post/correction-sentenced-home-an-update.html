Last week I posted about the 2007 documentary Sentenced Home and the Youtube update to the documentary. In that post (which I have now deleted), I got some major facts mixed up. Many Uch--one of the individuals at the heart of Sentenced Home--reached out to correct the record. (THANK YOU!)

Sentenced Home follows the lives of three young men who came to the United States as child refugees, got into legal trouble, and faced deportation to Cambodia, the country their families sought refuge from decades ago.

Many Uch is one of the men featured in the documentary. Uch joined gang in his youth. At 18, Uch was arrested for driving the getaway car in the armed robbery of a rival gang member's house. He spent three years in prison for the crime. After prison, Uch entered the immigration system. He was ordered deported and kept in detention. At the time, Cambodia was not accepting deportees with criminal records. After litigation, Uch was released from detention. Ultimately, Uch was pardoned, became a U.S. citizen, got his U.S. passport and, in 2022, Uch returned to Cambodia to visit with Lun and Kimho--the third man at the heart of Sentenced Home.
