 California Attorney General Rob Bonta has released his office's 2021 Hate Crime in California .  The report provides information and resources to support ongoing efforts across the state to combat hate.  2021 saw a 32.6% increase in overall reported hate crimes for a total of 1,763 bias events—the highest reported level since 2001. The report also states that hate crimes involving religious bias increased by 21.1% in 2021.   The Executive Summary states that

"Reported hate crime events involving a racial bias overall increased 33.1 percent from 875 in 2020 to
1,165 in 2021. (Table 11)
• Anti-black or African American bias events rose from 456 in 2020 to 513 in 2021, an increase of 12.5
percent.
• Anti-Hispanic or Latino events rose from 152 in 2020 to 197 in 2021, an increase of 29.6 percent.
• Anti-Asian bias events rose from 89 in 2020 to 247 in 2021, an increase of 177.5 percent." (bold added).
