A Mural at in the Courtyard at UC Davis School of Law

Happy Martin Luther King Jr. Day!  I commend you to the Aoki Center on Critical Race and Nation Studies's inaugural blog post by immigration professor, and co-director of the Aoki Center, Raquel Aldana. It is entitled "MLK and Border Justice."  Although written over a year ago, the post's conclusion remains relevant:

"President Biden is under extreme domestic pressure to control the border, seemingly at any human cost. Over the years, border policies have contributed to thousands of migrant deaths at the U.S.-Mexico border . . . . Domestic calls for tough on immigration control at the border, often a bipartisan project, have deadly consequences. They are also shortsighted. They will not work because they fail to address the root causes of migration, which include externalities from or the exportation of domestic U.S. policies to Latin America whose casualties are the migrants at our Southern border whose suffering we fail to heed."

Tomorrow, as part of a week of volunteer service in honor of Dr. King, UC Davis Law students are volunteering to the community.  Among the activities:

A donation drive for Wellspring Women's Center.

A blood drive at the School of Law.

Service day at Fourth and Hope in Woodland. Fourth and Hope's mission is to feed, clothe, shelter and facilitate recovery for those in need.

A luncheon program will feature a keynote address from Superior Court Judge Allison Williams. Before she was appointed as a superior court judge by Governor Gavin Newsom in 2021, Judge Williams served as an attorney for 15 years at the Sacramento Public Defender's Office. Tune in at noon to watch the celebration live.
