Courtneay Brown for Axios reminds us that "[p]lummeting fertility rates and major demographic shifts are expected to slow U.S. population growth in the years ahead, with a steady flow of immigrants needed to offset the economic consequences."  The collision of immigration policy and economic growth likely will continue to shape politics for decades to come.
