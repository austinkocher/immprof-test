In an article (Does the Crime Fit the Punishment?: Recent Judicial Actions Expanding the Rights of Noncitizens), the current issue of the California Law Review Circuit, Aarti Kohli of UC Berkeley's Earl Warren Institute examines the implications of Padilla v. Kentucky and Carachuri-Rosendo v. Holder on expanding the rights of immigrants facing deportation. She also argues for a more balanced approach to deportation with discretion for adjudicators and procedural protections for noncitizens.
