Upcoming Event: Bad Hombres Documentary Screening and Discussion
 
Friday, February 5, 2021 4:30PM - 6:30PM PST (7:30PM - 9:30PM EST)
 
Please join a screening of Bad Hombres, a documentary that tracks the stereotypes and policies used to other Latinx people as suspect criminals, both in the context of the criminal punishment system and the criminalization of immigrants. A conversation featuring film director Carlos Sandoval and President and General Counsel of LatinoJustice (formerly Puerto Rican Legal Defense and Education Fund) Juan Cartagena, moderated by CRS Faculty Director Laura E. Gómez, will follow.
