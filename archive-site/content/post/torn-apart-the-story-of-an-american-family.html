Read and watch "Torn Apart."  For a year, a San Jose a Mercury News reporter and photojournalist followed a San Mateo family on their emotional journey through the U.S. immigration system. Both parents came here as undocumented immigrants, but all six of their children are U.S. citizens.  The father was deported and the mother is fighting deporation.
