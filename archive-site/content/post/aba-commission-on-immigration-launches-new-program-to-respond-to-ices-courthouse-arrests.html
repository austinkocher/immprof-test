Immigration arrests at immigration court hearings have been on the increase.  The ABA Journal reports that the ABA Commission on Immigration is launching a new program to respond.  The Immigration Court Observation and Awareness Project aims to identify trends and systemic issues by asking volunteers to observe short preliminary hearings and record their observations. The commission plans to use this data to better support noncitizens’ access to counsel and due process rights.
