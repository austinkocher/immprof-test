The "Undocumented Immigrants" Who Are Actually Citizens

Thanks to confusing citizenship requirements, people can live for decades without knowing their true status.

Vice reports on a UC Davis Immigration Clinic case involving two men who believed they were undocumented and feared removal only to find out that they were U.S. citizens.
