ImmigrationProf blogger Austin Kocher reviews (and here) of Private Violence: Latin American Women and the Struggle for Asylum by Carol Cleaveland and Michele Waslin.  He writes that the book offers " a powerful look at the lives of Latin American women seeking asylum in the U.S. after fleeing domestic abuse and gang violence."
