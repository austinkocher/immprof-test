Road sign near Kapchorwa, Uganda, 2004

Fear of female genital mutilation can serve as the basis of an asylum claim.  

Samantha Schmidt for the Washington Post reported that a federal judge dismissed criminal charges against two doctors in a female genital mutilation case and ruled that Congress “overstepped its bounds” by passing a law banning the procedure. The case centered on two Michigan doctors and six others charged last year in connection with arranging to perform female genital mutilation on nine girls. While female genital mutilation has been a federal crime in the United States for more than two decades, the Michigan doctors were the first to be charged under the law.
