This June 1,  2017 U.S. General Accountability Office report finds that that the Executive Office for Immigration Review's (EOIR) case backlog—cases pending from previous years that remain open at the start of a new fiscal year—more than doubled from fiscal years 2006 through 2015 primarily due to declining cases completed per year.  For GAO recommendations on how to address the backlog, click here.
