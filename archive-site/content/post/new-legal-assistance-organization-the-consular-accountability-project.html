The Consular Accountability Project is a legal defense organization protecting the rights of visa applicants and bringing accountability to the consular process.

According to its website, "[t]he Consular Accountability Project is a legal defense organization fighting for accountability and oversight of the consular process. By educating and representing individuals, families and businesses through the consular process, raising public awareness of the impact of wrongful visa denials and engaging in strategic litigation, CAP will protect the rights of impacted parties, bring accountability to the consular process, correct executive abuse and protect the separation of powers."
