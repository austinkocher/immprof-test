--- title: "California attorney general asks lawyers to help immigrant children" date: 2014-07-01T12:00:00 url: "/post/california-attorney-general-asks-lawyers-to-help-immigrant-children/" aliases: ["/immigration/2014/07/california-attorney-general-asks-lawyers-to-help-immigrant-children.html"] type: post year: "2014" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" date_from_address: true ---

Reuters reports that California Attorney General Kamala Harris has asked several major law firms to provide pro bono services to unaccompanied immigrant children coming to the United States. "We've convened a group of law firms to make sure these children, some of them as young as 8 years old, have access to due process," Harris said on Thursday.
