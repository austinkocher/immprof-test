California Supreme Court Justice Goodwin Liu

UC Davis School of Law is proud to host the 2016 Conference of Asian Pacific American Law Faculty (CALALF) this weekend. There is a new addition to an already outstanding speaker line-up: California Supreme Court Justice Goodwin Liu.

Here is the program from the CAPALF website.
