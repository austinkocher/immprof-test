US adoptions from South Korean once accounted for the largest number of international adoptions. A human interest story from NPR explains how Korean-born adoptees who feel distant from both the country of their birth and the country where they were raised, but in recent years return to South Korean seeking to build ties with their birth families and better understand their heritage.
