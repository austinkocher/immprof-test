Interesting episode of the NPR Politics Podcast today titled "Biden Administration, NYC Spar Over Immigration Policy."

It analyzes the clashes between Mayor Eric Adams and the Biden administration on how to integrate asylum seekers in New York City. Among other things, Mayor Adams has pushed the Biden administration to speed up the process to provide work permits for asylum seekers.
