The Port of Entry

Latino USA recently took an in-depth look at a port of entry at the US/Mexico border.  The wait time for migrants seeking asylum at legal ports of entry along the U.S.-Mexico border has recently increased from hours to weeks, causing some families to camp out for days. We go to the border to meet some of the people waiting there and explain the asylum process in the United States.
