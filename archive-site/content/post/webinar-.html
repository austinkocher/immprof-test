On July 11, 2023 at 10 a.m. Pacific, the American Immigration Council is hosting an interesting webinar on the recent Supreme Court cases on immigration and what is to come next term.

The webinar will feature Karen Tumlin of the Justice Action Center and Ahilan Arulanantham of UCLA's Center for Immigration Law and Policy. The session will be moderated by Kate Melloy Goettel, Legal Director of Litigation at the American Immigration Council.
