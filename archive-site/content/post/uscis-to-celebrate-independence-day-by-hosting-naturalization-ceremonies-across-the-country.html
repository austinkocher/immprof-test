--- title: "USCIS to Celebrate Independence Day by Hosting Naturalization Ceremonies Across the Country" date: 2020-07-02T04:27:13 url: "/post/uscis-to-celebrate-independence-day-by-hosting-naturalization-ceremonies-across-the-country/" aliases: ["/immigration/2020/07/uscis-to-celebrate-independence-day-by-hosting-naturalization-ceremonies-across-the-country.html"] type: post year: "2020" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services has announced that it is celebrating the Declaration of Independence and the birth of our great country by hosting naturalization ceremonies across the United States from July 1 through July 7. Despite the pandemic, USCIS continues to welcome new citizens, demonstrating American resiliency and the importance of our independence.

USCIS began to conduct limited naturalization ceremonies in May and began to fully resume ceremonies in early June, after temporarily pausing in-person services to mitigate the spread of COVID-19. The ceremonies have been shorter to limit exposure to those in attendance, incorporating social distancing and other safety precautions that protect the health and safety of applicants and USCIS staff. USCIS has naturalized approximately 64,500 new citizens over the past month and anticipates completing nearly all postponed administrative naturalization ceremonies by the end of July.
