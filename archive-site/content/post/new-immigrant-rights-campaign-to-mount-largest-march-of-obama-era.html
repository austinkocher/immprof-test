We have been reporting on the March 21 immigration march.  Randy Shaw writes about the possibilities for this mass march on Huffington Post.  One wonders whether we will see anything like the mass marches of the spring of 2006, which resulted in the end of the deadly Sensenbrenner Bill and discussion of a much more balanced immigration reform bill in the U.S. Senate.  This march is a chance to show just how immportant immigration reform is to so many people.
