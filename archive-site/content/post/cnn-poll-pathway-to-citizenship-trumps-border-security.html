--- title: "CNN Poll: Pathway to citizenship trumps border security" date: 2014-02-06T14:23:09 url: "/post/cnn-poll-pathway-to-citizenship-trumps-border-security/" aliases: ["/immigration/2014/02/cnn-poll-pathway-to-citizenship-trumps-border-security.html"] type: post year: "2014" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

CNN reports that Americans overwhelmingly favor a bill that would give most undocumented immigrants a pathway towards citizenship, according to a new national poll. And a CNN/ORC International survey also indicates that a majority of the public says that the government's main focus should be legalizing the status of the undocumented rather than border security.
