Immigration law professors and students can join a discussion on Immigration Narratives in the Age of Disinformation, co-hosted by Define American and Cornell's Xenophobia Meter Project. Jose Antonio Vargas and Dr. Joan Donovan will discuss combatting xenophobia and misinformation targeting immigrant communities. The event (5:30 PM on Friday, September 8, registration here.  It will be a hybrid event.

It should be a lively event.  Vargas visited UC Davis a few years ago and it was entertaining as well as informative.
