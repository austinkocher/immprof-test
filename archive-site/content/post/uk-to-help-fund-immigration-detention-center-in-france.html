As Kit Johnson recently blogged, the United Kingdom is responding to domestic concerns with immigration.  The Guardian (UK) now reports that Britain will help fund a detention center in northern France as part of a £500m package to stop refugees trying to cross the English Channel, UK Prime Minister Rishi Sunak has said, amid continuing criticism of his plans to lock up and deport migrants arriving in small boats.

Sunak and Emmanuel Macron, the French president, announced that they had agreed joint funding for more French border patrols, including 500 additional officers and new drones.  The joint summit came after Sunak this week proposed legislation that would allow for the detention and deportation of migrants coming to the UK across the Channel on small boats.
