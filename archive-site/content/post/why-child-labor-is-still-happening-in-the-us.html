For Slate, Mary Harris had an interesting interview with Congresswoman Hillary Scholten (D-MI (Grand Rapids)), who has immigration law experience and condemned the recent reports of child migrant labor abuse.  Click here to listen to the interview.  The Congresswoman has experience in immigration, having worked as a staff attorney for the Board of Immigration Appeals from 2013 to 2017 and later the Michigan Immigrant Rights Center.  The interview covers Scholten's career. the reports of child migrant labor abuse, and possible congressional responses.
