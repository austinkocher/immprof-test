Karen Pita Loor and Cassidy Heverling for SCOTUSBlog preview oral argument on Monday in a pair of cases, Pugin v. Garland and Garland v. Cordero-Garcia, involving whether a federal law that allows the U.S. government to remove  noncitizens convicted of “an offense relating to obstruction of justice” applies to cases that are not connected to open investigations or judicial proceedings.
