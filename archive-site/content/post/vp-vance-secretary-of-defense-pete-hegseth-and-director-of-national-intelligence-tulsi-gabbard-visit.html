Vice President JD Vance, Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth, and Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard visited the US-Mexico border yesterday.

Kathryn Watson for The Hill reports that Vance said that "President Trump hopes to build the entire wall along the southern border by the time his term concludes . . . .  Vance made the comments as he, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and made a day trip to the border town of Eagle Pass, Texas, to highlight the Trump administration's efforts to combat illegal crossings and drug trafficking."
