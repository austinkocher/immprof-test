Lawful Assembly is a podcast featuring Rev. Craig B. Mousin, founder and former Director of the Midwest Immigrant Rights Center and an Adjunct Faculty member at DePaul University’s College of Law and The Grace School of Applied Diplomacy.

The most recent two episodes might be of particularly interest to immprofs.

  • Episode 11, Building a Welcoming City, celebrates the new amendments to Chicago's Welcoming City Ordinance.
  • And Episode 10, Rebuild Refugee Resettlement, encourages the rebuilding of Illinois' commitment for refugee resettlement.
