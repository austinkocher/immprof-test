The campaign for the White House continues.  Michael Scherer for the Washington Post reports that the "Trump campaign’s first television ad of the general election attacks likely Democratic nominee Kamala Harris as an evasive, weak and distracted leader who did not protect the U.S.-Mexico border from drug trafficking, increased migrant crossings and a possible terrorism threat during the Biden administration."

It is telling for the campaign that immigration is the topic of former President Trump's first television ad.  Expect much discussion of immigration through November.
