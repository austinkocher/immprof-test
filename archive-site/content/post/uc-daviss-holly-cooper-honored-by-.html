--- title: "UC Davis's Holly Cooper Honored by National Lawyers Guild Immigration Project" date: 2011-08-04T05:08:03 url: "/post/uc-daviss-holly-cooper-honored-by-/" aliases: ["/immigration/2011/08/uc-daviss-holly-cooper-honored-by-.html"] type: post year: "2011" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Holly Cooper of the UC Davis Immigration Law Clinic will receive the Carol Weiss King Award at the National Lawyers Guild "Law for the People 2011" convention, held in Philadelphia October 12-16. The award, presented by the National Lawyers Guild Immigration Project, honors excellence in the pursuit of social justice through organizing, litigating, and teaching.
