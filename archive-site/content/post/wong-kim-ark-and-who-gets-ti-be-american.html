The Trump administration is facing a legal battle over birthright citizenship for people born in the U.S. whose parents are here illegally. A central argument is a centuries-old question: Who is truly American, and who gets to decide? On NPR's The Sunday Story from Up First, examine these questions through the Supreme Court's 1898 decision in the Wong Kim Ark case that transformed the lives of generations of people. Listen ti expert commentary by immigration law professor Amanda Frost.
