Learn More
 
Dear fellow ImmProfs,
 
Please save the date for the 2023 Biennial Emerging Immigration Scholars Conference to be held at the George Washington University Law School in Washington, D.C. on June 15-16, 2023! 
 
This conference is intended to provide a space for immigration law professors with fewer than ten years in legal academia, or who are pre-tenured, to share and receive feedback on scholarship in a supportive setting. 
 
A call for papers, programming, and information about registration is forthcoming.
 
We hope to see you there!
 
Cori Alonso-Yoder & Tania Valdez
