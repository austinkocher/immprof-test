On November 2, a new lawsuit was filed in the Northern District of California challenging the Trump Administration’s recent effort to expand the crime-related bars to asylum eligibility. 

Four immigrant rights organizations—Pangea Legal Services, Dolores Street Community Services, Catholic Legal Immigration Network, Inc. (“CLINIC”), and Capital Area Immigrants’ Rights Coalition (“CAIR”)—filed the lawsuit against the Department of Homeland Security and the Department of Justice, challenging and seeking to enjoin the Administration’s new Procedures for Asylum and Bars to Asylum Eligibility Rule, which is scheduled to go into effect on November 20, 2020. The lawsuit details why the new rule violates the INA, the APA, and the Fifth Amendment of the U.S. Constitution.

The plaintiffs are represented by the Harvard Immigration and Refugee Clinical Program, the National Immigration Project of the National Lawyers Guild, the Immigrant Defense Project, and the law firm of Sidley Austin LLP.
