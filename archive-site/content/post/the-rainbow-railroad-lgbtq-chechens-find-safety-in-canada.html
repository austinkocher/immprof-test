For months we've been hearing about the persecution of LGBTQ individuals in Chechnya: arrests, interrogation, torture, round-ups, "cleansing."

It turns out that Canada was paying attention. A Canadian nonprofit called The Rainbow Railroad recently announced that it has been working with Canadian authorities to secure refugee status for 22 gay men and lesbians from Chechnya.

As the NYT reports,

The first among them arrived from a safe house in Russia in June. Another nine are expected to arrive in the coming week, and the program is ongoing, with more expected to arrive.

The Canadian government has been "tight-lipped" about the program, though Prime Minister Justin Trudeau has condemned the “reprehensible reports of violations of the human rights of gay and bisexual men in Chechnya.”
