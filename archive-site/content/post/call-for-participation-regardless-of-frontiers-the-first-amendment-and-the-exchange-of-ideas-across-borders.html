Call for Participation:  Regardless of Frontiers: The First Amendment and the Exchange of Ideas Across Borders

The Knight First Amendment Institute at Columbia University invites submissions for a symposium, “Regardless of Frontiers: The First Amendment and the Exchange of Ideas Across Borders,” to be held at Columbia University on Oct. 18, 2024. The symposium will explore the changing role of the international border as a venue, justification, or pretext for censorship or surveillance, and as an obstacle to censorship and persecution by foreign governments.
