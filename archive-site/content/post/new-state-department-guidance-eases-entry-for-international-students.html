Toward the end of 2021, the State Department revised its guidance to make it easier for international students to obtain visas. The updates, specifically under 9 FAM 402.5-5(E)(1), should ameliorate student visa denials based on sepculative determinations by consular officers.

More information about responses to the guidance is contained in an article by Stuart Anderson for Forbes.
