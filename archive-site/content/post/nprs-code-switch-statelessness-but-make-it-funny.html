Code Switch last week had an episode “Statelessness, but make it funny,” in which the hosts talk with Mo Amer, the creator of the comedy series Mo. I have watched the series and found it incredibly funny. The show focuses on a Palestinian man navigating the U.S. immigration system while he hopes to one day return to his family’s homeland. Amer talked about what it means to be viewed as a spokesperson for Palestinian Americans.
