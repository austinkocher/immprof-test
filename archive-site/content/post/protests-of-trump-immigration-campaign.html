Newsweek reports that protests have erupted across the country over the weekend in response to President Donald Trump's immigration deportation campaign with raids.

In Los Angeles, protesters gathered on LA's historic Olvera Street, a cultural site representing the Mexican history, before marching to City Hall. They called for immigration reform and carried banners with slogans like “Nobody is illegal.”
