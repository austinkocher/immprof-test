It is a tight 2024 presidential race and immigration is a big issue in the campaign.  

Most supporters of Vice President Kamala Harris want a tougher stance on tackling illegal immigration, according to polling conducted for Newsweek, which also shows that Americans feel the U.S. has lost control of the southern border.
