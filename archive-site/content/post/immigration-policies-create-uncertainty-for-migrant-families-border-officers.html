Whatever one thinks of the Biden administration's first year in office when it comes to immigration, there unquestionably is uncertainty of where the nation will go from here immigration-wise.   ABC News’ Mireya Villareal speaks exclusively to the U.S. Border Patrol chief who is preparing officers for a possible shift in policies as migrant families wait in limbo in Mexico.

Morale among the rank-and-file of the Border Patrol is said to be uneven if not poor.
