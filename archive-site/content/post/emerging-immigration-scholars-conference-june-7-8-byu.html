SAVE THE DATE

The 5th biannual Emerging Immigration Scholars Conference will take place at the BYU J. Reuben Clark Law School in Provo, Utah, on June 7 & 8, 2019. The focus of the conference will be supporting emerging scholars in the development of their works-in-progress.

We’ll provide updates about other programming and registration in the months to come. For now, mark your calendars, and plan to enjoy a gorgeous summer event in Utah.

We look forward to seeing many of you in June 2019.
