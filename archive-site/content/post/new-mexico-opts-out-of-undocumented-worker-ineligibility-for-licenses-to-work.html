A growing number of states are allowing undocumented immigrants to secure professional licenses.  Sachin Pandya on Workplace Prof blog (New Mexico Opts Out of Undocumented Worker Ineligibility for Licenses to Work) reports that "[l]ast week, New Mexico opted out of a federal statute that makes undocumented workers ineligible for State or local work licenses -- the licenses required by law to do certain jobs."
