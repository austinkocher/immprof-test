President Trump has repeatedly attacked MS-13, an undisputedly violent gang that includes many Latino youth.  In a thorough article in the New York Times Magazine, Hannah Dreier describes "How a Crackdown on MS-13 Caught Up Innocent High School Students."  She documents how "the Trump administration went after gang members — and instead destroyed the American dreams of immigrant teenagers around the country."

This article is a collaboration between the New York Times and ProPublica, the independent nonprofit investigative-journalism organization.
