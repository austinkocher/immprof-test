PRESS RELEASE FROM THE INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATION FOR MIGRATION:  Berlin/ Geneva, 21 March 2025 – At least 8,938 people died on migration routes worldwide in 2024, making it the deadliest year on record, according to new data collected by the International Organization for Migration (IOM).  

The analysis in this press release is based on data available as of 20 March 2025. For the latest figures, click here.
