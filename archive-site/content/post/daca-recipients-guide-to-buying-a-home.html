DACA Recipient’s Guide to Buying a Home.

Deferred Action on Childhood Arrivals ( DACA) recipients may face more challenges when finding a home loan.  This guide provides information before buying their first house.

The guide includes the following:

  • How to find a home loan as a DACA recipient 
  • List of required documents for a DACA mortgage
  • How the home-buying process works
