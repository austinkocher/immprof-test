CNN reports that the summer Olympics is a peaceful global event that brings us together every four years.  The coronavirus has another victim.  Cnn reports that Japan’s Prime Minister Shinzo Abe says he has reached an agreement with International Olympic Committee President Thomas Bach to postpone the Olympics for about one year.

Summer 2021 is the latest that these Olympics could be held, Abe said.
