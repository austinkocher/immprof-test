Anti-Blackness in Global Migration 

On Thursday, April 11th, 2024, at 10 a.m. EST, this webinar looks at how anti-immigration policies are impacting Black migrants around the world. Click here to register. 

In 2023, the Black Alliance for Just Immigration (BAJI) co-hosted an event on transnational anti-Black immigration policies. This was during the 2nd session of the Permanent Forum on People of African Descent, and our co-hosts were Global Research Forum on Diaspora & Transnationalism, Evangelical Lutheran Church in America (ELCA), and African Foundation for Development (AFFORD). Host countries around the world are violating migrant rights and imposing draconian, punitive, violent, and sometimes deadly policy responses. 

We are pleased to build towards the Third Session of the Permanent Forum for People of African Descent being held at the UN headquarters in Geneva, where we shall be discussing our recent report: Anti-Blackness in Global Migration: Findings and Recommendations for the Permanent Forum of People of African Descent and Beyond.
