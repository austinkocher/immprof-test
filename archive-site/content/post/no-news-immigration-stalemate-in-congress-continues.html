It is hard keeping up with what is going on in Congress in discussing immigration reform. 

Ed O'Keefe, David Nakamura and Mike DeBonis for the Washington Post reports that weeksof intense negotiations for a bipartisan deal on immigration collapsed in Congress on Thursday, leaving hundreds of thousands of young undocumented immigrants facing possible deportation. The rejection of four proposals in the Senate, coupled with a lack of consensus in the House, underscored the immense political pressures on Republicans and Democrats alike.
