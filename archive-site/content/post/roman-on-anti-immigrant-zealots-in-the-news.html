Ediberto Roman (Florida International) on Huffington Post rants about anti-immigrant, anti-Latino zealots who the media often give prominence -- and a free pass -- to. The appearance of Congressman Dana Rohrabacher (R-CA) on Bill Maher's show drove Professor Roman to the brink. Lucky for him, he has not had to deal with Rep. Rohrabacher's anti-immigrant diatribes for a generation; Rohrabacher was a big supporter of Proposition 187 in 1994.
