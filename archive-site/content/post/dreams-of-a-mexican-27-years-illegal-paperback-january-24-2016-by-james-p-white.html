Dreams of a Mexican: 27 Years Illegal by James P White

Roberto Hernandez (pseudonym) has lived illegally in the U.S. for 27 years. He first crossed the Mexican border when he was 17. His life shows the underbelly of American labor and the personal strength it takes to build character while facing a society organized against you.
