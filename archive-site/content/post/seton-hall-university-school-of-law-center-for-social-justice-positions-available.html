The Seton Hall Law School Center for Social Justice seeks to hire two Practitioners-in-Residence for one year, starting in July 2011, with the expectation of renewal for one additional year, dependent on grant funding and performance. One position is in the International Human Rights/Rule of Law Project, and focuses on the rights of immigrant women in New Jersey. The other is in the Urban Revitalization Project, with a focus on housing discrimination, foreclosure defense, and public education reform.
