SCOTUSBlog has a "Symposium before the oral argument in Department of Commerce v. New York." The case involves a challenge to the Trump administration's proposed citizenship question on Census 2020.  The district court enjoined the secretary of the Department of Commerce from reinstating a question about citizenship to the 2020 decennial census.
