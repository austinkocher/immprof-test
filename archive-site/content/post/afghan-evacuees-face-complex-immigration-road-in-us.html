We have been posting about the plight of Afghan refugees with the Taliban coming.   Aline Barros for Voice of America reviews some of the complex immigration avenues -- including Special Immigrant Visas, refugee status, and asylum -- for persons fleeing Afghanistan.  As Barros summarizes, "The tens of thousands of Afghan refugees who made it to the United States as part of a historic humanitarian evacuation are entering an extraordinary system with very different benefits."
