In a Pew Research Center release "Key facts about immigrants eligible for deportation relief under Obama’s expanded executive actions," Jens Manuel Krogstad offers important facts about the impacts of the expanded deferred action program currently before the Supreme Court:

1.  The largest group who could be eligible for relief are the estimated 3.5 million unauthorized immigrant parents to whom DAPA would apply.

2.  Another 330,000 unauthorized immigrants who arrived in the U.S. as children could be eligible for relief under an expanded DACA program.

3.  Immigrants from Mexico would benefit most under the actions providing the most relief.

4.  The expanded deportation relief programs would have their biggest impact on Western states with large Hispanic populations.

5.  Not all unauthorized immigrants are covered by the president’s 2014 expanded programs. Those ineligible for deportation relief are more likely to be unmarried and not have U.S.-born children living with them.
