Deaths of migrants on the Mediterranean Sea are all-too-common.  CNN reports on a family's grieving of the tragic death of a 14-year old Pakistani boy who was on a fishing trawler that capsized in the Mediterranean last month and killed hundreds of people.  "The boat was carrying up to 750 Pakistani, Syrian, Egyptian and Palestinian refugees and migrants. Only 104 people have been rescued."
