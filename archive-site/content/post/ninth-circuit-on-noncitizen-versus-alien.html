This blog has previously reported (and here) on the ongoing discussion of terminology in immigration law.  Earlier this week, a Ninth Circuit opinion by Chief Judge Mary Murguia, joined by Judge Marsha Berzon, in Avilez v. Garland:discussed the preference for the word "noncitizen" over "alien."  Born in Spain, Judge Carlos Bea disagrees.  Eugene Volokh apparently does too.
