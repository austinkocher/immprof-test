The East Coast is on hurricane watch.  The anxieties of immigrants in these times are even greater than others.  In "As hurricane season collides with immigration agenda, fears increase for those without legal status," Gabriela Aoun Angueira for the Associated Press outline the difficult issues facing immigrants facing possible entanglements with immigration enforcement officers.  To this point, "[f]ederal and state agencies have said little on whether immigration enforcement would be suspended in a disaster. It wouldn't make much difference to Maria: `With all we’ve lived, we’ve lost trust.'”
