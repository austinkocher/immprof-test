As NBC News reports, Feidin Santana, a barber who immigrated to the U.S. from the Dominican Republic, is at the center of a story that has transfixed the nation. It was Santana's cell phone video that resulted in Officer Michael Slager being arrested and charged with murder Tuesday for the shooting death of 50-year-old Walter Scott after a traffic stop in North Charleston, South Carolina.
