The New York Times reports that Gov. Jan Brewer of Arizona, a Republican whose approval of controversial immigration measures such as the ill-fated S.B. 1070 repeatedly put her in the national spotlight, announced that she would not run for re-election, ending speculation that she might seek a third term.

 

Will Sheriff Joe Arpaio of Maricopa County, Arizona, who is well-known for his extreme measures in the name of immigration enforcement, be next to go into the Arizona desert?  Or will Sheriff Joe run for Governor?
