In the latest chapter of governments playing politics with immigrant lives, the state of Florida has admitted to transporting vulnerable immigrants from the U.S./Mexico border to Sacramento, California.   The message below came from a group attempting to help the migrants:

*****

As many of you may know, the Governor of Florida has recently been involved in transporting migrants from Texas to California as part of a political stunt. These individuals are currently receiving legal support and other assistance from organizations in California including Sacramento ACT, PICO, California Rural Legal Assistance Foundation, and the FUEL Network.

These organizations along with Immigrant Defense Advocates have partnered to submit a letter to Attorney General Bonta asking him to grant U Visa certifications to migrants who have cooperated with the AG's office and its investigation into the scheme that resulted in their arrivals to California.
