Want to hear a different perspective than you get on the ImmigrationProf blog?  Then listen to the to The Green Line podcast.

Since its debut in 2012, “The Green Line” podcast has been a voice for the National Border Patrol Council, which represents thousands of Border Patrol agents around the nation. It has been an “unapologetically conservative” program whose hosts have spent years forcefully arguing that front-line Border Patrol agents have been ignored, under-resourced and cast aside by their leadership and politicians of both parties.  That changed when President Trump, whom the union endorsed, became president.  Click here for more background on The Green Line.
