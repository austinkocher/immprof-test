From The New Yorker: "We must make America somewhere no one wants to live," is the new message from the GOP.

The "root cause of immigration... is that the United States is, for the most part, habitable... We're going to change that" by eliminating “immigration magnets” from Obamacare ("catnip for immigrants") to drinkable water.

This isn't going to work overnight, GOP leaders warned. “Our crumbling infrastructure and soaring gun violence are a good start, but much work still needs to be done... When Americans start leaving the country, we’ll know that we’re on the right track.”
