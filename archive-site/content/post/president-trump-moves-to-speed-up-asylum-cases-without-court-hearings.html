Here is but another attempt by the Trump administration to fast-track cases in immigration court -- allowing judges to drop "legally deficient asylum cases without a hearing."  The policy is announced in this April 11 memo by the Executive Office for Immigration Review, a unit of the Justice Department that includes the immigration courts and Board of Immigration Appeals.
