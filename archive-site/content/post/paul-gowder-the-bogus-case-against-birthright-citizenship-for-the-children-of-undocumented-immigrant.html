Northwestern University law professor Paul Gowder dives into the ins and outs of birthright citizenship — and the flaws in arguments against it — in a piece on The UnPopulist. "In the end, every single legal argument for stripping U.S.-born children of undocumented immigrants of citizenship fails," he writes. 

Beyond the legal arguments, the U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops is among groups that have warned about the moral implications, reports Peter Laffin of the National Catholic Register.
