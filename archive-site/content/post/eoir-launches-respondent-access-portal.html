Yesterday, the Executive Office for Immigration Review announced the launch of its new Respondent Access Portal. This is "a secure online platform that allows unrepresented individuals who have a hearing scheduled before EOIR to view their case information and scheduled hearings online, download their electronic case file, and file documents with the immigration court."

The portal is a terrific innovation that will help pro se litigants. As online commenters noted, only counsel could access such data online previously.
