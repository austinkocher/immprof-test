Dr. Geeta Rao Gupta grew up in India--Mumbai and Delhi. She attended the University of Delhi (BA, MA, MPhil) and Bangalore University (PhD).

In 1985, she and her family moved from India to the United States. She spent many years working for the International Center for Research on Women and has become an internationally known leader in "gender equality and women's economic security."

Along the way, she became a naturalized U.S. citizen.

Today, as her daughter tweeted, Dr. Gupta was sworn in as the U.S. Ambassador on Global Women’s Issues.
