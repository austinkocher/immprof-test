On Thursday, September 9th the Conservatives for Comprehensive Immigration Reform (CfCIR) will hold a special conference call on immigration reform, live from the annual Christian Community Development Association Conference in Chicago, IL.. This is the 10th national conference call from CfCIR’s Securing American Values Campaign. These conservative leaders are calling for at least 1,100 pastors, priests and people of faith to join them by phone on Thursday, September 9th to symbolically represent the 1,100 deportations taking place in the United States every day. Evangelical, political, and civil rights leaders will be discussing the moral crisis in communities across the country as a result of our broken immigration system.

Congressman Luis Gutierrez will discuss his participation in the Families United/Familias Unidas tour, in which he travelled to at least 30 churches in cities across America, and heard from tens of thousands of families that fear deportation, or can’t get a visa to bring a close family member or a child here legally.

WHAT: CfCIR press and public conference call featuring prominent faith and political leaders praying for the 1,100 human beings who are deported from the United States each day, and discussing the need for comprehensive immigration reform

WHO: Congressman Luis V. Gutierrez, Representative of Illinois’ 4th Congressional district and champion of immigrant rights

Dr. John Perkins, Evangelical and Civil Rights Leader, author and co-founder of the Christian Community Development Association

Noel Castellanos, current CEO of the Christian Community Development Association

Dr. Juan Hernandez, Conservative political strategist (Moderator) WHEN: Thursday, September 9th at 12:30 EDT (11:30 CT, 9:30 PT)

HOW: For call information, please email info@conservativesForCIR.com

www.ConservativesForCIR.org
