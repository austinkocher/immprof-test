The Outlaw Ocean podcast has dropped season two.  In it, "Pulitzer-prize winning journalist Ian Urbina sheds light on the secretive Libyan prisons swallowing up seafaring migrants; flagrant human rights abuses in China’s massive off-shore fleet; the horrors of a shrimp processing plant in India; and the wild story of a modern-day James Bond — if he were a ship repo man."  Episode 1 of the eight episode season is entitled "A War on Migration, Funded by the EU (Libya Pt. 1)."
