Here is an invitation from Interim Dean and former ImmigrationProf blogger Rose Cuison Villazor.  Professor Mae Ngai (Columbia) will deliver (via Zoom) the Distinguished Immigration and Citizenship lecture at Rutgers Law School. Ngai will be speaking about her new book, The Chinese Question: The Gold Rushes and Global Politics.  Professor Jack Tchen (Rutgers) will offer commentary

The lecture is part of the new Asian American, Pacific Islander, and the Law Initiative that the Center for Immigration Law, Policy and Justice and Center for Gender, Sexuality and the Law launched this fall.
