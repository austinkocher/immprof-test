The Trump administration has been employing roving patrols in immigration enforcement operations in Los Angeles.  Immigrations operations continue, including one earlier this week.  The Ninth Circuit stayed a temporary restraining order prohibiting immigration officers' reliance on apparent ancestry, what some might call racial profiling in immigration enforcement. Amy Howe for SCOTUSBlog reports on the U.S. government's request for the Supreme Court to stay the TRO.  "According to the Trump administration, the lower court order puts `a straitjacket on law-enforcement efforts' and flouts Supreme Court precedent."  Stay tuned!
