In a time when universities are under attack, this story from UC Berkeley brings a positive perspective on what universities can do.   The Robinson International Scholars Program provides two-year awards covering tuition, housing and living stipends and is given to high-achieving students from Africa who are refugees, lack legal standing in their current country, or graduated from high school in a sub-Saharan or developing nation.
