News from the Board of Immigration Appeals.   The traditional rule had been that noncitizens in removal proceedings could be eligible for bond.   A decision yesterday by the Board of Immigration Appeals appears to change that and rules that detention is mandatory for anyone in removal proceedings who entered the without inspection.  See Matter of Jonathan Javier YAJURE HURTADO.
