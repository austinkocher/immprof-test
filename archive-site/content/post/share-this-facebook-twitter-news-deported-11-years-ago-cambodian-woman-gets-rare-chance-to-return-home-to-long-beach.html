Here is a human story to show the impacts of the criminal removal provisions of the contemporary immigration laws.  This detailed report tells of a Cambodian immigrant deported, and separated from her life and family, for a relatively minor crime.  That part of the story is not a surprise.  However, she is back in Southern California and reunited with family.
