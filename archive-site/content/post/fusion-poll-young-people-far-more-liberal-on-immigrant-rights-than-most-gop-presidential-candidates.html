--- title: "Fusion Poll: Young people far more liberal on immigrant rights than most GOP presidential candidates" date: 2015-10-05T16:31:15 url: "/post/fusion-poll-young-people-far-more-liberal-on-immigrant-rights-than-most-gop-presidential-candidates/" aliases: ["/immigration/2015/10/fusion-poll-young-people-far-more-liberal-on-immigrant-rights-than-most-gop-presidential-candidates.html"] type: post year: "2015" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Young people overwhelmingly support extending rights to undocumented immigrants, putting them out of step with most Republican presidential candidates, according to the first installment of Fusion’s Issues Poll, which will survey approximately 1,000 people between the ages of 18 and 35 regularly ahead of next year’s election.
