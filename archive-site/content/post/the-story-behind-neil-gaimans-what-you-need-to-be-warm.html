I'm kindov a big Neil Gaiman fan. Looking for something to read over the winter break? Pick up The Graveyard Book, American Gods, or Neverwhere.

As for his latest work -- the poem "What Your Need To Be Warm" -- The Guardian has its backstory.

It starts with Twitter where Gaiman asked his followers for their thoughts on warmth.

Gaiman took the nearly 1,000 responses he received and used those responses to craft his poem.

The purpose behind the poem is to launch the UNHCR's Winter Emergency Appeal in support of refugees across the Middle East.
