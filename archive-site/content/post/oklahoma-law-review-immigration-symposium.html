The Oklahoma Law Review invites you to its Annual Symposium on Friday, November 14, 2014, on the topic of immigration law. It will feature presentations by David Martin, Kevin Johnson, Margaret Taylor, Rose Cuison Villzor, Victor Romero, and Michael Scaperlanda. The symposium will be held from 9 a.m. till noon, in the Bell Courtroom, Andrew Coats Hall, OU Law Center, 300 Timberdell Road, Norman, OK. Lunch will be served following the presentations. Parking will be available in the south lot of the College of Law. Please RSVP to Amanda Lee at amanda.j.lee-1@ou.edu by Monday, November 10.
