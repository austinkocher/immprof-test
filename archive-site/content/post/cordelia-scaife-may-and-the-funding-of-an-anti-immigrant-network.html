The NYT has a fascinating story on heiress Cordelia Scaife May's funding of a network of institutions espousing anti-immigrant and nativist ideas. Some of these ideas and institutions are gaining influence in the modern political landscape. The article is part of a multi-part series on The New Nativists that examines the evolution of hard-line immigration politics. (Other stories cover the rise of global far-right nationalism and connections between the rhetoric of the El Paso shooter and conservative media.)
