Immigration news from DC. Complying with a court injunction, the Trump administration announced earlier this week that it will extend Temporary Protected Status (TPS)  for Haiti and three other countries until January 2020. The decision by the Department of Homeland Security gives thousands of TPS holders an additional six-month reprieve from deportation.  TPS holders still face uncertainty as the Trump administration continues to fight in the courts to end the program.

The decision affects Haitians, Salvadorans, Nicaraguans and Sudanese in the United States. Last fall, U.S. District Judge Edward Chen granted a preliminary injunction stopping the administration and the Homeland Security from terminating TPS for immigrants from Haiti, Nicaragua, El Salvador and Sudan.“ Here is further information about the litigation, Ramos v.
