The Immigration Advocates Network has been awarded the American Bar Association’s 2014 Keane Award for Excellence in eLawyering, which recognizes IAN’s “work in using technology to broaden access to justice and reduce barriers faced by vulnerable communities.”  The award will be presented on Thursday, March 27 at a luncheon for those attending the ABA’s TechShow in Chicago.
