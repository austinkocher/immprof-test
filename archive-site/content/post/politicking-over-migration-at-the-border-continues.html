News reports regularly have informed the nation that cities along the southern U.S. border have seen large numbers of migrants crossing into the United States from Mexico in recent weeks. Last week, House Speaker Mike Johnson led a visit to the border, where House Republicans renewed calls for President Biden to crack down on immigration. Meanwhile, Mexico is seeing similar activity at its southern border, where officials have broken up a migrant caravan. 

NPR's Eyder Peralta traveled with the caravan and says he saw a lot of hope turn into agony as families were put on buses and separated.
