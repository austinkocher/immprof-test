Check this UCLA Law Library website out with information galore on Arizona's SB 1070.  It began as a guide for those on-the-ground problem solvers facing--and those researchers interested in--Arizona's recently passed immigration law, SB 1070, but has evolved into a resource bank for all people interested in learning more about SB 1070 and its content, context, origins, justifications, responses, and possible consequences.
