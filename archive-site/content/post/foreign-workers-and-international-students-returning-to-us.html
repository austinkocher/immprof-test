As NPR reports, the number of guest workers and immigrants coming to the United States is slowly climbing again after steep declines during the pandemic. These workers migrate on a J- or H-2B temporary work visa to staff jobs at resort towns and amusement parks. Economists say that should ease labor shortages — and some, though not all, think it could help calm inflation too.

The wrinkle: many cannot themselves afford to live in places like California given inflated costs (according to WSJ).
