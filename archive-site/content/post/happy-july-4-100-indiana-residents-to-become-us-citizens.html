AP reports that 100 immigrants from nearly 40 nations will celebrate the Fourth of July holiday in a special naturalization ceremony in Indianapolis. The U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services will oversee the ceremony. The immigrants hail from 39 nations, including India, Mexico, and Nigeria. Nationwide, more than 7,800 people are scheduled to become citizens this week during more than 100 naturalization ceremonies.
