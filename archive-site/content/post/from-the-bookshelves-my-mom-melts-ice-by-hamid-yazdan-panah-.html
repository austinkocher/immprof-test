My Mom Melts ICE is a children's book, written by Hamid Yazdan Panah and illustrated by Jackie Gonzalez. It's published by Immigrant Defense Advocates (IDA) and is "tribute to all mothers who do immigration work and activism."

Here is one of the book's memorable couplets:

She uses her love
To change things for the better
My mom melts ICE
And keeps families together

So, if you're in need for a last minute mother's day gift for the immigrant-advocate-mom in your life... take advantage of that Amazon super-speedy shipping and order a copy tonight!
