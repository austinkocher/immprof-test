 As the Senate takes up S. 744, The Border Security, Economic Opportunity and Immigration Modernization Act, the Immigration Policy Center releases: A Guide to S. 744: Understanding the 2013 Senate Immigration Bill. What are the basics of the bill? What problems is it designed to address? How do you navigate the bill? The Immigration Policy Center has written this overview in order to provide policymakers, the media, and the public with an easy-to-understand guide to the main components of S. 744 and the purpose behind them. The guide follows the structure of the bill, with a separate section addressing the cost-benefit analysis of S. 744, a resources page, and a glossary.
