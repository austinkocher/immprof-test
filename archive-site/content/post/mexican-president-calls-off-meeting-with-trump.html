Bad news on the diplomatic front!  Mexican President Enrique Peña Nieto said today that he has canceled his planned trip to Washington to meet with President Trump.  Peña Nieto's message on Twitter came hours after Trump tweeted the meeting should be canceled if Mexico won't pay for a border wall.
