President Trump has ended the family separation policy and opted for detention of immigrant families pending removal hearings.  In assessing the policy, Professor Marjorie Cohn on Truthout is blunt:  "Indefinite detention violates the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights, the Refugee Convention and the Convention on the Rights of the Child."
