The Chicago area's Latino population is becoming an increasingly vital contributor to the local economy, disputing a perception that Latinos tend to take more than they contribute to society. With the Latino population expected to provide 25 percent of the Chicago metropolitan area's total workforce by 2015, the city's future prosperity may well hinge on whether Latinos thrive, according to the University of Notre Dame's Institute for Latino Studies report titled "The State of Latino Chicago 2010: The New Equation."
