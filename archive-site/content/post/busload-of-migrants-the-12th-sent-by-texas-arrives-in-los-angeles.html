Official Photo of Texas Governor Greg Abbott

The immigration publicity stunts -- with tragic consequences on immigrant lives -- continue.  A 12th bus from Texas filled with migrants arrived in Los Angeles earlier this week, just days after the Los Angeles City Council unanimously decided to investigate potential legal action against Texas and Governor Greg Abbott, reports Jon Healey of the Los Angeles Times. The first bus arrived with 42 migrants on June 14, which Councilman Hugo Soto-Martínez called a "23-hour bus ride with little or no food or water," as Julia Wick reported in the Times
