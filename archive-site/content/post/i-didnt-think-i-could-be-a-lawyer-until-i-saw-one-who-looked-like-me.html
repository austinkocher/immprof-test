In this essay, Karlens Direny, a first year student at UC Hastings School of Law and immigrant from Haiti, writes about lawyers in the United States. Throughout high school and college, Karlens mentored low income students of color. Karlens received a scholarship from California Bar Foundation in 2017.  He says:

"I immigrated from Haiti when I was seven years old. Now that I’m an adult, I see the very clear, very obvious differences between Haiti’s justice system and America’s. Like many countries with a legacy of colonialism, Haiti’s justice system is profoundly broken and ineffective.

Things are different in America."

KJ
