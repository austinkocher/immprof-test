American rapper Snoop Dogg has won his latest round with the UK government, which denied him entry into the country in 2007 based on some previous criminal problems in 2007 and requred him to cancel his British concerts.  The Asylum and Immigration Tribunal ruled that Snoop Dogg had been wrongfully barred from entry.
