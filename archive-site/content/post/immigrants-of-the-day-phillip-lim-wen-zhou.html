Wen Zhou and Phillip Lim

Phillip Lim and Wen Zhou are the partners behind the successful fashion brand 3.1 Phillip Lim. They are also our immigrants of the day.

Phillip Lim was born in Thailand to parents of Chinese descent. As this New York magazine profile details, his parents moved to Cambodia when he was a baby. Soon after, the civil war forced them to flee to Southern California. There, Lim's mother worked as a seamstress and his father as a professional poker player.

Wen Zhou was born in China and moved to New York when she was thirteen, as she told Forbes. Although her father had been a college professor in China, in the United States he worked as a dishwasher. Her mother, like Lim's, worked as seamstress and continued working in a garment factory in New York City. Zhou too spent her after-school hours sewing in factories.

Lim and Zhou went into business together when both were 31. He is the designer. She is the CEO and President of their company.

A fashion leader for more than 15 years, the 3.1 Phillip Lim brand continues to push the envelope today, focusing on sustainable fashion.
