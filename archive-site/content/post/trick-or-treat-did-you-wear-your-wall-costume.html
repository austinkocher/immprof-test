President Trump has been pushing for a wall (paid for by Mexico) along the U.S./Mexico border to reduce unlawful immigration.  It may be hard to believe but the wall made it on to some Halloween costumes.

It was reported a while back that Amazon is selling a “Border Wall” Halloween costume for $29.95. It’s a white bodysuit printed with a brick design and emblazoned with the words, “Mexico will pay!”

“I wore this to Wal-Mart on my weekly stock-up run and got so many compliments, big yuuuuge compliments, more compliments than anyone else has ever had,” read one review of the costume.
