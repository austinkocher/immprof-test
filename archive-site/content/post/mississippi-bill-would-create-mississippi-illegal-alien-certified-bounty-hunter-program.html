No doubt inspired by President Trump's mass deportation campaign, Mississippi is considering immigration enforcement legislation.  USA Today reports that "[a] new bill in Mississippi, if passed, would create a bounty hunting program to hunt down undocumented migrants."  Here is the legislation.  It provides for the development and implementation of the "'Mississippi Illegal Alien Certified Bounty Hunter Program', which [would] to certify applicants to the program as certified bounty hunters for purposes of finding and detaining illegal aliens in this state . . . ."  The legislation would "offer a $1,000 reward to registered bounty hunters for each successful deportation they help facilitate." 
 
UPDATE (1/29):  Missouri is considering a similar bill.
