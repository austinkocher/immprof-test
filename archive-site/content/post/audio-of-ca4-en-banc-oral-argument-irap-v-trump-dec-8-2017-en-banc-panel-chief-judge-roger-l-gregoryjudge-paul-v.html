Earlier this week, the Ninth Circuit heard oral arguments to the challenge to President Trump's travel ban 3.o.  Yesterday, the Fourth Circuit heard arguments in another challenge..

According to The Hill, at least seven of the 13 judges had a hard time accepting the administration’s argument that the president’s Sept. 24 proclamation is not a Muslim ban given Trump’s retweets last week of anti-Muslim videos originally posted by a fringe right-wing British group.
