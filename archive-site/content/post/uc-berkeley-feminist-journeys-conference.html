UC Berkeley School of Law, April 17, 2018 

The Berkeley Journal of Gender, Law and Justice will convene a group of activists, scholars, artists and organizers to discuss the concept of home: how we create it, how we regulate it, how we give it, how we find it. We also hope to think deeply, as feminists, about the invisibilized labor that supports the homes we make.

We are incredibly honored that the event will culminate with a keynote speech from Ai-jen Poo, Director of the National Domestic Workers Alliance And MacArthur Genius grantee.

This event is free and open to the public, so please share widely with your networks. We also aspire to create a meaningfully accessible space for all involved. Please send access requests and requests for linguistic translation to Lili or Ari at bglj.symposium@gmail.com
