Check out this Call for Papers from in the Rutgers Race and the Law Review. RRLR is looking for paper to accompany its conference entitled What Resistance Looks Like: Law, Organizing, and Everyday Acts in the Immigrant Justice Movement to be held at Rutgers Law School in Newark on October 16 and 17, 2025.
 
Proposals must be submitted by September 5, 2025.
 
Authors whose submissions are accepted for publication will be notified via email by September 19, 2025.
 
First drafts (5 to 10 pages, double-spaced with footnotes) of accepted articles and essays must be submitted by October 15, 2025.
 
Second drafts of accepted articles and essays must be submitted by January 4, 2026.
 
Final drafts must be submitted by March 1, 2026.
 
The Rutgers Race and the Law Review prefers articles between 10,000 and 14,000 words and essays between 4,000 and 6,000 words.
 
For more details, please see the attached document. If you have any questions, contact Gabriela Ulloa Parra, Symposium Editor at gru2 at scarletmail.rutgers.edu.
