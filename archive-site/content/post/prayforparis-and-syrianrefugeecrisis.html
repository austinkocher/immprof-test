#PrayforParis is flooding social media as the hearts and minds of the world mourn for the losses in Paris, which are tragic and painful. Social media also reflects the anxiety that many have over how the Paris attacks will affect existing narratives of migration and danger. Filmmaker Guillermo del Toro tweeted: “I pray that not all immigrants are lumped into a mass of hatred. Most of them flee this very same kind of terror, not cause it.” And this Instagram post by @karunaezara connects the current crisis back to the #SyrianRefugeeCrisis: https://instagram.com/p/-DmUOwJ0tC/.
