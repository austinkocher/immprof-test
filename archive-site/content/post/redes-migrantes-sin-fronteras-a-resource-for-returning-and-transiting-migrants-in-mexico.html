Professor Huyen Pham provided information for this important online resource that helps Mexican nationals and those in transit through Mexico find necessary social services.  Redes, Migrantes sin Fronteras is a digital map of non-profits and other associations that help returning and transiting migrants.  The map has features to help folks find services by geographical location and type of service needed. 

Redes is supported in part by the Mexican Reintegration Project that Pham, Luz Herrera, Angela Morrison, and Guillermo Garcia Sanchez.  

Please distribute widely in your networks, and if you know of non-profits that should be added to the map, please contact Redes directly at redes.migrantes@gmail.com.
