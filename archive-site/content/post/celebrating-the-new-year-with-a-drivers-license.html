As we pointed out a few days ago, California recently passed a law making driver's licenses available to undocumented migrants in 2015.

Today is the first official day of business in 2015 and folks have been jumping to take advantage of the program. WaPo has great coverage here.

The National Immigration Law Center has an even handier map indicating all the states that make driver's licenses available regardless of immigration status.
