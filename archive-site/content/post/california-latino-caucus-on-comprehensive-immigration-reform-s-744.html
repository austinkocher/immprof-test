On Aug. 27, 2013 the California Latino Legislative Caucus held an informational briefing on comprehensive immigration reform.  S 744 supporters were challenged by critics of the bill from a community advocacy prospective.  Watch the video to view highlights from this 3 hour dialogue.

Notably more than a dozen legislators and California Governor Jerry Brown were among the audience which included dozens of legislative staff and media as well. The contraincantes included Peter Schey of the Center for Human Rights and Constitutional Law, Arnold Torres of Torres and Assoc., Enrique Morones of Border Angels, and Arturo Carmona of Presente.org versus Angelica Salas of CHIRLA as well as representatives of the UFW and the the California Federation of Labor.
