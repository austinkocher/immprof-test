Cyrus Metha on the Insightful Immigration Blog considers President Trump's proclamation suspending immigration to be "a brazen attempt to rewrite US immigration laws under the guise of protecting Americans during the COVID-19 pandemic."  He proceeds to  "point out all its inherent contradictions to make the case that it is legally infirm and is vulnerable to challenges in court."
