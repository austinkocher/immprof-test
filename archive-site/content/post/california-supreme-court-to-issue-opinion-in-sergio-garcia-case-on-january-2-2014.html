--- title: "California Supreme Court to Issue Opinion in Sergio Garcia Case on January 2, 2014" date: 2013-12-31T10:57:13 url: "/post/california-supreme-court-to-issue-opinion-in-sergio-garcia-case-on-january-2-2014/" aliases: ["/immigration/2013/12/california-supreme-court-to-issue-opinion-in-sergio-garcia-case-on-january-2-2014.html"] type: post year: "2013" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

The California Supreme Court has announced that it will issue an opinion -- parhaps opinions -- in the admission of Sergio Garcia, an undocumented immigrant who has passed the bar examination and moral character check, to the California State Bar.  The opinion will be released at 10 A.M. PST on January 2, 2014.
