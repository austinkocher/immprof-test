News reports are aplenty that Hous conservatives have "just said no" to immigration reform in 2014. Speaker John Boehner (R-Ohio) is suggesting that the path for such legislation would be tough.    That just may well be.  Still, advocates of reform should push to keep reform on the agenda and be ready to act at the right time.  Political realities change and, when it comes to immigration, surprising things have happened in the past. 

KJ
