As Kevin has already noted, the United States is cracking down on medical deferred action--exercising discretion not to deport individuals on the basis of medical conditions.

One individual caught in the end (or good-as-end) of that program is Maria Isabel Bueso. Bueso came to the United States 16 years ago, at the invitation of Bay Area doctors, to participate in a clinical trial for a drug to treat a rare medical condition: mucopolysaccharidosis type VI.  She now faces deportation to Guatemala where, without treatment, she faces certain death.
