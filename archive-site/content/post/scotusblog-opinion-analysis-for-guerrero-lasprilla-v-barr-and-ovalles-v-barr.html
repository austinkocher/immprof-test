Over at SCOTUSblog you can find my analysis of the just-released opinion in the combined cases of Guerrero-Lasprilla v. Barr and Ovalles v. Barr. The Court sided with the immigrant petitioners, holding that 8 U.S.C. § 1252(a)(2)(D) does NOT block circuit courts from considering the BIA’s application of a legal standard to undisputed facts.
