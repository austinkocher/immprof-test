Today, Florida Governor Ron DeSantis highlighted immigration enforcement efforts and Operation Tidal Wave.

The Tallahassee Democrat reports that a  coordinated operation between U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and Florida local law enforcement found and detained about 800 undocumented immigrants.

"The effort, nicknamed `Operation Tidal Wave,' targets people in Miami-Dade and Broward counties and the cities of Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville, Stuart, Tallahassee and Fort Myers . . . .  All of them are areas with high immigrant populations."
