Earlier today, American singer John Legend and Colombian musician Juanes toured the Eloy Immigration Detention Center in Arizona. The two were not allowed to perform inside the facility, but they set up shop out and performed a gig.

America with Jorge Ramos has coverage. Check out this short video which features John Legend singing All Of Me in the background.

Juanes is a "leading social activist among his Latin music peers" according to Entertainment Weekly. When he performed for the pope back in September, he was hoping his appearance would bolster support for immigration reform.
