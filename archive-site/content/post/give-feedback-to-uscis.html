U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is using an online tool, called the USCIS Idea Community, to ask for your ideas and opinions on the services that we provide. We would like to thank those who have already joined the Idea Community and shared their thoughts with us. Our next idea campaign will focus on welcoming immigrants and citizenship preparation. Starting today, and active through Monday, April 14, 2014, you can visit the Idea Community to provide your feedback.
