Human Rights Watch (HRW) is critical of the call of Texas Governor Greg Abbott for a special session of the Texas legislature to consider immigration proposals that in the view of HRW "would increase human rights violations and lead to long sentences for thousands of young Texans . . . ."  The proposals being floated include creating a crime of illegal entry into Texas with a sentence of up to 20 years, authorizing state law enforcement to deport asylum seekers for illegal entry, and increasing mandatory minimum sentences for human smuggling.

Previously, Human Rights Watch has documented the impact of Texas's Operation Lone Star, finding that the program has led to injuries and deaths, increased racial profiling of border residents and violated the rights of migrants and U,S, citizens. An investigation of the Operation by ProPublica, The Texas Tribune and The Marshall Project undermined Governor Abbott's  claim that Operation Lone Star has disrupted drug smuggling networks.  Attorneys also report that the operation has resulted in racial profiling in border communities.
