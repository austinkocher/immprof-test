Austria announced yesterday that it will be building a metal fence along its 2.3-mile border with Slovenia. The fence will be completed in just six weeks.

The fencing is notable because Slovenia and Austria are both members of the Schengen Area. That means they are supposed to allow free travel between the countries - without border controls.

Hungary (another Schengen Area nation) has already built a fence along its border with Slovenia.
