--- title: "MALDEF SUES MICHIGAN COMPANIES FOR IMMIGRATION FRAUD AND LABOR VIOLATIONS: Companies fraudulently recruited immigrant workers and promised nonexistent employment" date: 2014-07-02T15:19:22 url: "/post/maldef-sues-michigan-companies-for-immigration-fraud-and-labor-violations-companies-fraudulently-rec/" aliases: ["/immigration/2014/07/maldef-sues-michigan-companies-for-immigration-fraud-and-labor-violations-companies-fraudulently-rec.html"] type: post year: "2014" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Today, MALDEF filed suit in federal court in the Eastern District of Michigan against Systems USA Inc. and Systems USA Consulting LLC for violating the Racketeering Influenced and Corrupt Organizations Act (RICO), the Trafficking Victims Protection Act (TVPA), the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), and the Michigan “Minimum Wage Law.” The companies recruited and enticed Mexican computer programmers to apply for nonexistent jobs, and systematically defrauded the federal government to obtain visas for those individuals by representing that they would be employing the programmers once they traveled to the United States. A copy of the complaint can be viewed here.
