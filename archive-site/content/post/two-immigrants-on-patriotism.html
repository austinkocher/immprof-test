It is Memorial Day weekend and, as we honor our veterans, it is an appropriate time to reflect on what we are as a nation.  NPR talked to two immigrants, Asma Naeem of the Smithsonian's National Portrait Gallery and author and professor Laila Lalami.  Both came to the U.S. as children. They discuss what patriotism means to them on Memorial Day weekend.
