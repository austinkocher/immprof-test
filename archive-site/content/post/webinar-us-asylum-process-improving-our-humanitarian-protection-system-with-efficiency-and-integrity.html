U.S. Asylum Process: Improving Our Humanitarian Protection System With Efficiency and Integrity

Date & Time:  Sep 14, 2023 10:00 AM in 
 
Join the American Immigration Council and the American Immigration Lawyers Association (AILA) to unpack the U.S. asylum process and how it can be improved. We will walk through what goes into preparing an asylum application and what complications, such as trauma and detention, can add significant time to this process. Finally, we will discuss how to inject efficiency into the existing asylum process and ensure the integrity of a system that has life-and-death consequences.
