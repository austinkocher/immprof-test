PBS’ Frontline premiered a new documentary last night featuring an inside look at the making of the Trump administration’s immigration policies. The documentary, Zero Tolerance, takes a closer look at how an “improbable group of outsiders” including Stephen Miller, Jeff Sessions and Steve Bannon wielded incredible influence in the White House.

This FRONTLINE episode investigates how President Trump turned immigration into a powerful political weapon that fueled division and violence. The documentary goes inside the efforts of three political insurgents to tap into populist anger, transform the Republican Party and crack down on immigration.
