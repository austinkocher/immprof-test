An executive immigration plan unveiled by President Barack Obama last fall can tremendously grow California’s economy in the next decade, state Attorney General Kamala Harris said in a White House conference call Monday.Harris has entered the race for Senate in California to replace longtime Senator Barbara Boxer.

Officials predict the U.S. economy will grow between $90 to $210 billion over the next 10 years.
