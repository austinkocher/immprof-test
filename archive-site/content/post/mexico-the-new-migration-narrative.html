Fundamental demographic, economic, and educational changes have set Mexico on a new path, significantly altering its migration-related priorities and concerns vis-a-vis the United States and Central America. This Migration Information Source article examines new migration trends, Mexico's role as a country of transit and increasingly of destination, the 2011 migration law, remittances, government policies on the Mexican diaspora, and more.

In addition, "A Demographic Portrait of Mexican-Origin Hispanics in the United States" by Ana Gonzalez-Barrera and Mark Hugo Lopez reports that a record 33.7 million Hispanics of Mexican origin resided in the United States in 2012. This estimate includes 11.4 million immigrants born in Mexico and 22.3 million born in the U.S. who self-identified as Hispanics of Mexican origin.
