We are pleased to announce that John Willshire Carrera and Nancy Kelly, co-directing attorneys of the Harvard Immigration and Refugee Clinic at Greater Boston Legal Services, have won the Harvard Law School’s Dean's Award for Excellence for their exceptional teaching and mentoring of students at Harvard Law School and for their leadership in developing child asylum and gender-based asylum law, as well as indigenous Guatemalan and gang-based asylum claims.
