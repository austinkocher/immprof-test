President Trump championed a "big, beautiful" wall along the U.S./Mexico border.  As the 2024 elections nears, the Biden administration is seeking to make moves on border enforcement.  Britain Eakin for Law360 (Listen to article) reports that the U.S. Department of Homeland Security is waiving a series of federal laws to quickly construct new barriers and roads along the border in Texas, designating it an area of "high illegal entry."

DHS Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas said in a notice of determination set to be published in the Federal Register today that "DHS will take immediate action" on the border barriers.  The notice does not include many details about the barriers to be built. Click here to read the notice.
