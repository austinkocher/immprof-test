Janet Garsd for NPR reports that multiple immigration lawyers report that they are seeing more cases of "clients showing up to what they believe are routine immigration appointments and getting detained."  "[L]awyers say they are seeing more on the spot arrests at immigration hearings. Immigration attorneys are now warning clients that they could be detained or deported when they show up for routine court hearings or appointments."
