UC Davis School of Law alum Rachel Ray '11 has been hard at work fighting for justice for immigrants.  Staff of the ACLU of Northern California will be traveling on a bus throughout California, the Southwest, and the South promoting their "Estamos Unidos" campaign, collecting stories, educating people, and conducting outreach about immigration and immigrants. 

Visit the "miACLU" website to learn more about the campaign. If you feel inspired to act against immigration enforcement laws like Arizona's and Alabama's, sign the ACLU's petition here.
