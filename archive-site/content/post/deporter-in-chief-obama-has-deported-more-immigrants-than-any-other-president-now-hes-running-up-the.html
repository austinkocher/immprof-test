President Obama has gotten much credit for his Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals and proposed expanded deferred action for parents of U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents.  The recent publicity surrounding the Obama administration's immigration raids and arrests of Central American women and children remind us of the enforcement emphasis of this administration.  As  Tim Rogers on Fusion writes, "When it comes to getting tough on immigration, Republican candidates talk the talk, but Obama walks the walk. President Obama has deported more people than any U.S. president before him, and almost more than every other president combined from the 20th century."
