NPR is running a series on internally displaced people.  According to the report, there are 40 million internally displaced people, or IDPs, across the globe – outnumbering refugees by more than 2 to 1. They have fled within their own countries – mostly in the Middle East and Africa, but also in Latin America and Europe.

In Georgia, a country of 4 million, roughly 1 in 20 people has been internally displaced by war in the past three decades. The government has done its best to accommodate them.  Most have been waiting, hoping against hope for decades, to go back home. 

The series allows three Georgia IDPs to tell their stories.
