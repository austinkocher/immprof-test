The Supreme Court has granted a stay of the lower court's injunction in the Los Angeles raids case.  There is no opinion for the Court.  Justice Kavanaugh concurred in granting the stay.  Justice Sotomayor, joined by Justices Kagan and Jackson, dissented.  Click here for the Associated Press summary of the ruling.   

UPDATE (1 p.m. PST):  Criticism has come fast and furious.  See California Alex Padilla's statement: Padilla Statement on Supreme Court Ruling Paving the Way for Racial Profiling in Los Angeles Immigration Enforcement; and the Mexican American Legal Defense and Education Fund (MALDEF) STATEMENT ON SUPREME COURT DECISION TO ALLOW ICE TO USE RACIAL PROFILING IN LOS ANGELES.
