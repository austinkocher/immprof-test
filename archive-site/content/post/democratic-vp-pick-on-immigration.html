Kamala Harris has selected Minnesota Governor Tim Walz to be her running mate.  Walz appears to have immigration views very different from those of the Donal Trump/JD Vance ticket.

Maggie Astor for the New York Times summarizes Governor Walz's record on immigration as follows:

"Mr. Walz supports a path to citizenship for some undocumented immigrants. . . . 

As a member of Congress years ago, he voted for stricter screening of refugees, but changed his position when he ran for governor. During his 2022 re-election campaign, he denounced his Republican opponent for saying that accepting immigrants without enough resources threatened `Minnesota’s fabric of life.' . . . 

He signed a bill last year that made undocumented immigrants eligible for Minnesota driver’s licenses, which he argued would make roads safer by reducing unlicensed driving. He does not have a clear public record on border-security policies."

Expect Republican attacks on Walz's immigration record.
