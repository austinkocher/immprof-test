A CNN report raises an important question.  Do lawful permanent residents and other lawful residents run the risk of being denied reentry into the country after a trip outside the United States?  There is a growing concern with the Trump immigration measures that it is not safe to leave the country.  At the same time, some lawful permanent residents do not feel that the current environment for immigrants in the United States is not comfortable.
