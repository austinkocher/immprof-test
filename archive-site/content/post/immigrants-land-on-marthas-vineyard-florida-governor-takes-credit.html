Why do some of the nation's political leaders play games with migrant lives?   Texas Governor Greg Abbott and Florida Gover Ron DeSantis have consistently used cynical publicity stunts that show utter disregard for the plight of migrants who have come to the United States.   Those have included busing of migrants to New York City, Washington D.C., and, most recently, Chicago,

Reuters reports the latest stunt from the Florida governor:

"[a] group of immigrants landed on the wealthy Massachusetts island of Martha's Vineyard on Wednesday, part of a campaign by Republican governors to shift the immigration burden to Democratic areas.

Florida governor Ron DeSantis took credit for the arrival of two planes of immigrants . . . ."
