The Arizona primary is on Tuesday and Donald Trump is leading the Republican presidential hopefuls in the polls.  News reports have highligheted that one of Trump's most well-known endorsers in Arizona is none other that the famous hard-line anti-immigration Maricopa County Sherifff Joe Arpaio.

The Los Anegeles Times reports on the events surrounding Trump's appearance at Fountain Hills, a suburb of Phoenix and Arpaio's hometown, yesterday.  More than 100 protesters blocked the main road leading to Trump's rally, chanting that he was a racist. “Get this clown out of my town!” they shouted, carrying signs that read, among other things, “Trump = Hitler” and “Combat white supremacy.”
