Texas Senate Bill 147 would ban citizens of China from buying property in the state. The proposed law would also target citizens of Iran, North Korea and Russia.

The legislation arrives as anti-Asian hate crimes have been on the rise, and critics say it evokes an ugly history of anti-Asian discrimination under state and federal law.  As Professor Keith Aoki wrote, states, including California and Washington, in the early twentieth century through the "alien land laws" barred ownership of real estate by "aliens ineligible to citizenship," which in the West primarily affected Asian immigrants.  Texas SB 147 sounds eeily similar
