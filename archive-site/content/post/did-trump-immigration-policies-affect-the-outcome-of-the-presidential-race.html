National election night polling released by Public Opinion Strategies (POS) shows that President Trump’s tough immigration policies made a plurality of voters less likely to vote for him. FWD.us President Todd Schulte breaks down the poll’s findings on Twitter: “The issue was a significant net negative among both Independents (27% more likely/48% less likely) and Democrats (4% more likely/76% less likely). Further, it was a significant net negative among suburban women (27% more likely/52% less likely).” The POS analysis is here.
