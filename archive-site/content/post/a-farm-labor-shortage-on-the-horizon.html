The Trump administration has claimed that increased immigration enforcement will benefit American workers.  Nonetheless, growers are finding it difficult to find farm workers -- especially U.S.-born workers.  The Los Angeles Times reports that, try as they have to entice workers with better salaries and benefits, companies have found it impossible to attract enough U.S.-born workers to make up for a shortage from south of the border.
