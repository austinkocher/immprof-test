Migrants Before the Law Contested Migration Control in Europe by Eule, T., Borrelli, L.M., Lindberg, A., Wyss, A. (Palgrave, 2019)

Offers detailed comparative perspectives on how state agents, non-state actors, and migrants shape the European migration regime Engages the rarely combined research areas of socio-legal scholarship and contestations over migration control Applies theoretical insights from critical political anthropology to the study of the ‘state of the state’ in Europe.
