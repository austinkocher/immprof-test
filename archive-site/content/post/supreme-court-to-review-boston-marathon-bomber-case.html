The Supreme Court today agreed to hear a government appeal to reinstate the death penalty for convicted Boston Marathon bomber Dzhokhar Tsarnaev, granting review of a lower court's ruling that errors during the trial had tainted the sentencing.

Tsarnaev was convicted of dozens of crimes in the 2013 Boston marathon bomber that killed three people and received a death sentence in 2015. The U.S. Court of Appeals for the First Circuit threw out the death sentence last year. 

As reported in an article in The Atlantic, In April 2002, a native of Krygyzstan, Anzor Tsarnaev apparently arrived in the United States on a tourist visa with his sons Tamerlan and Dzhokhar — the perpetrators of the Boston Marathon bombing. The family later secured asylum. Dzhokhar became a U.S. citizen.
