News from the front.  President Donald Trump said he plans to make changes to his administration's crackdown on immigration to protect migrant farm and hotel workers and others in the leisure industry who have been among those deported.

Trump promised the changes in a June 12 Truth Social post that acknowledged Immigration and Customs Enforcement officers have expanded arrests beyond just migrants convicted of violent crimes, who the administration have said are the primary targets of immigration raids and deportations.
