As 2015 draws to a close, the Migration Policy Institute’s online journal, the Migration Information Source, has launched its annual countdown of the Top 10 Migration Issues of the year. 

We are working backwards through the list to reveal a new trend each day, on our countdown to No. 1. Follow us @MigrationPolicy or on Facebook to keep up with the daily countdown to the top migration issues next week.

10.    Shine Wears Off Investor Visa Programs as Questions about Economic Benefits and Fraud          Lead to Reform

9.    Border Skirmishes Resonate in National Domestic Politics

8.    A Shared Challenge: Europe and the United States Confront Significant Flows of         Unaccompanied Child Migrants

7.    Climate Change and Natural Disasters Displace Millions, Affect Migration Flows

6.    Refugee Crisis Deepens Political Polarization in the West

Be sure to check back next week for the Top 10 Migration Issues of 2015 to see what made the top of the list!
