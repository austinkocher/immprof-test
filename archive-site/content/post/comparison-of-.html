A new Migration Policy Institute (MPI) brief issued compares major provisions of the comprehensive immigration reform bill approved by the Senate in June with those of individual bills that have been voted on to date in the House Judiciary and Homeland Security committees. The side-by-side analysis is the latest in a series of issue briefs published by MPI focusing on key aspects of the immigration policy debate.
