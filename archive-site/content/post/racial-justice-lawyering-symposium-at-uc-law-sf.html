UC Law SF (formerly UC Hastings) will be hosting an in-person symposium on Racial Justice Lawyering (March 22, from 10:30-3:15pm Pacific). The event is organized by the Center for Racial and Economic Justice (CREJ) and co-sponsored by the Center for Race, Immigration, Citizenship, and Equality (RICE).

Registration is free.
