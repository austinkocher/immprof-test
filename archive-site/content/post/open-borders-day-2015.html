Ilya Somin on the Volokh Conspiracy reminds us that today is Open Borders Day – an international day focusing attention on the injustices inflicted by state-imposed restrictions on international migration. This year, the Open Borders website has posted an "Open Borders Manifesto" that summarizes the major reasons why people with a wide range of ideological perspectives should oppose most currently existing migration restrictions.
