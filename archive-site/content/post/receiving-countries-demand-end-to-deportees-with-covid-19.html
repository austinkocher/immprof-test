As Ming noted earlier this week, "thousands of migrants have been returned to their home countries, including some who are sick with the [corona]virus, spreading disease contracted in the US to places ill-equipped to manage their health care."

Guatemala, for one, is taking steps to avoid receiving more COVID-19-infected deportees. As Reuters reports, Guatemala will no longer accept deportees unless the United States can certify that the deportees are virus-free. (El Salvador, Honduras, Haiti, Jamaica and Ecuador have also asked the U.S. to test their deportees for coronavirus.)

Naturally, wanting to keep the deportations flowing, the U.S. is taking efforts to test deportees for coronavirus. But ICE is unlikely going to be able to acquire a sufficient number of test kits to keep up with the pace of deportations that the agency would like to maintain.
