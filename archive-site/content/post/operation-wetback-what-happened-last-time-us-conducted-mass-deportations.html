Dan Gooding for Newweek looks at the possible comeback under President Trump of "Operation Wetback," the mass deportation campaign of 1954.   Historian Kelly Lytle Hernandez , who has written a history of the Border Patrol, offers important historical context and accuracy.  The U.S. government declared the operation success but it ended abruptly without really changing the immigration dynamics in the Southwest.  The operation "targeted Mexicans in particular, it was a highly-racialized campaign."  What is truly needed, Hernandez contends, is comprehensive immigration reform.
