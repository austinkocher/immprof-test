In a letter, the LIBRE Initiative, a conservative Latino group that is funded by the Koch brothers (click here for background on the group)_criticized what it sees as policies that "are not in line with our principles and are not in the best interest of the country." Specifically, the group pointed to calls to end birthright citizenship and mass deportation as problematic. Both of those ideas have been championed by Donald Trump, the current GOP front-runner.
