It looks like it will be a challenging few years.  Universities will be important voices in this time.  The Immigration Initiative at Harvard recently sent out a message confirming that it "remains steadfast [in] facilitating intellectual exchange that addresses the needs of immigrant-origin children and youth."  The Initiative's website "provide[s] some food for thought, paths to deeper understanding, and practical information to support children, youth, and their families in their learning environments and communities during these challenging times."  Click the link above the check out the resources.
