The Anti-Mexican Origins of the Criminalization of Undocumented Entry under the McCarran-Walter Act of 1952 by S. Deborah Kang

Abstract

This paper traces the anti-Mexican animus that motivated the passage of 8 U.S.C. ss. 1326 under the 1952 McCarran-Walter Act.

***

This article touches on issues currently being addressed in the courts.
