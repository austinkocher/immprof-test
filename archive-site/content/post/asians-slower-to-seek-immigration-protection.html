--- title: "Asians slower to apply for deferred action" date: 2015-02-02T06:11:51 url: "/post/asians-slower-to-seek-immigration-protection/" aliases: ["/immigration/2015/02/asians-slower-to-seek-immigration-protection.html"] type: post year: "2015" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

The Associated Press reports on some interesting trends in applications under the Obama administration's deferred action  program.  Asian immigrants have been slower to sign up for relief.  Community advocates are ramping up efforts to reach persons who are eligible for relief.

According to AP, "Many advocates have blamed the paltry turnout among young Asian immigrants for the administration's 2012 program on the stigma of being in the country illegally in their communities, where many feel lacking proper immigration papers is culturally shunned."
