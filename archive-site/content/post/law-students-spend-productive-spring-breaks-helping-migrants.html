In collaboration with UCDavis' Humanizing Deportation project, Professor @RaquelEAldana  and @UCDavisLaw

students traveled to travel to Tijuana to spend spring break offering free legal consultations to migrants. The students will discuss the trip at an April 4 @AokiKingHall  event.

 

Day 2 Tijuana: our afternoon stop today was Al Otro Lado’s Deportation Project, run by the phenomenal Pricila Rivas, herself a victim of Deportation. We met deportees who were US citizens and long termLPRs who plead guilty to unfair charges that made them deportable for life. pic.twitter.com/t7II0LY4wp
