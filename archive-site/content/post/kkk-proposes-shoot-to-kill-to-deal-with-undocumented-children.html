Here is a report the thoughts of some members of the Ku Klux Klan on addressing the flow of undocumented children at the U.S.?Mexico border. Al Jazeera America learned this week that they’re proposing a “shoot to kill” policy to deal with undocumented migrants at the border.  Reporter Robert Ray asked a KKK imperial wizard why they would ever do such a thing. He explained that the people coming to the border are “criminals” and will “continue breaking the law” when they come into the country, not to mention bringing third world diseases.
