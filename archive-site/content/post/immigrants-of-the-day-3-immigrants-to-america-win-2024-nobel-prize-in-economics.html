In Forbes, Stuart Anderson writes about the three immigrants -- one from Turkey and two from the United Kingdom -- to the U.S. who won Nobel Prizes in economics.  “This year’s laureates in the economic sciences—Daron Acemoglu, Simon Johnson and James Robinson—have demonstrated the importance of societal institutions for a country’s prosperity,” according to the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences press release issued for the 2024 Nobel Prize in economics. “ (bold added).
