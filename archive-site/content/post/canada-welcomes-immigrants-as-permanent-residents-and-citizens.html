The Economic Times and Bloomberg News report this week that Canada will increase lawful migration numbers above earlier targets to support post-pandemic economic growth in the country. Under the new plan, Canada will grant residency to 431,000 permanent residents this year, 447,000 in 2023 and 451,000 in 2024.

Canada also continues to welcome new citizens. In honor of National Flag Day of Canada and Black History Month, Prime Minister Justin Trudeau made an appearance at a virtual citizenship ceremony. As Trudeau wrote on his official Twitter account, "let’s continue to build a country that’s welcoming and inclusive – no matter who you are or where you’re from."
