CNN reports that that former White House chief of staff and retired Marine Gen. John Kelly has joined the board of directors for Caliburn International, the parent company of Comprehensive Health Services, which operates shelters for unaccompanied migrant children. Kelly served as President Trump's chief of staff until the end of last year.

Kelly's ties to the administration's policy of separating migrant parents and children date to when he served as secretary of the Department of Homeland Security.
