NPR reports that U.S. District Judge James Boasberg will demand answers from the Department of Justice today in a hearing about the Venezuelan migrants who were deported to El Salvador. Trump invoked the Alien Enemies Act of 1798 to target alleged members of Tren de Aragua, a Venezuelan gang he claims is "invading" the U.S. Planes with over 250 people were sent to El Salvador. The flights happened even though Judge Boasberg ordered the government to turn around any planes already in the air.

The Justice Department had to file additional details about the flight. However, Judge Boasberg says the department evaded its obligations.  Boasberg is attempting to understand if the administration disobeyed his orders, NPR’s Ximena Bustillo says. The Trump administration denies violating the orders and has called for Judge Boasberg’s impeachment.   Chief Justice John Roberts publicly criticized the call for impeachment.
