An alarming number of anonymous corpses is piling up in the border town of Laredo, Texas—many, migrants who die crossing into the United States. In this week’s episode of the HLN investigative series Inside with Chris Cuomo (Fri., Oct. 27 at 9pm ET/PT on HLN), CNN Anchor Chris Cuomo gets a first-hand look at the horrific cases a lone medical examiner encounters on a regular basis in this Texas town and the advanced forensic techniques she uses to figure out who they are why they’ve died. Often succumbing to the harsh conditions of rough terrain and wildlife predators, he embeds with members of the local border patrol whose job is to track those looking to cross into the United States unlawfully.
