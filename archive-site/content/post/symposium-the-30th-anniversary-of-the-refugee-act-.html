Penn State Law's Center for Immigrants' Rights is holding a colloquium marking the 30th Anniversary of the Refugee Act on November 12, 2010 at the Lewis Katz Building, University Park, PA.  Refugee scholars and experts will:

• Examine the history of the Refugee Act and development of domestic refugee law

• Is current refugee law and policy consistent with the principles echoed in the Refugee Act?

• Debate administrative and legislative solutions moving forward.
