Here are the top 5 immigration changes from Trump's first 100 days.  NPR has tracked the biggest immigration stories, policy changes and legal challenges. In its estimation, here are the top five issues that have changed the immigration landscape in Trump's second term:

1.  Alien Enemies Act

2.  Mahmoud Khalil

3.  Kilmar Abrego Garcia

4.  Birthright Citizenship

5.  (The Lack of) Asylum Access

It seems to me that some cross-cutting issues seem an unprecedented lack of commitment to Due Process for noncitizens facing removal and the rule of law.
