In "Funding for refugees has long been politicized − punitive action against UNRWA and Palestinians fits that pattern", Nicholas R. Micinski and Kelsey Norman on the Conversation write that the United States is among more than a dozen countries to freeze funding to the United Nations agency providing aid to displaced Gazans over allegations of complicity in the October 7 attack.
