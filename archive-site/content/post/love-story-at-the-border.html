An octagenarian couple has found a creative way to stay connected during the pandemic at the German-Danish border. Every day they meet for coffee at 3pm at the border. Each lives approximately 2 miles from their meeting spot and brings ingredients for a picnic. He brings the chairs; she brings a table and coffee. As the New York Times reports in a delightful human feature:

Every day since the police shut the border to contain the virus, Karsten Tüchsen Hansen, an 89-year-old retired farmer, and Inga Rasmussen, an 85-year-old former caterer, have met at the Mollehusvej border crossing to chat, joke and drink, while maintaining a modicum of social distance.
