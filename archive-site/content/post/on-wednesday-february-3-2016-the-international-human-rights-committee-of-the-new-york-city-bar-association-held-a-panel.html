On Wednesday, February 3, 2016, the International Human Rights Committee of the New York City Bar Association held a panel discussion on "Human Rights Obligations and the Refugee Crisis."  Professor Maryellen Fullerton will be the moderator, and the panelists include Alex Aleinikoff, Jesse Tampio, Bill Frelick, Eleanor Acer, Stephen Poellet, and Gina DelChiaro. In addition to addressing critical developments in the refugee crisis, the program will provide information on legal assistance efforts underway in the United States and projects seeking assistance from pro bono lawyers.
