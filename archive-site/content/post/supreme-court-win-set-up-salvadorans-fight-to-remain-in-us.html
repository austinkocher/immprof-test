César Cuauhtémoc García Hernández for SCOTUSBlog analyzes the saga of Kilmar Abrego Garcia, who has been targeted by the Trump administration for wrongful removal to El Salvador followed up by a Supreme Court ruling that the U.S. government facilitate his return, his return to the United States only to be charged with an immigration crime, released, and re-arrested with threatened removal to Uganda.
