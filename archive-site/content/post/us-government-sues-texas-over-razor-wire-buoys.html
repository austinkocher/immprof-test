No surprises but NPR reports that the Department of Justice has sued Texas Governor Greg Abbott over his refusal to remove a stretch of buoys surrounded by razor wire (and here) placed in the Rio Grande between Mexico and Texas.  The DOJ says Abbott's efforts are "unlawful" and presented "humanitarian concerns."  The lawsuit claims that the state is violating the River and Harbors Act, which prohibits the placement of obstructions in the water without federal approval.
