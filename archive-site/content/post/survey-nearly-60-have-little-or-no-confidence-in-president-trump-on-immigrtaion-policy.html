--- title: "Survey: Nearly 60% Have Little or No Confidence in President Trump on Immigration Policy" date: 2019-01-19T02:55:31 url: "/post/survey-nearly-60-have-little-or-no-confidence-in-president-trump-on-immigrtaion-policy/" aliases: ["/immigration/2019/01/survey-nearly-60-have-little-or-no-confidence-in-president-trump-on-immigrtaion-policy.html"] type: post year: "2019" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

According to a survey by the Pew Research Center, when it comes to making wise decisions about immigration policy, four-in-ten say they are at least somewhat confident in Trump (including 29% who say they are very confident). Nearly six-in-ten (58%) express little or no confidence in Trump on immigration policy, including 45% who say they are not at all confident in him on this issue.

The new Pew Research Center survey, conducted Jan. 9-14 among 1,505 adults, finds that a growing share of Americans say they trust what Trump says less than what previous presidents said while they were in office. Nearly six-in-ten (58%) say they trust what Trump says less than previous presidents, up from 54% last June and 51% in February 2017, shortly after he took office.
