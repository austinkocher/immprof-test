Sometimes you just need a feel-good story to end the day. Listen to this brief report about Falhad Ahmed Mohamud (far left) and her daughter Amina Mohamed (far right). They came to the US as refugees from Somalia decades ago. Falhad, a single mother, spent years raising her two daughters. Recently, she and her daughter Amina graduated together with masters degrees in IT, answering a promise Falhad made to her own father, years ago, before leaving Somalia, to pursue higher education.
