In an adaptation from Rebuilding American Capitalism: A Handbook for Conservative Policymakers, former U.S. Senator and Attorney General Jeff Sessions calls for immigration reform in this commentary in Newsweek.  He calls for reform, with an emphasis on the enforcement side of the equation -- for example, endorsing the mandatory use of E-Verify by employers.  Needless to say, I saw no endorsement to a path to legalization for undocumented immigrants, relief for Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA) recipients, etc.
