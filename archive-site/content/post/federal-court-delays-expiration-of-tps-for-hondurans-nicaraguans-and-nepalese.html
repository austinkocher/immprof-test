NPR reports that a federal judge has blocked the Trump administration from terminating Temporary Protected Status for citizens of Honduras, Nicaragua and Nepal. The decision delays the terminations of TPS until November and affects about 60,000 immigrants, many of whom have been living in the U.S. for years.
