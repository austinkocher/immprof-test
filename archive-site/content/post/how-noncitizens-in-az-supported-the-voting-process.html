Noncitizens are ineligible to vote in U.S. federal elections. That doesn't mean they weren't in a position to support the 2020 campaign.

As the Arizona Republic reports, undocumented migrants supported the door-to-door Unite Here labor union's get-out-the-vote work in Arizona. As one DACA recipient told the paper: "Although I can't vote, I want to make sure my community does."

Noncitizens in the United States have a keen interest in the outcome of the 2020 election as the new president will be able to dictate the future of programs such as DACA and TPS.

The article is a fascinating read as we wait with baited breath for the final tallies out of Arizona.
