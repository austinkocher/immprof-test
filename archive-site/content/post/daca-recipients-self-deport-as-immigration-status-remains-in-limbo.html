The Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals policy has been in limbo for several years after the Supreme Court rejected the Trump administration's attempt to rescind the policy.  ABC News' Devin Dwyer (and here) reports on DACA recipients self-deporting themselves out of the United States due to uncertainty in renewing their deferred action status every two years. One of the self deportees lived for nearly 20 years in the United States.  The a son of Colombian immigrants, Miguel has been living out his American dream without any certainty he would be allowed to stay in America.  Soon, he says, he's calling it quits on the U.S. and moving to Canada.  Others are leaving the United States as well.
