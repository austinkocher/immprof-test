Another casualty of the Trump administration.  Not as painful as some but Mexican singer Julión Álvarez postponed his concert in Texas over the weekend after his visa was revoked.  "Álvarez, who won an American Music Award on Monday, had been scheduled to perform Saturday at AT&T Stadium in Arlington. . . . Speaking in Spanish, the singer said he and his band were notified a day before the show that they would not be allowed to enter the United States."
