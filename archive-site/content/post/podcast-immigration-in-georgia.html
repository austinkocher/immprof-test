The latest episode of the Politically Georgia podcast is all about immigration.  The Atlanta Journal-Constitution's Jeremy Redmon joins host Greg Bluestein to discuss the ways various Georgia city, county and state officials are handling immigration and how the topic is impacting major 2018 races.  Georgia is seeing a few candidates pushing Trump-style immigration enforcement.
