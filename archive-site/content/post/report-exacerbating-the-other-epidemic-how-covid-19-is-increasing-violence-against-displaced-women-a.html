Refugees International is launching Exacerbating the Other Epidemic: How COVID-19 is Increasing Violence against Displaced Women and Girls. The  issue brief details the ways in which displaced women and girls are facing an increased threat of gender-based violence amid the pandemic, and highlights creative solutions and steps governments, donors, and communities must take to combat GBV and account for the needs of displaced women and girls, particularly amid the COVID-19 response.
