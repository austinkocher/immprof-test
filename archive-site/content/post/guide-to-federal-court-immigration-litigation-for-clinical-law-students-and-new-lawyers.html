Kate Melloy Goettel of the University of Iowa College of Law has shared a resource developed for students:  Guide to Federal Court Immigration Litigation for Clinical Law Students and New Lawyers ( Download Guide to Federal Litigation for Clinic Law Students & New Attorneys). This guide gives an overview of the types of immigration cases in federal court. It also discusses the elements of a federal court complaint.
