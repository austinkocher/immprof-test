Although WIP and incubator submissions have closed for the 2023 Emerging Immigration Scholars Conference, you can still join as an attendee!    

The 2023 Conference will take place June 15 and 16, 2023, at the George Washington University Law School in Washington, D.C. There is a great line-up of presenters and panel sessions, as well as some built-in time to visit with old friends and make new ones. There is a tour and research presentation at the Library of Congress! Please see the conference page and schedule of events (which is being continuously updated) here.

The registration deadline for the Conference is May 26. If you wish to book a hotel room at the conference rate, please do so here by May 14.

Feel free to email emergingimmscholars23@gmail.com or contact any member of the conference steering committee directly with questions or concerns.
