Our nation's birthday, July 4, is a popular day for naturalization ceremonies.  One of the most popular ones is that held at Monticello, the home of Thomas Jefferson, the drafter of the Declaration of Independence.

This year, British-born comedienne Tracey Ullman spoke at the ceremony at Monticello, which has had a star-studded cast of speakers, including Presidents Gerald Ford and George W. Bush, over the years.
