CNN reports that some knowledgeable conservative commentators are characterizing Republic presidential candidate Donald Trump as a fascist.  Trump's anti-immigrant rhetoric, call for a national Muslim database, etc. contribute to the claim.  "Historians say they see other characteristics of fascism in Trump in addition to his propensity for racial and ethnic stereotyping. Among them: nativist undertones, attempts to control the media; and even condoning violence against his critics."
