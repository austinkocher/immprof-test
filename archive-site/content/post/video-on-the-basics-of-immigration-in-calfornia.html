This Pubic Policy Instiute of California (PPIC) video provides an overview of immigration in California: it covers key facts, highlights important trends, and looks at Californians’ views on immigration reform.

Immigration has long had a significant economic and social impact in California. The state is home to nearly 11 million immigrants—about a quarter of the immigrant population nationwide.

Watch the video—available in both English and Spanish—to learn about immigrants in our state.
