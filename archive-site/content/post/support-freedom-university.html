It is that joyous time of the year again, when Freedom U students admitted to colleges across the nation, prepare to go and fulfill their dreams. Will you help them make this dream possible by donating to the scholarship fund now?

Freedom University opened its doors in October of 2011 in defiance of the University System of Georgia Board of Regents’ ban on undocumented students, offering college-level courses for free, regardless of immigration status. While we succeeded in our mission to allow students to pursue higher education in spite of harsh immigration laws and policy, we need your help in order for them to achieve their ultimate goal of attending accredited two and four-year institutions. Freedom University students are an ambitious group who want to contribute to their communities by pursuing careers in health, law, journalism, engineering, public relations, teaching, etc.

KJ
