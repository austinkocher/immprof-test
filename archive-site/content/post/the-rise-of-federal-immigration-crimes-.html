--- title: "The Rise of Federal Immigration Crimes: Unlawful Reentry Drives Growth" date: 2014-03-19T09:31:42 url: "/post/the-rise-of-federal-immigration-crimes-/" aliases: ["/immigration/2014/03/the-rise-of-federal-immigration-crimes-.html"] type: post year: "2014" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Not a surprise but dramatic growth over the past two decades in the number of offenders sentenced in federal courts has been driven primarily by enforcement of a particular immigration offense—unlawful reentry into the United States—according to an analysis of data from the United States Sentencing Commission (USSC) by the Pew Research Center.
