In this New Yorker article ("The Refugee Dilemma: What do we owe those we take in?"), Rachel Aviv looks at the challenges  facing refugees who have fled war and violence in Africa who are resettled in the United States.  The article summarizes the work on a case by Linus Chan and the University of Minnesota’s Detainee Rights Clinic on one cases in which a refugee facing removal was released from immigrant detention.
