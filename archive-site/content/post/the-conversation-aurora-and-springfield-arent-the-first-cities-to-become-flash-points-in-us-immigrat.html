Former President Donald Trump caused controversy in blaming Venezuelan gangsters for a crime spree in Aurora, Colorado and Haitians for eating pets in Springfield, Ohio. In "Aurora and Springfield aren’t the first cities to become flash points in US immigration debate − here’s what happened in other places used as political soapboxes" in The Conversation, Miranda Cady Hallett explains how, when anti-immigrant politicians bring their hateful rhetoric to those towns, locals tend to come down on the side of peace and inclusion.
