Always the source of great immigration data, TRAC Immigration provides some handy facts on immigration detention:

26,771 in detention on July 22, 2021
 
79.4% of ICE immigrant detainees have no criminal record
 
Texas is the state with the most ICE detainees in FY 2021
 
26,532 people booked into ICE detention during June 2021
 
769 ICE detainees at South Texas ICE Processing Center
 
107,795 monitored through ICE's Alternatives to Detention (ATD) programs
 
San Francisco leads country in use of ICE's ATD programs
