Kathy McCormack for the Associated Press reports that an immigration judge denied bond for a Tufts student from Turkey who has been detained by authorities for three weeks over what her lawyers claim is retaliation for an op-ed piece she co-wrote in the student newspaper. 

 

Rumeysa Ozturk’s lawyers filed a new request of the federal court to take jurisdiction of her detention case.
