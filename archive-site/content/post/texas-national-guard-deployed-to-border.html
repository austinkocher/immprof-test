WaPo reports this morning that the Texas National Guard has been deployed by Governor Abbot to the U.S.-Mexico border. They're setting up concertina wire blockades with the goal of funneling border crossings back to ports of entry. (Those POEs, meanwhile, are still turning people away under the Title 42 program.) The photos accompanying the WaPo report are stunning and worth a click through.
