An ironic story from The Guardian: "Trump supporter wrongly detained by Ice questions his support for president."  The story states that 

"Jensy Machado, a naturalized US citizen, said he was in a car when he was stopped by Ice agents while on his way to work in Manassas, Virginia, where a large-scale immigration enforcement operation was recently taking place . . .  . `I was a Trump supporter,' he said on Wednesday. `I voted for Trump last election, but, because I thought it was going to be like ... against criminals, not every Hispanic, Spanish-lookalike.'”
