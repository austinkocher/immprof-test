Crimmigration Realities & Possibilities by César Cuauhtémoc García Hernández

This Foreword to a special issue of the Ohio State Journal of Criminal Law, Crimmigration Realities and Possibilities, contextualizes four articles elucidating crimmigration law’s problematic origins and its law enforcement applications as tools for reproducing the ideology of white supremacy.
