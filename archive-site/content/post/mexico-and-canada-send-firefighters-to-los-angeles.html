I don't need to tell you that our national discourse on Mexico and Canada is, politely speaking, not the best. Americans tend to describe Mexico as an invading force and portray Canada as a potential subject of our own invasion. Despite that, Mexico and Canada have both sent desperately needed firefighters and firefighting equipment to California to help in tackling the devastating Los Angeles fires.

Huge gratitude for our neighbors, Mexico and Canada have sent fire fighters and army to help us.
