Frances D'Emilio for the Associated Press reports on the latest in the seeming nevere-ending mass deaths oof migrants seeking to cross borders.  "At least 43 migrants died when their overcrowded wooden boat smashed into rocky reefs and broke apart off southern Italy before dawn Sunday, the Italian coast guard said. Survivors reportedly indicated that dozens more could be missing."

Television reports quoted survivors as saying the boat had set out from Turkey, which recently suffered a devastating earthquake.

Italy’s coast guard, which was coordinating the rescue, said about 120 migrants were believed to have been on board.
