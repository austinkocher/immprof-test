The Neighborhood Kids is a fusion rap/hip-hop band out of San Diego. I was first introduced to their music via this short concerning the detention of migrant children: 

@theneighborhoodkidsmusic This is our song “KIDS IN THE CAGES” ❌🧊 our rage turned into sound, this song is a call to action and a demand for justice, no more silence, no more cages, abolish ICE. Artist: The Neighborhood Kids #nativetiktok #IndigenousTikTok #nativerapper #altmetal #numetal #hiphop #undergroundhiphop #raptok ♬ KIDS IN THE CAGES - The Neighborhood Kids

Turns out this is a snippet of the band's song Kids In The Cages off their album cheekily titled Every Child Left Behind.

You can find their music on Spotify and Youtube and, no doubt, other platforms too.
