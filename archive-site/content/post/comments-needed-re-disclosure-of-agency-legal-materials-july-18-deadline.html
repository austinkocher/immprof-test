The Administrative Conference of the United States is soliciting comments regarding the disclosure of agency legal materials. The deadline for comment submission is July 18.

Here is the project description:

This project considers whether the main statutes governing disclosure of agencies’ legislative rules, guidance documents, adjudicative decisions, and other important legal materials should be amended to consolidate and harmonize their overlapping requirements, account for technological developments, correct certain statutory ambiguities and drafting errors, and address other potential problems that may be identified. If warranted, the project will recommend statutory reforms to provide clear standards as to what legal materials agencies must publish and where they must publish them (whether in the Federal Register, on their websites, or elsewhere). The objective of any such amendments will be to ensure that agencies provide ready public access to important legal materials in the most efficient way possible.

There are a number of law professors developing a research report on this topic. None are immprofs.

This seems like an area where immprofs and immigration practitioners might have a lot to say.
