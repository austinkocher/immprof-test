AILA To Provide Free Legal Consultations To San Diego Immigrant Community, AILA Presents Ronald Wada with the 2011 Edith Lowenstein Memorial Award" date: 2011-06-14T16:09:29 url: "/post/bravo-aila-aila-to-provide-free-legal-consultations-to-san-diego-immigrant-community-aila-presents-r/" aliases: ["/immigration/2011/06/bravo-aila-aila-to-provide-free-legal-consultations-to-san-diego-immigrant-community-aila-presents-r.html"] type: post year: "2011" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

The American Immigration Lawyers Association (AILA) will host a three hour pro bono session for area residents in need of legal advice regarding immigration concerns on Wednesday evening beginning at 5:00 p.m. More than 60 immigration attorneys will be available to answer questions and assess each participant's situation. The clinic, which will be held at the Thomas Jefferson School of Law, is being hosted in conjunction with the AILA Annual Conference later this week in San Diego.

 
Also, at the annual conference, AILA will award Ronald Wada, Senior Counsel in the San Francisco office of Tafapolsky & Smith, LLP, with the 2011 Edith Lowenstein Memorial Award for excellence in advancing the practice of immigration law. He will receive the award on June 16.
