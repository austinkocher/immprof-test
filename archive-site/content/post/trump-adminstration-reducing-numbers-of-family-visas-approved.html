Demonstrating that his concern is not only with removing undocumented immigrants from the United States, President Donald Trump has ramped up calls on the U.S. Congress to stop legal immigrants from sponsoring extended family members who want to move to the United States, saying so-called “chain migration” poses a threat to national security.  Reuters reports that, not surprisingly, even without legislative action,, the number of immigrants approved for family-based visas has dropped this year to the lowest level in more than a decade. “The number of approvals dropped by nearly a quarter in the first nine months of 2017 to around 406,000 compared to the same period a year earlier when approvals were more than 530,000, despite a similar number of applications during both periods.”
