Today the CBP issued a new rule. All planes to the U.S. carrying passengers who "recently traveled to, from, or through Ebola-stricken countries" must be diverted to one of the five U.S. airports where CBP is implementing "enhanced screening procedures": JFK, Newark, Dulles, O'Hare, and Atlanta.

This is not going to help their standings in the "worst U.S. airport" rankings. 2013 clocked EWR as the fourth-worst airport in America. JFK won sixth-worst. -
