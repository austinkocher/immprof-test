The immigrant community is in fear of what may happen in the incoming Trump administration.  Recipients of Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals, which Donald Trump has promised to dismantle, worry that information that they revealed to obtain relief may be used to deport them.  Hamed Alcaziz in the San Francisco Chronicle explores the fears of the Trump administration in the immigrant community.   He also considsers more general fears in the San Francisco Bay Area's minority communities in this article.
