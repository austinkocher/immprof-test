UCLA Law Professor Hiroshi Motomura will be offering his annual Immigration 101 session on Thursday, June 8. It’s an attempt to teach the highlights from a full semester immigration law course in two hours. The core intended audience is law students who want a basic understanding of immigration law and immigrants’ rights for their work at nonprofit organizations or public interest law firms this coming summer. The organizers are the Center for Immigration Law and Policy and the David J. Epstein Program in Public Interest Law and Policy at the UCLA School of Law, in cooperation with the ACLU of Southern California. Registration for the webinar is here.
