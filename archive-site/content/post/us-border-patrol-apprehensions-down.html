--- title: "U.S. Border Patrol Apprehensions Down" date: 2011-12-13T09:43:45 url: "/post/us-border-patrol-apprehensions-down/" aliases: ["/immigration/2011/12/us-border-patrol-apprehensions-down.html"] type: post year: "2011" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) today released year-end data on fiscal year (FY) 2011 border enforcement and management efforts.  U.S. Border Patrol apprehensions decreased to 340,252 in FY 2011, down 53 percent since FY 2008 and one fifth of what they were at their peak in FY 2000. Of these apprehensions, 87,334 people had a record in the FBI’s Integrated Automated Fingerprint Identification System, a law enforcement database of individuals with criminal charges and convictions.
