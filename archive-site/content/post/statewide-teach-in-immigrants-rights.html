The Center for Immigrants' Rights Clinic will hold a teach-in covering: immigration law 101; safety planning and finding good legal representation; understanding the Muslim "registry" or NSEERS; understanding "sanctuary"; building immigration policy at the university level; and more! For more information on immigration post-election, please visit the resources on our website. 

For those wishing to attend in the Philadelphia area, the event will be live-streamed in room T-145 of Penn Law at the University of Pennsylvania.

The event is co-sponsored by:

Center for Immigrants’ Rights Clinic at Penn State Law: University Park
