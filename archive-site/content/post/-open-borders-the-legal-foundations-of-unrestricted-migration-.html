A Free Migration Project event.

Currently, American law makes it very difficult for people to safely and legally migrate. In this panel, two leading law professors will discuss why this is the case and offer ideas about how to change that. There will be a moderated question and answer session where participants are invited to comment. Daniel Morales, Associate Professor of Law, University of Houston, and Ilya Somin, Professor of Law, George Mason University.
