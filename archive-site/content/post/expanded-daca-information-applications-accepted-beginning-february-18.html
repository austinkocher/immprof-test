As previously announced, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services will begin accepting requests for expanded DACA on February 18, 2015. To help you prepare, CIS has updated some of our resources for Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA):

  • Frequently Asked Questions

 

  • Form I-821D Instructions (PDF)

 

CIS states that it will post the revised form soon.

Please note, the FAQs and form instructions are not effective until Feb. 18, 2015.

Visit uscis.gov/immigrationaction or uscis.gov/accionmigratoria to learn more about expanded DACA.
