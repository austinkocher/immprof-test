President Trump has ended Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for Venezuelans and Haitians.  News agencies report that the Trump administration is considering ending TPS for Ukrainians.   "The planned rollback of protections for Ukrainians would be part of a broader Trump administration effort to strip legal status from more than 1.8 million migrants allowed to enter the U.S. under temporary humanitarian parole programs launched under the Biden administration, a senior Trump official and three sources familiar with the matter told Reuters."
