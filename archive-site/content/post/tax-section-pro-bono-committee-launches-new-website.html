Blog Emperor Paul Caron posted earlier today on TaxProf blog about the news that the ABA Tax Section Pro Bono Committee has launched a new website.  The new chair of the Committee Professor (and UC Davis Law alum) Francine Lipman mentions in her announcement of the new website that "I am hopeful that we can make this website `the go to link' for all of your tax Pro Bono matters, concerns, issues, thoughts, dreams and goals. Toward that goal I hope you will send any and all of your relevant materials to me for posting and broad distribution."

Professor Lipman has written some cutting edge scholarship on the tax contributions of undocumented immigrants, who wrongly are characterized as tax free-loaders.
