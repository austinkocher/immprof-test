Rosanna Xia for the Los Angeles Times reports that a record high of more than 1 million foreign students came to the United States for higher education last school year. Four of the top 20 institutions that welcomed them were in California, according to a new report.  The four:

1.  USC (13,340 international students)

2.  UCLA (11,513)

3.  UC San Diego

4.  UC Berkeley

5.  UC Irvine

The number of foreign students in California was 10.5% higher than the previous academic year. USC had the second largest number of international students in the country. 

The top universities hosting international students:

1.  NYU

2.  USC

3.  Arizona State University

4.  Columbia University

5.  University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign

6.  Northeastern University

7.  UCLA

8.  Purdue

9.  Boston University

10.  University of Washington
