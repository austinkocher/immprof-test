Today, the Center for American Progress released “Securing Our Borders,” a new report (Download Dww_borders[1]) that studies the Secure Border Initiative (SBI) and offers recommendations on how to fix it. SBI is a major border enforcement and surveillance program run by the Department of Homeland Security that has been plagued with serial failures for over two decades.

The report makes clear that critical to addressing border security is enacting comprehensive immigration reform.  The report outlines 10 reforms that could make SBI into an effective, cost-efficient border enforcement program. These include:

• Congress should address border security through comprehensive immigration reform legislation. Regarding border issues, a new law could reduce the inflow of illegal immigrants and lessen the constant pressure on border technologies while also creating new revenue streams to fund technology upgrades.

• Continue to pursue technological solutions. Technology remains important to securing the border, and the general concept of fences, cameras, and sensors can work if they are successfully integrated with each other.

• Collaborate with Mexico on border security. Greater efforts should be made to bring Mexico into the planning process since it is the primary source of illegal immigrants. One proposal worth considering is to form a Binational Border Authority with a joint budget and staff that would address issues stretching beyond law enforcement and security.

• The federal government should maintain tight control over the performance of contractors and agencies working on SBI while setting schedules with the understanding that they may need to be adjusted. Potential problems with contracts and contractors should be dealt with before they become issues to be discovered by outside investigators and auditors.

• Improve the viability and performance of border technology programs. Additional funding should be given only after these projects are ready to build out. We suggest that DHS use CAP’s recommendations as a template for improving SBI, including adding transparency and accountability for the projects. If, after careful review, DHS and Congress determine the program is salvageable and should proceed, then Congress should create additional revenue streams.
