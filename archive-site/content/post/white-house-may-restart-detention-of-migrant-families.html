The Biden administration recently responded to public concerns with immigration with a proposed rule restricting asylum eligibility.  Now,  numerous news agencies (here and here) are reporting that the White House is considering restarting the detention of migrant families caught crossing the U.S./Mexico border, several current and former U.S. officials told Reuters.  In addition, The Biden administration also is reportedly weighing reviving immigration arrests of migrant families within the United States who have been ordered deported.
