Tran Dang, Executive Director of the Rhizome Center for Migrants, has created a fabulous cheat sheet of the acronyms and definitions of key Mexican immigration agencies and concepts. She's approved sharing it with the immprof community. You can download it here: Download Acronyms_Definitions.

This might be particularly helpful to those immprofs leading clinics. For example, a client might talk about receiving help from ACNUR during their journey to the US. It's helpful to know that ACNUR = UNHCR. Or a Mexican client might talk about their CURP. It's helpful to know that CURP refers to a unique alphanumeric identifier for Mexican citizens that is akin to the US social security number.

Thanks, Tran, for sharing your work!
