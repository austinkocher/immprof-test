Professor Josh Blackman, who submittted an amicus brief supporting the challenge to the expanded deferred action program on behalf of the Cato Institute in Texas v. United States, has posted a multi-part, detailed summary of the Fifth Circuit's ruling on his blog.   The blog also notes ImmigrationProf's on-line symposium on the case.

The amicus brief argued that DAPA violated the president’s constitutional duty to take care that the laws were faithfully executed.
