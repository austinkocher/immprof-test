In an interview this weekend, Republican Vice President Candidate J.D. Vance discussed former President Trump's promise of a mass deportation campaign.  

Experts recently told ABC News that mass deportations would be a "nightmare.."

Senator Vance said a new Trump administration would take a "sequential approach" to removals.

"You start with what's achievable," Vance said. "I think that if you deport a lot of violent criminals and frankly if you make it harder to hire illegal labor, which undercuts the wages of American workers, I think you go a lot of the way to solving the illegal immigration problem."

Vance's statements seemed a bit superficial.  Wish the policy issues were that simple.
