The Wall Street Journal's podcast has a new episode on the politics of immigration: Chasing the Base: How the Border Surge Changed Immigration Politics. The episode explores how voters in both parties think about immigration issues in casting their ballots. The episode interviews voters in Queens and Long Island about how the think about immigration issues, especially given the rise in migrant arrivals in New York City. As a WSJ poll released in March  found, now 20% of voters rank immigration as their top issue, up from 13% in December. This makes immigration more of a priority issue than any other, including the economy. 

Here is a description of the episode:

Illegal immigration across the southern border has long been an issue that animates Republicans, but recent surges have made it a top concern for voters across the political spectrum. More than 100,000 migrants have come to New York City, and thousands of people are living in shelters in neighborhoods around the city while attempting to obtain legal status. The presence of these newcomers made immigration the top issue there in a recent special election for Congress. The winner, Democratic Rep. Tom Suozzi, won by talking about border security, and his party-mates hope he has cracked the code for parrying GOP attacks on the issue.
