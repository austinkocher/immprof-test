It's official. Joe Biden is going to be the 46th president of the United States. And that's pretty darn exciting.

With the election in hand, Biden now needs to turn to thinking about January 2021. One important thing for him to decide is the makeup of his cabinet. I'm taking the moment to nominate Julián Castro for Attorney General.

Let's start with the necessary qualifications: Castro is an attorney. HLS 2020 in the house! (For the record, I never met him. I was just a UCB exchange student at the time.) He has previous cabinet experience (HUD secretary for Obama). Biden knows him both from their overlapping service in the Obama administration and also as a co-competitor for the Democratic presidential nomination. When Biden won that contest, Castro supported Biden's nomination and worked to mobilized Latinx voters for him.

Why am I so bullish on Castro? Because he understands immigration. Recall that back in June 2019, Castro called for the repeal of 18 U.S.C. § 1325. Imagine what having a democratic AG who thinks actively about immigration could do:

  • Repeal AG Sessions' April 6, 2018 setting a zero tolerance policy for prosecutions under 8 U.S.C. § 1325.
  • Repeal AG Sessions' April 11, 2017 memo prioritizing criminal immigration enforcement
  • Repeal Matter of A-B- to support asylum seekers fleeing domestic violence
  • Repeal Matter of L-E-A- to support asylum seekers whose family members have experienced violence
  • Repeal Matter of Castro Tum to reinstate administrative closure
  • Repeal Neguise which held that the bar to eligibility for asylum and withholding of removal based on the persecution of others does not include an exception for coercion or duress.
  • Eliminate the metrics for reviewing IJs that incentivize denials
  • Work to eliminate for-profit prisons, affecting both immigration detention and criminal incarceration

All of this plus a legitimate voice on civil rights... Castro would be fantastic.
