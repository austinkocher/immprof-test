The Legal Aid Society/Employment Law Center annual luncheon, one of the foremost gatherings of the Bay Area legal community, provides essential support for the organization's free legal services. Join more than 850 guests, including attorneys from a wide spectrum of Bay Area and national firms; Federal and State court justices; summer law firm associates; representatives from corporations, foundations, and businesses; public officials; community leaders; and law school professors.

Tuesday, July 9th, 2013 Reception at 11 AM | Luncheon promptly at noon San Francisco Marriott Marquis | 55 Fourth Street at Mission

Keynote Speaker Thomas A. Saenz President & General Counsel, Mexican American Legal Defense and Educational Fund (MALDEF)

 


Recipient of the Mathew O. Tobriner Public Service Award

José R. Padilla Executive Director, California Rural Legal Assistance, Inc. (CRLA) Presented by The Honorable Cruz Reynoso (Ret.) Professor Emeritus at UC Davis School of Law, former Associate Justice of the California Supreme Court
