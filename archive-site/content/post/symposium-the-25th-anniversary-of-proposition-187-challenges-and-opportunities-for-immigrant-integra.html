The UC Davis Law Review has published a symposium on the 25th anniversary of California's Proposition 187:

The 25th Anniversary of Proposition 187: Challenges and Opportunities for Immigrant Integration and Political Identity in California

  • Latino Political Participation 25 Years After the Passage of Proposition 187: Opportunities and Continuing Challenges Marisa Abrajano - UC San Diego, Lisa García Bedolla - UC Berkeley
  • Proposition 187 and Its Political Aftermath: Lessons for U.S. Immigration Politics After Trump Kevin R. Johnson - UC Davis School of Law
  • Dreamers Interrupted: The Case of the Rescission of the Program of Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals Rachel F. Moran - UC Irvine School of Law
  • Proposition 187 and the Legacy of Its Law Enforcement Provisions Huyen Pham - Texas A&M University School of Law
  • The First Anti-Sanctuary Law: Proposition 187 and the Transformation of Immigration Enforcement Rick Su - University of North Carolina School of Law
  • Reflecting on California and Prop. 187: From the Anti-Immigrant State to the Sanctuary State Rose Cuison Villazor - Rutgers Law School
  • Recollections of the Legal Battle Against Proposition 187 Thomas A. Saenz - Mexican American Legal Defense and Educational Fund (MALDEF)
