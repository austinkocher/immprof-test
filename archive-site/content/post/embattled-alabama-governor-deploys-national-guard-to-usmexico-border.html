Alabama Governor Robert Bentley, a staunch supporter of Alabama's strict immigration enforcement law known as HB 56, has been embroiled in a controversy over the nature of his relationship with a chief staffer.  Impeachment has been proposed.  The Governor's response: 

A small contingent from the Alabama National Guard has been ordered to Arizona to help protect the U.S. border with Mexico, Gov. Robert Bentley's office announced yesterday. A UH-72 helicopter and three pilots from Alabama will be serving in Marana, Arizona. 

"Securing the U.S. border is an issue that is important to every American, and the Alabama National Guard is supporting the effort to secure the border," Bentley said in a written statement. "The men and women who serve in the Alabama National Guard are some of the most dedicated and professional military service members in the country. I appreciate the Guard's effort to support the Southwest Border Mission."

The crew will remain in Arizona through September.
