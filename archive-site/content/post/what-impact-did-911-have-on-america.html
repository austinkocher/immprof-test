On the ten year anniversary of the events of September 11, 2001, expect there to be much in the news about the changes since that fateful day. The Guardian adds to the discussion with "What impact did 9/11 have on America? Our panel examines the cultural and political legacy of the al-Qaida attacks and the US response in the decade since. " The controbutors include Martin Kettle, Andrea LeBlanc, Hadley Freeman, Wajahat Ali, Michael Ratner, Peter Preston, Carie Lemack and Paul Pillar.
