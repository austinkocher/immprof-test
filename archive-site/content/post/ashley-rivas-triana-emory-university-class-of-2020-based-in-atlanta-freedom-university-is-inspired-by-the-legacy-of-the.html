Ashley Rivas-Triana: Emory University Class of 2020

Based in Atlanta, Freedom University is inspired by the legacy of the Southern freedom school tradition. It provided tuition-free education, college application and scholarship assistance, and social movement leadership training to undocumented students banned from public higher education in Georgia.

This year, 18 Freedom University students from our 2015-2016 class applied to college with the assistance of our faculty and mentors. We are thrilled to announce that six students won full, merit-based scholarships to colleges and universities across the country, including Dartmouth College, Emory University, Berea College, Tougaloo College, and Eastern Connecticut State University! One student, Mary, won numerous merit scholarships to allow her to pursue her pre-med studies at Oglethorpe University. The students' determination, the support of their families, the commitment of volunteers, and the generosity of hundreds of supporters helped make their dreams of attending college a reality!
