U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services celebrates the 235th anniversary of the adoption of the official national symbol—the Stars and Stripes—by welcoming thousands of new citizens in special naturalization ceremonies across the nation. Flag Day celebrations run June 8-22 and include events in towns from Alaska to Maine.  Here is the schedule.
