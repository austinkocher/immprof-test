Democratic Presidential candidate Elizabeth Warren yesterday released her immigration proposal on Medium and took aim at the immigration enforcement agencies and President Trump's immigration enforcement policies.

As CNN reported, the Massachusetts senator rolled out her plan hours before addressing LULAC, a Latino civil rights organization, in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.
