Yesterday, the Trump administration revoked Harvard University’s ability to enroll international students and proclaimed that current students must transfer to other schools or have their visas revoked. Read more from the Associated Press. 

Photo from DHS.gov website

Secretary of the Department of Homeland Security Kristi Noem reportedly has said that other universities should heed the warning.
 

According to the Associated Press, Harvard enrolls almost 6,800 international foreign students. Most are graduate students, coming from more than 100 countries. 

The Department of Homeland Security alleges that Harvard has created an unsafe campus environment by allowing “anti-American, pro-terrorist agitators” to assault Jewish students on campus. The Associated Press reports that "[i]t also accused Harvard of coordinating with the Chinese Communist Party, saying it hosted and trained members of a Chinese paramilitary group as recently as 2024."

 

This administration is holding Harvard accountable for fostering violence, antisemitism, and coordinating with the Chinese Communist Party on its campus.
