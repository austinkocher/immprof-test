Luke Broadwater for the New York Times looks at President Trump's challenge to the due process rights of noncitizens facing removal from the United States.  "While the Justice Department argues in court that it is working to comply with judges’ orders to provide migrants with due process before deporting them, President Trump and his top advisers are increasingly making a different argument altogether: Why should we?"

Absent notice and an opportunity to be heard, we can expect mistakes to be made.  And mistakes have been made -- Mahmoud Khalil was arrested without a warrant, immigration authorities have been instructing U.S. citizens (including immigration attorneys) to leave the country, and the mistaken removal of Kilmar Abrego Garcia.  Will the due process continue to protect noncitizens from arbitrary and mistaken governmental action?
