ACLU Immigrants Rights' Director (former) and Deputy Legal Director (currently) Cecilia Wang will speak on "The Challenges and Rewards of Impact Litigation." She will appear in a hybrid lecture at UC Hastings College of Law in San Francisco on Friday, March 18, 2022 (12-1pm Pacific). Zoom information and registration appears here.  

Cecillia Wang is a deputy legal director at the national ACLU and directs the Center for Democracy, which encompasses the ACLU’s work on immigrants’ rights, voting rights, national security, human rights, and speech, privacy and technology. She is a past director of the ACLU Immigrants’ Rights Project and a nationally recognized expert on issues at the intersection of immigration and criminal law, including state anti-immigrant laws, racial profiling and other unlawful police practices relating to immigration enforcement.
