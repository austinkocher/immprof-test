Nia Prater on the Intelligencer reports on the ripple effects of the Justice Department's dropping of corruption charges case against New York City Mayor Eric Adams, which led to the quick resignation of six department officials in protest.

Yesterday, Mayor Adams appeared with the Trump administration's border czar Tom Homan on Fox & Friends.  Prater writes that

"the Trump official appeared to revel in Adams’s newfound attachment to the administration. . . . Adams and Homan discussed the city’s improved working relationship with the White House, in the wake of Adams’s announcement that he will be signing an executive order to allow U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents to access Rikers Island. Homan noted that he had been a frequent critic of the mayor in years past, but no longer."
