 American Libraries reports that the Library of Congress Policy and Standards Division, which maintains the LC Subject Headings, has cancelled “Illegal aliens” as a subject heading because the phrase has become pejorative. The heading will be replaced by “Noncitizens” and “Unauthorized immigration.” The ALA Council had passed a resolution that called for retiring the heading.
