December has seen the Trump era Title  border expulsion order in the news.  As we end the year, it remains in place.

ABC News reports that Title 42, by failing to punish recidivist border crossers, may not be as effective as the ordinary border enforcement approach.  "[A] rare in-depth look at Border Patrol data reveals evidence that the end of Title 42 could in fact reduce border crossings long-term and ease the burden on the immigration system while allowing migrants to make legally authorized humanitarian claims."
