Reuters)reports on a Donald Trump campaign rally in Grand Rapids, Michigan.  Behind a placard stating "Stop Biden's Border Bloodbath," he called undocumented immigrants "animals" and "not human" in the speech, repeating rhetoric he uses regularly on the campaign trail.  "The Republican presidential candidate, appearing with several law enforcement officers, described in detail several criminal cases involving suspects in the country illegally and warned that violence and chaos would consume America if he did not win the Nov. 5 election."
