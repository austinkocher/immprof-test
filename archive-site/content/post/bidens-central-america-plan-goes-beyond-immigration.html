The Biden Plan To Build Security And Prosperity In Partnership With The People Of Central America takes a very un-Trumpian approach to migration from the Northern Triangle. Instead of focusing on thwarting migrants at the border or deporting those already present in the United States, Biden argues that:

[t]he Western Hemisphere has the potential to be secure, democratic, and prosperous from the northern reaches of Canada all the way to the southern tip of Chile. Critical to achieving this goal is ensuring that the nations of Central America–especially El Salvador, Guatemala, and Honduras, the Northern Triangle countries–are strong, secure, and capable of delivering futures of opportunity for their own people...

He does want to reduce migration from the Northern Triangle, but seeks to accomplish this goal by:

comprehensively address[ing] its root causes–the factors pushing people to leave their countries in the first place[.] ... engag[ing] the leaders of the region to take responsibility for improving economic prosperity through poverty reduction and regional integration programs, deepen[ing] security cooperation to reduce gang violence and combat transnational criminal organizations, and strengthen[ing] institutions through technical assistance [to] improve[] governance and transparent fiscal management[.]

Here are his literal bullet points:

  • Developing a comprehensive four-year, $4 billion regional strategy to address factors driving migration from Central America;
  • Mobilizing private investment in the region;
  • Improving security and rule of law;
  • Addressing endemic corruption;
  • Prioritizing poverty reduction and economic development.

As the LA Times reports, it's a more "holistic" vision for the US relations with the region.
