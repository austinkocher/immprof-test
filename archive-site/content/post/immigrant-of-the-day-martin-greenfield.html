The President's Tailor Survived the Holocaust 3:28 Born in Czechoslovakia, Martin Greenfield's story is extraordinary. He was liberated from Auschwitz as a 15 year old and immigrated to the United States in 1947. Grateful for freedom and his new life, Greenfield is a tailor of fine clothes.  He decided to fashion President Eisenhower a custom three-piece suit. Since then, Greenfield has created beautiful custom suits for every American president.
