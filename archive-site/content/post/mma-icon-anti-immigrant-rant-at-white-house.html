Mixed martial artist icon Conor McGregor criticized immigration in St. Patrick’s Day on a visit yesterday with Donald Trump in the White House.

CNN reports that Ireland’s leader has condemned the anti-immigration comments made by McGregor in an appearance by the former MMA star in the White House.

In the White House briefing room, McGregor said the Irish government had “abandoned the voices” of the Irish people and claimed rural towns in Ireland were being overrun by immigrants.
