FREE virtual screening of Sanctuary City play and live talkback with playwright Martyna Majok this week!

All re invited to a free screening of the award-winning play, Sanctuary City, followed by a talkback with the playwright Martyna Majok and immigrant rights activists and scholars. The talkback will be on Thursday, November 18, at 8:45 p.m. (immediately after the film's showing). You will have 96 hours in which to watch the play. Please register here, choose a date between November 16-18 to begin your streaming window, choose the "extended access" option, and use the code RUTGERS. An e-ticket to the play and talkback will be emailed.

About the play: DREAMers. Love(r)s. Life-long friends. Negotiating the promise of safety and the weight of responsibility, they’ll fight like hell to establish a place for themselves and each other in America. 2018 Pulitzer Prize winner Martyna Majok brings us an unforgettable story that asks what we’re willing to sacrifice for someone we love.
