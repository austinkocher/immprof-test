Maricopa County, Arizona Sheriff Joe Arpaio, who a federal court has found guilty of contempt of court as well as heading a sheriff's office guilty of mass racial profiling, is running for re-election.  Philanthropist George Soros is funding an anti-Arpaio group in an attempt to weaken his bid for a seventh straight term.

The group started sending fliers to Phoenix-area voters two weeks ago, and a mailing last week accuses Arpaio of separating a mother from her child because of an unpaid traffic ticket, botching hundreds of sex crimes investigations and scaring immigrants so much that that they don't report crime.

Arpaio denies the claims and is moving forward in the campaign before the Nov. 8 vote.  Hischallenger, Democrat Paul Penzone, is  a former Phoenix police sergeant who lost to Arpaio by 6 percentage points in 2012.  The entry of a Soros-linked group is a gift for Penzone.
