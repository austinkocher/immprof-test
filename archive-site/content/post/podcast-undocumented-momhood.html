Podcasts have grown in popularity in recent years.  Laura Rodriguez is a fashion designer, activist, mom, eco-friendly and business owner.  She also is undocumented. Originally from Mexico, she has lived in Iowa for more than 10 years and recently started a podcast, "Undocumented Momhood," to share her experiences. Kassidy Arena reports on Rodriguez and the podcast for Iowa Public Radio. Rodriguez told Iowa Public Radio she wants to put a face on immigration, and talking to people about her perspectives may help make Iowa an even more welcoming place.
