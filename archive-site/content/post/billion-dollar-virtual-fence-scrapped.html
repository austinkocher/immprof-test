Reuters reports that the Obama administration on Friday canceled the "virtual fence" project meant to better guard stretches of the vast U.S. border with Mexico. The project, begun in 2006, has cost about $1 billion and was designed to use video cameras, radar, sensors and other technologies to catch migrants  trying to cross the border.
