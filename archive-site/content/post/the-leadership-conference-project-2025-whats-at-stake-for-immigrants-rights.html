In Project 2025: What’s At Stake for Immigrants’ Rights (Aug. 2024),the Leadership Conference lists the top likely strategies for immigration restriction under the incoming Trump administration:

Enabling a Nationwide Deportation Machine

Militarizing the Border

Expanding Immigrant “Detention” Centers

Eliminating Popular Relief Programs and Visa Categories

Mandating Misguided Work Verification Programs

Entangling Local and Federal Enforcement

 

I might list them in a different order, with the last item as probably the first Trump priority.  Still, those interested in immigration should watch how the new administration employs these and other strategies to transform the U.S. immigration system.
