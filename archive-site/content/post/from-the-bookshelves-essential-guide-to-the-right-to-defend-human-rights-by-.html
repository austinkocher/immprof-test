United Nations Special Rapporteur Margaret Sekaggya has released a guide
(Commentary to the Declaration on human rights defenders) to the right to defend human rights, a key document aimed at supporting those who stand for rights by increasing understanding of the UN Declaration on human rights defenders and awareness on the dangers they face. The hope is to contribute to the development of a safer and more conducive environment for defenders to be able to carry out their work.

The Commentary is a 100-page downloadable document which maps out the rights provided for in the Declaration, based mostly on information received and reports produced by the two Special Rapporteurs on the situation of human rights defenders, Hina Jilani (2000-2008) and Margaret Sekaggya (since 2008), during the past eleven years.
