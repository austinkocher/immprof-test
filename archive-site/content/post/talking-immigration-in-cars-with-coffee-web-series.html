In a new UCLA Center for Immigration Law and Policy web series, Talking Immigration in Cars with Coffee, host Ahilan Arulanantham talks with legal scholars, advocates, community members, and leaders in the immigrants’ rights space.

Check out the 2-minute series teaser:

 

 
     

Episode one features CILP’s own Ahilan Arulanantham & Hiroshi Motomura delving into the legal authority underlying the California Opportunity for All Act. 

*Episode one aired on September 17, 2024. While the Opportunity for All Act passed both chambers of the California legislature, it was unfortunately vetoed by the governor on September 22. 

In Episode Two(out yesterday!), Ahilan talks with immigrants’ rights community leader and strategist Juan Escalante.
