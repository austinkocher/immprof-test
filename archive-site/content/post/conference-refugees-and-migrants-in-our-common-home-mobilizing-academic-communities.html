Villanova University Charles Widger School of Law
 
The conference is in Rome from October 1-3, 2025, just before the Jubilee for Migrants and Refugees (October 4-5, 2025).   It will be a global gathering of academics, researchers, NGOs, and community partners. Together, we will co-create an Action Plan addressing critical issues affecting migrants and refugees. The event will feature keynote addresses, panel discussions, and working groups where participants will collaborate to develop actionable strategies for higher education institutions to enhance teaching, research, and service to migrant and refugee communities.
