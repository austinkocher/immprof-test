U.S. District Judge Stephen H. Locher today granted a preliminary injunction against Iowa's Senate File 2340.

The Iowa law, you may recall:

(i) imposes criminal penalties under state law for certain immigration-related offenses; and

(ii) requires state court judges to order noncitizens to return to the foreign countries from which they came.

Judge Locher determined that the Plainitffs--the United States and the Iowa Migrant Movement for Justice--"established a likelihood of success on the merits of their position that federal immigration law preempts Senate File 2340 under both conflict and field preemption."
