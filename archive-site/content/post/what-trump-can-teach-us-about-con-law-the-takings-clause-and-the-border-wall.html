The latest episode of Elizabeth Joh and Roman Mars' podcast on What Trump Can Teach About Con Law looks at the Takings Clause and President Trump's proposed border wall.

  • 8- The Takings Clause

    Play Now

    To build a wall, Trump is going to need to seize private land. The Constitution has something to say about that and it’s known as the Takings Clause.

    The podcast looks carefully at the design of the border wall and the need to utilize eminent domain to build the wall on private property along the border.
