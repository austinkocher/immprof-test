The ABA journal identified not one but TWO immigration-related apps in its recent summary of 20 apps that help provide easier access to legal help."

Yesterday, we covered CitizenshipWorks. Today, we cover Pocket DACA - an immigration innovation that we covered back in August 2013.

Pocket DACA  is a free app aimed to help out-of-status kids in the US determine if they are DACA eligible. It also includes a searchable directory of immigration legal services providers in all 50 states. And it offers links to breaking news on DACA.

The app was created by the Immigration Advocates Network the American Immigration Lawyers Association, American Immigration Council, and Pro Bono Net as part of the Own the DREAM Campaign.
