He's back!  On the same day that a federal court found that his administration's extraordinary Title 42 order was arbitrary and capricious, Donald Trump announced that he would again run for President.  In his announcement, Trump made it clear that immigration control was a major plank in his platform.  

As readers know, President Trump made immigration a central policy priority while in office.
