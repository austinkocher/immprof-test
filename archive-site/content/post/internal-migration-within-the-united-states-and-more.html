The U.S. Census has a "Migration/Geographic Mobility," which refers to the movement of people within the United States from one location to another at various geographic levels. Movers are classified by type of move and characteristics of movers. People who moved from abroad are also included. There is an incredible amount of information here for you migration researchers.
