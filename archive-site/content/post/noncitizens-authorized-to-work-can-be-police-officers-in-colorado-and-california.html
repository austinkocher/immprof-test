--- title: "Noncitizens Authorized to Work Can Be Police Officers in Colorado and California" date: 2023-07-17T08:11:05 url: "/post/noncitizens-authorized-to-work-can-be-police-officers-in-colorado-and-california/" aliases: ["/immigration/2023/07/noncitizens-authorized-to-work-can-be-police-officers-in-colorado-and-california.html"] type: post year: "2023" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

New laws in Colorado and California permit noncitizens who are authorized to work in the United States to become police officers, reports Janelle Griffith for NBC News. The measures, which make DACA recipients eligible for law enforcement work, come at a time when some police departments are struggling to recruit and retain officers.
