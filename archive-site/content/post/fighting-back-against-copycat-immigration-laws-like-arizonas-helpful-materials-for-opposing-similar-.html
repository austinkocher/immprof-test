The Center for American Progress has a posting, Fighting Back Against Copycat Immigration Laws Like Arizona's: Helpful Materials for Opposing Similar Legislation by Henry Fernandez, which may be of interest to state and local activists. Since the passage of Arizona's SB1070 law similar laws have been proposed in other states around the country. It inlcudesd links to materials that can be helpful in resisting such laws.
