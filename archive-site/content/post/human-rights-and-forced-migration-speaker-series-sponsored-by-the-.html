The James H. Binger Center for New Americans is hosting a Human Rights and Forced Migration Speaker Series.

SESSION I, Climate Impacts as Drivers of Displacement, will take place Wednesday, February 22nd (12:15 PM - 1:15 PM CST)

The Session will focus on how the international community responded to climate change and related migration. Panelists will explore how the US has responded, and what can be done to better prepare for and respond to climate migration. In addition to what human rights violations result from climate change related migration.
