A Department of Homeland Security press release:  "The Interagency Task Force on the Reunification of Families (Task Force) submitted to President Biden its Initial Progress Report, which details ongoing efforts to identify and reunite children who were unjustly separated from their parents at the United States-Mexico border under the prior Administration."  Check out the report for details.  The bottom line is that a lot of work remains to be done.
