The Wake Forest Journal of Law and Policy is pleased to announce the publication of Volume 3, Issue 2, centering on the issues surrounding the Supreme Court’s 2012 decision, Arizona v. United States.  The  issue is lead off by a Foreword by Professor Jennifer M. Chacon(UC Irvine) and articles by Melissa Keaney & Alvaro M. Huerta (National Immigration Law Center), Christopher Lasch (Denver), Karla McKanders (Tennessee), and Kristina Campbell (DC Law School).
