What is the LEAST Latino State in the Union?

 

 

 

HINT

 

 

 
 

 

Answer:  West Virginia, where Latinos are slightly just over one percent of the state population, is the state with the smallest percentage of Latinos in the United States. The 2009 Census estimates that Hispanics make up 1.26 percent – or 22,662 -- of the state’s 1.8 million residents. That represented a growth for the state’s Latinos, who comprised .7 percent of West Virginians in 2000, and .5 percent in 1990, according to Census data.
