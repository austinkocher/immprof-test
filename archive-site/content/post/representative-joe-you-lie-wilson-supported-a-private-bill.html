Raw Story reports that the infamous Representative Joe ("You Lie") Wilson (R-SC), who badgered President Obama about allegedly lying about whether his health care reform proposal would provide coverage to undocumented immigrants, sponsored a private bill a few months back to prevent the deportation of an undocumented immigrant from Gambia who had lived in the United States for many years.  Such support for an undocumented immigrant is understandable but seems inconsistent with the general thrust of Wilson's criticism of the Obama health care reform proposal.

RaceWire previously reported that Wilson had claimed to have once been an immigration lawyer; those familiar with his career described Wilson as a real estate lawyer, however.
