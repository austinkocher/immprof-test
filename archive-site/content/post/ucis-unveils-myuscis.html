U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services has unveiled myUSCIS , which is designed to make immigration simpler.  MyUSCIS is a new service that helps you navigate the immigration process. At myUSCIS, you’ll find up-to-date information about the application process for immigration benefits, tools to help you prepare for naturalization, and resources to find citizenship preparation classes and doctors in your community.  

You can use myUSCIS anytime and on any device.
