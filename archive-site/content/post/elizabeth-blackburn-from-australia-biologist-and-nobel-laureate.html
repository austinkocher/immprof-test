Elizabeth Blackburn From Australia | Biologist and Nobel Laureate

Elizabeth Blackburn, the Morris Herzstein Professor in Biology and Physiology at the University of California, San Francisco, is a renowned researcher who has conducted groundbreaking work on DNA and cell division. She was previous a faculty member at the University of California at Berkeley, in the Department of Molecular Biology, and holds a Ph.D. from the University of Cambridge. The winner of many prestigious honors, she was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 2009.
