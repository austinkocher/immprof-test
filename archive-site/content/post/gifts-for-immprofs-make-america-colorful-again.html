Oakland artist Joey Yang is behind this gem of a holiday gift idea for the immprof in your life — Make America Colorful Again: A Donald Trump coloring book, badly drawn. One hundred percent of the proceeds from the sale of this book will go to International Institute of the Bay Area, a non-profit providing "high-quality immigration legal services, education, and civic engagement opportunities to immigrants, refugees, and their families as they join and contribute to the community."

SF Gate has a nice story on the project, as does Newsweek.

I know you're thinking "Trump gifts will be passé by the time the holidays roll around." To you I say "You cannot be sure - Vote, dammit!" And once my blood pressure normalizes, I'd point out that this is a fun project with funds going to a great cause. It's hard to do better than a gift like that.
