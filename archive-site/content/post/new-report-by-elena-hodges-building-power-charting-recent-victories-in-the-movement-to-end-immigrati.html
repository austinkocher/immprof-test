Elena Hodges, J.D. ‘22, N.Y.U. School of Law, has published a powerful new report titled: BUILDING POWER: CHARTING RECENT VICTORIES IN THE MOVEMENT TO END IMMIGRATION DETENTION IN THE UNITED STATES and available here.

The report discusses how the movement to end immigrant detention in the U.S. has won many important victories in recent years. These victories include legislation in six states to prohibit public and/or private contracts with ICE to detain immigrants. Additionally, many contracts between ICE and private contractors have been ended, or bed space has been reduced, due to organizing by people in detention.
