It looks like Blog Emperor Paul Caron's empire is coming to an end with the dismantling of Typepad, which is shutting down at the end of the month.  The ImmigrationProf blog has been operating since September 2005 and has more than 10.8 million hits.  Our blog team is figuring out how to proceed and will let you all know whatever is decided.  Thanks for all your support and consideration.
