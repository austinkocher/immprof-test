Check out 12:08-13:52 of the NBC Nightly News episode below. It covers the story of Mahud Villalaz, a U.S. citizen who sustained injuries when an individual threw battery acid onto his face. This happened Friday on the streets of Milwaukee following an altercation over parking. Moments before throwing the acid, the now-criminal-defendant told Villalaz: "You don't respect my laws. You can't invade my country. So go back to your country."
