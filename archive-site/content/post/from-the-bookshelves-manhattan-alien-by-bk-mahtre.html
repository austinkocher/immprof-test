Manhattan Alien by B.K. Mhatre

After visiting US for the first time, author wrote an email to friends and family about his experiences which they thoroughly enjoyed. This inspired the author to pen this book! While pursuing the American Dream, the author went through many interesting experiences. Some of the incidents in the book were experienced by the author and his friends, the rest are purely fictional. Those born and brought up in USA don't experience America, the way someone fresh off the boat does. The author portrays America through the eyes of an immigrant. an alien, indulging, at times, in satire.
