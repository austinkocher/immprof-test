On January 20, 2025, the Trump Administration rescinded policy long in place that protected churches, school, and hospitals from immigration enforcement and replaced it with a directive that gives Immigration and Customs Enforcement officers power to engage in enforcemen in any of these spaces using so-called “common sense.”

Ana B. Ibarra and Kristen Hwang for CalMatters report that U.S. federal immigration officers are routinely showing up at California medical facilities:

"They may come to the emergency room, bringing in someone who’s suffering a medical crisis while being detained. They may wait in the lobby, as agents did for two weeks at an L.A.-area hospital waiting for a woman to be discharged. Or they may even chase people inside, as federal agents did at a Southern California surgical center. 

The sight of these agents — often armed and with covered faces — makes many wary and may keep people from seeking care."
