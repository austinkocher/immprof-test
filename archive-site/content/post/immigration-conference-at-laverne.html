On February 19-20, the University of La Verne College of Law will host an immigration symposium with clinicians, scholars, and practitioners who will grapple with a wide range of cutting edge immigration issues. In coordination with the live event, the University of La Verne Law Review is publishing a Symposium Issue on Immigration Law, and will be accepting submissions through February 1, 2010. The keynote speaker is Hiroshi Motomura (UCLA) who will talk on "What Is `Comprehensive Immigration Reform'?" Download Ils-flyer[1]
