30,000 people came to Washington last week to raise their voice on immigration reform. Take 30 minutes to meet 5 Dreamers and discover who they really are. Today at 4pm/ET, 1pm/PT, watch the new 30-minute documentary about America's broken immigration system by Academy Award-winner Davis Guggenheim, "The Dream is Now." Putting a human face on the immigration reform issue, this thought-provoking film was created to keep the focus on Congress' efforts to pass reform that will give undocumented youth and their families the chance to earn their citizenship.
