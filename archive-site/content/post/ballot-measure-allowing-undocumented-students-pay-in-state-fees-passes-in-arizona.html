Lots of good election news from Arizona.  A new woman Democratic governor.  Senator Mark Kelly is reelected.  Here is an immigration ballot measure

Proposition 308, a ballot measure granting in-state university fees to undocumented students in Arizona, passed by a small margin.

Yes votes for Proposition 308 received 51% of the counted ballots.
