Amy Howe for SCOTUSBlog (Court dismisses Title 42 case) reports on the latest developments in the Title 42 case.  

As has been widely reported, the Biden administration last week lifted the Title 42 border closure order entered initrially by the Trump admionistration.

Yesterday, the justices sent Arizona v. Mayorkas, a case concerning a Trump-era border policy, back to the lower court with instructions to dismiss the dispute as moot because the Title 42, order expired. The justices agreed to take the case in December but later removed it from their argument calendar.
