CNN reports on a story that has me scratching my head.  "A man who worked for the Department of Justice was charged Wednesday after allegedly throwing a sandwich at a federal law enforcement officer in Washington, DC, amid President Donald Trump’s takeover of the city’s police and increase in federal law enforcement presence."

Attorney General Pam Bondi responded in the tweet above.  “If you touch any law enforcement officer, we will come after you,” Bondi wrote. “I just learned that this defendant worked at the Department of Justice — NO LONGER. Not only is he FIRED, he has been charged with a felony.”

The sandwich-thrower reportedly "was an international affairs specialist in the Criminal Division of the Office of International Affairs."

Here are the facts of the incident as reported by CNN:
 
"According to police, Sean Charles Dunn confronted a group of US Customs and Border Protection officers on 14th Street in Northwest DC on Sunday night, calling them `fascists,' according to court documents. During the incident, Dunn allegedly approached a CBP officer and shouted expletives, adding, `Why are you here? I don’t want you in my city!'

Dunn then crossed the street but returned and threw a `sub-style sandwich' at the officer’s chest, police said. Dunn then attempted to run away but was arrested, the documents said.

Dunn allegedly confessed while being processed, saying, `I did it. I threw a sandwich,' the documents stated. Dunn is charged with assaulting a federal police officer."

The U.S. Attorney for Washington, D.C. DC Jeanine Pirro touted Dunn’s arrest on Wednesday, saying in a video shared on X, “We’re going to back the police to the hilt. So there, stick your Subway sandwich somewhere else.”
