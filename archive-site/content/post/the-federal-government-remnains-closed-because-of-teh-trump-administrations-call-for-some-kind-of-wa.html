Happy New Year!  It is an unprecedented immigration news cycle.  The federal government is shut down over a budget impasse over congressional funding of "the wall" along the U.S./Mexico border.  Now, there is a debate over what precise kind of "wall" is wanted by the Trump administration.

As CNN reports, President Donald Trump on Monday pushed back against claims that his administration has abandoned a concrete wall on the border, despite his own outgoing chief of staff's statements that the idea was ruled out "early on in the administration."
