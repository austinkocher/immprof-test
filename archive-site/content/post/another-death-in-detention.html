For the second time this year, an immigrant in detention in Georgia has died, reports Lautaro Grinspan of The Atlanta Journal-Constitution. Cambric Dennis, 44 and originally from Liberia, died last week at the for-profit Stewart Detention Center. The official cause of death is pending. Indian national Jaspal Singh died in April after spending months at the Folkston ICE Processing Center elsewhere in Georgia.
