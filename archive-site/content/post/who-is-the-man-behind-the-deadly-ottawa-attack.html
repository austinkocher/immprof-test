One can only wonder what might cause someone to walk up to a war memorial, shoot a soldier, and then open fire in Canada's Parliament.  The attacker, identified as Michael Zehaf-Bibeau, was shot and killed before he could seriously hurt anyone inside Parliament.

News reports are offering a glimpse of Zehaf-Bibeau, a convert to Islam who had a criminal record and was seeking a passport so that he could travel to Libya.  He was born in 1982 in Canada.  His father is from Libya, while his mother is Canadian.  Zehaf-Bibeau's mother is the deputy chairwoman of the immigration division of the Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada.
