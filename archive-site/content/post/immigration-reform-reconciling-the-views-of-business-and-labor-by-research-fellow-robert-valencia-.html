Immigration Reform: Reconciling the Views of Business and Labor, by Research Fellow Robert Valencia (DC: Council on Hemispheric Affairs, 2010) lays out the argument for the new Republican Congress to bring business and labor together to reform the immigration laws so that they better address the economic needs of employers and workers and so that the U.S. immigration laws better meet the nation's economic needs.
