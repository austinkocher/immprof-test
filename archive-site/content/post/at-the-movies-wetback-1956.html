In some work with a museum, I learned about a film from 1956 starring Lloyd Bridges that broaches the topic of human trafficking using the parlance of the time.  The film is Wetback, which the IMDb database describes as follows:   "Hard on his luck, a fishing charter boat captain unwisely gets involved with a crime ring smuggling illegal Mexicans into California."

President Trump has suggested bringing back "Operation Wetback," a 1954 military style enforcement operation.  It resulted in the mass deportation of immigrants and U.S. citizens of Mexican ancestry.
