Possible Republican Presidential candidate Sarah Palin visited Ellis Island last week.  While extolling the virtues of past immigrants who waited in line to come to the United States, she expressed opposition to the DREAM Act for "usurp[ing] that . . . system."  Palin's viwews on the DREAM Act are not surprising.  She previously expressed her enthusiastic support for Arizona S.B. 1070 and exclaimed "We are all Arizonans now!"
