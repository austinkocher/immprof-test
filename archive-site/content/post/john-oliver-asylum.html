On his show Last Week Tonight, John Oliver tackled the issue of asylum. As Oliver notes: "The Trump Administration's attack on asylum has been focused, dedicated, and deeply resourceful. ... they've been truly disciplined about being truly evil." He focuses on three particular attacks on asylum:

  • Migrant Protection Protocols
  • Safe Third Country Agreements
  • Title 42 (shutting down the border due to the pandemic -- expelling 197,371 asylum seekers from the U.S. or its border)
