President Barack Obama is making his first trip as president to the U.S.-Mexico border and will call for reform of the U.S. immigration laws and try to cast the Republicans as standing in its way. The president's speech in El Paso, Texas today is the latest immigration events by Obama, who also recently hosted meetings at the White House with Latino lawmakers and others.  For the story, click here.
