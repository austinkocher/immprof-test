--- title: "State Department Changes Mind, Grants Visa to Colombian Journalist" date: 2010-07-28T05:50:21 url: "/post/state-department-changes-mind-grants-visa-to-colombian-journalist/" aliases: ["/immigration/2010/07/state-department-changes-mind-grants-visa-to-colombian-journalist.html"] type: post year: "2010" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

The U.S. State Department has changed its mind and decided to grant Colombian journalist Hollman Morris a visa so that he and his family can  go to Harvard where he will be on a fellowship for the next year.  The visa had initially been denied, possibly based on the "terrorist activities" provisions of the USA PATRIOT Act.
