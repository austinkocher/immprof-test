What are conditions like along the U.S./Mexico border?  The news reports about the number of migrants at the border tell contrasting and inconsistent stories.  For the last few weeks, stories have talked about the record number of migrants.  Watch the Fox Nes discussion above to get a sense of the so-called crisis of "open borders."  Offering a very different perspective, Ted Hesson and Kristina Cooke for Reuters report that "[t]he U.S. will reopen four legal U.S.-Mexico border crossings [tomorrow] as high levels of illegal immigration have receded and freed up personnel, a senior U.S. official said . . . ."

It is important to remember that it is election season and, once again, immigration is likely to be a topic deployed for political advantage.
