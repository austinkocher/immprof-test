You can read the as-of-now-unpublished Federal Register notice of Termination of Parole Processes: Cubans, Haitians, Nicaraguans, and Venezuelans. It will be officially noticed on Tuesday March 25.

The idea will be to end the parole period for the estimated 530,000 CHNV program participants 30 days after publication--so, April 25.
