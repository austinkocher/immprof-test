On Friday February 25, 2022 at 10:00 AM Eastern, Freedom House will be holding a briefing on its new report, Freedom in the World 2022. This new edition of the flagship annual report will assess the condition of political rights and civil liberties globally. The new report includes numerical ratings and supporting descriptive texts for 195 countries and 15 territories.
