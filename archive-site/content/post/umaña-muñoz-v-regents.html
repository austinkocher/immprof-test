Yesterday, the California Court of Appeals heard oral argument regarding the lawsuit brought by the Center for Immigration Law and Policy (CILP) at UCLA School of Law on behalf of Jeffry Umaña Muñoz and Iliana Perez. They have sued the Regents of the University of California (UC) alleging that UC is discriminating against students based on their immigration status by barring them from accessing on-campus employment and paid educational opportunities. The lawsuit centers on the UC's (mis)interpretation of the Immigration Reform and Control Act.
