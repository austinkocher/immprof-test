As ImmigrationProf has reported, the Republican Presidential candidates have been jabbing at one anoother about who would be tougher on immigration enforcement. Things may start to heat up. The Hill reports that Keep Conservatives United, a Super PAC backing Rep. Michele Bachmann (R-Minn.), will soon start hitting Texas Gov. Rick Perry (R) on immigration in a radio ad. The ad portrays Bachmann as hawkish on illegal immigration while claiming Perry has failed to clamp down during his tenure as governor of Texas.
