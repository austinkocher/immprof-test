Anil Kalhan  (Drexel) has a piece ("Detention Reform and Its Discontents") on Dorf on Law that looks at the Obama administration's mixed record on reforming immigration detention.  It is a thoughtful analysis showing the difficulties of reforming detention while also increasing immigration enforcement, record-breaking deportations, and no comprehensive immigration reform.
