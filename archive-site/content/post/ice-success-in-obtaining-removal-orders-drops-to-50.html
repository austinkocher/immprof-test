--- title: "ICE Success in Obtaining Removal Orders Drops to 50%" date: 2014-02-19T08:26:38 url: "/post/ice-success-in-obtaining-removal-orders-drops-to-50/" aliases: ["/immigration/2014/02/ice-success-in-obtaining-removal-orders-drops-to-50.html"] type: post year: "2014" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

New TRAC data shows that Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has had diminishing success in convincing Immigration Judges to issue removal orders. Such orders are now granted only about 50 percent of the time, the lowest level since systematic tracking began more than 20 years ago.
