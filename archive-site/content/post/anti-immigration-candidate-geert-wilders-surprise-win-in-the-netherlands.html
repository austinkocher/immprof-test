Far right Freedom Party candidate Geert Wilders has prevailed in this week's election in the Netherlands. As the New York Times reports, votes counted thus far suggest that Wilders has "a chance to become the Netherlands’ first far-right prime minister."

Wilders has said he will focus on reducing immigration. According to Reuters, Wilders said after the election: "But the first thing is a significant restriction on asylum and immigration. We don't do that for ourselves, we do that for all Dutch people who voted for us."
