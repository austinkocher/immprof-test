Axios publicizes the new data showing that a majority of unauthorized immigrants in the United States are not from Mexico. Mexicans no longer make up the majority of u unauthorized immigrants in the United States, which represents a significant change from the early 2000s — as more are leaving than arriving, according to a new study by the Pew Research Center.   According to the study, the percentage of unauthorized immigrants from Asia and the Northern Triangle has increased in recent years.
