The LA Times has a fascinating article about how some Latinos have responded to the Trump administration's racial hostility by following new career paths to help their affected communities: heading to law school with the goal of providing pro bono services to disadvantaged Latinos, gearing a therapy practice to the needs of the Latinx community, podcasting, even creating immigrant-friendly fashion(!).
