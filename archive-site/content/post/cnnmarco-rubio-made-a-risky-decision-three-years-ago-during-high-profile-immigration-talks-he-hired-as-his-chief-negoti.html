CNN reports that Republic Presidential candidate Marco Rubio made a decision three years ago during immigration reform negotiations that is now getting critical scrutiny.  Rubio hired as his chief negotiator a corporate attorney who represented clients with a stake in the legislation. The lawyer Enrique Gonzalez, now a partner at Fragomen Worldwide, has been a friend of Rubio's for many years. According to CNN, "Late in the talks, Gonzalez inserted narrowly tailored provisions in the bill that could have helped clients he had represented, including universities, cruise liners and media companies, according to a review of emails and interviews with multiple sources involved in the negotiations."

At the time, Gonzalez's firm told the media that he had made a "clean break" from the law firm and that there was no conflict of interest. Yet a few months after the bill passed the Senate in June 2013, Gonzalez returned to his old law firm, one of the nation's largest immigration firms.

The untold story of Gonzalez's role is getting new scrutiny in the presidential campaign.
