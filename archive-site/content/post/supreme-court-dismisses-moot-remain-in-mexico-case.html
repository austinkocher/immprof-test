Rafael Bernal  for the Hill reports that the Supreme Court yesterday dismissed as moot a legal challenge to President Trump's Migrant Protection Protocols (MPP), which  the Biden administration terminated earlier this month.  Amy Howe on SCOTUSblog explains the Court's dismissal of Mayorkas v. Innovation Law Lab.

The MPP, also known as the "Remain in Mexico" policy, allowed asylum-seekers crossing the U.S.-Mexico border to be returned to Mexico while their applications are being decided.  The MPP faced many legal challenges.  The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit in 2019 affirmed a district court injunction barring implementation of the MPP.  The Supreme Court stayed the injunction, at the request of the Trump administration.

President Biden on June 1 ordered the termination of the program.  With MPP's termination, the Supreme Court dismissed the case as moot.
