Brett Goodin for the Conversation reports that "[a]A record number of Americans are renouncing their citizenship. In just the first half of this year, 5,315 Americans gave up their citizenship. That puts the country on track to see a record-breaking 10,000 people renounce U.S. citizenship in 2020. Until a decade ago, fewer than 1,000 Americans per year, on average, chose to renounce their citizenship."  Why you might ask?  COVID-19?  President Trump.  No, it is the financial costs to expatriates of keeping U.S. citizenship.  Read Goodin's piece for an explanation.
