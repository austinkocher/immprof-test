Recently, a 50-ish man of "Asian appearance" died of apparent suicide in Sydney. As the BBC reports, the man had no identification of any kind - no wallet, phone, or documents. He only had a card for using public transportation but even that wasn't registered to an address, and it had been paid for with cash at a location without security cameras.

An autopsy of the man indicated that "he was most likely suffering from multiple myeloma, a type of blood cancer known to cause excruciating pain."

Officials suspect that this gentleman, who has still not been identified, was an "unlawful non-citizen." That's the Australian phrasing for undocumented migrants.

Australia does not have a large undocumented population. There are an estimated 50,000 to 100,000 "unlawful non-citizens" in the country whose overall population is just north of 25 million. Compare that to the United States where our population exceeds  325 million with an estimated 10.7 million undocumented individuals. (I'll do the math for you - Australia's undocumented population is about .4% while in the U.S., the undocumented population is over 3% of our total population.) As in America, undocumented individuals in Australia frequently overstay otherwise valid visa stays.
