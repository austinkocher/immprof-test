ProPublica and The Texas Tribune are reporting on a very disturbing complaint filed with the Department of Homeland Security’s Office of Inspector General. The complaint, according to these reports, alleges systematic sexual assaults at the El Paso Detention Center.

Female detainees report that guards at the detention center attacked them in areas of the facility not covered by security cameras.

The guards appear to be employees of a private company--Global Precision Systems--supervised by agents working under ICE.
