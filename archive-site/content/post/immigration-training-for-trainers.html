The Mass. Immigrant & Refugee Advocacy (MIRA) Coalition and National Lawyers Guild will present a "train the trainers" program on immigration law this Thursday at 6 p.m. EST.  Volunteers are desperately needed to handle workshops for immigration groups.  YOU DON'T NEED TO BE AN IMMIGRATION ATTORNEY to help.
 
Jessica Chicco, the Director of Training for MIRA, will conduct this first "TRAINING FOR TRAINERS" on Thursday, February 13 from 6 - 7 pm EST at MIRA's office at 69 Canal St, 3rd floor in downtown Boston (near the TD Garden).  The training will be a hybrid model, with up to 20 people able to attend in person and others attending via Zoom. 
 
If you can attend, please contact coordinator Emma King, e.reece.knight@gmail.com, and let her know how, in-person or by Zoom.  The Zoom link is https://us02web.zoom.us/j/86994305787?pwd=pSWWqNStYGFRqu2NwleJ6oZg2S9HTj.1.
