The Detroit Free Press reports that Palestinian activist Rasmieh Odeh was sentenced yesterday to 18 months for immigration fraud tied to her alleged role in bombings in Israel in 1969 that killed two civilians.  Click here for background on Odeh's case.

Odeh's case has drawn national attention. U.S. District Judge Gershwin Drain handed down the sentence in a packed courtroom. Her sentence was in the middle of of the recommended 15 to 21 months for her crime of not disclosing on her immigration and citizenship forms. Judge Drain rejected the government's request to sentence her to five to seven years, saying that she doesn't pose a danger to society. According to the Free Press, Judge Drain said that Odeh had lied under oath.  He added:  "She was a member of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine. I think she was involved in some terrorist activity." Drain also revoked Odeh's American citizenship; she will be deported at the conclusion of her sentence. She is now free on bond pending appeal.

Both sides claimed the sentencing was a victory.
