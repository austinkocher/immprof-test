A new ProPublica series (The new Immigration:  How Recent Arrivals at the Border Have Changed the Country and Its Attitudes) aims to "explain the real challenges — as opposed to the ones being made up to scare you — posed by immigration trends at the Southwest border over the past decade."  

In the opening article, Mica Rosenberg and Jeff Ernsthausen set the stage: They cover the United States’ need for workers, the complex legal limbo in which many immigrants live, the changing demographics of newcomers and the "hardening in public opinion."
