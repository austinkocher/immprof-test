Mary Waltermire, Immigration Attorney
 
What are the added legal complications of undocumented immigration for those seeking residency through same-sex marriage? Earlier today on Capital Public Radio,  The View From Here presents its final story in a three-part series exploring undocumented immigration in California.  Long-time Contributing Reporter Julia Mitric tells the tale of two young men who grew up Mexican, Catholic and gay in California. Their story sheds light on how culture, identity and immigration status are intertwined in complex and unexpected ways. To expand that conversation on Insight, Pamela Wu talks in-depth with immigration lawyer MaryWaltermire, a UC Davis School of Law graduate. She worked with Juan Morales and Chris Guillen, the married couple at the heart of the The View From Here's "Out Of The Shadows And Into Marriage."
