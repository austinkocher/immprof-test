Texas Public Radio reports that Makers of the humorous adult party game Cards Against Humanity claim in a lawsuit  that SpaceX, the private spacecraft manufacturer, launch service provider and satellite communications company founded by Elon Musk, has trespassed on and damaged land it owns near the U.S.-Mexico border.

Cards Against Humanity has created a website to publicize the lawsuit.
