Did you know that the Florida GOP has its own merch store? Apparently, it's where one goes to purchase an authentic "Alligator Alcatraz" tee or your "Participation trophies are for libs" beer koozie. 

Here's the new bit. On Thursday, Kevin told y'all about the latest Florida detention center -- Deportation Depot. And, given that the Florida GOP has this store, they decided to offer Deportation Depot merch using logos and coloring that looked an awful lot like Home Depot. Turns out, Home Depot was not amused. The home repair company "reached out" to the Florida GOP about the issue, and the Deportation Depot items were soon removed from the store.

Man, I would love to see that cease and desist letter!
