A few weeks ago, the American Immigration Council held a fascinating webinar called Litigate Your FOIA Requests: How to Sue the Government to Get Information You and Your Clients Need. That webinar is now om youtube. Score.

For clinical folks, you might get some new tips and tricks for your students to employ as you move cases forward. For podium folks like me, it was a really helpful, easy, and entertaining talk to get grounded in the practice side of the law.

The runtime is about an hour or so--less if you skip the Q&A.
