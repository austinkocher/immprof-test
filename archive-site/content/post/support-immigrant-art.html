Alexandra Eguiluz is a Peruvian artist based in Paris, where she is completing her graduate studies at the Beaux Art de Paris. Her multimedia work explores social and political themes. At the moment she is working on a video project about immigrant identity, specifically about how many immigrants adopt multiple identities to live and survive in today`s America. Filming will take place in Winston-Salem, North Carolina – the current home of individuals who have agreed to collaborate with the project. Work on location is scheduled for July and August. Editing and post-production will take place in Paris. The video has a December 2018 target release date.

 

Alexandra is trying to raise money to support her endeavor. For more information, and to donate, click here.
