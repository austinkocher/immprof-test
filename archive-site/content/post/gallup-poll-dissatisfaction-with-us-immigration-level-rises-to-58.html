A new Gallup poll should have pro-immigrant folks a bit worried.  But things are a bit more complicated than suggested by this Newsweek headline:  "Majority of Americans Dissatisfied With Amount of Immigration to U.S.: Poll."

According to the poll, 58 percent of those polled said they are dissatisfied with the current level of immigration to the United States, according to a Gallup poll released today.

The new survey's dissatisfaction level is an 8 percent increase from a similar Gallup poll last year. "Of those who said they are dissatisfied in the latest poll, 35 percent said they want immigration into the country to decrease, while 9 percent said they would like it to increase. Fourteen percent said they were dissatisfied with the level of immigration, but want it to stay at the same level." (bold added).

The new poll showed Republicans are especially unhappy with the current level of people entering the country. A November Gallup poll found only 31 percent of those polled approved of how President Biden was handling immigration.

The percentage of respondents who said they want less immigration (35 percent) was higher from the 19 percent last year. The percentage of those polled who said they support more immigration decreased from 15 percent in 2021 to 9 percent.
 
The poll was taken from January 3-16.
