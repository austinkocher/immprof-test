On August 15, 2024 from 1:00 PM to 2:30 PM (ET) / 10:00 AM to 11:30 AM (PT), the National Immigration Project August Removal Defense will hold a webinar on Defending Noncitizens Released After a Border Encounter.

As border policies continually change, noncitizens apprehended at the border and released by DHS are subjected to removal proceedings across the United States. During this 90-minute webinar, faculty will discuss key legal developments impacting the implications of a noncitizen’s release in the United States by DHS at or near the border. Faculty will provide tips and strategies for zealously representing noncitizens released after a border encounter.
