The American Immigration Council has released Why Caution is Needed Before Hiring Additional Border Patrol Agents and ICE Officers. This paper by Josiah McC. Heyman, Ph.D., explains the inherent risks with any rush to increase staffing levels. Similar expansions in recent history, as detailed in the report, demonstrate that poor hiring practices in the immigration agencies result in inadequate vetting, poor supervision, and serious misconduct.
