As the WSJ reports, asylum seekers who present themselves at Nogales (aka Tuscon, AZ) will now need to find their own way to Juárez (aka court in El Paso, TX). The migrants are being returned to Mexico via the Migrant Protection Protocols. Now they have the added burden of finding a way to travel 300 miles to their court appointments. I'm sure that will be no trouble at all.
