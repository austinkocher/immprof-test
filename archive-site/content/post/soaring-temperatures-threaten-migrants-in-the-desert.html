Fronteras reports that, as temperatures approach 120 degrees in the Arizona desert this week, immigrants crossing the border illegally are finding themselves in trouble. U.S. Border Patrol trauma agents have already rescued hundreds of people in the weeks before the first day of summer. And now they anticipate more.
