Rick Baldoz in The Conversation (From the Chinese Exclusion Act to pro-Palestinian activists: The evolution of politically motivated deportations) offers the perspective of an immigration historian who believes that removal orders targeting student activists by the Trump administration "echo America’s long past of jailing and expelling immigrants because of their race, what they say or believe – or all three."

Many years ago, I looked at the history of ideological regulation in the immigration laws.  President Trump relies on many of these historical episodes as evidence of the administration's near absolute authority over immigration.
