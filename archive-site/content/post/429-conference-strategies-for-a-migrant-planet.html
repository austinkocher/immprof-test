On April 29, the DePaul Migration Collaborative will be hosting its Inaugural Immigration Summit: Strategies for a Migrant Planet. Registration for in-person and remote attendance can be found at this link.

The summit will feature talks by Hiroshi Motomura, Erika Lee, and Donald M. Kerwin Jr., who "will address innovations and strategies in immigrant defense, refugee resettlement, mental health, climate migration and detention, among other topics."
