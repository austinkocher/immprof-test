It is not only the United States that is seeing troubling immigration developments.  ferment is afoot in Europe as well.  The latest is that, as reported in The Independent,  Slovakia has announced that it will only accept Christian migrants when it takes in Syrian refugees under an European Union relocation plan. A Interior Ministry spokesman said the Eastern European nation will accept 200 Christian migrants from camps in Turkey, Italy and Greece, as Muslim migrants would not integrate as easily into Slovakia's predominantly Christian population.
