Public universities can hire them" date: 2023-07-01T12:00:00 url: "/post/undocumented-students-need-jobs-to-pay-for-college-public-universities-can-hire-them/" aliases: ["/immigration/2023/07/undocumented-students-need-jobs-to-pay-for-college-public-universities-can-hire-them.html"] type: post year: "2023" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" date_from_address: true ---
ImmigrationPropf blogger Ming Hsu Chen's commentary in the San Francisco Chronicle discusses the  importance of expanding opportunities for undocumented students after the Supreme Court's recent decision on affirmative action.  She explains the lawfulness of the University of California Regents plan, announced in May, to permit UC hiring for campus jobs, regardless of immigration status; the basic reason:  federal laws do not forbid state entities from authorizing state employment.
