Rebecca Santana for the Associated Press reports on President-elect Donald Trump's attacks on Mexico and Canada, accusing them of allowing migrants to enter the U.S.  He threatened to impose 25% tariffs on goods coming into the U.S. from those two countries. Tariffs and immigration restrictions could have dramatic economic impacts on the United States.  

Santana reviews the data and studies on border crossings, fentanyl smuggling and immigration and crime.
