PBS News:  President Donald Trump’s border czar Tom Homan said yesterday that the administration plans to “flood the zone” in sanctuary cities with U.S. immigration enforcement officers.

“We got 10,000 more agents coming on, we’re going to flood the zone,” Homan told reporters outside the White House.

State and local officials of the zones to be flooded reportedly are not keen on the idea.
