Donald Trump made headlines last week, visiting Mexico last week and meting with President Enrique Pena Nieto. The fallout is -- well -- still falling out.

The Washington Post reports that an angry Mexican lawmaker has proposed legislation preparing for a potential Trump victory in November. Trump has criticized the North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA) and proposed "pulling out" from NAFTA.  The proposed bill would, according to reports, give the Mexican Senate the power to review dozens of existing bilateral treaties with the United States, including the 1848 Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo, in which Mexico ceded more than half a million square miles of its territory to the United States. The proposal, pushed by center-left Sen. Armando RÍos Piter, probably will not pass, but it is a sign of the outrage felt in Mexico over Trump's rhetoric. It would prohibit Mexican funding of Trump's proposed border wall.
