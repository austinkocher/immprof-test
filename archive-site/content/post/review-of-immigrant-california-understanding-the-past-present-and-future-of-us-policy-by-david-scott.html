For Contemporary Socioology ("Lessons from California on Immigrant Integration," I offer some thoughts on the book Immigrant California: Understanding the Past, Present, and Future of U.S. Policy by David Scott FitzGerald & John D. Skrentny, Stanford University Press, 2021.

 
Here is my intro:
 
"To understand California’s immigration transformation, Immigrant California: Understanding the Past, Present, and Future of U.S. Policy, edited by David Scott FitzGerald and John D. Skrentny, provides a timely primer on immigrant integration policy, offering a variety of historical, economic, social, cultural, and political perspectives. The book provides valuable lessons for immigrant integration policy for the nation and in effect serves as a capsule summary of the myriad immigration issues facing states from coast to coast.

This review first summarizes the chapters. It then proceeds to identify some questions about immigrant integration raised by Immigrant California."
