The Institute for Justice & Democracy in Haiti has released its biannual human rights update. The Update covers key recent developments ono human rights in Haiti from December 2023 through May 2024.  

The Update offers an overview of the ever-deepening insecurity and humanitarian catastrophe: violence by increasingly well-armed and -organized armed groups with clear ties to political and economic elites continues to increase in incidence and brutality, while the economic and humanitarian situation deteriorates further. The primary drivers of these harms include widespread government corruption and collusion with armed groups and institutionalized impunity. International actors continue to engage in ways that are disruptive to Haiti’s long-term stability, democracy, and human rights, including by pushing through a foreign intervention that many are concerned will be ineffective at best, and deeply harmful at worst.
