Nolan Rappaport for the Hill looks at the UK and Australian immigration points-based systems as possible alternatives for the United States. 

A bill introduced in Congress, the RAISE Act, provides for a points-based system that would reduce the number of immigrants coming annually to the United States. It has been challenged as racially discriminatory and aimed at redirecting immigration away from Mexico, China, and India, where many of today's immigrants come from.
