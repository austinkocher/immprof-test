--- title: "ICE Plans to Start Destroying Records of Immigrant Abuse, Including Sexual Assault and Deaths in Custody" date: 2017-08-30T15:11:05 url: "/post/ice-plans-to-start-destroying-records-of-immigrant-abuse-including-sexual-assault-and-deaths-in-custody/" aliases: ["/immigration/2017/08/ice-plans-to-start-destroying-records-of-immigrant-abuse-including-sexual-assault-and-deaths-in-custody.html"] type: post year: "2017" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

ICE is seeking National Archives and Records Administration approval to destroy a range of documents related to abuses in ICE custody, including death investigations, solitary confinement records, and sexual abuse investigations. In some cases, the destruction timeline is as short as 3 years; the longest is only 20 years. NARA has preliminarily approved the request.
