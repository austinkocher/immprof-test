The Republican National Convention is in the books and Donald Trump and J.D. Vance are the nominees. 

As Fox News reported, former President Donald Trump in his acceptance speech "showed off a chart on immigration during the Republican National Convention Thursday night that he credits with literally saving his life last Saturday

`Less than four years ago, I handed this administration the strongest border in American history, but you can see on the chart that saved my life... that was the chart that saved my life,' Trump said as the chart was put up on the screen behind him."
