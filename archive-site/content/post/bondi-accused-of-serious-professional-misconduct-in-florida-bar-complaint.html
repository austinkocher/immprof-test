The Hill reports that U.S. Attorney General Pam Bondi is accused once again of “serious professional misconduct” in a Florida Bar complaint, the Miami Herald reported today.  "Bondi’s record as the head of the Justice Department is being slammed by close to 70 law professors, attorneys and former Florida Supreme Court justices via a Florida Bar ethics complaint filed Thursday, according to the Herald."

It is hard to see the complaint going anywhere.
