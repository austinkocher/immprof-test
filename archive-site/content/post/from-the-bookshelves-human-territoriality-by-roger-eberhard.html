Human Territoriality by Roger Eberhard will be released in July. The Washington Post has the skinny on Eberhard's photographic journey into borders.

First the backstory: "Eberhard spent 3  1/2 years flying, driving (13,000 miles), climbing mountains, hiking forests and hitching boat rides to capture images of borders that have changed physically or politically over centuries. The resulting book of photos, maps and brief histories provokes thinking about nationhood before we trot the globe."

Then, this incredibly evocative description: "Eberhard’s photographs portray the mostly healed scars of aggression. Dividing lines are seams — stitches in the earth — that knit us together and outline the parts of the whole."

Sounds like a perfect table book for the immprof in your life!
