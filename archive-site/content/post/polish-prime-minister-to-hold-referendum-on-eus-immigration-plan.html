Report on border controversy in Poland a year ago

Poland has historically been a nation in which immigration has been a hot political issue.  The Guardian (UK) reports (and here for the Associated Press report) that Poland’s right-wing prime minister has promised to go ahead with a referendum on European Union migration reforms.  Voters will be asked if they are willing to accept “thousands of illegal immigrants from the Middle East and Africa.”  Sounds given that language that the vote outcome is predestined.

Mateusz Morawiecki announced in a video published online that the referendum would coincide with a parliamentary election on October 15.  Migration and security will be central topics of the election, as Morawiecki’s ruling party, Law and Justice (PiS), seeks to retain power.  PiS’s campaign to remain in office kicked off last week when the defense minister announced on public radio that he was placing 10,000 troops “nearer the border with Belarus to frighten the aggressor so that they do not dare to attack us.”
