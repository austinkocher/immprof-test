To honor her parents, Jennifer Rocha took her UCSD college graduation photos alongside her family, in the same vegetable fields where she's worked with them since high school.

"I wouldn't have the degree and the diploma if it wasn't for them," she said. https://t.co/02cwVCqwW5 pic.twitter.com/F10O9bmlKj

NPR reports on some special graduation photos that have gone viral on social media:

"Jennifer Rocha wanted to hear the rustle of her black graduation gown against the bell pepper bushes in the California farm fields. She wanted to see the hem float above the dirt paths that she and her parents have spent years walking as a family while plucking heavy gallons of perfectly ripe fruits and vegetables that end up in America's grocery stores.

That's why she decided to take her college graduation photos in the same hot vegetable fields in Coachella, Calif., where she has worked with her parents since she was in high school.

`I'm proud that that's where I come from,' says Rocha, who graduated from the University of California, San Diego [last week]. `It's a huge part of who I am.'"

Rocha's mother and father had both labored in the fields of Michoacán, Mexico, as young children before emigrating to the U.S.
