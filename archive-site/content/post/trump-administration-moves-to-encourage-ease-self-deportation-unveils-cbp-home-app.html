NBC News reports that the Trump administration revived the CBP One app under another name and with a different purpose. The Biden administration's version of the app allowed migrants to apply to enter the U.S. legally as asylum-seekers. 

Check out the new website.  Relaunched as the CBP Home app, the application has "a self-deportation reporting feature," DHS announced Monday. 

Revamping the app is part of a messaging campaign from the U.S. government that includes ads with Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem encouraging undocumented immigrants to leave the country. Click here for more on the campaign.
