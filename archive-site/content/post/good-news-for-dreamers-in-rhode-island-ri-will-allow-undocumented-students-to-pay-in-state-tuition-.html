--- title: "Good News for DREAMers in Rhode Island: RI will allow undocumented students to pay in-state tuition" date: 2011-09-27T13:10:49 url: "/post/good-news-for-dreamers-in-rhode-island-ri-will-allow-undocumented-students-to-pay-in-state-tuition-/" aliases: ["/immigration/2011/09/good-news-for-dreamers-in-rhode-island-ri-will-allow-undocumented-students-to-pay-in-state-tuition-.html"] type: post year: "2011" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

The Rhode Island Board of Governors for Higher Education unanimously adopted a policy that allows undocumented students to pay resident tuition rates at public colleges. The board heeded the urging of Governor Chafee and ignored the protests of others.
