An immigrant himself, Arnold Schwarzenegger, the former Republican governor of California, has waded into the immigration debate in an appearance on The View, and his remarks going viral across social media platforms.

"You have a responsibility as an immigrant to pay back to America," the Austrian-born actor said. "When you come to America, you're a guest and you have to behave like a guest."
