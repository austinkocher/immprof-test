My friend Garrett Epps opines for The Atlantic that Trump's 3.0 travel ban will be more difficult to successfully challenge than the first two.    In sum, "[t]hat ban may prove much harder to challenge than the first two. In fact, the whimpering fate of the first two travel bans may say less about the resilience of the American legal system than about the incompetence of the early Trump White House. Better preparation for the ban at the beginning might have turned the result the other way."
