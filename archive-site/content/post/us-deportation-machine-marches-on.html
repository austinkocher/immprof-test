Immigration Impact reports on an immigration landmark that is not that far off.  No one can say with certainty when the Obama administration will reach the grim milestone of having deported two million people since the President took office in 2008. Regardless of the exact date this symbolic threshold is reached, however, it is important to keep in mind a much more important fact: most of the people being deported are NOTt dangerous criminals.
