CNN ("The New 'Londoners'") reports on an interesting photo series with an immigration twist. 

Chris Steele-Perkins considers himself an immigrant, and his photo series, "The New Londoners," looks at other immigrant families who have moved to the city.
He started the photo series in early 2014, but his photographs are particularly poignant today. It was only a few months ago that the UK voted to leave the European Union.
Photo courtesy of CNN  The David family in London has Iraqi and Syrian roots.
