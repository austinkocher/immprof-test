Colorado Law: The 33rd Annual Ira C. Rothgerber Jr. Conference: Immigration & National Borders

Friday, April 18th, 11 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. | Wolf Law Building

Join us for the 33rd annual Ira C. Rothgerber Conference, presented by the Byron R. White Center! This year's theme is "Immigration & National Borders" and will kickoff with a keynote by Professor Hiroshi Motomura of UCLA Law. He will be discussing themes from his most recent book, Borders & Belonging: Toward a Fair Immigration Policy.
