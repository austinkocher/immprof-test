Allissa Wickham on Law360 reports on possible immigration cases in the Supreme Court in the 2015 Term.  The Court has has already granted certiorari in Torres v. Lynch and will decide whether a New York arson conviction counts as an aggravated felony under immigration law.  Cert petitions are on file in a number of other immigration cases as well, including a suit about a border patrol shooting and one on absentia removal orders.
