CNN reports House Speaker John Boehner (R-Ohio) today "repeated his long standing opposition to the Senate-passed immigration bill and his pledge the House would never vote on it, but he went a step further, drawing a bright line: `I'll make clear we have no intention ever of going to conference on the Senate bill.'"
