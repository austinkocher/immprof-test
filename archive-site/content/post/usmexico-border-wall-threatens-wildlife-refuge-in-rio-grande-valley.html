NPR reports:  The 110 miles of proposed border wall in Texas could quickly turn a wildlife refuge into a “tragic situation.” The wall would run through the 40-year-old Lower Rio Grande Valley National Wildlife Refuge, and residents fear the construction will significantly harm newly restored populations of deer, native plants and cougars.
