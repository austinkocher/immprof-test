The Indiana International & Comparative Law Review is issuing a call for papers for its symposium Moving to Opportunity: Examining the Risks and Rewards of Economic Migration, to be held February 21, 2013, with papers to be published in the early 2015. Proposals are due by October 18, 2013.

We are now accepting proposals for papers and presentations on subjects related to the intersection of Economic Migration which address international law or comparative legal aspects of the subject. Panels are set to include the Feminization of Migration; Trafficking and Slavery; Students, Education and Migration; and High-Skill Migrants.

To be considered for publication, please send a brief abstract and a current CV to emahern@iupui.edu

Any questions may be sent to Executive Live Symposium Editor, Emma Mahern at the e-mail address listed above.

The Indiana International and Comparative Law Review is a student-edited law Journal devoted to the study and analysis of current international legal issues and events. Published continuously since 1991, the Review provides a specialized and unique format for students to take broad legal topics and shed an international and/or comparative light onto them. This often amounts to the proffering of views or propositions that are novel in a particular field and become influential going forward.
