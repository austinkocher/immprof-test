On October 28, 2016, the Binational Dialogue on Border Control and Human Rights will be held.  It will feature government speakers from both sides of the border:  

1. It is held at the College of the Northern Frontier in Tijuana, Mexico.

2. It is the first time in recent memory that the Assistant Port Dir. of the Customs and Border Protection in San Ysidro has agreed to attend a conference hosted by professional organizations outside of the San Diego area. 

3. Attendees will be provided with four CLE units for free. These credits are also good for satisfying the Board of Specialization continuing education requirements. [Must show proof of LA County Bar Membership to receive CLE credit]

4. Transportation provided by the to and from the border will be free for the first 30 people to sign up.

Be a part of history and join us for this historic event.  All you have to pay is your lunch at the Colegio!
