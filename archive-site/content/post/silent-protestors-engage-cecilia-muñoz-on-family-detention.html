Several immigration attorneys attending the AILA National Conference in Washington, DC today engaged in silent protest during the keynote speech by the director of the White House Domestic Policy Council, Cecilia Muñoz.

The protestors, who oppose family detention, held up signs saying "Shame" and "Vergüenza."
