Gov. Jerry Brown today vetoed legislation that would have allowed legal immigrants to be eligible for jury service. His veto message said that "[j]ury service, like voting, is quintiessentially a prerogative and respoinsibility of citizenship.  This bill would permit lawful permanent residents who are not citizens to serve on a jury.  I don't think that's right."
