A New Path: A Guide to the Challenges and Opportunities After Deportation. 
 
Published by the Education Justice Project at the University of Illinois, A New Path provides critical guidance to people facing deportation, including how to get ready for deportation or return, and how to set up one’s life after deportation, including meeting basic needs, finding a job, enrolling in school, and more.
 
The guide is discussed in this Chicago Tribune story.
