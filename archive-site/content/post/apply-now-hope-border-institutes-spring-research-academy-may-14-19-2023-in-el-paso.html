The Hope Border Institute invites applications to its 2023 Spring Research Academy!

The Research Academy will take place from May 14-19, in El Paso, Texas.

Applications are encouraged from graduate students, junior researchers and policy makers, and residents from both sides of the border interested in re/thinking the current border and migration policy discourse.

More information on how to apply is available here. Deadline to apply is March 3, 2023.
