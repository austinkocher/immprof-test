As President Trump proved to the world, race and immigration and deeply intertwined in the national consciousness.  Philip Bump of the Washington Post analyzes contemporary evidence of that link.  "PRRI released its annual American Values Survey (AVS), asking questions that get at the heart of that political impulse. What it shows is that race, immigration and right-wing politics continue to overlap — and overlap in, at times, alarming ways." (bold added).
