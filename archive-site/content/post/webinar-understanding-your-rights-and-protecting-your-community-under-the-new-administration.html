Webinar: Understanding Your Rights and Protecting Your Community Under the New Administration, Feb 24, 2025 07:00 PM PST

Please join the Ignatian Spiritual Life Center and Lawyers’ Committee for Civil Rights of the San Francisco Bay Area (LCCRSF) for a free online and telephonic webinar to review the basics of what immigration policies have changed in the first weeks of the Trump administration, what your Fourth Amendment rights are, and what you may do when encountering immigration enforcement officials. This webinar is to remind you of the protections that you already have, and provide resources on how to protect yourselves and your communities in an atmosphere of intentional fear. All impacted community members, advocates, and allies are invited to join. Simultaneous interpretation in Spanish will be provided. Registration is required to receive the online/telephone call-in numbers.
