The Pew Research Center came across some interesting data about the most common age by racial group:

White                         55 years

Asian                             33 years

Native Hawaiian         25 years

Black                              24 years

American Indian         23 years

Hispanic                     8 years

The age differences undoubtedly have public policy and voting consequences.
