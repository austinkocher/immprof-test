In its "Annals of Crime," the New Yorker has this story by Ed Caeser on "The Kingpin Who Kidnapped Migrants for Ransom."  Here is the story:  "An Eritrean trafficker promised to help Africans desperate to reach Europe—then brutalized them inside a Libyan compound while extorting their families back home. With his fortune, he partied in Dubai."
