Today, the House released the text of the pending 2021 Consolidated Appropriations Act. It's a beast: 3126 pages long. Here are the references to immigration:

  • p. 150: EOIR $ including "not less than $22,500,000 shall be available for services and activities provided by the Legal Orientation Program"
  • pp. 178-9: $244,000,000 for the State Criminal Alien Program
  • p. 656: $20,000,000 shall be for the Office of the Ombudsman for Immigration Detention
  • p. 662: $12,908,923,000 for CBP
  • p. 663: $1,839,634,000 for CBP procurement
  • pp. 664-5: $7,875,730,000 for ICE support + $97,799,000
  • p. 672: $31,000,000 for CBP
  • p. 677: ICE money can't go towards 287g if DHS inspector general "determines that the terms of the agreement governing the delegation of authority have been materially violated"
  • p. 677: ICE money can't go towards "any contract for the provision of detention services if the two most recent overall performance evaluations received by the contracted facility are less than ‘adequate’ or the equivalent median score in any subsequent performance evaluation system."
  • p. 702: ICE cars
  • p. 711: funds cannot be used to amend the oath of allegiance
  • p. 713: funds cannot be used to employ workers described in 8 U.S.C. 1324a(h)(3)
  • p. 725: rescission of funds
  • p. 726: rescission of funds
  • p. 727: $840,000,000
  • p. 911: $77,810,000 foreign labor certifications
  • p. 940-1: Flexibility With Respect To The Crossing of H2B Nonimmigrants Working In The Seafood Industry
  • p. 941-2: Prevailing Wage Determination for H2B
  • p. 946: Rescission of unobligated funds.
  • p. 957: referencing INA and Refugee Education Assistance Act -- $448,805,000 (immunization, respiratory diseases); $596,272,000 (emerging and zoonotic infectious diseases)
  • p. 1016: DHHS can accept "donations from the private sector, nongovernmental organizations, and other groups independent of the Federal Government for the care of unaccompanied alien children"
  • p. 1018: DHHS $ can't be used for housing unaccompanied alien children in facilities not state-licensed, unless there's an emergency.
  • p. 1020: appropriations committee to receive reports about unlicensed facilities for UACs
  • p. 1021: $ can't be used to block Senators or Reps from touring for oversight purposes UAC housing
  • p. 1359-60: $ shouldn't be given to foreign governments determined to be controlled by terrorist organizations
  • p. 1445: terrorists shouldn't get money
  • p. 1448: no $ to support Iran
  • p. 1584: terrorists shouldn't get money
