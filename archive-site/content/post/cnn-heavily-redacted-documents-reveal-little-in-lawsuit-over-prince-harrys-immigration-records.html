CNN reports that documents unsealed yesterday in a conservative think tank’s case do not disclose much information about Prince Harry's immigration records:

"The heavily redacted documents were released in connection with a lawsuit from the Heritage Foundation, filed after its request for the release the Duke of Sussex’s immigration records under the Freedom of Information Act was previously denied by DHS. The group argued `the public has an interest in knowing' whether Prince Harry `was properly vetted prior to or during admission into the United States.'”

Prince Harry and his wife, the former Meghan Markel, have moved and now live in the United States.
