Luc Cohen for Reuters reported that an associate of Donald Trump's former adviser Steve Bannon was sentenced last week to 4-1/4 years in prison after admitting to defrauding donors to a campaign to build the wall along the U.S./Mexican border, Brian Kolfage was sentenced by U.S. District Judge Analisa Torres after pleading guilty last year to misappropriating funds for the "We Build the Wall" campaign, which solicited donations.  According to the story, "Kolfage, who lost his legs and right hand in a rocket attack in Iraq, led the fundraising push alongside Bannon.

Judge Torres also sentenced another former Bannon associate, Andrew Badolato, to three years in prison following his guilty plea.

Donald Trump pardoned Steve Bannon, who had been indicted for fraud in raising funds to build a wall along the U.S./Mexico border.  

Federal prosecutors had charged Bannon and three others with defrauding donors as part of a fundraising campaign purportedly aimed at supporting Trump's border wall.
