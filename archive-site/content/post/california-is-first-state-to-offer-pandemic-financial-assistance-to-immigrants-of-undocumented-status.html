--- title: "California Is First State to Offer Pandemic Financial Assistance to Immigrants of Undocumented Status" date: 2020-05-21T10:13:12 url: "/post/california-is-first-state-to-offer-pandemic-financial-assistance-to-immigrants-of-undocumented-status/" aliases: ["/immigration/2020/05/california-is-first-state-to-offer-pandemic-financial-assistance-to-immigrants-of-undocumented-status.html"] type: post year: "2020" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Shani Saxon for Colorlines reports that the state of California, a self-declared sanctuary state, has began allowing undocumented immigrants to apply for financial relief during the coronavirus pandemic. The $125 million coronavirus relief fund, which Governor Gavin Newsom announced in April, is the first state-run fund committed to helping those ineligible for federal stimulus payments and unemployment benefits.
