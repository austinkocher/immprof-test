The U.S. government is facing criticism for its conflicting stances on Haiti, write Rosa Flores and Sara Weisfeldt of CNN. As the American Embassy has warned that Haiti is too unsafe for U.S. citizens, ICE is deporting Haitians back to the country despite a surge in violence there. Experts believe that puts deportees at risk, given the control of violent gangs in Haiti's capital.
