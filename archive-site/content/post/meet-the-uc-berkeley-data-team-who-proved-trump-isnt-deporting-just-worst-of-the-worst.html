The students at Berkeley Law’s Deportation Data Project  have proved that the Trump administration is lying about who is being targeted for removals.  The data is being cited in hundreds of news stories by CNN, NY Times, and Los Angeles Times.

Launched in March, the Deportation Data Project is a free repository of immigration enforcement data. Since the Trump administration has limited release of immigration statistics, the project has been the only source of detailed Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) data since the inauguration.

The most striking finding from the project’s data is that despite the Trump administration’s claims, most migrants deported this year have no criminal conviction. In Los Angeles, most people arrested had no criminal record. As arrests numbers rise, non-criminals are increasingly getting caught in the immigration enforcement crosshairs.
