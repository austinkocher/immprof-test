Amy Howe for Howe on the Court explains how the Supreme Court has rejected the U.S. government's requests to expedite the review of the appeal of the injunctions barring the rescission of the Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals policy. At the earliest, the case will be heard in the 2019 Term.

CNN explains the political fallout of the Court's action.
