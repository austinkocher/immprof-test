Lassana Bathily. Photo: AFP

France will grant citizenship to Lassana Bathily, the brave supermarket worker who saved some 15 people by hiding them in the store's walk-in freezer during the Paris shooting last week. Bathily emigrated to France from Mali in 2006.

More than 300,000 signed an online petition calling for Bathily to be awarded French citizenship.
