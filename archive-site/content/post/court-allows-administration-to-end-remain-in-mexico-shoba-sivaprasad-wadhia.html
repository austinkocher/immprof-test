On the last day of the 2021 Term, the Supreme Court decided Biden v. Texas, the "Remain in Mexico" policy case.  It culminated a momentous Term.  That Term included five immigration decisions.

In "Court Allows Administration to End `Remain in Mexico'” in the Regulatory Review, Shoba Sivaprasad Wadhia looks at Biden v. Texas.  She concludes that "the Supreme Court’s decision should signal a broader reflection by the Biden Administration on its border policies and how it can use its discretion to roll back provisions that undermine its own principles, the rule of law, and our shared humanity."
