London Bus by Aurelijus Valeiša

I was absolutely sucked into this story from the BBC following "Sunny" (not his real name).

Sunny sought asylum in the UK -- fleeing Nigeria after facing execution for political activities. He did not succeed. But instead of returning to Africa, Sunny stayed. Homeless, he found solace riding London's buses.

After 21 years of living underground, Sunny recently received "leave to remain" and is no longer homeless, though he still rides the bus.
