Through a Freedom of Information Act request, the American Immigration Lawyers Association obtained a list of the 107 immigrant detainees who died in Immigration & Customs Enforcement custody from October 2003 to the present.  The most frequent cause of death was "illness," although "suicide" and AIDS were also sprinkled through the list.
