On the heels of an impeachment trial, President Trump delivered the State of the Union speech.  The President praised ICE officers and criticized "sanctuary" states, including California, and cities, naming New York City, and calling for federal legislation allowing crime victims and their families to sue sanctuary cities and states for crimes committed by undocumented immigrants.  Here is a summary of the border themes in President Trump's speech, which offers what is likely to be a preview of his immigration pitch in the 2020 campaign.
