This intriguing article (The Status of Citizenship by Yakov Malkiel)  came out last week.  I wanted to share it with blog readers.  The article begins:

"Immigration laws control transitions between `alienhood' and citizenship.  These two legal categories are statuses:  that is, they assign rights and obligations to people based on their unchosen characteristics, in this case their birthplaces and their parents’ identities.  The arc of legal history has bent away from relying on statuses and toward treating all people as equals.  By continuing to revolve around statuses, our immigration laws thus reflect attitudes that we have mostly rejected."
