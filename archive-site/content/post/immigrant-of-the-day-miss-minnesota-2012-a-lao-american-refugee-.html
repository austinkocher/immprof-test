This year's Miss Minnesota came to the United States after her parents dodged soldiers in Laos and her father built a bamboo raft to escape across the Mekong River. Born in a refugee camp in Thailand, Miss Minnesota USA Nitaya Panemalaythong, age 26, won the only beauty contest that she has ever entered and is the first Asian American to be named Miss Minnesota USA.

Panemalaythong entered the competition in hopes of winning scholarship so that she can attend college. For more details about our Immigrant of the Day, click here and here.
