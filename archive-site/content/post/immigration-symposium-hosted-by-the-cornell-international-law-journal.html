The Cornell International Law Journal is hosting an interesting symposium titled "The (Im)possibility of Immigration Reform?"

The Symposium will be held on Friday, November 8, 2024 and will celebrate the career of Professor Stephen Yale-Loehr and his contributions to the field of immigration law.
