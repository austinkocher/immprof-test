The Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR) today released of its Fiscal Year (FY) 2014 Statistics Yearbook. The book is a representation of data that EOIR tracked and compiled during the previous fiscal year. As in previous years, the figures and tables contained within the book examine respondents’ cases by nationality, language, and disposition, and provide detailed information surrounding asylum cases.
