Later today, District Judge Jon Tigar in the Northern District of California will hear a challenge to the Biden administration's new rules on seeking asylum. The policy, which took effect in May, gives migrants a pathway to asylum through a mobile app called CBP One. The Biden administration says that the policy change has led to a drop in unlawful border crossings. Thousands face long waits at the border for appointments. Many wait because the new rules make gaining asylum much harder if they cross the border unlawfully.

🎧 Immigrant advocates say the app can't be the only way to access asylum, as it creates a bottleneck, according to NPR's Joel Rose. National immigrant rights groups filed a lawsuit as soon as the rules went into effect, saying they were identical to Trump's policies. Meanwhile, Rose adds that immigration hardliners are also critical of the app, saying it creates a  pathway that Congress never intended.
