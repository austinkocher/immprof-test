The GAO recently issued a report on immigration detention: "Additional Actions Needed to Strengthen Management and Oversight of Facility Costs and Standards."

GAO undertook its study in light of the large uptick of individuals held in immigration detention. In 2005, some 230,000 migrants were held in immigration detention and, in 2013, that number increased to 440,600.

The report looks at how ICE tracks costs and standards of immigration detention as well as the agency's oversight over detention facilities. It is filled with snazzy and informative charts like these two:

 

The GAO's ultimate recommendations include:

that ICE assess the extent to which it has appropriate controls for tracking facility costs, document reasons why facilities cannot be transitioned to the most recent standards, and review reasons for differences in inspection results.

Lots of good stuff in here for immprofs writing on detention and for practioners looking to challenge their client's detention.
