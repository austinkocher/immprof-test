The University of Denver Sturm College of Law's Nancy Leong is back with her weekly video podcast on current civil rights issues: The RightsCast. And César Cuauhtémoc García Hernández returns to continue his discussion of immigration law - specifically, the detention of migrants. Detention, not imprisonment, because, after all, immigration detention facilities are not supposed to be prisons.
