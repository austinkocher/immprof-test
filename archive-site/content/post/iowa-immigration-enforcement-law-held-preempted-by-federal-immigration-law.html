Mitch Smith for the New York Times reports that the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit yesterday held that Iowa could not enforce a law that made it a crime for undocumented immigrants to enter the state. The ruling affirmed a district court injunction that blocked implementation of the Iowa law.

"The three-judge panel of the . . . Eighth Circuit . . . was composed entirely of jurists appointed by Republican presidents. It included one judge appointed by Mr. Trump, one by former President George W. Bush and one by former President George H.W. Bush."
