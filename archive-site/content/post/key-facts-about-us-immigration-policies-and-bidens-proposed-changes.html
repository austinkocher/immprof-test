The Pew Research Center (JENS MANUEL KROGSTAD AND ANA GONZALEZ-BARRERA) reviews the Biden administration's efforts to reverse Trump administration restrictions on legal immigration to the United States. The steps include plans to increase refugee admissions, preserving relief for DACA recipients, and not enforcing the tightened “public charge” rule that denies visas to low- and medium-income immigrants who might access public benefits.
