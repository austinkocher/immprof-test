The Supreme Court yesterday canceled (and here) oral arguments set for March 1 in the Title 42 case allowing for swift expulsion of migrants.  The Biden administration said the case would be moot when the order expire in May.  In a brief filed last week by Solicitor General Elizabeth B. Prelogar, the United States made the mootness claim.

The high court had been scheduled to hear oral arguments on March 1 in a case presenting the question whether a group of Republican-led states should be allowed to defend the legality of Title 42, which the Biden administration has sought to end. 

Adam Liptak writes that "The court’s action on . . . indicated that it was inclined to agree [that the case is moot] and that, barring other developments, it would dismiss the case and lift a stay that had kept the measure in place."
