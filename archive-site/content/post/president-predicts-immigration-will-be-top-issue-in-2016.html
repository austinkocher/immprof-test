MSNBC News reports that President Barack Obama said Monday that he expects immigration to be a major issue in the 2016 election. Speaking at a news conference after the G-7 summit in Germany, Obama renewed a call for Congress to act on the issue. 

Immigration already is an issue that many of the declared presidential candidates have taken positions on.
