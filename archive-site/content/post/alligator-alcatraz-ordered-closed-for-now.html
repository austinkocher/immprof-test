CNN reports that a federal judge has ordered the detention center in the Florida Everglades known as Alligator Alcatraz to stop taking detainees and remove infrastructure.  U.S. District Judge Kathleen Williams issued the preliminary injunction in a lawsuit filed by environmental groups and a Native American tribe concerned about the impact the facility on the environment.
