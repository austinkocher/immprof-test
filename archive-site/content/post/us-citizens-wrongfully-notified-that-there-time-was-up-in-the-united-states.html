The Trump administration has been moving with a fervor to remove noncitizens from the United States.  Errors have been made.  Many readers have followed the story of Kilmar Abrego Garcia, who the administration wrongfully deported to a Salvadoran prison. 

There has been a growing number of reports (here, here, here) of the U.S. government notifying U.S. citizens that it was time for them to leave the United States.   One "email said [that] a 68-year-old retired college administrator and teacher, and American citizen born in western Pennsylvania — was in the United States on humanitarian parole and his parole was being terminated."

Good enough for government work?
