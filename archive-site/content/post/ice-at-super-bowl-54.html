It is Super Sunday, a day that many have waited months for!  While many people are asking important questions like whether the San Francisco 49ers will beat the Kansas City Chjefs,  ICE is touting its role in protecting public safety, combating counterfeit Super Bowl products and human trafficking, and more.

Jorge Rivas for Spinter ("Inside ICE's Annual Super Bowl Propaganda Blitz") offers a critical assessment of the ICE marketing effort:

"It’s all part of an annual promotional blitz which takes advantage of the huge Super Bowl audience to brand the agency as a group of patriots protecting fans from everything from terrorists to fake NFL jerseys. There’s everything from Twitter and Facebook posts to multiple videos on YouTube."
