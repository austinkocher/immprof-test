My colleague Shayak Sarkar has a fascinating op/ed in the San Francisco Chronicle about the denial of tax benefits to undocumented immigrants and the use of the tax and financial laws to enforce borders.  The commentary is based on his growing body of scholarship on the financial regulation of immigrants:

Capital Controls as Migrant Controls, 109 California Law Review  (Forthcoming, 2021).

Tax Law's Migration, 62 Boston College Law Review (Forthcoming, 2021).

The New Legal World of Domestic Work, 32 Yale Journal of Law &Feminism 1 ( 2020).

Consumer Expectations and Consumer Protection, 88 George Washington Law Review 949 ( 2020).

Financial Immigration Federalism, 107 Georgetown Law Journal 1561 (2019).

Crediting Migrants, 71 Stanford Law Review Online 281 (2019).
