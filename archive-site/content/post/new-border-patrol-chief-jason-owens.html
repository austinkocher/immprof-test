The Biden administration has named Jason Owens as the new Chief of Border Patrol, WaPo reports. Owens takes on this role following his position as leader of the USBP Del Rio sector in Texas. As NPR reports, Owens also has experience serving as sector chief in Maine and leading the Border Patrol academy in New Mexico.

DHS Secretary Mayorkas describes Owens, a 27 year veteran of USBP, as “a talented, selfless, and inspiring leader who is dedicated to the Border Patrol’s law enforcement mission, the men and women who fulfill it, and the country that we all serve.”
