Participants in the EB-5 investor visa program are looking for resolution after U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services announced in FAQs that a critical part of the program had to be reauthorized by Congress.

A lawsuit in California seeks clarity.

This is the latest in a series of challenges in the EB-5 program.
