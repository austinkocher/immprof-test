The Financial Times and CBS News report that tight labor markets, increasing labor costs, and inflation are in part a result from immigration restrictions.   Business is calling for easing the restrictions while many Republican politicians criticize the Biden administration for dismantling some of the Trump administration's strict immigration policies.
