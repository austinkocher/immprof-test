NPR

President Trump has said often that he is not anti-immigrant, just anti-illegal immigrant.  But legal immigrants are receiving restrictive treatment.

There is a slowdown in processing adjustment of status applications.  Lawful permanent resident Mahmoud Khalil is in immigration detention because of protest activities. 

NPR reports that returning to the United States from abroad has become riskier under the Trump administration's immigration enforcement "crackdown — even for people with valid visas and green cards, immigration lawyers say."

Immigration lawyers are advising lawful permanent residents to naturalize.  "Some immigration lawyers are telling green-card holders not to leave the country, especially if they have any criminal record in their past — no matter how minor."
