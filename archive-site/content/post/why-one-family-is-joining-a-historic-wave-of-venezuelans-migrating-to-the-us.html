This NPR report follows a family joining the 7.7 million migrants who have left Venezuela in recent years to escape political and economic turmoil. "That's about one-quarter of the population Venezuela had when the crisis began around 2014. It tops the number of refugees who have fled either war-ravaged Ukraine or Syria and amounts to the largest displacement crisis ever recorded in the Western Hemisphere."  Although most have fled to other nations in Latin America,  a growing number of Venezuelans have been coming to the United States.  "For the first time, Venezuelans in September became the largest nationality arrested for illegally crossing the southern U.S. border, outnumbering Mexicans, according to figures released by U.S. Customs and Border Protection. Venezuelans were arrested 54,833 times by the Border Patrol that month, more than double the figure for August."
