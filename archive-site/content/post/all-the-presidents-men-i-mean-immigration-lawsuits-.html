Stuart Anderson for Forbes looks at the Trump administration's major immigration suits.  He notes that "No president has been sued over his immigration policies as much as Donald Trump. Court decisions on many Trump administration policies are expected within a year and will affect millions of people. Trump’s authority over immigration and the power of future presidents to restrict entry into the United States will be decided."

Anderson proceeds to list the major immigration issues in litigation.  I might list them in a different order but here is how he listed them:

Using the President’s 212(f) Authority to Limit Immigration

Using the Public Charge Regulation to Limit Legal Immigration

Temporary Protected Status (TPS)

Asylum and Detention Policy

H-1B Visas

State and Local Immigration Enforcement

Building a “Wall” (With Other People’s Land and Money)

Executive Order to Limit Refugee Resettlement
