Here is the latest from Arizona.  On November 1, Broadside Books will release a book by Arizona Governor Jan Bewer, who championed the state's controversial immigration law known as SB 1070, entitled “Scorpions for Breakfast: My Fight Against Special Interests, Liberal Media, and Cynical Politicos to Secure America’s Border.” The book is available for pre-order on Amazon.com.

Brewer annnounced today that she will seek U.S. Supreme Court review of the Ninth Circuit decision upholding the district court injunction barring the immigration core of the Arizona law from going into effect.  For the press release of the Governor's decision, see Download PR_050911_BrewerPetitionUSSupremeCourtSB1070[1]
