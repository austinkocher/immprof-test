In 2017, Europe was slow to respond to the exodus of refugees fleeing mass violence there.  Now, Reuters reports that "European countries put asylum applications from Syrians on hold until further notice [yesterday] after rebels seized the Syrian capital and President Bashar al-Assad fled to Russia following 13 years of civil war."
