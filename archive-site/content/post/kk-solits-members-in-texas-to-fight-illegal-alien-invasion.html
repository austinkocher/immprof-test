Flyers from the the Ku Klux Klan soliciting members to join reportedly were distributed (including to an African American family) in a racially mixed Houston, Texas suburb.  The membership pitch:  join the Klan to work to control the borders and combat the flow of "illegal aliens" across the U.S./Mexico border.
