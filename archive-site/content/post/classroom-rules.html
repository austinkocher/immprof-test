I had the opportunity to visit Greenwood Rising in Tulsa, OK last week. It's a museum that documents the creation and destruction of Black Wall Street in Oklahoma. It's a phenomenal experience--Smithsonian level quality--that I urge you to visit if you find yourself in Tulsa.

On my way out the door, I noticed this wonderful poster on "The Rights & Responsibilities of Dialogue." I took a quick pic (which is why it's partially cut off), and I added this to my introductory slides for crimmigraiton. It strikes me as a wonderful way to set up expectations for students in discussing tough topics, whatever the course.
