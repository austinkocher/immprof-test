Today is May Day, known in many countries around the world as International Workers Day.

As CNN reports, it's a holiday for celebrating not just spring, but also working people, and it's often  a day of civil protest.

In that spirit, there will be immigration marches around the country. UndocuMedia has a list here. From California to Texas to New York, today is a day you can join together to, as one event has put it, "join the fight for justice for immigration families."

Or, as another organization has said, "Marchamos...
POR RESPETO, DIGNIDAD, JUSTICIA e IGUALDAD."
