Sofya Aptekar for The Conversation reports on why immigrants serve in the U.S. military.  Military service provides an expedited path to U.S. citizenship.  Many assume that the desire for citizenship is what pushes immigrants to enlist. Aptekar interviewed 72 noncitizens from 28 countries who enlisted in the U.S. military for her book Green Card Soldier: Between Model Immigrant and Security Threat.  Her primary finding:  "[T]he fast track to citizenship is not as important in explaining immigrant enlistments as economic factors like poverty and debt, and cultural factors, such as valuing warrior masculinity and legitimization of war."
