Haiti is in the midst of a "constitutional crisis," as WaPo reports, "with rival claims to the presidency, allegations of a coup attempt and police deployed to the Supreme Court."

Despite this unrest, the U.S. deported 72 migrants to Haiti on Monday. As reported by the Guardian, the group included a two-month-old infant and another 21 minors.

Why exactly did the deportations happen given the Jan. 20, 2021 memo ostensibly putting most deportations on hold for 100 days? Well, just days after issuing that  memo, the state of Texas sued to enjoin it. And on Jan. 26, a U.S. federal judge issued a nationwide temporary restraining order against that pause, leaving ICE free to continue deportations, notwithstanding the requested pause.
