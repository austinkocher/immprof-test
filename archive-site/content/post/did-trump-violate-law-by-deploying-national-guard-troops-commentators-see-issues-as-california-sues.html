Was President Trump's deployment of federal troops in Los Angeles lawful?  The ABA collects some expert opinions.  One view:  "Because Trump didn’t invoke the Insurrection Act, the National Guard troops won’t be allowed to engage in ordinary law enforcement activities without violating the Posse Comitatus Act, wrote Stephen Vladeck, a professor at the Georgetown University Law Center, on his One First blog on Substack."

The last time that the federal government deployed he national guard without a governor’s request was in 1965, when former President Lyndon B. Johnson sent troops to Alabama to protect civil rights demonstrators, another expert told the New York Times.
