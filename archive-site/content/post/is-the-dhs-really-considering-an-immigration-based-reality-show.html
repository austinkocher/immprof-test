Time magazine reports on a story that is the sign of the immigration times.

A producer behind reality shows like The Millionaire Matchmaker, Duck Dynasty, and Alpha Dogs has pitched a new reality show idea to the Department of Homeland Security (DHS)—one in which immigrants compete for U.S. citizenship.

“I'm putting a face to immigration. This is a great celebration of America,” Rob Worsoff told CNN in an interview that aired on Friday, May 16, noting that he himself is an immigrant from Canada.

 

UPDATE (May 21):  The controversy lives on.  "Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem . . . accused DailyMail.com of lying about her support for a reality TV competition that would pit immigrants against each other to fast-track their US citizenship. . . . DailyMail.com stands by its reporting and can now reveal that it has seen DHS HQ internal communications showing not only was Noem aware of the proposal by a Duck Dynasty producer, but she also endorsed it."  See Internal DHS memo shows ICE Barbie Kristi Noem endorsed immigrant reality TV show
