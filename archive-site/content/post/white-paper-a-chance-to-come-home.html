The National Immigrant Justice Center (NIJC) has initiated a campaign called “Chance to Come Home,” calling for a meaningful process for return for the unjustly deported.  The campaign received coverage in the Washington Post when Howard Bailey, a Jamaican immigrant and U.S. army veteran, finally became a U.S. citizen, after a decade of exile.  He had been deported based on a drug conviction many years ago (for which he was pardoned by the governor of Virginia).  Mother Jones did a long piece as well.
