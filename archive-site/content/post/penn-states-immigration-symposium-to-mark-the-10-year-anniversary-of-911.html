Register now (free but required) for Penn State's immigration symposium to mark the 10 year anniversary of 9/11. The event will be held on Friday, September 16 at Penn State Law's University Park Campus. The symposium will feature panels on race, national security and human rights as well as an exhibit of photographs from Professor Steven Rubin, whose work includes acclaimed photos of U.S. immigration detention centers. Other speakers include

Susan M. Akram (Boston University)

Donald Kerwin Jr.(vice president for programs at the Migration Policy Institute)

Wendy Patten (senior policy analyst, Open Society Foundations)

Kareem Shora (senior policy advisor at the Department of Homeland Security)

Margaret Stock (Lieutenant Colonel (Retired); Counsel to the Firm at Lane Powell PC; Adjunct Professor, University of University of Alaska, Anchorage)
