The town council in Summerville, South Carolina, a quiet bedroom community, on Wednesday refused to pass a proposed ordinance banning illegal immigrants from residing in their town. The 4-3 vote to "table the proposal came after some council members warned approving the measure could result in court challenges that could cost taxpayers millions of dollars." Associated Press.
