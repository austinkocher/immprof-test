Today, Arizona Governor Doug Ducey appointed Goldwater Institute litigator Clint Bolick to the Supreme Court of Arizona. Here is the official announcement.

Bolick, an independent, specializes in constitutional law, and also handles cases involving state and federal regulatory law, business and property regulation, health care, education and public pensions. 

Bolick co-authored a book with Republican Presidential candidate Jeb Bush on immigration reform.  The books consciously embraces a moderate approach to immigration reform.

Bolick is a graduate of UC Davis School of Law.
