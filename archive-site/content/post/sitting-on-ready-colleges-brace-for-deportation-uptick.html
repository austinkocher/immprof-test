Since the election of President Trump, immigrant students -- and students with immigrant parents -- have been increasingly anxious about immigration enforcement.  The Supreme Court's decision to review the DACA rescission cases will likely increase tensions and concerns in the fall when students return in number to university campuses for the fall term.  

Diverse Issues in Higher Education ("Sitting on Ready: Colleges Brace for Deportation Uptick") looks at how universities can best address student concerns with immigration enforcement within the confines of the law.
