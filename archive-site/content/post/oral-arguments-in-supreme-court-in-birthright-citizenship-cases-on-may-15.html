On May 15, the Supreme Court will hear oral arguments in the birthright citizenship cases, Trump v. CASA, Inc. consolidated with Trump v. Washington and Trump v. New Jersey.

Docket No. Op. Below Argument        
24a884 4th Cir. May 15, 2025        

Issue: Whether the Supreme Court should stay the district courts' nationwide preliminary injunctions on the Trump administration’s Jan. 20 executive order ending birthright citizenship except as to the individual plaintiffs and identified members of the organizational plaintiffs or states.

Dahlia Lithwick on Slate (registration required) ("Trump’s Birthright Citizenship Arguments at the Supreme Court Are Epically Bad") is not a fan of the U.S. government's arguments.
