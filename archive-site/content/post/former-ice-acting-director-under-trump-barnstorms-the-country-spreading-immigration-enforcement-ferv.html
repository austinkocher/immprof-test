Tom Homan, former acting Director of Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) in President Donald Trump’s administration, is part of a group traveling the country and promoting misinformation about the border, a multi-outlet team reports. 

Journalists from The Border Chronicle, Lighthouse Reports, the Arizona Center for Investigative Reporting, the Texas Observer, Palabra and Puente News Collaborative looks into Border911, a nonprofit Homan and others founded that promotes border enforcement to end the "border invasion."  

"Trump comes back in January, I’ll be on his heels … and I will run the biggest deportation force this country has ever seen," Homan said during an immigration panel in Washington, D.C., over the summer. Trump has promised to bring Homan back if the election goes his way.
