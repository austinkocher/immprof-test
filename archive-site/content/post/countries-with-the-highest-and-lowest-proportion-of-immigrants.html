My colleague and friend Joel Dobris shared this very interesting list of the top "Countries with the Highest Proportion of Immigrants" from the Visual Capitalist.  Here are the top 10:

Country Immigrants as a percentage of population
 United Arab Emirates 88%
 Qatar 77%
 Kuwait 73%
 Bahrain 55%
 Oman 46%
 Singapore 43%
 Saudi Arabia 39%
 Jordan 34%
 Australia 30%
 Switzerland 29%
 

Source: UN via World Population Review. Note: Only countries with a population of greater than one million are included.
