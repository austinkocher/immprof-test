The U.S. Supreme Court denied a cert petition in Fitisemanu v. US. The case (on appeal from the 10th Circuit) raised the question whether birthright citizenship should apply in the US territories, specifically in America Samoa. No reasoning was provided.

The Biden administration had opposed review of the case, which provided an opportunity to revisit the Insular cases.
