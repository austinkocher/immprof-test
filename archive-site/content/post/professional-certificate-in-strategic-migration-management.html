The number of international migrants worldwide has continued to grow rapidly in recent years. The migration pressures of this century can only be dealt with through comprehensive policies that give consideration to issues such as migration and development, forced migration and the challenges to migration. Successful international migration management also requires a highly cooperative approach across countries. 

The International Centre for Parliamentary Studies is offering its professionally accredited specialist five-day training programme on how to best manage migration flows, the Professional Certificate in Strategic Migration Management which will take place in London from 15th - 19th May 2017 in London, UK. 

Due to high demand, there are now only limited places remaining on this course. If this training programme benefit you or your organisation, final remaining individual or group places can be secured via online registration.

Participants have the opportunity to: 

  • Obtain an internationally recognised Level 7 Chartered Management Institute (CMI) qualification
  • Analyse the causes and impacts of international migration
  • Construct, develop and implement highly effective migration management policies
  • Network and share ideas with colleagues from around the world
If you are not able to attend on these dates, places remain on our Professional Certificate in Strategic Refugee and Forced Migration Policy. This programme focuses on the principles, practices, processes and challenges in establishing and implementing policy initiatives in this area, and explores topics from the safeguarding of migrant children and young people, to international law and safety assessments of home countries.
