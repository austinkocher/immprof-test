Michael Luo for the New Yorker reminds us that on September 2, 1885, in one of the most gruesome episodes of racial terror in American history, a group of white miners in Rock Springs, Wyoming massacred at least 28 Chinese residents.  

Luo has a forthcoming book on the story of the Chinese in America.
