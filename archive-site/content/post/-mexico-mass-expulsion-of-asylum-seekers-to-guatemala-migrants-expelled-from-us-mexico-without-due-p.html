According to Human Rights Watch, Mexico has been carrying out mass expulsion of migrants and asylum seekers to a remote area of the jungle in Guatemala, putting lives at risk.  Those expelled include families and unaccompanied children, some of whom were first expelled by air from the United States,

Human Rights Watch calls on Mexican President Andrés Manuel López Obrador and US President Joe Biden to immediately stop these dangerous and unlawful expulsions and ensure that future deportations are carried out in line with Mexican, US, and international law.
