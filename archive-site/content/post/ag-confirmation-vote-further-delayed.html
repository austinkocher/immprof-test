--- title: "AG Confirmation Vote Further Delayed?" date: 2015-03-16T04:21:16 url: "/post/ag-confirmation-vote-further-delayed/" aliases: ["/immigration/2015/03/ag-confirmation-vote-further-delayed.html"] type: post year: "2015" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

It may not be surprising news but Senate Majority Leader Mitch McConnell (R-Ky.) said Sunday that the confirmation of Loretta Lynch as attorney general may hinge on whether Congress works out its gridlock over a human trafficking bill. "We need to finish this human trafficking bill that came out of the Judiciary Committee unanimously . . . because the next week we'll be doing the budget and the next two weeks after that Congress is not in session," McConnell said on CNN's "State of the Union."

The Justice for Victims of Trafficking Act, authored by Sen. John Cornyn (R-Tex.), has been stalled after Democrats accused Republicans of sneaking anti-abortion language into the bill. McConnell noted that Democrats on the Judiciary Committee unanimously agreed on the bill before forwarding it.

Some Republican Senators have expressed opposition to Lynch's nomination because of her support for the President's executive actions on immigration.
