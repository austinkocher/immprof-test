In this article, Elina Treyger (George Mason University School of Law) considers the incentives of local police to arrest persons for purposes other than crime enforcement and how those incentives increase arrests but not necessarily the number of "good" arrests for criminal activity.  One of the "collateral incentives" for arrests are enforcement of the immigration laws and programs such as Secure Communities and the Priority Enforcement Program.
