Canadian actress Jasmine Mooney was detained last month for 12 days by U.S. immigration authorities when she tried to apply for a visa at the U.S./Mexico border. She spoke with NPR's Morning Edition about her experience. 

Mooney said she met about 200 women without criminal records.  She reports that there is no communication or ability to contact attorneys. Mooney felt that the system was "rigged" to keep noncitizens in detention.
