My heart breaks over the devastation in Haiti following the deadly earthquake this weekend. ⁰⁰I pray for the safety, recovery, and health of all those affected and remain committed to doing all we can to support Haiti's full economic and political recovery.

— Congresswoman Cori Bush (@RepCori) August 16, 2021

Congresswoman Cori Bush is getting national attention (and here).  She took a courageous stand on evictions and changed the entire course of things for the nation.  Now she is taking a stand with Haitians devastated by political turmoil and natural disaster. 

It is a tough time for refugees.  Many Americans worry about numbers.  We can expect a flow of refugees from Afghanistan.    Will we be willing, for example, to accept more Haitians?  President Trump worried about too many noncitizens from "s---hole countries,"  including Haiti. We will see how the Biden administration responds.
