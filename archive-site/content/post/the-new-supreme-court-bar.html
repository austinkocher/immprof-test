Adam Liptak of the N.Y. Times has an interesting article on the emergence of the pro bono Supreme Court bar, which includes the Stanford Supreme Court Litigation Clinic.  There has been some controversy over the new bar.  The immigration bar expressed concern with Fernandez-Vargas v. Gonzalez (2006) in which an attorney not a specialist in immigration argued the case before the Court and lost in a lop-sidded 8-1 decision.  Immigration law professor Nancy Morawetz has an article forthcoming in the NYU Law Review that questions the incentives for the new Supreme Court specialists and points to the Fernandez-Vargas as an example of what not to do.
