The 1997 "Flores settlement" addressing the terms and conditions of the detention of migrant children has been in the news.  In this short piece on The Conversation, I attempt to explain the background behind the settlement, which can be seen as lasting achievement of public interest lawyers who settled a case that had been litigated for years.
