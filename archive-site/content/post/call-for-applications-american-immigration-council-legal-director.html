The American Immigration Council seeks to shape how the nation thinks about and acts towards immigrants and immigration and by working toward a more fair and just immigration system.

Position Summary 

The Legal Director leads the Council’s litigation efforts to achieve a fair and just immigration system. The Legal Director supervises a team of litigators and directs the Council’s impact litigation, including class actions, multi-plaintiff lawsuits, and amicus brief strategies. The Legal Director oversees the Council’s other legal advocacy, including the drafting and issuance of practice advisories, the convening of litigation strategy meetings, and the development and review of other legal strategies, often in coordination with the other Council teams. The Legal Director is a senior staff position.
