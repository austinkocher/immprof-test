Rosa Flores for CNN reports that the City of El Paso, Texas, launched a migrant dashboard Saturday to follow activity at its migrant welcome center.  The city has served more than 14,900 migrants since early September.  More than 8,000 migrants have been transported from El Paso to New York and about 2,300 to Chicago since August 23, when busing program began, according to the dashboard. The migrants are offered the city-funded journeys after being processed and released by the Border Patrol.

The dashboards provide the situational awareness and updated daily.
