British boxing title holder Amir Khan (on left in photo above), who is a Muslim of Pakistani descent, ran into visa problems that jeopardized his Madison Square Garden fight on Saturday night.  Khan began training in Los Angeles but had to leave the country for Canada while his visa issues, which reportedly stemmed from traffic offenses in the UK, were being worked out.  After being granted a P1 visa, Khan made it to teh Big Apple and dominated the fight over a battered Paulie Malignaggi until the referee stopped the fight in the 11th round.
