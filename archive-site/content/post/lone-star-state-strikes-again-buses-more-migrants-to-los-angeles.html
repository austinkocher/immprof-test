A news story from earlier this week

Transporting migrants out of Texas for the last year, the state of Texas continues to keep its immigration enforcement measures in the news.

Fox News reports that the fifth group of migrants carried on buses from Brownsville, Texas, yesterday arrived in Los Angeles as Texas continues to transport migrants to urban centers across the United States.  According to the report,

"[a]bout 48 migrants, including 18 minors, arrived in downtown Los Angeles late Saturday morning, Mayor Karen Bass' office said, according to Fox 11. The two buses arrived at Union Station at around 11:30 a.m."
