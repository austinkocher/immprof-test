The Georgetown Immigration Law Journal is currently in the selection process for our next two issues, and is seeking Articles in the field of immigration law. If you, or anyone you know, has a piece that they would like to be considered, we invite you to submit the Article for consideration.
 
If you are interested in submitting an article, we ask that you create an account and submit your article via ExpressO, which can be found here. If you have any general questions, please feel free to reach out directly at lawgilj@georgetown.edu.
