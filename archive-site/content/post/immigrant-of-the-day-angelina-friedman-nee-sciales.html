Angelina Sciales was born in 1918 on board a ship transporting her mother and siblings to the United States from Italy. Her mother did not survive childbirth, and Angelina's sisters kept her alive for the remainder of the journey to America where they reunited with their father.

To survive that beginning, Angelina had to be a fighter. Now 102, she still fighting. As CNN reports, Angelina has survived 2 (TWO!) bouts with Covid-19.

Should I live so long, let my smile be as wide and my fashion choices as on point.
