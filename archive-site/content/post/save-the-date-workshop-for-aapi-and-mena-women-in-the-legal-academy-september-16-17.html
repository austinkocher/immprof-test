Suzanne Kim shares the date about the second annual AAPI women workshop, expanded to include Middle Eastern and North African women, which will be held in-person at UC Davis School of Law. The purpose of the workshop is to suport and mentor women aspiring to enter or who are in the legal academy. The workshop provides a space for workshopping incubators and WIPs, exploring shared identities and history, and dialoguing about professional development, wellness, and building community.
