The Immigration Advocates Network invites you to join a free webinar, "Practice Before the Board of Immigration Appeals," on Tuesday, April 30, 2013 at 2:00 pm Eastern / 1:00 pm Central / 12:00 pm Mountain / 11:00 am Pacific. The panelists will discuss procedure, standards of review, case selection, tips for brief writing, the BIA Pro Bono Project and more. The panelists are David Neal, Chairman of the Board of Immigration Appeals; Charles Adkins-Blanch, Vice Chairman of the Board of Immigration Appeals; Karen Grisez, Public Service Counsel, Fried Frank; and Lauren Graham Sullivan, Advocacy Attorney, Catholic Legal Immigration Network, Inc.
