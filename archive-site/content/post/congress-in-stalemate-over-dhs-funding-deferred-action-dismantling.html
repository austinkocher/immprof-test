Congress continues to be debating whether to intervene in the Obama administration's deferred action programs.  Yesterday, Senate Democrats blocked passage of a Homeland Security spending bill that would have ended the programs. Senators voted 51-48 on a procedural motion to bring the bill to the Senate floor, short of the 60 votes needed. Republican Dean Heller of Nevada joined with unanimous Democrats and independents to block the bill. Unless a compromise on a new bill can be reached by the end of the month, funding for the Department of Homeland Security will expire.

UPDATE (Feb. 5):  On Wednesday, the Senate again failed to pass the same bill.  More votes are possible.
