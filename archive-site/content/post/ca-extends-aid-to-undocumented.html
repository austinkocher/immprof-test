California's governor, Gavin Newsom, just announced that some 150,000 undocumented adults living in California will receive a one-time payment of $500. That money comes from a combination of taxpayer funds and charitable contributions, CNBC announced.

This is a huge benefit for undocumented workers in the state. Especially given that none are eligible for federal relief.
