The International Labour Organization (ILO) is launching its annual Global Media Competition. Its aim is to distinguish fair and balanced media reports on labour migration. This is especially important because of the impact that negative and stereotypical public narratives can have in terms of fueling discrimination and abusive practices towards migrant workers.

Professional journalists and students can now submit articles or multimedia content to the International Labour Organization’s 2024 Global Media Competition on Labour Migration and Fair Recruitment. Entries must have been published between Oct. 31, 2023 and Oct. 31, 2024. Winners will be able to choose between a cash prize or a paid
fellowship.
