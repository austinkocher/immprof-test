The latest polls have Donald Trump leading by a large margin the other candidates running for the Republican nomination for President.  You may have missed it but another candidate, Ohio Gov. John Kasich, released the video above that goes after Trump. The 60-second spot, titled "Trump's Dangerous Rhetoric," features retired Air Force Col. Tom Moe, a prisoner of war in Vietnam, speaking at an event before Trump's campaign stop in Columbus. Moe paraphrases Protestant pastor Martin Niemöller, who spoke out publicly against the Nazi regime and spent years in Nazi concentration camps, as images of Trump's controversial rhetoric flash on screen.
