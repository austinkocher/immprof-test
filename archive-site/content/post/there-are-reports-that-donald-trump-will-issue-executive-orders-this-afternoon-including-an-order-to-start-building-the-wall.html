There are many news reports that President Donald Trump will sign a number of executive orders at the Department of Homeland Security this afternoon, including an order to start building "the wall" between the United States and Mexico.   CNN reports that President Trump will be directing federal resources toward building a wall along the southern border but may face challenges. "The length of the wall, when construction will begin, how much it will cost and who will pay for it remain fundamental questions."

Reuters also reports that President Trump is expected to sign executive orders that include a temporary ban on most refugees and a suspension of visas for citizens of Syria and six other Middle Eastern and African countries.  Trump, who tweeted that a "big day" was planned on national security, is expected to ban for several months the entry of refugees into the United States, except for religious minorities escaping persecution, until more aggressive vetting is in place.

Another order will block visas being issued to anyone from Syria, Iraq, Iran, Libya, Somalia, Sudan and Yemen, said the aides and experts, who asked not to be identified.

In his tweet late on Tuesday, Trump said: "Big day planned on NATIONAL SECURITY tomorrow. Among many other things, we will build the wall!"
