Check out this profile of Lisa Campbell from the BBC. Lisa traveled to Greece in November 2015 with the idea of doing a short service tour, helping with the recent influx of refugees. She ended up staying in Greece for two years, running a refugee camp in Oinofyta, north of Athens. The camp was closed last month.
