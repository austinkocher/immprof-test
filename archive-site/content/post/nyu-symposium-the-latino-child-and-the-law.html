On Friday November 7, the Bickel & Brewer Latino Institute for Human Rights Symposium at NYU School of Law is holding a symposium on "The Latino Child and the Law." The topics of discussion include immigration reform, access to quality education, and juvenile justice reform. There is no cost to attend and both CLE and meals are included with registration.
