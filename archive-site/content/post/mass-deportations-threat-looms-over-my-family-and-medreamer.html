Wondering what immigrants are feeling and thinking as President-elect Trump is on the verge of retaking office?  "Oscar Silva, a 24-year-old `Dreamer' from San Luis Potosi in Mexico, has lived in the United States for 23 years but is experiencing overwhelming anxiety over the threat of mass deportations." (bold added).  "A single traffic stop could result in me being separated from my wife for 10 years. The stress of my potential deportation weighs on me heavily," he said.  Check out the interview in Newsweek.
