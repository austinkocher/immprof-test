Check out this political cartoon by Nick Anderson for the Houston Chronicle.

President Trump did pardon Arpaio the same day that Hurricane Harvey made landfall in Texas. You might not have made that connection given your understandable outrage over the pardon itself and/or terror for friends and family in Harvey's wake. It was the same day.

And a few days later, as Texas was dealing with historic flooding (which pretty much everyone agrees will require federal help to recover from), President Trump doubled down on his call for a wall between Mexico and the United States.

That's significant because, as you'll recall, President Trump has threatened to shutdown the government if that wall isn't funded. And when the president was asked whether he'd stand by his call for a shutdown in the face of the Harvey crisis, he said that Harvey "has nothing to do with" the "separate" issue of a wall-related shutdown.
