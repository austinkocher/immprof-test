Some real life stories do in fact have happy endings!  

Tony & Janina’s American Wedding, a feature length documentary previously discussed on ImmigrationProf, poignantly tells how the U.S. immigration system tore apart Tony and Janina Wasilewski’s family.  After years living peacefully and starting a family in the Chicago area, Janina was deported back to Poland, taking her and Tony's 6 year old son Brian with her.

The film follows the Wasilewski’s 3-year struggle to be reunited and tells the untold human rights story of undocumented immigrants in America today.﻿

Earlier this month, Janina and Brian, with the U.S. government's permission, returned lawfully to the United States.  For the details on the reunion, click here.
