Elon Musk is often in the news.  CNN looks at his immigration views - both for legal immigration and calling for border controls -- as well as his and his family's immigration journey to the United States.  It is interesting reading.

In response to details his mother, Maye Musk, has shared on X about her own immigration journey, Elon Musk has called legal immigration to the US “a laborious Kafkaesque nightmare” and noted that becoming a US citizen “was extremely difficult and took over a decade.”  He described his prior immigration status as a “gray area.”
