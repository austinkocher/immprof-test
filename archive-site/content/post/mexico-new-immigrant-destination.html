--- title: "Mexico New Immigrant Destination" date: 2013-09-23T05:58:11 url: "/post/mexico-new-immigrant-destination/" aliases: ["/immigration/2013/09/mexico-new-immigrant-destination.html"] type: post year: "2013" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" --- Damien Cave of the New York Times reports that "Mexico, whose economic woes have pushed millions of people north, is increasingly becoming an immigrant destination. The country’s documented foreign-born population nearly doubled between 2000 and 2010, and officials now say the pace is accelerating as broad changes in the global economy create new dynamics of migration."
