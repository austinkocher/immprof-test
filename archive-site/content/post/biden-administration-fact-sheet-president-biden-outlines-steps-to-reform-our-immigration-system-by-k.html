Here is the latest today from the Biden administration on immigration ("FACT SHEET: President Biden Outlines Steps to Reform Our Immigration System by Keeping Families Together, Addressing the Root Causes of Irregular Migration, and Streamlining the Legal Immigration System").

According to the fact sheet, executive actions to be announced later today will:

Create a Task Force to Reunify Families.

Develop a Strategy to Address Irregular Migration Across the Southern Border and Create a Humane Asylum System.

Restore Faith in Our Legal Immigration System and Promote Integration of New Americans.
