PS 58: The School of Heroes

This news report can only make one wonder where the Trump administration is headed in immigration enforcement.   that

An immigration agent tried to search for a fourth-grader at Queens school — but was sent packing by staff, according to city officials.

“Mayor’s been briefed on a fed immig agent showing up at Queens’ PS58 Thurs. asking about a 4th grader. School turned him away,” Mayor de Blasio’s press secretary Eric Phillips tweeted.

The immigration officer did not have a warrant and he was dismissed by an administrator and a school safety official.

The federal agent went to the front security desk, but did not get any further.
