NPR reports that more than 6,000 migrants who came to the United States to ask for asylum have been sent back to Ciudad Juárez, Mexico, just across the border from El Paso. "Remain in Mexico" is a cornerstone of President Trump's immigration efforts. "I have never seen people as scared, who are just viscerally terrified," says one immigration lawyer. In another effort to punish immigrants in the country illegally, the Trump administration this week threatened individuals with $500,000 fines.
