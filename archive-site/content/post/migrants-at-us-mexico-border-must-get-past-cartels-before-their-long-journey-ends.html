For a change of pace, here is some non-coronavirus analysis.

Paola Diaz on The Conversation focuses us on the physical violence encountered by migrants along the U.S./Mexico border.  She spent weeks talking to migrants on their journey to the United States during sociological field research in two northern Mexican towns in Sonora state: Altar, a village of about 8,000 (which I visited and blogged about in 2006); and nearby Caborca, a city of about 100,000. Many, but not all, of them were Central American.

In Diaz's view, "passage from Sonora to the border is entirely controlled by organized crime."  And the result is violence, with women migrants especially subject to horrible violence.
