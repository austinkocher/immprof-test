Looking for a podcast on topical immigration news.  Birthright citizenship is in the news.  And here is a history podcast for you on the topic.  🎧 In August of 1895, a ship called the SS Coptic approached the coast of Northern California. On that boat was a passenger from San Francisco named Wong Kim Ark. He was returning home after visiting his wife and child in China. The customs agent barred him from getting off the ship under the Chinese Exclusion Act, which denied citizenship to Chinese immigrants. Though Wong Kim Ark had been born in the U.S. and lived his whole life there, the agent said he was not a U.S.  citizen.
