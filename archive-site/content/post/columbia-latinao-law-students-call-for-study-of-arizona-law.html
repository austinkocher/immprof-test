The Columbia Latina/o Law Students Association (LaLSA) urges you to learn more about the Arizona immigration law. "Much of our work at the law school seeks to ensure reasonable debate and discussion about important public policy issues concerning the Latino community. Because in our legal studies we are at times insulated from law's real-life implications, we think it is important that everyone is aware of this issue."
