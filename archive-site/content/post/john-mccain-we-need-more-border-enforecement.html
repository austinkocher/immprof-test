Senator John (Lucas) McCain continues to endorse increased border enforcement.  Saying if his and U.S. Senator Jon Kyl’s 10-point border security plan had been enacted last year, the U.S. border with Mexico would be in better shape today, McCain said the proposal will be reintroduced next week in the Senate.
