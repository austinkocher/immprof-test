The statement of Immigration Judge Jack Weil that 3-4 year olds could be taught enough immigration law to represent themselves in remocval proceedings has gone viral.  There understandably much much criticism of Judge Weil's claim.

Judge Weil made the statement in a deposition in a lawsuit brought by the ACLU seeking to ensure that unaccompanied minors be provided with representation.  The American Civil Liberties Union, American Immigration Council, Northwest Immigrant Rights Project, Public Counsel, and K&L Gates LLP filed a nationwide class-action lawsuit on behalf of thousands of children challenging the federal government's failure to provide them with legal representation in removal hearings.
