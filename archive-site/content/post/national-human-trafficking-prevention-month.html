President Joe Biden has proclaimed January 2024 as National Human Trafficking Prevention Month.  He has "call[ed] upon businesses, civil society organizations, communities of faith, families, and all Americans to recognize the vital role we play in combating human trafficking and to observe this month with appropriate programs and activities aimed at preventing all forms of human trafficking."

The State Department offers history and resources for the month.
