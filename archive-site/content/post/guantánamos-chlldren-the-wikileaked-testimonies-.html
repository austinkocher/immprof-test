The UC Davis Center for the Study of Human Rights in the Americas has released a study of the Guantánamo files recently made public by Wikileaks. The report can be found here: Guantánamo's Chlldren: The Wikileaked Testimonies. In a nutshell, the report finds that military documents in the public domain now acknowledge that fifteen children were imprisoned, at some time or another, at Guantánamo. This is three more than the twelve the State Department acknowledged to the public after an earlier report on the subject, and seven more than the eight the State Department reported to the UN Committee on the Rights of the Child.
