Human Rights Watch reports that the U.S. and Salvadoran governments "have subjected more than 200 Venezuelan nationals to enforced disappearances and arbitrary detention . . . .

On March 15, 2025, the US government removed 238 Venezuelans to El Salvador, where they were immediately transferred to the mega prison known as the Center for Confinement of Terrorism . . . known for its abusive conditions."

The Venezuelans have been held incommunicado. Authorities have not disclosed a list of the people removed.  CBS News published a leaked list of names. "Relatives of people apparently transferred to El Salvador told Human Rights Watch that US authorities said that they were unable to share any information on their relatives’ whereabouts."
