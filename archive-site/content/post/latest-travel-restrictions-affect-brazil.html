The Trump administration has issued another travel restriction linked to COVID-19. This time the target is Brazil.

As CBS reports, nonimmigrants will not be allowed into the United States if they've traveled to Brazil within 14 days of the expected U.S. entry. LPRs are exempt from this restriction.

Brazil is currently experiencing a significant outbreak of COVID-19. The country has the second highest number of COVID cases worldwide, behind just the United States.
