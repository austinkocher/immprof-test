Texas Governor Greg Abbott has taken aggressive immigration initiative.  Here is the latest.

The Houston Chronicle and the San Antonio Express-News published reports that Governor Abbott’s troops have been ordered to push migrant children and babies back into the Rio Grande. Congress member Joaquin Castro shared an excerpt of the reporting:

". . . troopers came across a group of 120 people camped out along a fence set up along the river. The group included several small children and babies who were nursing, the trooper wrote. The entire group was exhausted, hungry and tired, the trooper wrote. The shift officer in command ordered the troopers to “push the people back into the water to go to Mexico,” the email says.  The trooper wrote that the troopers decided it was not the right thing to do “with the very real potential of exhausted people drowning.”

In a statement posted today, Castro called the orders the troops received “absolutely monstrous, inhumane policy.”
