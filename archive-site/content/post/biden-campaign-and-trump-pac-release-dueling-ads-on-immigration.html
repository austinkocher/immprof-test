The 2024 presidential campaign will be ramping up.  Look for immigration to be an issue, with very different views expressed by the Biden and Trump campaigns.
 
Megan Lebowitz for NBC Newa reports that the Biden campaign and a pro-Trump super PAC have competing immigration ads that paint vastly different pictures of the issue.

The advertisement from President Joe Biden’s campaign largely focuses on Latino Americans but also goes after former President Donald Trump and his rhetoric.    “Now he says immigrants are poisoning the blood of our country,” Biden says in the spot.

In an ad released by the pro-Trump super PAC called MAGA Inc., audio of Biden criticizing the border wall is played over video of migrants pushing through part of a border fence.
