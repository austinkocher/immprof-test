The Hill reports that the Supreme Court has scheduled a rare May oral argument on the Trump administration’s request to narrow a series of nationwide injunctions on President Trump’s executive order that would restrict birthright citizenship. 

The administration has "asked the high court to rein in lower judges that went beyond the parties who sued to block the president’s order nationwide."

Today's order defers a ruling on the administration's request until after oral arguments on May 15.  Trump’s executive order will not be enforced for now.
