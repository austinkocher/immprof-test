Kevin McGill for the Associated Press reports that oral arguments in the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit on the future of the Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA) policy. That policy celebrated its tenth anniversary in June.    Last year, U.S. District Judge Andrew Hanen’s ruled DACA was and barred the Biden administration from accepting new applications.  Those already granted DACA relief have been permitted to apply for renewal.

The Biden administration's Department of Justice is defending the policy in Texas v. United States.  Joining the U.S. government are the state of New Jersey, the Mexican-American Legal Defense and Education Fund, and a coalition of corporations, including Amazon, Apple, Google and Microsoft, which argue that DACA recipients are “employees, consumers and job creators.”

The arguments will be heard by Chief Judge Priscilla Richman, an appointee of President George W. Bush; and two Trump appointees, judges James Ho and Kurt Engelhardt.
