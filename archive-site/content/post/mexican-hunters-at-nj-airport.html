As ImmigrationProf has reported on numerous occasions (and here), racial profiling has long plagued -- and continues to plague -- immigration and border enforcement.  CNN reports that security screeners at Newark Liberty International Airport singled out Mexican and Dominican passengers for nearly two years, according to a federal report obtained by The Star-Ledger newspaper. The racial profiling was so notorious that some TSA employees at the airport referred to their colleagues as "Mexican hunters."

A TSA spokeswoman described the situation in Newark as "isolated." Hmmmmmmmm.
