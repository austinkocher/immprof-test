A poignant story in the New York Times by Deborah Sontag (‘Yo Soy la Mamá’: A Migrant Mother’s Struggle to Get Back Her Son) of our laws at work    Olga, a Honduran immigrant, was separated her 5-year-old son, Ricardo.   After navigating immigration and child welfare bureaucracies, Olga's story ended well.  Immigration Professor Lori Nessel is quoted in the article.
