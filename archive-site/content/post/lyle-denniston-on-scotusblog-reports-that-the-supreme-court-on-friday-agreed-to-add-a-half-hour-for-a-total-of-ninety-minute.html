Lyle Denniston on SCOTUSBlog reports that the Supreme Court on Friday added a half-hour, for a total of ninety minutes, to its scheduled argument on April 18 on United States v. Texas, which is poised to decide the legal fate of President Obama’s expanded deferred action program announced in November 2014 and put on hold since then. That argument order is included with this list.

Both sides had requested additional time. The federal government will share fifteen of its 45 minutes with a lawyer for an intervening group of undocumented immigrant parents (represented by the Mexican American Legal Defense and Educational Fund). The state of Texas, speaking for the twenty-six states that challenged the policy, will share fifteen of its 45 minutes with a lawyer for the U.S. House of Representatives, which has filed an amicus brief supporting the  challenge.
