Acting Immigration and Customs Enforcement Director Thomas Homan said California "better hold on tight" after the state's "sanctuary" law to take effect this week.

California Governor Jerry Brown claims that the law will protect undocumented immigrants from unjustified enforcement efforts.

"I think it's terrible," Homan said, adding that Brown's action put politics in front of public safety.
