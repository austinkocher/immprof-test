The Associated Press reports President Barack Obama said Wednesday that he "fundamentally rejects" the notion that House Majority Leader Eric Cantor's primary election defeat means that immigration reform is not possible.  The President told about 40 donors in a Boston suburb that  "I fundamentally reject that and I will tell the speaker of the House he needs to reject it."
