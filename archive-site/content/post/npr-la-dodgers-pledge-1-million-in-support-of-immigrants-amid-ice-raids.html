LA Dodgers pledge $1 million in support of immigrants amid ICE raids:

The Major League Baseball Los Angeles Dodgers have made immigration news in recent days.  Here is the latest from NPR.  "The Los Angeles Dodgers have announced they will commit $1 million towards financial assistance for families of immigrants `impacted by recent events in the region.'"  Here is the announcement, which "comes amid ongoing federal immigration raids targeting migrants in the area, and calls from the Dodgers' fanbase for the organization to speak out against them."
