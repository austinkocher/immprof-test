CARACEN SF has created a video with information for people who are protected under the Temporary Protected Status Program (TPS) from El Salvador. On June 21, the U.S. Department of Homeland Security published in the federal register the extension of the TPS designation for Salvadorans who have been protected under this program since March 9, 2001.
