The coronavirus is dominating the news, everywhere all the time. The anxiety and fears are palpable.  The U.S. government's latest travel restrictions have resulted in massive lines for returning Americans.  As reported in USA Today, "U.S. travelers flying back from Europe late Saturday were greeted with snaking lines and hours-long waits at major airports as expanded coronavirus screenings required by the government's new European travel restrictions took effect."

Melissa Cruz for Immigration Impact focuses on how immigration is affecting immigration (separate and apart from the travel bans).  Cruz notes that, with the number of confirmed cases on the rise, the coronavirus has started to affect several facets of immigration.

How government officials handle the virus could have a significant impact on people navigating our immigration process, their health, and the immigration system at large. Among the government responses that have already made an impact.
