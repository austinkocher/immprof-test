Happy St. Patrick's Day, a celebration of the Irish in America.  Here are ten surprising facts on the day from TIME.  A most surprising one to me: 

"The first St. Patrick’s Day parade was held in the U.S.   The first recorded parade on the Catholic Feast Day of St. Patrick was held on March 17, 1601, in a Spanish colony in modern-day St. Augustine, Florida. More than a century later, Irish soldiers serving in the English military marched in Boston in 1737 and in New York City in 1762."
