 What are the economic effects of immigration? Ian Goldin, the economist and director of the Oxford Martin School recommends five books on the economic benefits on immigration.  Here they are:

1.  Bloody Foreigners by Robert Winder

2.  Borderless Economics by Robert Guest

3.  The Age of Migration by Stephen Castles et al.

4.  The Age of Mass Migration by Timothy Hatton and Jeffrey Williamson

5.  A Man of Good Hope by Jonny Steinberg
