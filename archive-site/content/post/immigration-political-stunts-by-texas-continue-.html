Texas continues high profile immigration measures, which some claim are little more than political stunts.   A bus carrying dozens of migrants from Texas arrived in Los Angeles yesterday — the fourth such bus to arrive in the city in recent weeks. This latest event comes as Texas Governor Greg Abbott and Florida Governor Ron DeSantis have sent migrants to Democratic-led states in protest of the Biden administration’s immigration enforcement efforts.

The arriving migrants were greeted by an immigration coalition and community groups that provided them with food, clothes and consultations with legal immigration attorneys.
