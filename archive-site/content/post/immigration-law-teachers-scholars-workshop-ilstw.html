The conference has been in the works -- and now the website is up.  This year’s Immigration Law Teachers & Scholars Workshop (ILSTW) will take place at LMU Loyola Law School in Los Angeles on June 5-7.  ILSTW 2022 brings together our community to look beyond resistance toward the future of immigration law. 
 
UPDATE: A call for works-in-progress, incubator ideas, and discussants was posted yesterday on Immigrationprof Blog.
