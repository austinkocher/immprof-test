The Migration Policy Institue (MPI) has a variety of explainers.  The latest is Immigrants and the U.S. Economy, and gives a quick tour of the findings on the impact of immigration on the U.S. economy

Other recent U.S. explainers review immigrants and crime, noncitizens and voting, immigrants and public benefits eligibility and use, and a profile of the U.S. immigrant population.
