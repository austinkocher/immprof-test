The American Bar Association (ABA) is calling on lawyers to urge the Trump administration to reinstate funding for legal services for tens of thousands of unaccompanied children who entered the United States.

The Trump administration last week terminated funding for direct legal representation for more than 26,000 children who are in or have been released from U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office of Refugee Resettlement custody.

The ABA news release is here. It states in part:

"Abruptly ending the program today, without appearing to consider how children who are currently represented or how court proceedings will be impacted, is deeply troubling and could leave thousands of immigrant children—many of whom have already experienced severe trauma and violence—vulnerable to further harm."

The Associated Press and the New York Times have coverage of the development.
