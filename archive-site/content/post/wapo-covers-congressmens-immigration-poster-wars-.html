Photo via WaPo & Office of Congressman Mo Brooks

This story from WaPo cracked me up. It's about two Congressmen and their offices in the Rayburn House Office Building on Capitol Hill. Representative Joaquin Castro (D-Tex.) has an office right across the hall from Representative Mo Brooks (R-Ala.).

A while ago, Representative Castro put up a poster outside his office highlighting DREAMers from his state. Representative Brooks responded by creating an identically-formatted posted highlighting families who have been the victims of crimes committed by unauthorized migrants.

You can take the kid out of middle school but you can't take the middle school out of the kid apparently.
