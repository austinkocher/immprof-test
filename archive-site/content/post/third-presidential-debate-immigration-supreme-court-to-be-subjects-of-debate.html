On October 19, the third presidential debate between Donald Trump and Hillary Clinton will be held at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas.  The topics selected for the debate include Immigration, the Suprreme Court and "fitness to be President."   The bipartisan Commission on Presidential Debates announced Wednesday that Fox News host and moderator Chris Wallace had selected the topics for the debate.

Looks like the debate is set up for fireworks!  Stay tuned.
