The Consular Accountability Project invites you to an MCLE online seminar, Trump’s First 100 Days: Seismic Shifts in Visa Policy on May 22 at 9:00 am PT (12:00 pm ET). This event offers 1.5 California Bar MCLE credits (pending approval).  Registration is now open.

The second Trump administration’s executive actions have dramatically reshaped the landscape of visa adjudication, impacting consular nonreviewability, SEVIS enforcement, and constitutional protections for visa holders. This seminar will feature expert analysis from leading litigators, former consular officials, and constitutional law scholars offering advanced insights and practical strategies for navigating the latest developments, including recent litigation on consular denials, SEVIS terminations, First Amendment retaliation, and the impact of recent Supreme Court decisions.
