In this New Yorker article ("Should Hotel Chains Be Held Liable for Human Trafficking?"), Bernice Young writes that, "[f]or decades, franchised hotels have been a common scene of sex-trafficking crimes in the U.S. A new legal strategy is targeting the corporations that collect royalties from them."  As she explains in a new report, the organizational structure of franchise hotels also “allows chains to argue they shouldn’t be held responsible for safety and security problems that emerge at a property they don’t directly oversee.”
