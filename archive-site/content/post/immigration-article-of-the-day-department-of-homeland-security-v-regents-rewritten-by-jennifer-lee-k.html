Department of Homeland Security v. Regents, Rewritten  by Jennifer Lee Koh

Abstract

The following is a rewriting of the majority opinion in Department Homeland Security v. Regents, in which plaintiffs challenged the Trump administration’s efforts to rescind Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA). The rewritten opinion will appear in FEMINIST JUDGMENTS: REWRITTEN IMMIGRATION LAW OPINIONS (Kevin Lapp, Kathleen Kim & Jennifer J. Lee, eds., forthcoming Cambridge University Press 2023).

I had the honor of commenting on Professor Koh’s rewritten opinion in the volume.
