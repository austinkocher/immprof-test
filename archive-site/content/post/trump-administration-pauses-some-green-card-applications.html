Here is the latest.  Many news outlets (here, here, here) are reporting that a U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) official told Newsweek that there has been a pause in approval of some so-called adjustment of status applications under executive orders signed by President Trump.  According to one report, the instructions are to pause the applications of noncitizens granted refugee status and asylum.

UPDATE (April 4): 

Lindsay Harris on the Hill explains why the Trump administration's "pause on ‘green card’ processing is harmful to refugees and asylees."
