In a week that saw the Dow Jones tumble, the beginning of a trade war with China, and turmoil in the administration, President Trump through tweets and policy measures again put immigration in the news this week, calling for troops on the border, increasing immigration criminal prosecutions, and ending 'catch and release"  (which also was done in one of the President's January 2017 executive orders).  

In addition, as Maria Sacchetti reports from the Washington Postt, "Federal officials arrested 97 immigrants at a meat-processing plant in rural Tennessee on Thursday in what civil rights organizations said was the largest single workplace raid in a decade and a sign that the Trump administration is carrying out its plan to aggressively ramp up enforcement this year."
