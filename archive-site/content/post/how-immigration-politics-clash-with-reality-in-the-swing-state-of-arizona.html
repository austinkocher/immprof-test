This in-depth NPR report ("How immigration politics clash with reality in the swing state of Arizona"), Ximena Bustillo thoughtfully considers the complexities of life on the border and how popular demands to "close the border" simply is not possible.  As one border resident put it, "I think for our federal government, they need to acknowledge that the whole border economy is dependent on this binational way of life," . . .  It's frustrating that there's a lot of arguing back and forth and very little action and planning."   As the comments suggest, commerce between the United States and Mexico is the lifeblood of the border region.
