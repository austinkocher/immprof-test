President Trump has repeatedly said that he is not anti-immigrant, just anti-illegal immigrant.  His administration's immigration enforcement measures directed at lawful permanent residents and student visa holders suggest something different.  The Hill reports that Secretary of State Marco Rubio said today "that at least 300 foreign students have had their visas revoked amid the Trump administration’s immigration crackdown, a far higher number than was previously known."
