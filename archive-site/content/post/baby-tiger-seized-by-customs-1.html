This may or may not be the most unusual seizure by customs this year, but it surely is the most adorable. As the BBC reports, a California teenager bought this baby tiger off a man in Tijuana for $300. He then tried to bring it into the US but was stopped by customs at Otay Mesa. The tiger cub was seized and is being cared for at the San Diego Zoo.
