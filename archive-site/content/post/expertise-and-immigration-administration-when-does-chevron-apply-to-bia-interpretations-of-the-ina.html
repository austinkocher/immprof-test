I recently ran across a fine note on a very topical immigration law issue that I wanted to commend to you.  Paul Chaffin, Note, Expertise and Immigration Administration: When Does Chevron Apply to BIA Interpretations of the INA?, 69 N.Y.U. Ann. Surv. Am. L. 503-85 (2013), offers a thorough and well-done review of the law surrounding deference under Chevron U.S.A., Inc. v. Natural Resources Defense Council, Inc., 467 U.S. 837 (1984) to Board of Immigration Appeals Interpretations of the Immigration and Nationality Act.  Chevron deference is the bread-and-butter of many of the Supreme Court's immigration decisions in recent years,  see, e.g., Scialabba v. Cuellar de Osorio (2014); Holder v. Gutierrez (2012), which are analyzed in this article.
