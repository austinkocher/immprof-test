The Associated Press provides an overview of some of the immigration changes brought by President Trump. U.S. immigration news editor Elliot Spagat discussed The Associated Press' latest coverage with Rebecca Santana, who covers the Department of Homeland Security, and Christopher Sherman, who is the news director for Mexico and Central America. They answered questions about Trump’s immigration announcements, including what’s been done already, what might happen in the next few months, and what might take longer to happen, if it ever does.
