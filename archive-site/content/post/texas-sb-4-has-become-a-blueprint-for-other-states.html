CNN reports on the Iowa legislature's passage of a bill that would allow local police to arrest undocumented immigrants and give state judges the power to order deportations. The Iowa governor signed it. The law is scheduled to go into effect on July 1,”

Iowa and Tennessee are among the states that recently have weighed restrictions mirroring portions of the Texas immigration law SB 4. Proposals are still pending in some states. In others, they’ve failed to pass.
