As shown in this NBC News report, President Barack Obama speaking to a group in Santa Monica, California on Thursday blamed the Republican Party for being short-sighted on immigration.  "If they were thinking long-term politically, it is suicide for them not to do this because the demographics of the country are such where you are going to lose an entire generation of immigrants who are looking around and saying, 'You know what, that party does not seem to care much about me and my life,' " Obama said.

Obama's town hall was at Cross Campus, a place that brings together people who have launched startup companies and venture capitalists.
