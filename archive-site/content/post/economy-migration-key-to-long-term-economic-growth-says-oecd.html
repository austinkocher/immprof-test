--- title: "Economy: migration key to long-term economic growth, says OECD" date: 2010-07-12T10:18:28 url: "/post/economy-migration-key-to-long-term-economic-growth-says-oecd/" aliases: ["/immigration/2010/07/economy-migration-key-to-long-term-economic-growth-says-oecd.html"] type: post year: "2010" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

International migration has fallen during the economic crisis, but as the recovery moves into gear migrants will once again be needed to fill labour and skill shortages, according to a new OECD report. The OECD’s International Migration Outlook 2010 says that the inflow of immigrants to OECD countries fell by about 6% in 2008 to 4.4 million people, reversing five years of average annual increases of 11%. Recent national data suggest migration numbers fell further in 2009.
