The Trump administration's public charge rule continues to garner attention.  We posted yesterday about the rule's impacts on lawful use of public benefits by immigrants.  The court challenges to the rule have not ended yet.

The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit will rehear en banc a split panel's ruling that allowed the Trump administration to implement the new public charge rule.   Order attached | Read full article from Law360.

In August, the Fourth Circuit, in an opinion by Judge Harvie Wilkinson, had lifted an injunction barring implementation of the Trump administration's public charge rule.
