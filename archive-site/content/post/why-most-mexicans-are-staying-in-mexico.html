Damien Cave reports tht the latest data shows that migration from Mexico to the United States has reduced to a "trickle."  He writes that "A growing body of evidence suggests a mix of developments — expanding economic and educational opportunities, border crime and shrinking families — are suppressing illegal traffic as much as economic slowdowns or immigrant crackdowns in the United States."
