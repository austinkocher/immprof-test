Immigrants are more willing to enlist in the military than native-born Americans, reports Patrick Tucker of Defense One. A recent study indicates that immigrants also are more inclined toward public service.  Immigrants thus may be the answer to military recruitment struggles.

60 percent of U.S. respondents expressed a willingness to fight for their country, and immigrants were nearly 30 percent more likely to do so than native-born citizens, according to a study highlighted in Armed Forces & Society.
