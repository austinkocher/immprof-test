Today, California Governor Jerry Brown sent a letter to President Trump in support of maintaining the Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA). The states of Texas, Louisiana, Alabama, Nebraska, South Carolina, Tennessee, Arkansas, Idaho, West Virginia and Kansas sent a letter to the President threatening litigation if his administration does not begin phasing out DACA by September.  The Governor is urging the President to defend the program and work with Congress to pass the DREAM Act of 2017.  Here is Governor Brown's letter.  Download Governor Brown's Letter to President Trump on DACA
