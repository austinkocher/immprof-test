--- title: "ICE Deportations: More Than 46,000 Deportees had U.S. Citizen Child" date: 2012-04-02T07:42:54 url: "/post/ice-deportations-more-than-46000-deportees-had-us-citizen-child-/" aliases: ["/immigration/2012/04/ice-deportations-more-than-46000-deportees-had-us-citizen-child-.html"] type: post year: "2012" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) in the first half of 2011 deported 46,686 parents who had at least one U.S. citizen child, says a report made public by ICE on Monday.
