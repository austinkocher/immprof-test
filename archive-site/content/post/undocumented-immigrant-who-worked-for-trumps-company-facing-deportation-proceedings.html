David A. Fahrenthold and Joshua Partlow for the Washington Post report the following:

"An undocumented immigrant who worked for President Trump’s private company — and then spoke publicly about Trump’s longtime reliance on undocumented workers — is facing deportation proceedings, her attorney said Thursday.
 
Victorina Morales, 47, had asked the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services to grant her asylum because of violence in her home country of Guatemala."
