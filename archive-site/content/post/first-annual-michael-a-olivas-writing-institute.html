We recently lost immigration professor legend Michael Olivas.  His friends and colleagues are honoring him appropriately.

First Annual Michael A. Olivas Writing Institute, July 21-22, 2022



 


The First Annual Michael A. Olivas Writing Institute is organized in loving tribute to and honor of Professor Emeritus Michael A. Olivas by Professor Ediberto Roman, Florida International University College of Law;  and Professor Emile Loza de Siles, University of Hawai’i at Mānoa William S. Richardson School of Law (Summer 2022).

 This First Annual Michael A. Olivas Writing Institute will be held virtually by Zoom.  Registration is free.  To participate, please register here.  The Program details are below.  All times are Eastern.  

 The Institute is sponsored by the Olivas Faculty Recruitment Initiative,.  Photographs, Houston Chronicle & University of Houston Law Center.
