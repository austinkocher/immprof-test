Alligator Alcatraz.  Speedway Slammer.  Now Deportation Depot!   I think the first two names are catchier.  What about Deportation Home Depot, given all the enforcement operations at Home Depot stores?  See also this breaking story.  Person struck by car after running from immigration operation at Monrovia Home Depot

The South Florida Sun-Sentinel reports that Florida Governor Ron DeSantis’ administration is planning to open the state’s second immigration detention center at an empty state prison.  A court is considering challenges to the first, the Alligator Alcatraz facility.

The new center at the Baker Correctional Institution will hold more than 1,300 detainees, Governor DeSantis said.  "State officials have dubbed the new facility the `Deportation Depot.'”
