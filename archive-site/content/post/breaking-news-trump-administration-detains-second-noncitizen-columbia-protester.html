Earlier this week, the Trump arrested a Columbia University protester and placed him in removal proceedings.  The President promised more arrests.

Breaking news from Newsweek:  "Immigration officials have arrested a second participant in pro-Palestinian protests at Columbia University and have revoked the visa of another student, the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) announced [today]."  A Palestinian from the West Bank reportedly was arrested for overstaying her student visa.  "Her visa was terminated in January 2022 due to lack of attendance, and she had previously been arrested for her involvement in Columbia protests in April 2024, according to the department."

The Trump administration also revoked the visa of an Indian citizen and doctoral student on March 5 for "advocating for violence and terrorism." He decided to leave the country, according to the DHS.
