Scholars are invited to submit a 300-word abstract for the Eighth Interdisciplinary Conference on Human Trafficking at the University of Nebraska. The conference will take place from Thursday, October 6 through Saturday, October 8 in Lincoln, Nebraska. Anyone who has academic or professional work to present should submit an abstract of up to 300 words, plus answer additional questions, on the submission website by April 15.

The presentations will normally be 25 minutes with 15 minutes for discussion. The organizing committee is willing to consider other formats, such as panel presentations or poster presentations. We are not seeking workshops, however, but presentations of facts, knowledge, ideas, theories, on-the-ground approaches, methods, program evaluations, research agendas, and research needs. Information on the conference, submission of abstracts, registration, dates, and venue are available here.
