On June 27, 2017, the University of Houston Law Center celebrated the 35th anniversary of the Supreme Court decision in Plyler v. Doe (1982) with a panel discussion hosted by the Institute for Higher Education Law & Governance and the Mexican American Legal Defense and Educational Fund.  Watch the discussion on the video above. 

In Plyer v. Doe, the U.S. Supreme Court invalidated a Texas law that effectively barred undocumented students from the public K-12 schools.

 

Peter Roos, left, Thomas Saenz, Vilma Martinez and University of Houston Law Center Professor Michael A. Olivas, right, analyzed the implications of Plyler v. Doe during a panel discussion.  Photo courtesy of the University of Houston Law Center.
