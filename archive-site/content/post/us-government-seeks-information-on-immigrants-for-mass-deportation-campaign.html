The Justice Department has requested lists of all noncitizen prisoners being held in California jails.  California Attorney Gen. Rob Bonta's office immediately questioned the legality of the demands and said it would review the directive and monitoring its implementation "for compliance with the law."

In addition, Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) will have access to Medicaid records.  ICE reportedly plans to “receive identity and location information on aliens identified by ICE,” according to an agreement obtained by The Associated Press.  According to the Los Angeles Times, "[w]hile immigrants who have entered and remained in the U.S. illegally are not eligible for Medicaid, every state is required to provide emergency Medicaid coverage for life-saving situations. Some states also make exceptions for children and certain adults. . . . That means, despite not qualifying for the federally funded coverage, some noncitizens’ information is still available in Medicaid records."
