A few days ago, the White House leaked a list of immigration demands before relief will be granted to the recipients of DACA, which President Trump recently rescinded.

Miller Smite on the Daily Beast reports how, for weeks, top officials to the president have been working behind the scenes to upend a DACA deal that President Trump had been struck with Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer and House Minority Leader Nancy Pelosi. And spearheading the campaign of behind-the-scenes impairment was White House senior policy adviser Stephen Miller.
