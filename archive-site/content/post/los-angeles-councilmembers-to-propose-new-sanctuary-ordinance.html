While Florida Governor Ron DeSantis pushes the immigration enforcement lever, California continues to move in a different direction.   The latest news from Los Angeles is that city councilmembers are expected to announce a proposal for a new sanctuary city law.  Councilmembers Eunisses Hernandez, Nithya Raman and Hugo Soto-Martinez will host a press conference on March 7, along with immigrant rights' advocates announcing a proposal "to direct the city to report back" on an ordinance that would establish L.A. as a "sanctuary city."  The ordinance would also prohibit any city resources, property or personnel from being used for any federal immigration enforcement.
