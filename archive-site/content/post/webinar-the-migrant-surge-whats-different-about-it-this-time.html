Webinar registration:

A conversation between Muzaffar Chishti, senior fellow at the Migration Policy Institute, and Stephen Yale-Loehr, Professor of Immigration Law Practice, Cornell Law School. Join Mr. Chishti and Professor Yale-Loehr as they discuss the history of migration to the United States, the current migrant surge at the border, the impact on cities and states beyond the border, and possible impacts on federal immigration policy.

The event is on November 7, 2023 from 12:15 p.m. to 1:15 p.m. EST at Myron Taylor Hall G85 of Cornell Law School.
