President Joe Biden said in an interview with ABC News' George Stephanopoulos yesterday that his message to migrants is "Don't come over."

Amid concerns with migrants and unaccompanied minors on the U.S. southern border, Stephanopoulos asked the president, "Do you have to say quite clearly, 'Don't come'?" "Yes, I can say quite clearly: Don't come over," Biden said.
