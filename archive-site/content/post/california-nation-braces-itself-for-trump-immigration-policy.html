The immigrant community in the United States is on figurative pins-and-needles as speculation abounds about how, and if, the Trump administration might implement the campaign promises of Donald Trump with respect to immigration law and enforcement.  We will have to wait and see.  For even-handed analysis of the potential impacts of a Trump administration's immigration policies on California, see this report by Chris Nichols on Capital Public Radio.  I offer some thoughts in this interview with Steve Magagnini of the Sacramento Bee.
