One might have thought that the Obama Administration's Secure Communities, which some states are now fighting tooth and nail, could not become more controversial.  But now Huffington Post reports that documents released on Wednesday reveal more details about how the government planned to roll out its Secure Communities immigration-enforcement plan and confirm that the FBI plans to implement additional security programs in the next five years -- whether or not states consent to participate. The documents show the FBI was more involved than originally known in the decision to make Secure Communities effectively mandatory.
