Jill Family (Widener) reports from the ABA Annual Meeting in Toronto that yesterday the American Bar Association adopted a Resolution supporting the elimination of the post-departure bar on filing motions to reopen or to reconsider removal proceedings in 8 C.F. R. § 1003.2(d). The Immigration Committee of the Administrative Law Section drafted the Resolution, which won the support of the Administrative Law Section and then was adopted by the ABA House of Delegates. Jill, Dan Kanstroom (Boston College) and Rachel Rosenbloom (Northeastern) coordinated the effort.

Click here for links to Resolution (104a), as well as Resolutions 103a-d and 118. Resolution 118 calls for improved access to immigration counsel, and Resolutions 103a-d address a variety of issues calling for the protection of immigrant children.
