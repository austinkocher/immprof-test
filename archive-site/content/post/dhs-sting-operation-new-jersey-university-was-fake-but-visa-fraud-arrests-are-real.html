Liz Robbins of the New York Times reports on a student visa sting operation conducted by the Department of Homeland Security. 

On the surface, the University of Northern New Jersey seemed legitimate. It had a website, with a seal featuring the Latin words “Humanus, Scientia, Integritas,” a list of business-oriented degrees offered and a promise of “an exceptional educational experience.” However, the university was a fake, set up by the DHS as part of a sting operation to ensnare criminals involved in student visa fraud. Yesterday, the operation resulted in the arrests of 21 people, the United States attorney for New Jersey and the director of United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement, announced at a news conference.
