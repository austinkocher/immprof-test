A coalition of nonprofit groups have submitted a letter o the U.N. Committee on Enforced Disappearances and the U.N. Working Group on Enforced or Involuntary Disappearances regarding troubling DHS practices in relation to the treatment of migrants.

A copy of the letter is available here. Groups signing onto the letter include Haitian Bridge Alliance, Americans for Immigrant Justice, USCRI, RAICES, Human Rights First, and the Florence Project.

The letter documents how migrants, many of whom are asylum seekers, are regularly subjected to short-term enforced disappearances by various DHS policies—including in the context of pushing back or returning migrants to Mexico in the Migrant Protection Protocols program, also known as Remain in Mexico.
