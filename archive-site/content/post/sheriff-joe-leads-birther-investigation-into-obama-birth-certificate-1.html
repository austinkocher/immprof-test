Maricopa County, Arizona Sheriff Joe Arpaio is in the news again. It appears that Sheriff Arpaio is now a birther. Last Thursday, he unveiled preliminary results of an investigation conducted by members of his volunteer task force into the authenticity of President Barack Obama's birth certificate. Arpaio, whose office was chastised in December by the U.S. government for violating the civil rights of Latinos and immigrants, said the probe revealed that there was "probable cause" to believe Obama's birth certificate is a forgery. He also said the selective service card completed by Obama in 1980 also was most likely a forgery.
