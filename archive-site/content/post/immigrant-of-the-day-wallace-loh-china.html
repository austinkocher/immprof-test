Wallace D. Loh became the 33rd president of the University of Maryland on Novmber 1, 2010.

Prior to coming to Maryland, Dr. Loh served as executive vice president and provost at the University of Iowa. Before joining the University of Iowa, he served as dean of arts and sciences at Seattle University; chief policy adviser to Governor Gary Locke of the State of Washington; vice chancellor for academic affairs and dean of faculties at the University of Colorado-Boulder; and dean of the University of Washington Law School.

Dr. Loh has been a professor of law at the University of Washington, Vanderbilt University, Emory University, University of Houston, Cornell University, and Beijing University. His teaching and scholarship are in the areas of criminal justice and law and social science. He was elected president of the Association of American Law Schools.

Born in Shanghai, China, he immigrated with his family to Lima, Peru, and graduated from high school there. He holds a J.D. from Yale Law School, a Ph.D. from the University of Michigan, an M.A. in from Cornell University, and a B.A. from Grinnell College.
