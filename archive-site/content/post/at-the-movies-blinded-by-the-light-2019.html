For all you Bruce Springsteen fans doubling as immprofs out there, Blinded by the Light is a movie you might want to check out. Here's the trailer. Directed by Gurinder Chadha, Blinded by the Light takes place in the 1980s in a small town in England. It features a high-school age boy, Javed, a son of immigrants from Pakistan who wants to become a writer and falls in love with Springsteen's music along the way. It explores many important themes, including the anti-immigrant and racist treatment of Javed and his family and the harsh economic impact of Thatcher's policies on working people. With a predictable large dose of Springsteen music throughout, Blinded by the Light also underscores the universal themes in Springsteen's powerful lyrics that find meaning across borders and in different contexts.
