Thomas Lee writes about how immigrants helped make the economic engine known as Silicon Valley. As he puts it, "Silicon Valley and America owe much of their success not just to capital flow and big-name universities but also to the country’s ability to absorb immigrants into the workforce."

That the United States continues to hold such a competitive advantage is a big reason why Silicon Valley has remained successful, according to LinkedIn co-founder and Chairman Reid Hoffman.
