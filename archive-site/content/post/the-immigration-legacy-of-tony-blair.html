The United Kingdom's Prime Minister from 1997-2007, Tony Blair, left behind an immigration system that has been fundamentally reshaped. As Migration Policy Institute's Will Somerville explains, policies under Blair "managed" migration to favor migrants coming for work and study and will have long-term positive impacts on the UK.
