Official Portrait of U.S. Department of Homeland Security Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas

The news is out.   Late yesterday, House Republicans reversed last week's failed vote to impeach Homeland Security Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas.   The House voted 214-213 in favor of a resolution to impeach Mayorkas. It failed by a 214-216 vote last week.  The resolution accuses the secretary of ignoring the immigration laws and making "false statements" about the U.S./Mexico border. 

Mayorkas is the first U.S. Cabinet secretary to be impeached since 1876. He will face an impeachment trial in the U.S. Senate.

In an election year, an impeachment trial focused on the U.S./Mexico border will no doubt be explosive.

President Biden has condemned the impeachment.  Many others have as well.

 

Congressional Hispanic Caucus (CHC) Chair Nanette Barragán (CA-44) and CHC Leadership released the following statement in response to the impeachment of Mayorkas:

“House Republicans’ impeachment of Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas is another political stunt. This impeachment of the Secretary is not about his failure to secure the border. House Republicans have no interest in securing the border. They have said it themselves.

Republicans refuse to fund the resources requested by Secretary Mayorkas and the Biden-Harris Administration to address challenges at the Southern Border.  They do not want to do anything to help, because they want to campaign on the issue. House MAGA extremists can't have it both ways. 

This MAGA fabricated impeachment failed to show any evidence of a high crime or misdemeanor by Secretary Mayorkas. The impeachment of Secretary Mayorkas creates a dangerous precedent where cabinet members will be targeted by Congress over policy disagreements.  We are confident the Senate will agree and end this sham impeachment. 

The Congressional Hispanic Caucus stands ready to work with any member, regardless of party, on legal pathways for those already here paired with actual solutions to address our challenges at the Southern Border.”

 

 

The National Association of Latino Elected and Appointed Officials (NALEO) Educational Fund released the following statement from Chief Executive Officer Arturo Vargas after the U.S. House of Representatives vote to impeach Mayorkas:

“NALEO Educational Fund is disappointed in the House’s actions to impeach Secretary Mayorkas. This effort distracts Congress from carrying out the actual work our nation needs, including agreeing on a federal budget, averting a government shutdown, and addressing a wide range of issues affecting the well-being of Latinos and all Americans.

“This impeachment also undermines the bipartisan collaboration required to craft legislation that would ultimately achieve immigration reform, a goal envisioned by the NALEO and NALEO Educational Fund Boards and articulated in our principles for immigration reform. These principles call for a sound and humane approach to immigration enforcement and policies that would provide a path to U.S. citizenship for certain immigrants and their families. 

“It is deeply disillusioning that members of Congress chose to use valuable time and resources on a futile impeachment effort instead of coming together to pursue bipartisan, practical, and effective solutions to the wide range of issues facing the nation — including immigration. NALEO Educational Fund calls on the Senate to quickly resolve and end this effort to remove Secretary Mayorkas from office.”
