President Trump and Vice President Pence today issued a statement on potential immigration legislation.  The bottom line from the White House:

"[O]ur position has been clear and very clear from the beginning.  Any legislation on DACA must secure the border with a wall.  It must give our immigration officers the resources they need to stop illegal immigration and also to stop visa overstays.  And, crucially, the legislation must end chain migration.  It must end the visa lottery.  Dangerous.  And I think many of the Democrats agree with us on that now."
