LatinoJustice is seeking to hire an attorney with 5 to 10 years of post-bar litigation experience for our Southeast Regional Office in Orlando, Florida.  Please forward this posting to your local networks and contacts. And don’t hesitate to contact me about any candidates who might be interested in the position.  
 
The ideal candidate is bilingual (ENG/SPA), with a record of commitment to social justice work, and interested in joining a group of dedicated professionals in support of advancing the civil rights of the Latino community in the United States.
 
We need your assistance in disseminating the job posting far and wide, so please share the enclosed position announcement within your network, or refer them to our website here.
