Systemic racism is embedded in the U.S. immigration laws and their enforcement.   Immigration Courtside highlights a Danae King USA Today article ('Guilty until proven innocent'? Advocates say Black immigrants face racial bias in court) (subscription required) that the deck is stacked against Black immigrants in the U.S. immigration courts.   For example, the report points out that, in 2020, asylum seekers from Africa were deemed not credible in 8.5% of interviews, over 37% more often than, on average, for all nationalities that year.  This data is based on the August 2022 U.S. Shadow Report to the United Nations Committee on the Elimination of Racial Discrimination.
