Former Texas Governor George W Bush ran for President as a moderate on immigration. In many respects, his administration's immigration policies were more even-handed than those of the Clinton administration. President Bush's deportations have been dwarfed by those of the Obama administration, which set another record (almost 4000,000) last year.

If the pundits have it right, another Texas Govenor may vie for the Presidency. Benjy Sarlin on TPM writes an interesting story about how Gov. Rick Perry attempting to balance the two immigration wings of the Republican Party, may have irritated everyone, including Latino voters, immigration moderates, and immigration hawks.

 

 

UPDATE Huffington Post has a story about how Perry may not be nativist enough for the Tom Tancredo wing of the Republican Party as well as Tea Party activists.
