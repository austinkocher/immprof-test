Through executive orders, harsh rhetoric, and dramatic action, the Trump administration has cracked down on undocumented immigration.  Now it appears that the administration will seek to reduce legal immigration.  Earlier this week, the administration put on hold a program for the admission of immigrant entrepreneurs.  The administration also floated possible restrictions on foreign students.

There are many reports, including from POLITICO and Breitbart, that the Trump administration, even as President Trump and the entire White House is embroiled in Russiagate, is working with two Republican senators to dramatically scale back legal immigration.

The plan is for President Trump to champion an immigration bill being introduced later this summer by GOP Senators Tom Cotton of Arkansas and David Perdue of Georgia.  The bill reportedly would "slash in half the number of legal immigrants entering the country each year . . . .  Currently, about 1 million legal immigrants enter the country annually; that number would fall to 500,000 over the next decade."
