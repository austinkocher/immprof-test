--- title: "Citizenship delays imperil voting for hundreds of thousands of immigrants in the 2020 election" date: 2020-09-03T16:15:22 url: "/post/citizenship-delays-imperil-voting-for-hundreds-of-thousands-of-immigrants-in-the-2020-election/" aliases: ["/immigration/2020/09/citizenship-delays-imperil-voting-for-hundreds-of-thousands-of-immigrants-in-the-2020-election.html"] type: post year: "2020" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

ImmigrationProf blogger Ming Hsu Chen on The Conversation explains how "[t]he long wait for naturalized citizenship imperils those rights for a growing number of immigrants."  The delays loom ominously over the 2020 election.  The article covers steps that "could overcome barriers to naturalization and voting rights in the time remaining before the election."
