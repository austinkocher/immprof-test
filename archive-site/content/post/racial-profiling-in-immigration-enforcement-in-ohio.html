Records Shed Light on Border Patrol’s Racial Profiling of Immigrants in Ohio

 

Government data exposes several concerning enforcement practices employed by U.S. Border Patrol agents at the Sandusky Bay station to detain immigrants in northern Ohio. The information released today by the American Immigration Council and Advocates for Basic Legal Equality (ABLE) confirms reports by local community organizations of widespread use of racial profiling and problematic apprehension practices by both  the Border Patrol and local law enforcement. The report calls for greater transparency and oversight of Border Patrol’s enforcement activities near the nation’s northern border.
