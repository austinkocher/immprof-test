Here is an upcoming webinar:

National Immigrant Womens Advocacy Project

Free National Webinar

VAWA Confidentiality Protections for Immigrant Survivors: Myths, Fact, and Best Practices
 
 

Thursday - January 30, 2025,   9:30 am - 11am PT / 12:30 pm - 2pm ET

This national webinar will provide judges, advocates, and attorneys with up-to-date information about VAWA confidentiality protections for immigrant survivors of domestic violence, sexual violence, and other crimes. Participants will learn to identify which victims qualify for VAWA confidentiality protections and when these protections apply. Faculty will address common myths and facts about how VAWA confidentiality protects victims from removal and ensures protection at specific locations, including courthouses. The webinar will also cover best practices for ruling on or preparing for state court discovery requests that seek information protected under VAWA confidentiality. Participants will receive links to statutes, government policies and regulations, legislative history, and case law related to VAWA confidentiality.

Faculty

Edna Yang, Immigration Attorney, American Gateways

Leslye E. Orloff, Adjunct Professor and Director, NIWAP, American University Washington College of Law

Judge Lora Livingston, Senior Judge, State of Texas Austin, Texas
