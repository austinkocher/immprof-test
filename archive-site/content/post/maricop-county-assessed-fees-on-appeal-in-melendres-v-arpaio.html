Law 360 (registration required) reports that the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit last week ordered Arizona’s Maricopa County to pay $400,000 in attorneys' fees and expenses that class counsel spent on the appeal of its case accusing the Sheriff's Office, headed until recently by Sheriff Joe Arpaio, of racial profiling.  Maricopa County paid millions of dollars in fees to defend the civil rights case.

In Melendres v. Arpaio, the Ninth Circuit affirmed a district court finding that the Maricopa County Sheriff's Office had engaged in a pattern and practice of discrimination against Latinos in the name of immigration and criminal law enforcement. Sheriff Arpaio was later charged with criminal contempt for violating court orders in the case.

Arpaio lost his bid for re-election last November and new Sheriff Paul Penzone is changing his tough-on-immigration policies.
