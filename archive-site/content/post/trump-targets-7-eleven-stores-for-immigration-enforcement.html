There have been rumblings that the Trump administration would step up workplace enforcement of the immigration laws.  Now, immigration enforcement officers are making 7-Eleven stores a target in their crackdown on employers who hire undocumented immigrants.

CNN reports that agents with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement today descended on 98 7-Eleven franchise stores across the country, where they began audits and interviewed employees and managers, according to the agency.
