Question: Can you name the two foreign-born directors who have each won two Best Director Oscars, but neither of whom have had their films win Best Picture?

Answer: Mexico’s Alfonso Cuaron ("Roma" and "Gravity") and Taiwan’s Ang Lee ("Life of Pi" and "Brokeback Mountain").

This was the final jeopardy question during Thursday's Greatest of All Time episode. Not one of the contestants got it right!
