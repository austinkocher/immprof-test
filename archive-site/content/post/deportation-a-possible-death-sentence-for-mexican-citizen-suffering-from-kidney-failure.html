Here is a heart-wrenching story of a Mexican national, Ignacio Viera Mesa, who lived in the United States for 26 years but had criminal drug convictions and previous deportations. On Friday, Mesa was removed to Mexico. He suffers from end-stage kidney failure and will be unable to obtain the medical treatment, including dialysis three times a week, that he needs in Mexico. It is uncertain how long he will live there.

Students from the UC Davis Immigration Law Clinic fought hard to stave off the deportation.
