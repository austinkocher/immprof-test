The Leadership Conference on Civil and Human Rights, along with 129 groups, today issued a solidarity statement to stand with Asian American and immigrant communities confronting a spike in COVID-19 related hate incidents. The groups also call on local, state, and national leaders to join them in condemning the hate and bigotry that threatens to divide us, and to ensure that communities have the support and resources they need to combat this rise in hate and xenophobia.
