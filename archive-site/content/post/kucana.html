On remand from the Supreme Court, the Seventh Circuit, in an opinion by Chief Judge Easterbrook (Download XE1FFXOP[1]), denied the petition to review the denial of a motion to reopen in Kucana v. Holder. The court ruled that "The only question open today is whether the Board [of Immigration Appeals] abused its discretion when concluding that the evidence did not show a material adverse change in country conditions between 2002 and 2006. That decision did not constitute an abuse of discretion, so the petition for review is DENIED."
