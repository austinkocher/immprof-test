At the end of the 2021 Term, the Supreme Court handed the Biden administration in the Remain in Mexico case and given a green light to dismantle the policy.   SABRINA RODRIGUEZ for Politico writes that, although "having previously moved quickly to end the `Remain in Mexico' policy, the [Biden] administration has suddenly decided to take its time.

The White House and Department of Homeland Security have been mum on their plans following the Supreme Court’s ruling. Immigration advocates asking about next steps have been met with a similar silence. In that void, a question has emerged: What, exactly, is the hold up?"
