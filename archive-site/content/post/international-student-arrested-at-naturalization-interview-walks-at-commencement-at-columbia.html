Mohsen Mahdawi, who the Trump administration placed in custody when he appeared at his naturalization interview, "formally received his degree in philosophy in October, painted a scale of justice in gold on his graduation cap [for commencement ceremonies at Columbia], which was also adorned with symbols of refugees, hope and love," according to Gothamist.

A lawful permanent resident, Mahdawi is among several pro-Palestinian university students detained by the Trump administration and put into removal proceedings on grounds that their views amounted to antisemitism and were harmful to U.S. foreign policy.
