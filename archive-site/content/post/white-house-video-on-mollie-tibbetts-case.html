The heartbreaking case of Mollie Tibbetts continues to make the news.  Kit Johnson has placed the tragic case in some perspective.  

The White House in  the video above tries to capitalize on the Tibbetts' case by focusing on "family separation" of parents who lost their children due to the crimes of undocumented immigrants, a reference to the now-abandoned Trump policy of separating migrant families in detention.
