INTERSECTIONS: Art and Law at the Border—a bi-national symposium exploring the intersection of art, law, and activism. Details below!
 
California Lawyers for the Arts is proud to present the seventh annual INTERSECTIONS: Art and Law at the Border—a bi-national symposium exploring the intersection of art, law, and activism within the unique geopolitical landscape of the San Diego–Tijuana border region.

This year, we focus on the evolving legal realities impacting artists, humanitarian workers, and border communities. Join us for a day of powerful conversations, legal workshops, and cross-border exchange featuring artists, lawyers, scholars, and organizers from both sides of the border. We hope you will join us and help spread the word! 

📍 The Front Gallery, San Ysidro
🗓️ Saturday June 14th, 2025
🎧 Live English–Spanish interpretation provided
