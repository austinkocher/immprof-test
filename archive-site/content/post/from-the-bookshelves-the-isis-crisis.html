The Islamic State of Iraq and al-Sham (ISIS) has metastasized throughout the Middle East, taking great swathes of territory into its self-proclaimed caliphate. The group’s expansion has resulted in widespread bloodshed, exacerbated a refugee crisis, and dominated the U.S. national security agenda.

The ISIS Crisis, the latest Foreign Affairs ebook, offers a broad range of analysis on the group and considers the real questions worthy of debate: What does ISIS want? How great a threat does it pose, and to whom? How can it be stopped?
