Last week, the Trump administration threatened state and local officials who "obstruct" federal immigration enforcement measures.  In The Conversation ("Federal threats against local officials who don’t cooperate with immigration orders could be unconstitutional − Justice Antonin Scalia ruled against similar plans"), Claire B. Wofford questions whether that would be constitutional.  "If a federal prosecutor were to put a city’s mayor or a state’s governor in jail, what may be the greatest source of conflict in the Constitution will become clear to the public."
