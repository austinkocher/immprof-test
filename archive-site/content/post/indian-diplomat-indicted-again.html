Just when she thought she was safe!  Yesterday it was reported that charges against an Indian diplomat for visa fraud had been dismissed.  Now, CNN reports that she has again been indicted by the U.S. government and faces arrest if she returns to the United States.  In an indictment filed today, Devyani Khobragade faces one count of visa fraud and one count of making false statements for allegedly lying on a visa application about how much she paid her housekeeper. An arrest warrant reporetedly has been issued.
