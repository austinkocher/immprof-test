In the wake of a federal court finding that his Maricopa County Sheriff's Office had engaged in a pattern and practice of racial profiling of Latinos in the name of immigration enforcement, controversial Sheriff Joe Arpaio has "temporarily" suspended all his immigration efforts, an Arpaio spokesman told The Associated Press.  This development -- which is a long time coming --  should bring some relief to Latinos in Maricopa County, Arizona.
