The saga of Kilmar Abrego Garcia, who the U.S. government wrongfully deported to El Salvador, is not going away and will be a topic of scholarly commentary for years to come.   The Supreme Court has ordered the Trump administration to facilitate his Abrego's return to the United States. 

In the latest chapter, in an exclusive interview with ABC News to mark his 100th day in office, President Donald Trump yesterday said he “could” secure the return of Kilmar Abrego Garcia, who currently is in a maximum security prison in El Salvador.
