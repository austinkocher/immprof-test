The title of Ezra Klein's latest podcast is deceptive -- "Trump is Building His Own Paramilitary Force." I mean, sure, he talks about the militarization of LA and DC, but the real focus is immigration. His guest for the episode is journalist Radley Balko, and their conversation is wide-ranging and fascinating. 

One thing the two discuss is Balko'ssubstack, The Watch, and his reporting from April 2025: The courage to be decent. It's about an attorney who was asked to provide pro bono representation to a family with their immigration issues. When doing some relevant research, he got a visit from federal agents who did not identify themselves or their agency but who simply said: "We have information that you are obstructing an ongoing immigration investigation." Like any self-respecting attorney, this guy immediately declined to talk and lawyered up. The substack goes on to talk about other intimidation tactics against attorneys that we're more familiar with--the targeting of law firms themselves to discourage immigration-related pro bono work.

All-in-all, an interesting listen. Thanks, immprof Liz Keyes (Baltimore) for the tip!
