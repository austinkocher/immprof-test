In this Analysis, Carrie Rosenbaum explains Lozano v. City of Hazleton, 2010 U.S. App. LEXIS 18835 (3d Cir. Pa. Sept. 9, 2010), the long-awaited Third Circuit decision on the City of Hazleton's controversial ordinances to combat employment of and rentals to noncitizens not authorized to work or to live in the United States. The decision raises issues of federal preemption (express, field, and conflict), and it may foreshadow the U.S. Supreme Court case on an Arizona law, which will be argued in the 2010 Term.
