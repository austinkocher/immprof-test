Yale Law School seeks applicants for a clinical fellowship in the Worker & Immigrant Rights Advocacy Clinic. Responsibilities include representing clients, supervising students, assisting in teaching classes, and pursuing a scholarship agenda.

To apply, email a resume, cover letter, writing sample, and names, addresses and telephone numbers of three references to Osikhena Awudu, Assistant Director of Clinical Operations, The Jerome N. Frank Legal Services Organization, clinical.fellow@yale.edu. Applications will be accepted until March 1, 2024 but will be reviewed on a rolling basis (early applications encouraged).
