Today the Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR) announced 39 new immigration judges.

These new judges will serve in immigration courts in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Louisiana, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, New Jersey, New York, Ohio, Texas, Virginia, and Washington.
