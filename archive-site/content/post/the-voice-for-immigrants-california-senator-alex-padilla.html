Official Senate Picture

As public opinion on immigration appears to be leaning right, one voice speaks up for immigrants.  Stephen Groves for the Associated Press profiled California Senator Alex Padilla, the senior senator of California.  Senator Padilla "has taken the opportunity — from face-to-face moments with the president to regular calls with top White House staff and sometimes outspoken criticism — to put his stamp on the Democratic Party’s approach to immigration. . . . The son of Mexican immigrants and first Latino to represent his state in the Senate, Padilla has emerged as a persistent force at a time when Democrats are increasingly focused on border security and the country’s posture toward immigrants is uncertain."

"Padilla, in a series of interviews with The Associated Press, expressed a deep reserve of optimism about his party’s ability to win support both from and for immigrant communities."
