Maria Hinojosa on Latino USA looks in depth (almost 52 minutes) at the changes in the lives of recipients of Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals relief (as well as immigrants generally) under President Trump.  Besides explaining the concrete changes for hundreds of thousands of people, the report follows a Mexican immigrant -- and DACA recipient -- as he goes through his check-in with ICE and is asked to buy his own plane ticket to get deported.  The story offers perspectives on immigrant detention, immigration court proceedings, and navigating the complex immigration bureaucracy (often without a lawyer).
