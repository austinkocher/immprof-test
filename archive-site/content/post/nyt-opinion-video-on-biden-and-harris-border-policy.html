Interesting NYT Opinion Video by By Alexander Stockton is available today on the NYT webpage covering border enforcement.

Stockton argues that Biden and Harris have transformed the border. He says: "Despite sounding pro-immigration in public, behind closed doors they were fulfilling many conservative aspirations."

The video traces three major steps taken by the administration to secure these results. First, they encouraged policy changes in other countries that have stemmed the flow of migrants to the U.S. In addition, the opinion video discusses how new asylum restrictions imposed by the Biden administration have drastically restricted access to asylum. Finally, they created more legal pathways, with the goal of discouraging more people from coming illegally.
