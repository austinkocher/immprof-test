Oregon’s Immigrant Justice Fellowship provides new and emerging lawyers with a fully funded position at an Oregon nonprofit to provide universal representation services through Equity Corps of Oregon. Through a competitive process, up to 6 new and emerging attorneys are selected each year for one-year, renewable, fellowships.  Click the link above to see application detail;s.
