U.S. Senate Photo of Senator Joe Manchin (D-W VA)

A key Democrat supports immigration reform through the budget reconciliation process, which has some support.  The Hill reports that U.S. Senate Photo of Senator Joe Manchin (D-W VA), a key moderate, yesterday said he supports including immigration provisions in a budget reconciliation bill, easing the path toward resolution on an issue that threatened to divide Democrats.

"I'm a 2013 immigration supporter. You can look at the 2013 bill and I thought that was a great bill. If we had that bill then, we wouldn't have the problems we'd have today," he said, referring to a comprehensive immigration reform bill that passed the Senate in 2013 but was never taken up by the House.
