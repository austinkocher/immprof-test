The Immigration Lawyers' Podcast by immigration attorney John Khosravi has a wide array of interesting conversations with leading practitioners in the field of immigration law. It covers topics ranging from career advice on how to obtain a job in the field of immigration field to technical aspects of immigration law and policy. Many members of our immigration law professor community have given terrific interviews discussing their work, including Professors Kari Hong, Shoba Wadhia, and Stephen Yale-Loehr.
