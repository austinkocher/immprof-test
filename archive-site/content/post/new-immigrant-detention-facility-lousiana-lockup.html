The Trump administration has given catchy names for immigrant detention facilities -- Alligator Alcatraz (now defunct), Speedway Slammer, Deportation Depot, Cornhusker Clink.   FOX reports on a new Immigration and Customs Enforcement detention facility dubbed "Louisiana Lockup," part of the notorious Angola Prisons,  that is now open at the notorious Louisiana State Penitentiary to house some of the "worst of the worst" criminal illegal migrants.
