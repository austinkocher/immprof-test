Are you having trouble keeping track of the many, many changes that this second Trump administration is making in the area of immigration law? First of all, you're definitely not alone. If you're looking for a summary resource that will save you from plowing through every news article, check out the NYC Bar's Report on the Trump Administration's 2025 Changes to Immigration Law. At the moment, it's current through June 16, 2025. Fingers crossed that the organization keeps this effort going. It's incredibly useful!
