How to Pack for the “Other Refugee Crisis”: My Diary from Dilley by immprof Claire Thomas is a gripping page turner. I encourage everyone to give it a read. And to consider assigning it in class. It's a vivid portrayal of the challenges of providing adequate legal services to migrants in an isolated detention facility.
