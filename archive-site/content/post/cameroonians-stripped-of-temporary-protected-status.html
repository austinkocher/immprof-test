President Trump has shown that he is not a fan of Temporary Protected Status for citizens of certain designated nations subject to widespread violence and natural disasters.  Among others, Salvadorans and Haitians have been stripped of TPS status.  Bloomberg Law reports that the Department of Homeland Security determined that Cameroon no longer meets the requirements for a TPS designation, according to a Federal Register notice released yesterday. "TPS allows immigrants to stay in the US with work authorization for up to 18 months when conditions like armed conflict make it unsafe to return to their home countries."
