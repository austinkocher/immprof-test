Remember when the Trump administration. changed the mission statement of U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) to remove the phrase "nation of immigrants" and instead focus on "safeguarding its integrity" and "securing the homeland"?

CNN’s Geneva Sands reports that this morning, under the leadership of Director Ur M. Jaddou, USCIS announced a new mission statement that captures the possibility of the United States: "USCIS upholds America’s promise as a nation of welcome and possibility with fairness, integrity, and respect for all we serve."
