Immigration lawyer Jennifer Peyton spent ten days crying in Artesia and five days laughing in Dilley. She has blogged about her experiences here.

Peyton ends with a call to action - for more volunteers to go to Dilley to help with intake, bond reduction hearings, and file management. Those interested can contact Michelle Mendez of CLINIC and Susan Timmons of AILA.
