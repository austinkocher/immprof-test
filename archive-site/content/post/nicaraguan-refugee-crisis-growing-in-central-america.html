 NPR's "All Things Considered" reported on an emerging refugee crisis in Central America.  "Hundreds of Nicaraguans are arriving in Costa Rica daily, fleeing the conflict in their country. The country is straining to provide services to the refugees and undergoing a sense of deja vu back to the days of the Nicaraguan Revolution."
