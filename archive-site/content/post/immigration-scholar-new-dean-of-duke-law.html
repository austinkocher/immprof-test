Kerry Abrams is the new of dean of Duke Law. As the Duke new release states: 

"Abrams’ scholarly work has explored the history of immigration law, observing how courts and legislatures deployed cultural norms of marriage to exclude some racial groups while encouraging the migration of others. Her other work explores contemporary immigration and citizenship law, outlining the conflicting ways in which state family law and federal immigration and citizenship law define marriage and parentage."
