Access to counsel is a fundamental American value. But only 14 percent of detained immigrants have legal representation–at this very moment when due process and the integrity of the immigration courts are under attack.

That is why, in partnership with the American Immigration Lawyers Association (AILA), we have launched the Immigration Justice Campaign, an initiative designed to challenge the due process failures of our immigration court system and bring in new pro bono allies to help serve vulnerable detained immigrants who would otherwise go without a fighting chance. We will amplify this work through national advocacy campaigns and by giving volunteers the tools to speak out directly. Together, our voices for change will be stronger than ever. The Justice Campaign needs your help as we fight for fairness and justice. Whether you are an attorney, social worker, translator, or partner in the immigrant rights movement, you can contribute!

To connect you directly to volunteer opportunities at our local legal service partners, we’ve launched a new website. Join a discussion group with fellow Campaigners, learn more about our advocacy work, and sign up for specific pro bono engagements.
