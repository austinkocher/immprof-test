--- title: "GOP rep: 'Doing nothing' on immigration is the real 'amnesty'" date: 2014-01-09T07:36:48 url: "/post/gop-rep-doing-nothing-on-immigration-is-the-real-amnesty/" aliases: ["/immigration/2014/01/gop-rep-doing-nothing-on-immigration-is-the-real-amnesty.html"] type: post year: "2014" category: "Current Affairs" category_slug: "current-affairs" byline: "Immigration Prof" ---

Fox News reports that Republican Rep. Mario Diaz-Balart (R-Florida) appealed to his colleagues to press forward on immigration reform, saying that "doing nothing is not an alternative" -- and tantamount to "amnesty." Diaz-Balart has been an advocate for immigration reform.  If Congress does not act, the GOP takes the risk of being blamed for not trying to fix immigration, he said. Diaz-Balart said the Hispanic community can relate to GOP’s ideals but said their lack of support to date for immigration legislation has kept potential supporters away.
