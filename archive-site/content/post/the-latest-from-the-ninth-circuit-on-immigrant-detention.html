Litigation over immigrant detention continues.  The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, in an opinion by Judge Fisher (and joined by Judges Hall (recently deceased) and Bybee, held that an individual facing prolonged immigration detention under 8 U.S.C. § 1231(a)(6) is entitled to release on bond unless the government establishes that he is a flight risk or a danger to the community.
