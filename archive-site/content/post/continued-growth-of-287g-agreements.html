In April, co-editor Austin Kocher noted that, under the second Trump administration, 287(g) agreements were experiencing significant growth. Kocher identified 444 active 287(g) agreements as of April 11--more than ever before in place.

As of 9am today, the number of active 287(g) agreements has grown to 628 with an additional 65 agreements pending.

These include:

  • 99 Jail Enforcement Model (JEM) agreements that authorize deputized officers to question arrested noncitizens about their immigration status and issue immigration detainers;
  • 223 Warrant Service Officer (WSO) agreements that authorize local law enforcement to execute ICE administrative warrants; and
  • 306 Task Force Model (TFM) agreements that allow local law enforcement to enforce immigration laws during routine police duties.
