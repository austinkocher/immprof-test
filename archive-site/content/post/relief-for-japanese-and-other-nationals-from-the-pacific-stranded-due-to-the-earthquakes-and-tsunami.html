U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services has issued an advisory for Japanese and other foreign nationals from the Pacific stranded in the United States due to the earthquakes and tsunami devastation. If you have exceeded or are about to exceed your authorized stay in the U.S., you may be permitted up to an additional 30 days to depart.  Click the link above for more.
