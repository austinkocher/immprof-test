Visitors beware!  The Trump administration is reviewing all 55 million foreigners with visas for violations

The immigration crackdown is not limited to undocumented immigrants.  The Trump administration said yesterday it is reviewing more than 55 million people who have valid U.S. visas for any violations that could lead to removal from the United States. Read more.

According to  the Associated Press, the State Department said all U.S. visa holders, which can include tourists, are subject to “continuous vetting,” with an eye toward any indication they could be ineligible to enter or stay in the United States.  If such information is found, the visa will be revoked.  If the visa holder is in the United States, he or she would be subject to deportation.
