The use of unmanned drones by the United States in military operations has recently made the news. The Los Angeles Times reminds us that, as previously reported on immigrationProf, the U.S. Customs and Border Protection, operates eight Predators on the country's northern and southwestern borders to search for illegal immigrants and smugglers. Besides enforcing the border, the drones assist local, state and federal law enforcement. Congress first authorized Customs and Border Protection to buy unarmed Predators in 2005.
