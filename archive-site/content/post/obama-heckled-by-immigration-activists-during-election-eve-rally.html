The DREAMers are not going away.  The Washington Post reports that, even while rallying the faithful on behalf of Democratic Gov. Dan Malloy in Bridgeport, Connecticut on Sunday, President Obama was interrupted at least four times by activists who urged him to use executive action to address immigration. At least one protester wore an “Obama deports parents” t-shirt.
