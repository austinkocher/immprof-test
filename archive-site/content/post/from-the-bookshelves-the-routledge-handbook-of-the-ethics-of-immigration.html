The Routledge Handbook of the Ethics of Immigration Edited By Sahar Akhtar (2025)

Immigration poses major moral, economic, and political challenges. Questions of the state’s responsibilities toward immigrants, open borders, security, coping with the displacement of people caused by climate change and natural disasters, and deciding who has a "right to remain" are a few of the significant issues facing governments, policymakers, and the greater community.

The Routledge Handbook of the Ethics of Immigration is a reference source to this topic.  It includes 25 chapters by philosophers, economists, political scientists, and legal theorists.
