Yesterday was Open Borders Day.  Ilya Somin on the Volokh Conspiracy reminds us of the opportunities of open borders.  As he explains, Open Borders Day is an international event created for the purpose of focusing attention on the injustices inflicted by government-imposed restrictions on international migration. On the last two Open Borders Days, Somin has set out the general case for Open Borders and provided some links addressing various possible questions related to the issue.  This year, he discusses how immigration seems to be splitting Americans along ideological and political lines.
