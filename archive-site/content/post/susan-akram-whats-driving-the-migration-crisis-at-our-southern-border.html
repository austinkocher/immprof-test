Susan Akram explains the findings of a three-year research and fieldwork study that provides detailed insight into the reasons migrants from Guatemala, Honduras and El Salvador (known as “the Northern Triangle”) are leaving; why thousands have disappeared over the last decade; and why there has been so little accountability for their fates.  Click here to read how U.S. policies have contributed to migration from Central America.
