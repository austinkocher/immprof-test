Brittny Mejia of the LA Times – in an article available here – reported on an immigration raid at a Home Depot in Hollywood that occurred at 7:30am on Thursday, June 19. ICE targeted a community of venders that had been selling food for decades outside the Home Depot. Dozens of agents, many in masks, with assault rifles converged on the parking lot.

 

Witnesses and family members say that the agents apprehended more than 12 vendors, day laborers, and customers. Mejia reminds readers that in May, White House Deputy Chief of Staff Stephen Miller reportedly told top ICE officials to increase their arrest numbers by going beyond their usual target lists, and suggesting they make arrests at places like Home Depot and 7-Eleven to reach a 3,000 arrests per day. The same day as the Hollywood raid, ICE agents raided another Home Depot in San Fernando.

 

Pablo Alvarado, co-executive director of the National Day Laborer Organizing Network, is quoted: “This is a systematic attack against the most vulnerable members of the migrant community. It is the working poor. That’s who they are going after. These are the spaces where they feel they can do this. They don’t even have legal parameters.”
