Eric Lipton and Julia Preston report that a "surge in migrant traffic across the Southwest border into Texas has resulted in a milestone: the front line of the battle against illegal crossings from Mexico has shifted for the first time in over a decade away from Arizona to the Rio Grande Valley of South Texas."  Expect the Texas in Congress who oppose immigration reform, such as Senator John Cornyn, to emphasize this in attacking the comprehensive immigration reform bill being debated in Congress.
