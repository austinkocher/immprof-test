NPR reports that Rodney Scott, former chief of the U.S. Border Patrol, is President-elect Donald Trump's choice to lead Customs and Border Protection, the agency that manages ports of entry and manages irregular immigration at U.S. borders.

The President-Elect also tapped Caleb Vitello to serve as the acting director of Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).  Vitello is currently the assistant director of the Office of Firearms and Tactical Programs. Vitello reportedly has worked for ICE for over 23 years, and served as chief of staff for Enforcement and Removal Operations.
