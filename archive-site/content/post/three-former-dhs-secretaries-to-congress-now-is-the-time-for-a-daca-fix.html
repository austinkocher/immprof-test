Former Secretary of the Department of Homeland Security Michael Chertoff

The New York Times reports that three former homeland security secretaries warned congressional leaders and officials of both parties today that the window for legislative action to protect Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA) recipients will close by the middle of January. The letter — signed by Jeh Johnson and Janet Napolitano, homeland security secretaries under President Barack Obama, and Michael Chertoff, a homeland security secretary under President George W. Bush — was sent as congressional leaders and aides to President Trump prepare for a meeting on Capitol Hill, where DACA is expected to be discussed.
