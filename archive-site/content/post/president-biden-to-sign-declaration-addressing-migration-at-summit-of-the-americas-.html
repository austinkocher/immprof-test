The Summit of the Americas 2022, which is held every three years by the governments of the American nations, is taking place from June 6 to June 10, in Los Angeles.  Controversy surrounds the nations, including Mexico, snubbing the Summit.

Migration between the nations of the Americas obviously is a continuing issue.  Alex Gangitano for The Hill reports that President Biden will hold a meeting with governments at the Summit of the Americas to sign a declaration that aims to address migration.  The article states that "[t]he Los Angeles Declaration on Migration `is an unprecedented and ambitious step by the United States and regional partners to work together to address the migration crisis in a comprehensive manner,' a senior administration official said.The meeting to sign the declaration will be on Friday, which is the last day of the Summit of the Americas in Los Angeles."
