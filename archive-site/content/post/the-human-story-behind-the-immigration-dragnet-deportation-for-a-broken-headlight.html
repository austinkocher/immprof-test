Claiming that Immigration & Customs Enforcement (ICE) is focusing deportation efforts on serious criminal offenders, the Obama administration has championed controversial programs like Secure Communities. The evidence has demonstrated that many petty criminal offenders are caught up in the immigration dragnet as the administration annually sets reords for the number of removals.

Here is a real life story of a man, Luis Luna, who was brought by his mother to the United States when he was 3 and, at age 20, was arrested by local police in Washington for a broken head light, was turned over to ICE, and then deported to Mexico. Luna now dreams of returning home to the only country that he really knows.
