Earlier this month, the Trump administration announced that it would pay $1000 to every noncitizen who self deports.  The paid for self deportations have begun!

Rebecca Beitsch for The Hill reports that the "Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has conducted its first charter flight for migrants who agreed to `self-deport' . . . The flight took 64 citizens of Colombia and Honduras to their home country."
