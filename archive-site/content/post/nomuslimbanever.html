This week, the House of Representatives passed the National Origin-Based Antidiscrimination for Nonimmigrants Act—H.R. 2486— the NO BAN Act.  The Act would repeal the Muslim ban and make it harder for future presidents to abuse their power by enacting biased bans.

Watch this Brennan Center video and read this Twitter thread to learn more about how the No Ban Act came to be and what must happen next for it to become law.
