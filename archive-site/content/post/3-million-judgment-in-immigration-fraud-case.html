KIRK SEMPLE in the N.Y. Times reports that " A businesswoman in Jackson Heights, Queens, who falsely promised to secure legal immigration status for dozens of illegal immigrants has been ordered to pay $3 million in restitution and penalties by the State Supreme Court, Attorney General Andrew M. Cuomo announced on Monday."
