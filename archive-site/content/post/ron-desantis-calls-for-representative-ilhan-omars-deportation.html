Although no longer a Republican Presidential candidate, we have not heard the last word from Florida Governor Ron DeSantis.  He yesterday called for Congress member Ilhan Omar (D-Minnesota), to be expelled from Congress, have her citizenship revoked, and deported from the country over a speech. 

The clip of Omar’s Jan. 27 speech appeared to show Representative Omar, who was born in Somalia, "assuring her constituents that she would use her influence to ensure the breakaway Republic of Somaliland would not enter a sea-access deal with Ethiopia. "
