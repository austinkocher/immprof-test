CNN reports that a new Texas law (SB 4) that gives local law enforcement the authority to arrest migrants “violates the US Constitution,” the Department of Justice said in a letter to Governor Greg Abbott that became public yesterday.  

Earlier this month, Governor Abbott signed Senate Bill 4 into law.  The law is slated to take effect in March.

Principal deputy assistant attorney general Brian Boynton in his letter said that “the United States intends to file suit to enjoin the enforcement of SB 4 unless Texas agrees to refrain from enforcing the law.”  While the federal government is “committed to … securing the border,” Boynton wrote, the Texas law is “contrary to these goals.”

Governor Abbott slammed the Justice Department’s letter.
