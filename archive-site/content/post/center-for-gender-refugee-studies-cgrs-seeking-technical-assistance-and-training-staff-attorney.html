The Center for Gender & Refugee Studies (CGRS) is seeking a Technical Assistance and Training Staff Attorney passionate about training, resource development, and mentorship in the representation of asylum seekers.  CGRS will review applications on a rolling basis and encourages individuals to apply by January 3, 2024.
