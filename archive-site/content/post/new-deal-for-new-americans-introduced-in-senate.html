BREAKING NEWS Senator Ed Markey introduced federal legislation in the Senate to promote immigrant integration today

The New Deal for New Americans Act is a legislative proposal based on the “New American Dreams” policy priorities of the membership of the National Partnership for New Americans (NPNA) and its allies. The bill reflects a proactive, affirmative agenda that seeks to ensure that the federal government partners with state/local government to ensure immigrants and refugees (1) defend their legal foothold in the U.S. with the necessary legal support; (2) are received in welcoming and inclusive cities and neighborhoods; (3) have equitable access to U.S. citizenship and (4) can pursue English language learning and workforce development programs that support their full social, economic a civic participation in the United States.

The Senate bill follows introduction of a House bill introduced November 2019 by U.S. Representatives Grace Meng (NY); Jesús García (IL), and Pramila Jayapal (WA). (Prior immigrationprof blog post.)
