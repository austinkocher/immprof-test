AlterNet reports that Phoenix taxpayers will pay approximately $21 million over the next year and a half for as a resulting of a court ruling which held that the Maricopa County Sherriff’s Office, led by Sheriff Joe Arpaio, racially profiled Latinos The cost estimates were provided by the county officials by the Sheriff’s office as part of a budget-making process expected to end in May 2013.
