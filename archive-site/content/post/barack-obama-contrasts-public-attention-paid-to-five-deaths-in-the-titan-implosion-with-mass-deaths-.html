President Barack Obama shined a spotlight on a contrast that we should all think about.  In an interview with CNN’s Christiane Amanpour, Obama contrasted the media furor over the wealthy explorers who died while trying to view the Titanic wreckage with the scant attention paid to hundreds of migrants who died off the coast of Greece in the Mediterranean Sea while trying to seek a better life.  Race and class no doubt help explain the disparity.

Death on the Mediterranean Sea has long been a central feature of the Fortress Europe.   As of May 2022, more than 3,000 people had died or gone missing while attempting to cross the Central and Western Mediterranean and Atlantic last year to Europe. See the report released by United Nations High Commissioner on Refugees, the UN Refugee Agency, which calls for urgent support to prevent deaths and protect refugees and asylum seekers who are embarking on dangerous journeys.  Here is the report.
