The Immigration Policy Center has released a fact sheet on the future flow of immigrants.  As it acknowledges, "[o]ne of the greatest challenges in immigration reform is the need to realistically assess our future employment- based immigration needs. This includes permanent and temporary visas, high- skilled and low-skilled workers."
