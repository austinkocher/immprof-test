WaPo tagged along to a recent ICE hiring expo in Texas. The paper interviewed several prospects--from a kid willing to drop out of college to a retired police officer ready to jump back into the workforce. Apparently some 3,000 people showed, hoping to talk ICE into moving their applications forward. 

Side note: were you aware that ICE is currently offering a 50K bonus for "top recruits"?  

Four paragraphs from the bottom, WaPo quotes one prospective hire--a former MMA fighter--and it's pretty grim:

“I keep seeing these memes where Indians are bragging about taking our tech jobs... So I said, ‘Oh yeah? Well I’m going to work with these guys that are going to arrest you, slam your face on the pavement and send you home.’”
