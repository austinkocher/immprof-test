Cornell Refugee Assistance Resource List - Google Docs

With the U.S. withdrawal from Afghanistan and the Taliban now in power, the plight of  Afghan refugees has been of paramount concern.  Professor Stephen Yale-Loehr and Cornell law student Evan O’Neill  have compiled a resource list for Afghans at risk.  It is also available here.  The list includes links to many of organizations.
