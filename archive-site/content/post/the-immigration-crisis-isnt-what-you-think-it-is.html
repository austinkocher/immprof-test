There has been much talk of the "crisis" along the U.S./Mexico border.  Historian Glenn C. Altschuler on the Hill contends that many Americans' perception of the true "crisis" is misplaced.  He states that:
 
"Stigmatizing individuals and families who enter the country legally as well as illegally and denying or downplaying the contributions of undocumented people who have lived and worked here for decades, [those claiming that the numbers of migrants at the border constitute a crisis] they do not recognize that increased immigration is essential to addressing inflation and the great and growing labor shortage in the United States."  (bold added).

Altschuler is the Thomas and Dorothy Litwin Professor of American Studies at Cornell. He is the co-author (with Stuart Blumin) of Rude Republic: Americans and Their Politics in the Nineteenth Century.
