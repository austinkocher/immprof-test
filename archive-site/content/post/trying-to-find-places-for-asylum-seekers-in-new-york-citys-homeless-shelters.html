As part of his various immigration enforcement efforts, Texas Governor Greg Abbott has been sending migrants by bus to New York City.  In Trying to Find Places for Asylum Seekers in New York City’s Homeless Shelters by Eric Lach in the New Yorker, "[a]n immigrants’-rights advocate describes receiving busloads of migrants from Texas at the Port Authority Bus Terminal."
