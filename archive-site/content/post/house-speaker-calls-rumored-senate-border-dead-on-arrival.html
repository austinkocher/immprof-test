Previously, news stories discussed a possible border deal in the U.S. Senate.   House Speaker Mike Johnson (R-La.) reportedly is saying that, if rumors about the details of the Senate compromise are true, it would be "dead on arrival" in the House.

"House Republicans are already balking — and turning their sights on impeaching Homeland Security Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas. What he's saying: Johnson said a floor vote on impeaching Mayorkas will be held shortly after the Homeland Security Committee takes up articles of impeachment upon the House's return next week."
