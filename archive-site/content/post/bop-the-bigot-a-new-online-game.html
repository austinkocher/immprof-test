Maricopa County (Arizona) Sheriff Joe Arpaio has had a tough time of it in recent months.  He is in a tough re-election campaign.  The U.S. government has filed criminal contempt charges against him for violations of court orders in a civil rights lawsuit. 

Now, the Bazta Arpaio campaign in Arizona is announcing the launch of Bop the Bigot, a new online game where users are invited to bop Donald Trump, Sheriff Arpaio, and the former ku klux klan leader running for the Senate in Louisiana, David Duke, with a chancla (sandal). 

"We want the game to be a fun outlet for anyone who’s been insulted, frustrated, or harmed by the haters running for office this year." explained B. Loewe of Mijente and the Communications Director for the Bazta Arpaio campaign in Arizona.  "We wanted to give people a way to nonviolently smack down the candidates’ racism and hopefully motivate them to get to the polls on election day."
