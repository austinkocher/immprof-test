Now in print: Crimmigration Law: An Open Casebook version 2.0 and its partner Immigration Law: Statutory Supplement 2025. These are open-source/open-access teaching materials that will be available to download in .pdf and .docx format later this month. 

Feel free to add to, delete from, abridge, rearrange, and alter the works as best fits your courses. The books can be a jumping-off point for your own bespoke course materials.
