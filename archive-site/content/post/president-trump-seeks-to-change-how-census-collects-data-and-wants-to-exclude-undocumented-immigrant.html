Trump seeks to change how census collects data and wants to exclude immigrants in US illegally

 

News agencies reported on the announcement that President Donald Trump has instructed the Commerce Department to change the way the U.S. Census Bureau collects data, seeking to exclude immigrants who are in the United States illegally, he said Thursday. Read More.

In 2019, the Supreme Court rejected the first Trump administration's effort to include a U.S. citizenship question in Census 2020.
