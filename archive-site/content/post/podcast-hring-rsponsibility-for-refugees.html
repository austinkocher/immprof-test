Introducing Borderlines from Berkeley Law, a show about global problems in a world fragmented by national borders.

The host is Katerina Linos, Tragen Professor of International Law and co-director of the Miller Institute for Global Challenges and the Law. In the latest episode, Linos is in conversation with Tendayi Achiume and Seth Davis on questions of responsibility sharing vis-à-vis migrants from Afghanistan, Central American, Haiti, and Syria from an international law perspective. 

In each episode of Borderlines, Professor Linos invites three experts to discuss cutting edge issues in international law. responsibility sharing vis-à-vis migrants from Afghanistan, Central American, Haiti, and Syria from an international law perspective.
