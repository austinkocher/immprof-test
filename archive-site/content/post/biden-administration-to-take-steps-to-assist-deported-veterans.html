Several news outlets reported over the long Fourth of July weekend that the Biden administration is taking steps to assist veterans who have been deported. Rebecca Bietsch of The Hill reports that officials will "track down veterans who have been deported as part of an effort to provide a pathway to citizenship along with Veterans Affairs benefits." DHS officials also mentioned that they will work to "avoid future unjust removals" of immigrants serving in the military. The LA Times has also followed the steps that DHS is planning to take to address the unjust removals of veterans in this article.  Immigration attorney Margaret Stock is quoted in the article, explaining that the existing bureaucratic process has delayed naturalizations for servicemembers, particularly those stationed overseas. As the 2016 ACLU report detailed, servicemembers are promised full citizenship after even just one day of active duty, but due to failures to timely process their paperwork, many have been stripped of this right and banished from the country they served.
