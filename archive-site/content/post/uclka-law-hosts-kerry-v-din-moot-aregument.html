It seems that everything is on Facebook nowadays.  And, according to Facebook, the UCLA Law American Constitution Society sponsored a moot of Mark Haddad, head of the Supreme Court and Appellate Practice and Partner at Sidley Austin, moot his argument in the case of Kerry v. Din, which is set for argument in front of the Supreme Court on February 23, 2015.  Haddad represents Fauzia Din, a U.S. citizen challenging the visa denial of her noncitizen husband.
