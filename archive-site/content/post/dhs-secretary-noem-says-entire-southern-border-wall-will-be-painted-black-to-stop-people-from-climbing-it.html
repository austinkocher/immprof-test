Deaths of migrants on the U.S./Mexico have long been a fact of life.    Is that a concern of the U.S. government?  Apparently not.

CBS News reports that U.S. Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem said yesterday "that the entire wall along the U.S.-Mexico border is going to be painted black to make it hotter and deter illegal immigration — an idea she said was `specifically at the request' of President Trump. . . . Noem spoke during a visit to a portion of the wall in New Mexico, where she also picked up a roller brush to help out with the painting."
