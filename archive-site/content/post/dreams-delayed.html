Catherine Orr recently completed her graduate thesis, a multimedia documentary web project about three students and a teacher whose daily lives are affected by access to higher education for undocumented students. The site is called Dreams Delayed. It includes:

- A video story about mixed-status siblings whose college dreams are divided by a social security number

- A video story about a teacher who is working to keep her students from giving up hope

- An interactive video Q&A where an undocumented student answers people's tough questions about college access for undocumented students.
