Immprof Elizabeth Keyes (Baltimore) recently shared the following video. It's absolutely delightful.

I've pointed y'all to Foil Arms and Hog before. They have a spectacular series wherein travelers speak with screeners about their right to enter different countries--each question has a slightly off "correct" answer. For example, in their recent bit about trying to enter New Zealand, the questioner asks "What is the most expensive ad campaign run by the New Zealand tourist board?" The answer: "The Lord of the Rings."

Anyhoo, the above bit is slightly different. Now we have one Irish fella at the US embassy trying to get a visa. It's always nice to have a little humor to inject into the classroom.
