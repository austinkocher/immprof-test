Countries like Sweden and Germany have been lauded for their welcoming approach toward immigrants and their robust commitment to immigrant integration. That is changing as these countries confront challenges with COVID-19 and related economic fall-out. This article from Reuters details German's rollback of integration policies. This one from AP discusses Sweden's failures to include immigrants in their COVID-19 response.
