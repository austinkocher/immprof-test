It has been said that a grand jury will indict a ham sandwich.  But apparently not a Subway sandwich.  Its a fun news day.
 
In Prosecutors fail to get felony indictment of DC sandwich-thrower in The Hill, Filip Timotija reports on a man bites dog kind of story.   I know, lots of mixed (up) metaphors.  In any event, "[f]ederal prosecutors failed to secure a felony indictment against a former Justice Department (DOJ) employee accused of throwing a sandwich at a federal law enforcement official in Washington amid President Trump’s police takeover . . . ."
 
As previously reported on the ImmigrationProf blog, Sean Charles Dunn was charged with a felony in August after allegedly throwing a Subway sandwich at a Customs and Border Protection agent.  The incident was caught on video.  Attorney General Pam Bondi announced Dunn's firing.  It is not likely that he will get his job back.
