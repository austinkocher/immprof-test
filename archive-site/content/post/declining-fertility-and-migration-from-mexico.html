The Economist reports that "Mexico’s birth rate, once among the world’s highest, is in free-fall. In the 1960s Mexican mothers had nearly seven children each (whereas women in India then had fewer than six). The average now is just over two—almost the same as in the United States. The UN reckons that from 2040 the birth rate in Mexico will be the lower of the two."  The decfline in fertility ion Mexico likely would havew an impact on migration from Mexico to teh United States, which is discussed in the rest of the article.
