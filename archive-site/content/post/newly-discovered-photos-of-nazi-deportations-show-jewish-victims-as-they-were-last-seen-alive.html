In The Conversation "Newly discovered photos of Nazi deportations show Jewish victims as they were last seen alive," Wolf Gruner discusses how descendants of survivors helped researchers identify 279 deportees and tell their stories. ‘We give previously faceless victims a voice,’ says project’s co-founder.

In most cases, these are the very last pictures taken of Holocaust victims before they were deported and perished. That fact gives the project its name, #LastSeen.
