The Immigration Law Section of the Association of American Law Schools issues this Call for Papers for a Works-in-Progress (WIP) session at the AALS annual meeting (San Diego) in January 2018.   The WIP Session will take place on January 6, 2018 (Saturday) between 3:30 PM and 5:15 PM right after the section’s main program, “Immigration Adjudication in an Era of Mass Deportation.”

Papers can be on any topic that relates to immigration and citizenship law. Drafts must be submitted by August 31, 2017, 5:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) to immprof2018@gmail.com. The WIP Session provides speakers the opportunity to present their work and receive feedback from commentators. Papers that are slotted for publication by January 2018 are not eligible. Pre-tenured and non-tenure track professors are strongly encouraged to submit.
