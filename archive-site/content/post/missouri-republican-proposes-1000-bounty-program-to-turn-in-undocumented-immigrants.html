A sign of the times?   News from Missouri:  An incoming Missouri Republican lawmaker introduced a bill this week that would offer $1,000 bounties to residents who turn in undocumented immigrants to the state highway patrol.  Senator-elect David Gregory introduced the bill requiring the Missouri Department of Public Safety to create phone and email hotlines and an online portal for Missouri resident to report alleged undocumented immigrants.
