Inflation has been an economic issue of concern.   NPR's Daniel Estrin asks Daniel Costa, Director of Immigration Law and Policy Research for the Economic Policy Institute, about the connection between immigration and inflation.  Fewer immigrants means less labor, higher labor prices and increased prices, i.e., inflation.
