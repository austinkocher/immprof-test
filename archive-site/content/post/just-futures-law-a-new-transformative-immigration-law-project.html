Just Futures Law is a a women of color-led immigration legal project that will defend and build the power of immigrants’ rights and criminal justice activists, organizers and base building community groups working to disrupt and dismantle our deportation and mass incarceration systems. 
 
Many of you have known us as legal advocates, case strategists, educators, or litigators specializing in immigration law and “crimmigration.” We came together to build Just Futures Law so that we can be part of building the future of our dreams - a future without bans or walls, a future without prisons or police.
 
“I joined the JFL board because I believe that JFL lawyers are smart and strategic,” said Silky Shah, Executive Director of Detention Watch Network. “But more importantly, I believe that JFL lawyers center the experiences of directly impacted people and activists in their work which is critical to movement building.”
 
“For years, grassroots and base building groups have called for legal support for their transformative and visionary campaigns. JFL attorneys are known for doing this,” said Jacinta Gonzalez, National Field Director at Mijente. “I believe that their brand of lawyering is essential now because it defends and supports grassroots movements to fight back.” 
 
“We believe in JFL because our movement needs lawyers who can step up and step outside of traditional lawyering. JFL attorneys have a strong record of doing that,” said Angélica Cházaro, Assistant Professor of Law at the University of Washington School of Law. 
 
Through our partnerships, we will file litigation aligned with organizing, develop legal and advocacy strategies to disrupt criminalization and build a political home for legal workers and lawyers who want to do legal work aligned with movements.
