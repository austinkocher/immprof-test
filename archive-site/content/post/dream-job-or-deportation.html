The Washington Post  reports on a Georgetown University senior, who is graduating magna cum laude and has a job offer from a major Wall Street investment bank. The problem is that he is undocumented and facing possible deportation to Colombia, a country he hasn’t seen since he was 2 years old.

Ted Alden in a Newsweek website story tell stories of a number of skilled immigrants who this nation is turning its back on in what he refers to as "America's `National Suicide.'"
