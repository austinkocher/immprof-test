You may know immprof Michael Scaperlanda from his numerous articles on immigration, his book for the Federal Judicial Center Immigration Law: A Primer, his Law School Legends CDs on immigration law, or his work as a co-editor on this very blog. I know him as a wonderfully supportive colleague here at OU who graciously allowed me to teach immigration law during my tenure at the institution despite his seniority and prior claim to the topic!

And now Michael embarks on a new and exciting adventure. He's just been appointed the new president of St. Gregory's University.

Congratulations, Michael!
