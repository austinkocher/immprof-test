Just a week or so ago, Florida Govenor Ron DeSantis took credit for transporting migrants from the border to Sacramento, California.  It appears that Texas will not be outdone when it comes to playing a cynical political game with people's lives.

NBC News reports that 42 arrived in downtown Los Angeles yesterday after a nearly 24-hour bus ride from Texas, where Governor Greg Abbott called their removal "much-needed relief" for his state.

Official Photo Courtesy of Texas Governor Website

"Texas’ small border towns remain overwhelmed and overrun by the thousands of people illegally crossing into Texas from Mexico because of President Biden’s refusal to secure the border," he said in a statement.  See Governor Abbott Announces First Bus Of Migrants Arrives In Los Angeles.

According to NBC News, the migrants were given food, shelter and legal aid at a church in downtown Los Angeles.  The group "included at least eight children and several older people, immigration advocates said. . . . [I]mmigration advocates and city officials . . . said several of the passengers came from Venezuela, Guatemala and Honduras, with two of African descent and one of Asian descent."
