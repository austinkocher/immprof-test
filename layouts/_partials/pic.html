{{- /* The picture for a post in the homepage's section columns. A post with a picture of its own shows it; a post without one shows none, and its column starts with the headline (Austin, November 26, 2026: "Leave it as it is. Pictures where posts have them, text where they do not."). A picture counts only when it can fill the frame: at least 400 pixels wide and between 1 and 2.4 times as wide as tall. It must not be animated or nearly blank (the same measure the lead story uses), since an editor's animated signature shows as an empty white box. That leaves out the signature strips and small logos that some posts have as their only picture, which showed as a white box with a sliver of a logo. A file that cannot be read, or that reports no height (one such file stopped the build of the full site), counts as no picture. Called with dict "p" post "w" width. Returns the HTML, or nothing. */ -}} {{- $img := .p.Params.image -}} {{- $w := .w -}} {{- if and $img (not (strings.HasSuffix (lower $img) ".svg")) -}} {{- with resources.Get $img -}} {{- $r := . -}} {{- if and (reflect.IsImageResourceProcessable $r) (not (strings.Contains $r.Content "ANIM")) -}} {{- with try $r.Width -}}{{- if and (not .Err) (gt $r.Height 0) -}} {{- $ratio := div (float .Value) $r.Height -}} {{- $dense := div (float (len $r.Content)) (mul .Value $r.Height) -}} {{- if and (ge .Value 400) (ge $ratio 1.0) (le $ratio 2.4) (ge $dense 0.025) -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}{{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}
