César Cuauhtémoc García Hernández  on SCOTUSBlog (Supreme Court asylum decision burdens already overworked DOJ) critically analyzes the unanimous Supreme Court decision a few weeks ago in Urias-Orellana v. Bondi holding that courts of appeals "must defer to immigration judges and their colleagues who hear appeals. Although that decision may sound somewhat technical, it has major consequences: The court’s decision puts more responsibility on decision-makers who are stretched thin by mounting caseloads, while making it more difficult for migrants to ask federal appellate courts to overturn asylum denials."
