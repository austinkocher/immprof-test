In a video shared via social media, former immigration judge George Pappas in strong words calls on immigration judges to resign. “The time has come,” he says, “to look at the machinery you are operating, recognize its profound moral bankruptcy, and resign… The immigration courts are no longer tribunals of law. They are administrative assembly lines engineered for indiscriminate cruelty.”
