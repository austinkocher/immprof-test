FROM ACLU Press Release:

"In recent weeks ICE has killed Lorenzo Salgado Araujo in Texas and Johan Sebastián Durán Guerrero in Maine, following the killings of Alex Pretti and Renee Nicole Good in Minneapolis. Dozens more people have died in ICE's detention centers.

Federal law enforcement are committing civil rights violations at a scale without precedent in our nation’s recent history, and with the support of an administration that encourages violence and disregard for the law.

ACLU affiliates are fighting back by filing 54 claims across 17 states and Washington, D.C. against the federal government to demand justice for citizens and noncitizens alike who have suffered serious violence, abuse, and neglect at the hands of federal agents. Each claim tells an individual story. And together, they reveal a widespread pattern of civil rights violations and abuses that endanger us all.
