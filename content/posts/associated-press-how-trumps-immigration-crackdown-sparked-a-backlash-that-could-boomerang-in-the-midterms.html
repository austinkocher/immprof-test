Tim Sullivan and Nicholas Riccardo for the Associated Press reports that President Trump’s effort to fulfill his promises on immigration "has turned into a backlash, undermining one of the core issues that fueled his return to the White House. While Trump maintains some support for his handling of the border, polling shows voters disapprove of his approach to immigration, a striking turnaround that could undermine Republican attempts to maintain control of Congress in the midterms."
