After 13 seasons in the NFL, former NFL star and Chicago Bear, Charles Tillman joined the FBI, enrolling in the bureau's training program in Quantico, Virginia in 2018.  

"However, Tillman, 44, has now revealed that his discomfort with the Trump administration's immigration policies and the subsequent [immigrtaion and Customs Enforcement (ICE)] raids forced him out of the job. . . . Tillman detailed the day last January when he and his fellow FBI agents in Chicago were informed of Trump's border czar Tom Homan and TV personality 'Dr. Phil' McGraw's imminent arrival in the Windy City. . . . They brought ICE along with them, and the FBI, alongside the DEA, the ATF, the Border Patrol and the US Marshals, was expected to join them on their illegal immigration crackdown. . . . . Tillman revealed that rather participate in the raids, he attended his daughter's basketball game that day. . . . Soon after, he resigned from the FBI."
