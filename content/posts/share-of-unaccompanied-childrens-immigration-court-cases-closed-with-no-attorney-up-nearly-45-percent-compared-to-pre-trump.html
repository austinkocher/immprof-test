Today, the Vera Institute of Justice updated its Immigration Court Legal Representation Dashboard to include representation data for children, including unaccompanied children. The data shows the number of children, including unaccompanied children, facing removal without an attorney.  

The Vera press release states that

"[m]ore unaccompanied children have their cases decided in immigration court without ever having an attorney than before Trump’s second inauguration. Since retaking office, President Trump has weaponized immigration courts, narrowed legal pathways, empowered ICE agents to conduct violent enforcement operations, and dismantled the unaccompanied children's program, leaving children, some less than a year old, to navigate legal proceedings alone."
