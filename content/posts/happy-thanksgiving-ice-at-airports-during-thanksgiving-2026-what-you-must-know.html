The fallout over the ICE arrest at the Seattle airport has gotten immigrants uneasy with air travel.

Brooks Law cautions noncitizens that

"Millions of people will fly through U.S. airports over the Thanksgiving holiday, and for immigrants, that routine trip now carries real risk. Throughout 2026, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has expanded its presence at airports across the country, detaining travelers at check-in counters, arrival gates, and even during connecting flights. "
