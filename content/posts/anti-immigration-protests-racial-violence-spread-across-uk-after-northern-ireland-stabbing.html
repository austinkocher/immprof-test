"Anti-immigration protests and racial violence escalated across parts of the UK in the aftermath of a stabbing incident that took place Monday in Northern Ireland. What initially broke out in Belfast by Wednesday spread to Scotland, where Muslim worshippers in Glasgow had to be locked up for their protection amid alleged racially motivated violence."
