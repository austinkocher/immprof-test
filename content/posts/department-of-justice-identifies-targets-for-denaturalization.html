From the New Republic: "The Trump administration is reupping its efforts to denaturalize U.S. citizens . . . . The New York Times reported [yesterday] that the Department of Justice has named 384 foreign-born Americans from whom it plans to revoke citizenship. The DOJ also told civil litigators to prepare to file suit against the individuals in 39 regional attorneys’ offices."
