The White House has released a statement regarding Tom Homan's trip to Minnesota: Border Czar Affirms Commitment to Public Safety, Removing Violent Criminals in Minnesota. (You can watch the entire press conference at this link.)

Spoiler alert: there is nothing that addresses the murders of two US citizens at the hands of ICE.

The closest that Homan came to the issue was to say: "What we’ve been working on is making this operation safer, more efficient, by the book. The mission is going to improve because of the changes we’re making internally.”

Or maybe this: "One of the words [President Trump] said to me when I came up here — he didn’t want to see anybody die… I don’t want to see anybody die — even the people we’re looking for… I think my conversations the last two and a half days are leading us to a safer community.”
