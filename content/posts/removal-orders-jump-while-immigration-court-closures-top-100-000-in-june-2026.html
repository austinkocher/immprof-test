100,773 immigration case closures in June 2026, marked an all-time high, according to a TRAC analysis of new ICE data. The latest data release, which follows an unexplained three-month pause from the agency, also shows nearly 66,000 people are in immigration detention nationwide. That’s down from a record high topping 70,000 in January 2026.
