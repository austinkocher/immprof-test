If you plan to attend this year’s Immigration Law Teachers & Scholars Workshop at Duke Law School, please register by TOMORROW, May 1, 2026 to avoid the late registration fee.

Duke Law School is hosting the 2026 Immigration Law Teachers & Scholars Workshop on June 5-7.  You can find the preliminary program and a list of confirmed speakers on the event website. 

This always is a great event. The late Professor Michael Olivas organized the inaugural workshop at the University of New Mexico.
