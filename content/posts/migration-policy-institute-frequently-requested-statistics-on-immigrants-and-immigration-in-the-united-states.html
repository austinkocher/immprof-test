Frequently Requested Statistics on Immigrants and Immigration in the United States

By Jeanne Batalova

Immigrants' share of the U.S. population is at a record high, matching the level set in 1890, and a roughly threefold increase since 1970. Meanwhile, over recent decades the U.S. immigrant population has evolved in terms of its composition, geographic distribution, immigrants' countries of origin, and more.

This essential resource offers top statistics about immigration trends, immigrants, and the immigration system in the United States. It draws on authoritative sources and the latest data available to offer a range of valuable insights.
