Dear Friends

Please consider this opportunity for shorter pieces:

The University of San Francisco Law Review Forum is the online companion to our print journal and a space for emerging ideas, timely commentary, and engagement with ongoing legal debates.

We publish shorter, more accessible pieces than those published in the traditional Law Review.

What We Publish:

We accept three types of submissions:
1. Short-Form Articles: Traditional law review-style scholarship in condensed form, commenting on a legal issue or development
2. Responses: Reactions to or extensions of works published in the print USF Law Review
3. Teaching Points: Notes on a new or emerging area of law suitable for classroom or practitioner use

Submission Guidelines:
- Length: Roughly 1,000–3,000 words (approximately 3–12 pages)
- Footnotes: Typically 40–80, depending on complexity of the piece
- Style: Clear, well-organized, and engaging, with proper Bluebook citations
- Format: MS Word or comparable word-processing software

How to Submit:
If you have a work in progress or an idea that is still taking shape, we encourage you to share it with us. We are happy to answer any questions at usflrev@usfca.edu.

Please send submissions to usflrev@usfca.edu.
