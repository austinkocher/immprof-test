From Refugees International:

"Over the last year, our advocacy and investigations have helped expose the Trump administration’s cruel third country deportation agreements, raise the alarm on the immense human toll of global aid cuts, and document abuses and atrocities – from Sudan to Gaza to Myanmar. Through it all, we have invested directly in refugee leadership through our Refugee Fellows program, ensuring that people who have experienced displacement have a voice in the decisions that shape their lives."
