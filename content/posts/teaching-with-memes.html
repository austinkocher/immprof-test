Today's immigration class focused on the admission process.

As you might imagine, one of the central themes was waiting. Waiting for your employer to decide to file an EB petition. Waiting for prevailing wage information. Waiting on the DOL. Waiting on USCIS. Waiting on visa availability.

I love a good meme that helps bring the material home and the above Winnie the Pooh one is solid. It gave us a chance to refresh our knowledge of the EB2 (and EB1!) immigrant visas, the H1B nonimmigrant visa, and visa wait times (bonus: 7% per country cap)! All while talking about the admission process. Score.
