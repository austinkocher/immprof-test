The Associated Press reports that an internal CBS News battle over a “60 Minutes” story critical of the Trump administration has exploded. "[A] correspondent charge[s] it was kept off the air for political reasons and news chief Bari Weiss say[s] the story did not `advance the ball.'”

"Two hours before airtime Sunday, CBS announced that the story where correspondent Sharyn Alfonsi spoke to deportees who had been sent to El Salvador’s notorious CECOT prison, would not be a part of the show." Weiss, CBS News editor-in-chief since October, said it was her decision.
