Check out this Catch-22!

"Do what a federal court orders and break the law. Ignore what a federal court orders and also break the law. That’s the losing deal that a migrant faced when a federal court told him he had to stay in Oklahoma, then federal prosecutors convicted him of the crime of being “found in” the United States without the government’s permission. Last month, Justice Sonia Sotomayor, joined by her colleague Neil Gorsuch, criticized the prosecutor’s treatment of Jesús Herrera-Salazar, but, despite their criticism, his conviction remains untouched."
