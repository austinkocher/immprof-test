This podcast features author Marla Ramírez and her book Banished Citizens: A History of the Mexican Women Who Endure Repatriation (Harvard University Press 2025). The conversation reviews the history of mass deportations, racial profiling, family separation, and the targeting of Mexican American communities during the repatriation era - when even US citizens were expelled. The book reveals the intergenerational impacts of the Mexican repatriation.
