AP Exclusive: Trump’s immigration crackdown has detained dozens of military spouses and parents

More than 50 parents and spouses of active-duty service members have been detained since Trump took office for a second term. At least six have been deported and one self-deported, an AP investigation found in the first accounting of such detentions, which the government does not track. Read more.
