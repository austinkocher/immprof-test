Kelsey Dallas for SCOTUSBlog looks at Temporary Protected Status, which is at the core if a case that the Supreme Court announced will be argued late April. The issue is the lawfulness of the Trump administration’s effort to remove protected immigration status from Syrian and Haitian nationals. Dallas offers "a brief overview of the Temporary Protected Status program, what’s at stake in the related disputes, and what the court has said in the past year about the administration’s authority to revoke protected immigration status."
