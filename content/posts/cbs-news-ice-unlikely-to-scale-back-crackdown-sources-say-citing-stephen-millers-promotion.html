CBS News reports that the Trump administration is unlikely to scale back its aggressive crackdown on immigration following the midterm elections, according to multiple U.S. officials, citing the elevation of immigration hawk Stephen Miller as White House chief of staff.

Republicans suffered huge losses across the country on Election Day. "Exit polls indicated that immigration was one of the top issues, alongside the economy, among Texas Latinos, who swung heavily toward Democrats. And 58% of voters nationwide said the Trump administration's deportation crackdown had `gone too far.'" 

In January, Democrats will take control of both chambers of Congress. Expect fireworks!
