Writing in response to a request for comment from the Department of Homeland Security on the issue of “Public Charge Ground of Inadmissibility,” Urban Institute experts raise concerns based on prior research that the proposed rule would reduce participation in essential health, nutrition, and other programs among low-income families with children, including many US citizen children.
