Kyle Cheney for Politico reports that the Trump administration acknowledged violating court orders issued by New Jersey federal judges more than 50 times over the past 10 weeks in cases involving the Trump administration’s mass deportation campaign. "Associate Deputy Attorney General Jordan Fox . . . said those violations were spread across more than 547 immigration cases that have flooded the courts since early December . . . ."
