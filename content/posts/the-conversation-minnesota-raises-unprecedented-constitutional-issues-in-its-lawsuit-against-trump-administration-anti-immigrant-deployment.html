Minnesota raises unprecedented constitutional issues in its lawsuit against Trump administration anti-immigrant deployment

A federal judge heard arguments yesterday, as the state of Minnesota sought a temporary restraining order to stop the Trump administration’s immigration enforcement operation in the state. The administration has sent 3,000 immigration agents to Minnesota. The state argues, in part, that it amounts to an unconstitutional occupation and violates the 10th Amendment. Alfonso Serrano, a politics editor at The Conversation U.S., spoke with Andrea Katz, a law professor at Washington University in St. Louis, about the lawsuit.
