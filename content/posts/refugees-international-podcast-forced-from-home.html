From Refugees International:

We’re excited to share something new with you: Refugees International’s podcast, Forced from Home.

In each episode, we’ll explore the causes and consequences of global displacement through conversations with frontline community leaders, humanitarian experts, and the decision-makers shaping how the world responds.

Hosted by Refugees International President Jeremy Konyndyk, Forced from Home goes beyond the headlines to unpack the realities driving displacement around the world – and the policies and politics shaping solutions.

We hope you’ll join us. You can listen to our trailer today and subscribe so you’re ready for our first episode.
