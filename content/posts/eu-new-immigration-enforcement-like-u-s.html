The EU has passed new regulations on immigration enforcement to facilitate deportations of undocumented migrants, reports the Guardian.

  • Enforcement officials will have the power to "search people’s homes 'or other relevant premises' and seiz[e]... personal belongings in order to ensure compliance with a deportation order."
  • Those facing deportation can now be held in detention for 30 months, up from 18.
  • Off-shore "return hubs" will be established to hold people for "unspecified periods" until return to their country of origin.
  • Those deemed a "security risk" will face a lifetime ban from the EU, up from a 10-year ban.
