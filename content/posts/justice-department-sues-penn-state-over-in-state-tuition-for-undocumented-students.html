The Hill reports that the Justice Department filed a lawsuit yesterday against Pennsylvania State for allowing in-state tuition for undocumented students.

"The Trump Justice Department has brought 27 lawsuits challenging in-state tuition policies for undocumented students around the country. 

Six of those lawsuits in Texas, Kentucky, Oklahoma, Nebraska, Illinois and Kansas have resulted in favorable outcomes for the administration. Blue states such as New York and California are fighting the Trump administration’s efforts."
