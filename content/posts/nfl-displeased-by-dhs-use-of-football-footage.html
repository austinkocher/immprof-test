The official "X" account for the Department of Homeland Security made a tasteless tweet yesterday. Someone reported a "Huge migrant caravan headed towards the US southern borden." And DHS responded: "Our time has come," adding a video compilation featuring the former Philadelphia Eagles defenseman Brian Dawkins.

The NFL took issue with the tweet, asking DHS to remove it according to news outlets. As for the player in the video, he told ESPN "I have very respectfully not given permission" to DHS to use the video in this manner.
