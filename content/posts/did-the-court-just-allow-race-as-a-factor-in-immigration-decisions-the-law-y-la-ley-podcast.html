In this episode of The Law y La Ley, Professors Ediberto Román and Craig Jackson break down the U.S. Supreme Court's recent decision on Temporary Protected Status (TPS) and what it means for immigrants, employers, and the future of immigration law in the United States.

The discussion explores the legal reasoning behind the Court's decision, the extent of executive authority over TPS designations, the limits of judicial review, and the broader constitutional and humanitarian implications of the ruling. The professors also examine how this decision could affect current and future TPS recipients, while providing historical context and practical insight into one of the most significant immigration cases in recent years.

Whether you're a law student, legal professional, or simply trying to understand the latest developments in immigration law, this episode offers an accessible and thoughtful analysis of a landmark Supreme Court decision.
