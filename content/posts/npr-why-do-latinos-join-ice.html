Geraldo Cadava, professor of Latino Studies talks with NPR about why Latinos make up at least 50% of all Customs and Border Patrol agents and 20% of Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents. He "break down[s] some of the reasons Latinos join ICE, and he tells us, there are many people who believe in the mission of immigration enforcement."
