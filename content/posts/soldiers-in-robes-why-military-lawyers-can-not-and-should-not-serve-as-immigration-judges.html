The Department of Justice recently appointed 25 temporary immigration judges, two-thirds of whom are military lawyers. The Pentagon says it plans to send hundreds more to help relieve the strain on immigration courts. For criticism of these steps by Mark Nevitt and Margy O’Herron on Just Security, click here. They claim that "[t]hese unprecedented moves violate federal law and breach a foundational American principle: military power must not be wielded over civilians in domestic affairs."
