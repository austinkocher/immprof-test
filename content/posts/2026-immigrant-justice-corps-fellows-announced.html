Immigrant Justice Corps has announced its 2026 class of Justice Fellows. "These 30 exceptional advocates, deeply committed to fairness, dignity, and the rule of law, are answering the call at a time when the need for skilled, fearless immigrant legal representation has never been more urgent."

I am proud that a UC Davis Law graduate, Nayarith Franco-Vasquez, has been selected as part of the 2026 Class of Justice Fellows. She will serve as a staff attorney at Esperanza Immigrant Rights Project in Los Angeles, providing legal assistance to low-income immigrants in complex immigration matters, including removal defense.

The Immigrant Justice Corps is a New York City-based fellowship program that provides free legal counsel to low-income immigrant communities. It was conceived of by Chief Judge Robert Katzmann of the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit.
