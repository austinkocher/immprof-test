Congress Has Let Trump Go Wild. The Midterms Could Fix That.
What’s next for the new Congress? As lawmakers take office, they’ll have the chance to tackle immigration. That includes scrutinizing the billions of dollars allocated to immigration enforcement, investigating abuses in detention, holding ICE officials accountable for misconduct, and addressing the loss of legal protections that have put millions of immigrants at risk.
