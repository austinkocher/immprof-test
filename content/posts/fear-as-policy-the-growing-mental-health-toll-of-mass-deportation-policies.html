Fear as Policy: The Growing Mental Health Toll of Mass Deportation Policies
The Trump administration’s immigration policies nurture a climate of fear and uncertainty that carries profound long-term mental health consequences for communities living under the constant threat of detention, deportation, and having to navigate an increasingly complex and rapidly changing immigration policy landscape.
