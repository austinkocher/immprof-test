American Immigration Council has published a new report: Mass Delegalization.

In it, they write:

The second Donald Trump administration is carrying out the largest delegalization campaign in U.S. history. While the full scale of the effort is impossible to quantify based solely on public records, the most conservative estimate holds that more than 1.3 million immigrants who were legally protected from deportation in January 2025 are now deportable under US law and have lost the legal authority to work in the United States, a figure which does not even include hundreds of thousands of individuals who lost humanitarian parole granted through the CBP One process. This delegalization campaign is ongoing and will likely continue across the rest of President Trump’s second term, ensnaring an ever larger number of people.
