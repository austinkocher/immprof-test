I've been thinking about what music to pair with the topic of self deportation. I think both Billy Joel and The Clash would work.

Billy Joel: Movin' Out. From the album "The Stranger" no less. Key lyrics: "Good luck movin' up / 'Cause I'm moving out."

The Clash: Should I Stay or Should I Go. Key lines: "If I go, there will be trouble / And if I stay, it will be double."
