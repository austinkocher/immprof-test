The Jeffery Epstein hearings today were rambunctious to say the least. Sadly, I did not find this story surprising: "Attorney General Pam Bondi at a House Judiciary Committee hearing seemed to have a printout of Rep. Pramila Jayapal's history of searches of the Department of Justice's database of documents related to Jeffrey Epstein." Jayapal (D-Washington) has been a vocal critic of the Trump administration. Here is the Department of Justice's "Epstein Library." I am not looking at anything in that Library!

According to the story, "[p]hotos of a black binder that Bondi had at the hearing showed the words `Jayapal Pramila Search History' and a list of documents whose numbers coincide with the number of Epstein files."

Congressmember Jayapal questioned Attorney General Bondi in the hearing. She asked Bondi how the administration has handled the Epstein files. She addressed survivors seated behind Bondi in the hearing room, saying, "if you are willing, please raise your hands if you have still not been able to meet with this Department of Justice." She noted that every survivor present raised their hand.
