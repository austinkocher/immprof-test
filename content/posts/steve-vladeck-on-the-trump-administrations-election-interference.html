As the election nears, the Trump administration has upped its allegations of noncitizen voting. Steve Vladeck considers the Department of Justice's suit against California seeking to "keep it from counting the mail ballots of roughly 20,000 voters the federal government claims are both (1) “likely” noncitizens and (2) on California’s voter rolls."
