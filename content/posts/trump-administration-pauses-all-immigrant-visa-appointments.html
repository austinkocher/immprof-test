--- title: "Trump Administration \"Pauses\" All Immigrant Visa Appointments" date: 2026-08-26T11:41:17-04:00 slug: "trump-administration-pauses-all-immigrant-visa-appointments" url: "/trump-administration-pauses-all-immigrant-visa-appointments/" sections: ["Policy"] tags: ["Federal litigation", "Visas and legal immigration", "White House and agencies", "International"] authors: ["Kevin Johnson"] ---

Reuters reports that the Trump administration has issued a pause on immigrant visa appointments for applicants around the world. According to a U.S. State Department spokesperson, "it launched a global training initiative at all U.S. embassies and consulates worldwide and that appointments for visa services will be adjusted to accommodate the training."
