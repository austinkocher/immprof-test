Carolina Nunez has organized a symposium on Citizenship for the BYU Law Review (November 6-7, 2025). The keynote speaker is Rose Cuison-Villazor and panelists include Sam Erman, Anna Pagler, Rachel Rosenbloom and myself (among others speaking on non-immigration related aspects of citizenship).

The symposium takes place on-person in JRCB 472 or online here.
