This afternoon, I caught back-to-back reports from Latino USA on NPR. Both are very much worth a listen.

This one aired first: Chicago is Our Home: Haitians Left In Immigration Limb ("Latino USA host, Maria Hinojosa, travels to the deep south side of Chicago to see how the ending of TPS is impacting their Haitian community both spiritually and financially. We go to a church, a Haitian restaurant, and we also speak directly to a Haitian man who lost TPS to better understand the ripple effects that this change in immigration policy is having.")

The second was this: Chicago is Our Home: How Some South Shore Residents Are Taking on the Federal Government ("In this episode, Maria Hinojosa heads to the South Shore neighborhood in Chicago to ask: how can victims affected by a massive militarized raid pursue accountability from the federal government?"). It discusses a Federal Torts Claim Act case brought by residents of an apartment building that was raided by ICE in September 2025.
