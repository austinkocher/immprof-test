The Associated Press reports that "[t]he Senate early [today] approved Homeland Security funds to pay Transportation Security Administration agents and most other agencies, but not the immigration enforcement operations at the heart of the budget impasse that has jammed airports, disrupted travel and imposed financial hardship on workers."

This is very good news for TSA workers as well as the flying public.
