Minnesota residents sue the Trump administration for racial profiling, stops without suspicion, and warrantless arrests. 

Here is the ACLU press release.

Citizens described unlawful and violent interactions with ICE in which they felt like they had “no rights” in court testimonies as part of a recent class action lawsuit.

The American Civil Liberties Union, ACLU of Minnesota, Covington & Burling LLP, Greene Espel PLLP, and Robins Kaplan LLP filed a class-action lawsuit against the Trump administration on behalf of three community members — and a class of similarly situated people — whose constitutional rights were allegedly violated by federal agents, including Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), Customs and Border Protection (CBP).
