NPR's Ximena Bustillo reports that agriculture employers and some Republicans want to expand a visa program that brings foreign workers to U.S. farms. "Dozens of farmers — including dairy farmers and blueberry, apple and peach growers — and lobbying powerhouses like the American Farm Bureau Federation took to Washington this month to advocate for their labor needs."
