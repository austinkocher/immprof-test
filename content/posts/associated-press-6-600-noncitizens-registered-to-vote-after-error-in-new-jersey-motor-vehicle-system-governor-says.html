The Associated Press reports that about 6,600 noncitizens obtaining New Jersey driver’s licenses and other state IDs were improperly registered to vote in 2023 and 2024.

Governor Mikie Sherrill, a Democrat,

"blamed a Motor Vehicle Commission system software error but said a preliminary analysis showed that fewer than 400 of those who registered that way went on to cast ballots.

The revelation comes as President Donald Trump and other Republicans continue to assert that voting by noncitizens is rampant in U.S. elections, even though it’s rare and, when caught, can be punished as a felony that can lead to deportation."
