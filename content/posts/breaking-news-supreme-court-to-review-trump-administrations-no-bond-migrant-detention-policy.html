Britain Eakin for Law360 reports that the U.S. Supreme Court today agreed to review whether the Trump administration can detain undocumented immigrants without bond until their immigration cases conclude.

The Court in an order granted certiorari in a case from the Second Circuit, which ruled against the U.S government.
