Journal of International Law and Policy Symposium

Please join the UC Davis School of Law's Journal of International Law and Policy for a scholarly discussion of human rights and global justice. Professor Roxanna Altholz, Director of the Human Rights Clinic at UC Berkeley School of Law, will deliver the keynote address.

Location: UC Davis School of Law, Room 1301, 400 Mrak Hall Dr, Davis, CA 95616

Date and time: Fri, Feb 20, 2026 8:00 AM

AGENDA:

8 - 9 a.m. Breakfast and Check-In

9 - 9:15 a.m. Opening Remarks by JILP

9:15 - 10:45 a.m. Panel 1: Prioritizing Empathy in Human Migration

  • Jeffrey Kahn, Associate Professor of Anthropology and Sociocultural Wing Chair at UC Davis
  • Holly Cooper, Continuing Lecturer and Co-Director of the UC Davis Immigration Law Clinic
  • Raquel Aldana, Martin Luther King Jr. Professor of Law and Co-Director of the Aoki Center for Critical Race and Nation Studies
  • Lindsay M. Harris, Professor and Director of University of San Francisco's Frank C. Newman International Human Rights Clinic

10:45 - 11 a.m. Snack and Coffee Break

11 - 12:30 p.m. Panel 2: Global Responsibilities in Aligning International Business Law with Human Rights

  • William Dodge, Lobingier Professor of Comparative Law and Jurisprudence at George Washington University
  • Omo Abe, Adjunct Professor of Law at University of the Pacific
  • Hannah Buxbaum, Martin Luther King Jr. Professor of Law and President of the American Society of Comparative Law

12:30 - 1:30 p.m. Lunch

1:30 - 2:45 p.m. Keynote Lecture delivered by Professor Roxanna Altholz, Director of the Human Rights Clinic at UC Berkeley School of Law

2:45 - 3 p.m. Closing Remarks by JILP
