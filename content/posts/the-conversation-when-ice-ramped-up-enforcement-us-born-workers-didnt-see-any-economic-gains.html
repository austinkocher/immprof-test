Chloe N. East and Elizabeth Cox write for The Conversation that, contrary to the belief that an immigration crackdown would lead to more jobs for US-born workers, ICE enforcement has not produced economic gains for Americans. The bottom line: "[A]s our most recent research shows, mass deportations don’t create new job opportunities for American citizens. Presidents seeking to strengthen the labor market will need to look elsewhere."
