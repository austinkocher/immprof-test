The New Republic analyzes a new Trump administration proposal that would leave out millions of U.S. citizens in addition to many noncitizens. "A proposal from the Census Bureau could exclude as many as 38 million people from the population count, a new report from the Coalition on Human Needs finds.

The Commerce Department, which runs the census, put forward a new rule in September that would change how people are counted for the 2030 census. This would erase most noncitizens from the population count for the first time in the survey’s history."
