The Trump administration’s “mass delegalization” push has stripped at least 1.3 million people of status since January 2025, with about 1 million of those individuals previously holders of TPS. A American Immigration Council

"report examines the Trump administration’s campaign of `delegalization,' the process by which people who lawfully obtained temporary immigration protections lose those protections through broad policy decisions rather than any individual wrongdoing. Many of those affected followed every rule the government established. They registered with federal agencies, submitted biometric information, paid application fees, renewed their status on time, and built lives around the promise that compliance would allow them to remain in the country legally. 

By documenting the administration’s efforts and their consequences, this report shows how millions of immigrants, along with the families, employers, and communities that depend on them, are being pushed into uncertainty."
