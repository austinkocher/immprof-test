What has this nation become? The Associated Press reports that masked immigration officers detained two people on an airport jet bridge in Seattle as people were boarding yesterday,

"causing what the airline called a `distressing' scene for passengers and employees and delaying the Los Angeles-bound flight.

Video of the incident showed officers dragging away at least one person at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport as a woman screams that her daughter is being taken and she has a work permit.

Immigration and Customs Enforcement said in a statement that its officers conducted a targeted enforcement operation to arrest, an immigrant from Mexico who `overstayed his visa in violation of our nation’s laws.'” Such a commotion over a visa overstay?

The ICE statement states that another passenger “hurled hot coffee on the officers.”
