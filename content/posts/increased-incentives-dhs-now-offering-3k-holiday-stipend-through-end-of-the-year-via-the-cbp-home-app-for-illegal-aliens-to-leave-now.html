Happy Holidays from the Department of Homeland Security! Undocumented immigrants "who sign up to self-deport through the CBP Home app by the end of the year will receive a $3,000 stipend in addition to a free flight home. Using the CBP Home app also qualifies recipients for forgiveness of any civil fines or penalties for failing to depart the country." The stipend previously had been $1000.
