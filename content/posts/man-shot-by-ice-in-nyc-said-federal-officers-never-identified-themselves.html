The man shot by federal immigration agents in New York City last week says that he did not know that the men who shot him were U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers until he was in the hospital.

Documents filed in federal court "includes key details that contradict the Trump administration’s version of the events." The shooting victim claims in the filing that the ICE officers — many of them masked — did not identify themselves before approaching his vehicle with firearms drawn.
