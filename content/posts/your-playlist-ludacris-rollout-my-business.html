I think I've found the absolute best song to accompany your class on marriage fraud this year: Ludacris' Rollout (My Business).

Fair warning, you might want to read the lyrics before playing. The video below features the clean version, but even then it's not necessarily a video you'd want to show your class.

The song is a goldmine of Stokes questions:

Now, where'd you get that platinum chain with them diamonds in it?
Where'd you get that matchin' Benz with them windows tinted?
Who them girls you be with when you be ridin' through? ...
You's a superstar, boy, why you still up in the hood?
What in the world is in that bag? What you got in that bag? ...
What in the world is in that case, what you got in that case?

But really, the very best line is clipped below– a mere 3 seconds to sing "Now, tell me who's your housekeeper, and what you keep in your house?"
