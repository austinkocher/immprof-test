Latino USA talks with Pulitzer Prize winning journalist Mirta Ojito about the inspiration behind her new novel, Deeper Than the Ocean (2025). Mirta reflects on the shipwreck that sparked the idea for her novel, her immigration story of leaving Cuba during the Mariel boatlift, and her fear of water. She explores the meaning of memory, the emotional cost of covering immigration, and the power of storytelling, across generations and the ocean.
