The Associated Press reports that the Trump administration is preparing to revoke up to 200,000 business and tourist visas for noncitizens who are seeking asylum in the United States. "If it happens, the move would be the largest single mass revocation of visas in U.S. history and would likely face legal challenges."

The State Department is expected to announce in the coming weeks the revocation of B1 and B2 visas issued between 2016 and 2026 whose holders have sought asylum or are now seeking asylum,
