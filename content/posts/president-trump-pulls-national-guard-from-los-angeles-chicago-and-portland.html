President Donald Trump announced yesterday that the National Guard will be pulled from several cities following months of deployment, followed by a threat that troops would return “perhaps in a much different and stronger form, when crime begins to soar again.”

In a post on Truth Social, the president said that National Guard members will be removed from Chicago, Los Angeles and Portland.

“Portland, Los Angeles, and Chicago were GONE if it weren’t for the Federal Government stepping in,” he said.
