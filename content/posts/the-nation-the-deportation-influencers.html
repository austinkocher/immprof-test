Iliana Hagenah for The Nation (The Deportation Influencers) looks at the growing group of U.S. deportees who are documenting their "post-deportation lives on social media."

"[A] growing group of US deportees . . . are documenting their new, post-deportation lives on social media. Their videos run the gamut from travel vlogs to food challenges. But they also reveal the human cost of a Kafkaesque immigration system that deposits thousands of people in unfamiliar countries—whether a country they left as infants or a `third country' they’ve never known. The videos offer a glimpse of the lives often flattened by political agendas and a media landscape that either temporarily thrusts them into the spotlight or forgets about them completely."
