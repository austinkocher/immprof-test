University of Maryland Francis King Carey School of Law Professor Maureen Sweeney is a recipient of this year’s Gender Justice & Equity Impact Award from the city of Baltimore’s Office of Equity and Civil Rights.

In a letter announcing the award, Baltimore’s Director and Chief Equity Officer Amber Greene wrote,

“Your commitment to equity, justice, and service reflects the very spirit of this year’s celebration and honors those who work tirelessly to make Baltimore a fairer, more just city for all. Through your leadership and dedication, you have made a lasting impact in your community and city and have helped advance the principles of civil and human rights that continue to strengthen our city.”

Congratulations Professor Sweeney!
