There is a call for submissions for an edition of the Russell Sage Foundation Journal of the Social Sciences on “The New Asylum Seekers: Subnational Dynamics of Migration Governance in the United States” with affiliates of the Center for Comparative Immigration Studies. Rebecca Hamlin, Abigail Fisher Williamson, and Angela Garcia are co-editing the volume.  The deadline for submissions is January 7, 2026.
