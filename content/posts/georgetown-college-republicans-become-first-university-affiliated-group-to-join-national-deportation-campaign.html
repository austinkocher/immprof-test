The Georgetown University College Republicans (GUCR) have partnered with a national organization working to escalate U.S. immigration enforcement, the group announced.

The Mass Deportation Coalition advocates for a 21-part plan to deport more than one million noncitizens in 2026 and remove all undocumented immigrants from the United States by 2029. "GUCR is the first official university group to join the organization, alongside other conservative policy groups and pundits."
