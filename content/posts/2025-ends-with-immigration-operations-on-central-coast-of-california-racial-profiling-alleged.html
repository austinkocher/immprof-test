The beat goes on. Ruben Vives of the Los Angeles Times reports that immigration raids ended 2025 in Central Coast region of California, with the arrest of 147 people. "Federal immigration agents took people from their homes, job sites, businesses and on the streets as they ran errands, Santa Maria City Councilmember Gloria Soto" is quoted as saying at a press conference. "It’s devastating because this is happening during the holiday season when people are supposed to be with their loved ones."

Official Photo of Santa Maria City Councilmember Gloria Soto

"The operations, Soto said, have brought terror to her community and destabilized the local economy." “Children are returning home to empty houses, [elderly people] are starting to isolate themselves and workers are staying home out of fear," she said. "We cannot live in terror."

The executive director of 805 UndocuFund, a nonprofit, said the operations were mostly carried out in working-class towns with predominantly Latino populations. “What we are witnessing is the indiscriminate racial profiling of communities of color,” she said. (bold added).
