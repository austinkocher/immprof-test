UCLA Law's Behind Bars Data Project has published new and comprehensive data on ICE deaths in custody.

The number of people detained in ICE custody has reached historic highs. This important research shows that "deaths have followed, reaching 31 deaths in 2025, the highest number since 2004." 

You can explore the new data here on an interactive map organized by ICE detention facility location.

You can also explore the number of deaths year by year on the chart copied above. an interactive feature on the chart also allows you to search for trends in ICE custody deaths by state.
