Yesterday, President Trump re-truthed a clip of Florida Congressman Randy Fine appearing on Newmax to talk about immigration. In that clip, Fine states:

Well I think immediately we need to stop it all. All legal immigration of any kind because clearly it's out of control. People coming here on tourist visas protesting the country. People claiming that they're refugees but then scamming the system and going on vacation to the countries they supposedly fled from. People coming and taking American jobs. We need to restructure our legal immigration system to only allow people who love America, share American values, want to add value and assimilate to America, and don't want any free stuff. So we need to stop it ... and then we need to rebuild the system so the right kinds of people are coming here, not those who hate America and want to rip off America.
