Immigration Law Professor Jayanth Krishnan has been named the recipient of the John W. Ryan Award for Distinguished Contributions to International Programs and Studies, an honor recognizing outstanding leadership in advancing Indiana University’s global mission. The Award honors faculty and administrators who have made exceptional contributions to international education, research, and engagement across the Indiana University system. Jay's research, teaching, and service make him a true globe-trotter in every respect.

Jay is the Milt and Judi Stewart Professor of Law at the Indiana University Maurer School of Law, is widely known for his scholarship and leadership in comparative law, international legal development, and global justice.

Among his many recent immigration articles, I am a fan of The 14th Circuit, 102 Washington University Law Review 1225 (2025), which looks at Steve Legomsky's call for a Professor Legomsky argued for creating an Article III Court of Appeals for Immigration (CAI), replacing both the Board of Immigration Appeals and the circuit courts.
