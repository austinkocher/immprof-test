Freedom House is hosting a launch event for its new 2026 report. Freedom House has tracked global trends in political rights and civil liberties each year since 1973.

The event will take place on March 19, 2026 at 7:30 AM Pacific Time. You may register here.

The report’s authors will present key findings on the global state of democracy and freedom in 2025 and provide an analysis of major trends impacting fundamental rights over the last two decades. An expert panel discussion examining the implications of the report’s findings for the international system and the threat posed by emboldened autocrats will follow.
