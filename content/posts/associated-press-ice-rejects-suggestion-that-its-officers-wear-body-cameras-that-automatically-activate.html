 Rebecca Santana for the Associated Press reports that federal immigration officials need to ensure that body cameras worn by officers are uniformly activated when needed, a government report released [yesterday] said . . . . The agency rejected the recommendation, saying that using cameras that automatically activate when an officer pulls their weapon or activates a car siren could risk privacy concerns."

ICE officers have been involved in numerous incidents fired weapons during enforcement operations but officers were not wearing body cameras.
