The Center for Migration Studies of New York has published an edition of the Journal on Migration and Human Security, The Trump Administration’s Migration-Related Policies: Causes, Effects, and Future. Featuring 12 articles, the collection provides a critical examination of the second Trump administration’s migration initiatives, their implementation, and their consequences for noncitizens, families, migrant-sending communities, U.S. communities, and U.S. institutions.
