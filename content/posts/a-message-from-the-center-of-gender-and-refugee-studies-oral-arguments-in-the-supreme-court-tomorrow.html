Tomorrow, we will stand before the Supreme Court to defend the Ninth Circuit’s decision affirming the government’s obligations to inspect and process people seeking asylum at our borders and allow them to pursue their claims in the United States.

 

Our case, Noem v. Al Otro Lado, challenges the government’s unlawful turnback policy, under which border officers physically blocked thousands of asylum seekers at ports of entry along the southern border—denying them their legal right to seek protection. Our message is clear: no administration can unilaterally slam the door on those fleeing persecution.

 

Watch the short video below to hear from our partners about why this moment matters.
