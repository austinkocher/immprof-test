This week the American Immigration Council published a new report: Where Can You Win in Immigration Court? The Impact of Lawyers, Detention, Geography, and Policy.

The full report is available here.

The report uses immigration court data from fiscal year (FY) 2019 through FY 2024. And, the authors make this data accessible in a new interactive data tool. Explore the tool here to examine the role of legal representation in shaping outcomes in immigration court proceedings.
