News from Immigration and Customs Enforcement: "Todd Lyons is to resign as acting director of Immigration and Customs Enforcement . . . . His last day as ICE head will be May 31, Department of Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin announced" yesterday.

Lyons oversaw the Trump administration's "mass deportation campaign," which included controversial operations in Los Angeles, Minneapolis, and other cities.
