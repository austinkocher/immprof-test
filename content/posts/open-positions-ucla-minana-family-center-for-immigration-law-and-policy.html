UCLA's Miñana Family Center for Immigration Law and Policy (Miñana Center) is hiring for a Director of Litigation & Advocacy/Clinical Instructor and Staff Attorney.

Director of Litigation & Advocacy and Clinical Instructor, Miñana Family Center for Immigration Law and Policy, UCLA School of Law

We are seeking a talented and committed individual with significant immigration litigation and policy advocacy experience to serve as Director of Litigation & Advocacy and Clinical Instructor for the Miñana Center and its associated Immigrants’ Rights Policy Clinic.

The Director will assist in shaping the Center’s strategic litigation and advocacy projects, and contribute to its research and teaching programs, working both within and outside of the Clinic. They will also support development and implementation of strategic litigation and policy projects for the Center, including those well-suited to experiential learning; supervise Center staff attorneys and students participating in litigation and policy projects through the spring Clinic; and support both the Clinic and other Center litigation, policy advocacy, and public education projects.

Application deadline: We will begin reviewing applications beginning on December 20, 2026, with applications accepted through January 14, 2027. 

Staff Attorney, Miñana Family Center for Immigration Law and Policy, UCLA School of Law

We are seeking a staff attorney to engage in creative, high-quality research, analysis and litigation related to immigration law and policy. The Staff Attorney will further the Center's mission by conducting legal and multi-disciplinary research on a range of immigration law and policy related issues, including the Center's work related to exploring state and local-level innovation in immigration law. The Staff Attorney will also participate actively in the Center's litigation projects, including potential new litigation as well as current cases involving immigration detention, the Temporary Protected Status program, and others. The Staff Attorney will provide research and writing support to senior scholars at the Center and will contribute to reports, academic articles, public comments, and other Center work.

Application deadline: December 20, 2026
