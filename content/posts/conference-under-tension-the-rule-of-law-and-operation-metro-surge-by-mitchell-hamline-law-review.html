2026 Symposium: Under Tension: The Rule of Law and Operation Metro Surge

November 5, 2026
8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Central Time
Mitchell Hamline School of Law Auditorium
In person and livestreamed via Zoom

Under Tension: The Rule of Law and Operation Metro Surge: Join us for a timely conversation on the rule of law in the wake of Operation Metro Surge. We'll dig into the constitutional questions this moment has raised, and what they mean for how power is exercised and checked in Minnesota.

Hear from scholars, practitioners, public officials and community voices, including keynote speaker Dean Erwin Chemersinky of UC Berkeley School of Law. It's a great opportunity to connect, learn, and exchange ideas with others who care deeply about justice and constitutional governance.
