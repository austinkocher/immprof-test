Washington Post headline: "Trump administration says immigration enforcement threatens higher food prices." No surprises here. "The Labor Department warned in an obscure document filed with the Federal Register last week that `the near total cessation of the inflow of illegal aliens' is threatening `the stability of domestic food production and prices for U.S. consumers.'”
