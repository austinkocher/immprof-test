Reports of increased immigration enforcement in California are raising questions about arrests and federal operations:

"Reports of increased immigration enforcement have affected daily routines in parts of the Central Valley, where residents have expressed concern about traveling to work and visiting public places. . . .

A Fresno city councilmember also reported declining school attendance as families responded to concerns about immigration activity. . . .

For agricultural businesses, reduced worker attendance can create operational difficulties during periods when crops require harvesting or processing. The broader economic effects of the reported enforcement increase remain uncertain . . . ."
