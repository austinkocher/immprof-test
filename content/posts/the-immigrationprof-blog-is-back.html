The ImmigrationProf blog is back!  Thanks to the hard work of Austin Kocher, we have found a new home.  Our previous posts since 2005 have been preserved and will be available.  Immigration news continues to come from Washington.  The Trump administration seeks review in the Supreme Court of the President’s executive order dismantling birthright citizenship. The administration announced a $100,000 fee on H1B visas.   Stay tuned for the latest developments in immigration law and policy.
