Courthouse News reports that a federal court in Massachusetts ruled that updated policy guidance allowing U.S. immigration enforcement in and around houses of worship unlawfully burdens religious exercise.

“It is of course true that the presence of millions of illegal immigrants within the borders of the United States justifies a substantial government response,” U.S. District Judge F. Dennis Saylor IV said in the opinion. “But the need to address that problem cannot override the fundamental liberties on which our nation was founded."
