Ling Tat for the Orange County Register provides information on more than two dozen immigration-related bills are making their way through the California legislative process:

"Some would make it harder for U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement or U.S. Border Patrol agents to operate in California or aim to regulate their conduct. Others examine conditions at detention facilities or address private companies and other organizations that contract with or are viewed as having some affiliation, either directly or indirectly, with ICE operations."

You can read about them on the Orange County Register's immigration-related state bills tracker, which is updated.
