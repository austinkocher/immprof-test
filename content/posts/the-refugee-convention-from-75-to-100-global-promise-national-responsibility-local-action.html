Event at St. Louis Law:

Tuesday, November 10, 2026. 4:15 PM - 6:00 PM (CST)

The Refugee Convention from 75 to 100: Global Promise, National Responsibility, Local Action

What does the Refugee Convention’s promise mean today and what will it require over the next 25 years?

Marking the Convention’s 75th anniversary, this conversation brings together perspectives from international protection, domestic immigration law, local legal services, and faith communities. Speakers will consider how global commitments become national responsibilities and local action, and what protection can make possible in people’s lives.
