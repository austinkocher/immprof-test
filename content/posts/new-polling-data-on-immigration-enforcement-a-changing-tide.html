Third Way and UnidosUS have released new national polling that sheds light on how voters— especially Latinos — are responding to the Trump administration’s immigration policies.
Third Way and UnidosUS worked with Impact Research and BSP Research to conduct a phone and text-to-web survey of 1,000 likely voters and an oversample of 850 Hispanic voters.

Here's what the polling data showed:

  • Voters have soured on Trump’s immigration enforcement campaign.
  • Erosion on this issue among Hispanic voters is more acute, helping to erase their 2024 bump in support for both Trump and the Republican party.
  • There is support for reforms to ICE that are being considered by policymakers, and voters want to see those reforms enacted before the agency received any more funding.

The survey underscores a broader consensus: voters want both a secure border and immigration policies that respect rights and due process. Read the full memo to better understand where public opinion stands.
