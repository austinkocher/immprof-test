The National Immigration Project, with Ready to Stay and CLINIC, has issued this resource: Practice Advisory: Options for Relief Under Asylum Law for LGBTQ Applicants. This updated advisory discusses new LGBTQ precedent. The resource also includes a sample brief, sample country conditions table of contents, and sample CLP briefing in its appendices.
