Chris Hardee on Just Security critically assesses a recent Wall Street Journal editorial titled “How the Deep State Thwarted ICE Administrative Warrants,” in which Department of Homeland Security General Counsel James “Jimmy” Percival outlined the administration’s legal case for why Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) can enter homes without a judicial warrant.
