From Latino USA:

Caitlin Dickerson, a Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist at The Atlantic, is one of the leading voices on immigration coverage for more than a decade. In this episode on Latino USA, Dickerson talks about her recent reporting exploring why some mixed-status families are choosing to “self-deport,” what that actually looks like and why we should all consider the impact of what gets left behind. Later, she breaks down the latest on what children and their parents are experiencing in the Dilley family detention facility in Texas.
