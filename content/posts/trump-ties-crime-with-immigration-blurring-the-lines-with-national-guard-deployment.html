NPR reports on noncitizen fears because of uncertainty about the armed perons enforcing the immigration laws. "We already don't know who is grabbing us, whether it's ICE or whether it's people who disguise themselves as ICE," a noncitizen said in Spanish. "With the National Guard, it's going to be even harder, scarier."

After focusing on Los Angeles for months, the Trump deportation campaign has made its way to Chicago, generating fear in immigrant communities.
