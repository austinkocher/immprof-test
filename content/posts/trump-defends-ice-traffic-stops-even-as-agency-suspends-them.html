As Kevin wrote yesterday, ICE moved to suspend most traffic stops in the wake of the agency's TWO deadly shootings just this week. However, President Trump reversed course. He took to social media to defend the practice writing: "we CANNOT give up one of I.C.E.'s most important and effective Crime Fighting tools, THE TRAFFIC STOP!"
