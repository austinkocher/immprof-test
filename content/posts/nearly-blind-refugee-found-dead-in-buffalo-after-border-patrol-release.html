A nearly blind 56‑year‑old refugee was found dead in downtown Buffalo days after being released to the U.S. Border Patrol, according to city officials. He was reported missing on February 19 after Border Patrol agents took custody of him from a local jail following his release. He was left at a café about 5 miles from his home without his family or lawyer being notified, his attorney told a news outlet.
