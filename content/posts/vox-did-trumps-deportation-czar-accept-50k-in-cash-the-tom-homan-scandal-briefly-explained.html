While we were on hiatus, an alleged scandal involving President Trump's Border Czar Tom Homan made headlines. Cameron Peters for Vox provides the basic details.  The Trump administration ended a federal bribery investigation into Homan. "Last year, . . . `border czar' Tom Homan was reportedly recorded accepting $50,000 in cash from `businessmen' — undercover FBI agents — in exchange for promising to help secure government contracts, spurring a Justice Department investigation."
