The roving immigration enforcement patrols in Los Angeles, Chicago, Minneapolis, Charlotte, New Orleans, and other cities have had an impact. There has been a 600% increase in “at large” arrests, occurring within communities and at worksites, during the first nine months of Trump’s second term, according to a new report from the American Immigration Council. Are our communities safer today than they were in December 2025?
