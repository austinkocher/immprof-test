When I recommended Sublimation four days ago, I'd only made it through half of the book. I have now finished it, and I'm here to tell you READ THIS ONE. Seriously. It is the most original and engrossing fictional take on immigration that I've ever read.

And if you read it, please get in touch. I'm desperate to talk to someone about it.

Oh, and as an audiobook aficionado, I definitely recommend the audiobook. It does some really interesting things that leave me wondering what those particular moments look like in print. (I'm going to pick up a print copy to see.)

Happy summer reading everyone!
