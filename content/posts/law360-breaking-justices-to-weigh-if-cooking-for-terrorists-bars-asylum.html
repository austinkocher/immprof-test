Britain Eakin for Law360 reports that the U.S. Supreme Court today granted review "whether a Nigerian man's short stint as a cook for a terrorist group constituted `material support' that would render the already deported man ineligible for asylum."

The Court granted the U.S. government's petition for certiorari to review a Fourth Circuit decision, "which held that Izuchukwu Ozurumba's work preparing meals for leaders of a group known as the `Unknown Gunmen' did not sufficiently help the group carry out terrorist activities in Nigeria."
