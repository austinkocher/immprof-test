The White House recently issued a press release proclaiming that President Trump’s Immigration Policies Are Saving American Lives. Specifically, the release states that:

  1. Traffic fatalities have fallen to the second-lowest level in American history following President Donald J. Trump’s crackdown on illegal alien truck drivers.
  2. Overdose deaths fell 11.5% amid record fentanyl seizures and the lowest levels of cross-border drug trafficking in years, driving down fentanyl pill purity.
  3. Violent crime dropped 9.3% in 2025, including an 18.1% drop in murder and nonnegligent manslaughter — the largest annual drop in violent crime ever recorded — while ICE conducted a record 491,000 immigration arrests and 483,000 deportations in FY26. 
  4. Migrant deaths fell nearly 80% in FY26 from Biden-era peaks as the Trump Administration delivered 18 straight months of zero releases at the southern border and drove illegal crossings down 95% from the Biden-era monthly average. 
  5. Approximately 157,000 of the roughly 300,000 unaccompanied migrant children lost under Biden have been located in President Trump’s second term.
  6. The Trump Administration has rescued more than 7,200 children from traffickers and predators (a 42% increase over the previous Administration) and arrested more than 3,500 predators and human traffickers (a 102% increase).

Once again, it's time for a reminder that correlation does not equal causation. Just ask Tyler Virgin, the genius behind spurious correlations.
