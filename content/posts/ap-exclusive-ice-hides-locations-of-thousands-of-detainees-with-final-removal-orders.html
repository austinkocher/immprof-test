Rust never sleeps. The Associated Press reports a troubling development. Immigration and Customs Enforcement has stopped publicly disclosing where it is detaining thousands of detainees who are subject to final removal orders. The change in policy makes it more difficult for lawyers and relatives to locate detainees. Where are they?
