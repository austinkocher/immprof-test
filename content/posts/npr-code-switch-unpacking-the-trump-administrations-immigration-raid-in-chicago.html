This episode of NPR's Code Switch looks at the recent immigration enforcement actions in Chicago. After midnight on September 30, helicopters hovered over a large Chicago apartment building, and heavily armored agents rappelled from the choppers onto the roof. What unfolded swept up both undocumented migrants and U.S. citizens. This is one of the most high-profile and aggressive raids in President Trump’s ongoing mass deportation campaign.
