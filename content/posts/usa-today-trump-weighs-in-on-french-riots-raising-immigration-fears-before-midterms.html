USA Today reports that "Two weeks out from the midterm elections, President Donald Trump alleged that demonstrations in France were the fault of immigrants and politicians on the left, though French leaders have said they're centered around affordability.

After anti-government protests spiraled into looting and rioting across the country over the weekend, Trump told reporters at the White House that `migrants have taken over France.'" (bold added).
