CONFERENCE: Got Habeas? New Clinics, Theories, and Developments in Immigration Detention Litigation and Teaching, Friday, Oct. 9, 2026 | 10 a.m. – 4 p.m. Pacific | King Hall, Room 1001 | MCLE Credits Available | No Registration Fee | Livestreamed. Lunch provided to registrants. Register in advance.

  • Chloe Dillon, Criminal Immigration Defense, San Mateo County Private Defender Program

Who Should Attend

Practitioners and clinicians who work on immigration cases — or who might want to — as well as law students and others interested in this emerging area of law. The conference is open to lawyers, faculty, and students interested in providing representation to detained persons.
