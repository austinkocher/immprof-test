Retired Federal Judge Nancy Gertner in The Atlantic offers thoughts on how the Supreme Court has made Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) unaccountable for violating the law. Her conclusion: "Much of what ICE is doing is not remotely constitutional. The Court decisions that laid the groundwork for the agency’s lawlessness no longer stand up to basic scrutiny. "
