Ami Hutchinson clued me into The Appeal's ICE Contract Database, something the news organization put together as part of its investigation Deportation Dollars: ICE Contracts With More Than 1,000 Businesses Across the U.S..

Naturally, I looked at Oklahoma first. Nothing too interesting there (go us!). Then I plugged in my sister's L.A. neighborhood. I found all sorts of connections including to a title company and a software company for accountants and legal professionals.

I could see pulling up this database in class and having students check their home zip codes to see the range of entities that have relationships with the agency.
