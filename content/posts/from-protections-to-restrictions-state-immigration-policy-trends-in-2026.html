A report from the American Immigration Council on state immigration policy trends in 2026 found many state legislatures across the country passed bills intended to support and safeguard immigrant communities, including efforts to reduce states’ roles in immigration detention and protect state-held immigrant data, while other states pursued restrictive policies focused particularly on reducing voter eligibility for noncitizens.
