News from Oregon.

"Shocking video shows the moment ICE agents slashed the tires of a couple as they filmed.

After damaging the car, the agents left without making any arrests or speaking to the couple, the footage shows. The video was released by the American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU), which has been documenting ICE operations across the country after Donald Trump's anti-immigrant crackdown began last year."
