Amy Howe for SCOTUSBlog ("Trump administration again urges justices to allow deportations to third-party countries" writes that "[t]elling the justices that it had been deprived of `an essential tool to remove certain aliens, including some of the worst criminal aliens,' the Trump administration . . . asked the [Supreme Court], for the third time, to clear the way for the government to deport noncitizens to countries that are not identified in their removal orders – a procedure known as `third-country removals.'”
