--- title: "Grand Jury Indicts ICE Agent Who Ran Over Fleeing Migrant" date: 2026-11-01T16:04:14-05:00 slug: "grand-jury-indicts-ice-agent-who-ran-over-fleeing-migrant" url: "/grand-jury-indicts-ice-agent-who-ran-over-fleeing-migrant/" sections: ["Lawsuits"] tags: ["Use of force and misconduct", "Criminal prosecutions", "Arrests and raids"] authors: ["Kit Johnson"] ---

Three weeks ago, we all watched the horrifying video of a migrant being run over by an unmarked SUV driven by an ICE agent.

Although the government said the injured migrant "ran in front of an ICE vehicle," the video contradicts that characterization. And a Houston grand jury has now indicted the driver, ICE agent Mario Alberto Morales, for the second-degree felony offense of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon.

Harris County District Attorney Sean Teare told the press that "An ICE badge does not give you a free rein to injure, kill, or violate the constitutional rights of anyone" and that he hopes the prosecution "sends a loud and clear message to every ICE officer who feels they have a free pass to terrorize our streets."
