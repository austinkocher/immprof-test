The saga of Kilmar Abrego Garcia, the Maryland resident who was wrongfully removed and imprisoned in El Salvador, ordered returned by the Supreme Court, and indicted for trafficking immigrant workers. Earlier this week, an immigration judge denied Abrego Garcia's latest effort to seek asylum. The court rejected a motion to reopen his asylum case. Abrego Garcia has 30 days to appeal the ruling to the Board of Immigration Appeals.
