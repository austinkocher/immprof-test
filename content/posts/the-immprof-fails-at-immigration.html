Today I am en route to the UK having purchased a ticket late yesterday afternoon. My husband took me to the airport way earlier than I would normally arrive, and thank goodness. Because this immprof failed at immigration.

Did you know that you have to get visa approval before heading to the UK these days? I did not. It didn't even occur to me. I had the whole visa waiver program in my mind--forgetting the fact that even VWP travelers need to complete an online checkin before heading to the US. I didn't even look to see if the UK had such a thing--they didn't when I last traveled to England a few years ago. I just pulled up to the ticket counter with my passport thinking I'm all set. And. no. I was not.

I'm grateful to report that the process of getting approval is really simple. I downloaded the Home Office's "UK ETA" app. Uploaded all my info. And prayed that I'd get approval in time to make my flight. Thankfully, I did. Woot. Woot.
