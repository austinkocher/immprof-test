Kansas Attorney General Kris Kobach, chairman of the Republican Attorneys General Association, joins the Center for Immigration Studies podcast Parsing Immigration Policy for a discussion of immigration enforcement, voter integrity, and state-federal cooperation. I incorrectly had thought that this immigration enforcement advocate was out of politics. He has Kansas assisting in U.S. immigration enforcement.

Kobach shares with host Mark Krikorian insights into the efforts that states like Kansas are taking to combat illegal immigration.
