Adam Feldman for SCOTUSBLOG ("What oral argument told us in the birthright citizenship case") "closely analyze[s] the transcript to really understand how the argument unfolded – who drove it, what it centered on, and what it tells us about a possible outcome." His bottom line: "Overall, the justices tested both arguments – and the data suggest they found both sides’ positions vulnerable at specific points, but a majority appeared skeptical of the government’s theory. We should know for sure by late summer, when a decision is expected."
