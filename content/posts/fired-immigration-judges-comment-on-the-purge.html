We have been posting regularly about the Trump administration's purge of immigration judges. This report ("Former judges speak out on Trump admin's immigration court purges") from CBS News offers comments from some of the fired immigration judges. "Former immigration judge Ryan Wood told us there is no doubt among immigration judges these days as to what the administration wants: `Zero doubt, they want numbers, they want deportations. They want to keep as many people detained as possible, and stress the system,' he said."
