Yesterday, 60 Minutes aired a report on third-country removals. It's the result of a 5-month investigation, and it clocks in at a compact 13 minutes:

The report follows the story of one man who had received withholding of removal protecting him from removal to his home country of Mali. The U.S. deported him to Ghana where he was held under guard and then forcibly returned to Mali.

DHS counsel James Percival appears on the segment, pushing these messages: (1) migrants can choose to return to their home countries (and lie about whether they so chose); and (2) the country where migrants are deported are free to follow their own processes and procedures and to determine that it is safe to deport these migrants to their countries of origin.

The reporter pushes for an answer about whether the U.S. knows that the countries it is sending migrants to are simply turning around and sending those migrants to countries that a U.S. judge has determined to be unsafe for the affected individual. Percival responds: "I'd really like to talk about the public safety threats associated with this population of aliens and the fact that every administration before President Trump absolutely abdicated their responsibility to the American people by letting criminals back on the street and doing no diplomatic efforts to get these people out of our country."
