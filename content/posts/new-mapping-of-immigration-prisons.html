The Institute to End Mass Incarceration has a new resource developed in conjunction with their Exposing Prisons Project.

The tool is an interactive map that includes prisons, jails, and immigration detention facilities in the U.S.

Filters allow you to explore specific facilities, including isolating ICE detention and contract facilities, and to find connected information about state assembly districts, state senate districts, and federal districts.
