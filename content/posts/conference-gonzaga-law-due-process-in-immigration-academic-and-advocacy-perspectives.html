Gonzaga University School of Law invites you to a full-day summit exploring the evolving landscape of due process in immigration law. Join local and national stakeholders for conversations on current developments in immigration law and policy. Topics will include recent changes impacting the education and business sectors, as well as the effects of new rules and regulations on individuals seeking asylum and other forms of relief.


Through two dynamic panel discussions, attendees will gain insight into the intersections of legal scholarship and advocacy within the immigration system. The event will feature a keynote address by Hiroshi Motomura, a renowned immigration and citizenship law scholar from UCLA School of Law.
