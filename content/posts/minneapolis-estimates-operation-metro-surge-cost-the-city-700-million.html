From Minnesota Public Radio: "The city of Minneapolis estimates the federal immigration enforcement surge from December 2025 to April 2026 cost the city, residents and businesses $700 million." That figure includes losses in business revenue and wages for employees, and additional costs to the city, such as police overtime.
