Handbook on Migration and Human Rights

Edited by Ruth Rubio Marín. Elgar Handbooks in Migration
This Research Handbook examines the complex issues faced by migrants and refugees in securing their human rights. By challenging and reformulating the crisis narrative often perpetuated by states and international organizations, it provides a gendered, cutting-edge, in-depth investigation of key themes central to the human rights implications of migration.
