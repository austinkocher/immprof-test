    Gemma Ware, The Conversation

    Daniel Cueto-Villalobos speaks to The Conversation Weekly podcast about the origins of people in Minneapolis coming together to protect their neighbours.

Check out the podcast about a grass roots response to ICE excesses. This episode of The Conversation Weekly podcast allows us to hear from Daniel Cueto-Villalobos, a doctoral candidate at the University of Minnesota, who lives in southern Minneapolis and studies race, religion and social movements. He tracks the neighborhood groups that have sprung into action in response to the ICE presence back to mutual networks set up during the 2020 COVID pandemic, and in the wake of the murder of George Floyd by a Minneapolis policeman.
