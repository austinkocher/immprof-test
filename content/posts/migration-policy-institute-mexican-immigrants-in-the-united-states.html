Mexican Immigrants in the United States

By Andres Ayala and Jeanne Batalova

Mexican immigrants remain the largest foreign-born group in the United States, but their numbers are down. The U.S. Mexican-born population has fallen modestly from a 2007 peak of more than 11.7 million.

Mexican immigrants are concentrated in a handful of states and metropolitan areas.

This article provides information on the Mexican immigrant population in the United States.
