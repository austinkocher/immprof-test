Is the public opinion tide turning on immigration. Kit Johnson blogged yesterday about a NBC News poll showed that the public has mixed views on immigration. Another poll from Fox News conducted before President Trump announced last week that he will nominate Senator Markwayne Mullin (R-OK) to replace Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem. "The survey finds nearly 6 in 10 voters disapprove of ICE’s performance, while about 4 in 10 approve. This is the highest disapproval rating recorded, up 7 points since last fall and 17 points since the question was first asked in 2018."
