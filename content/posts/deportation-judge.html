A recent job posting by the Department of Justice seeks applicants for the position of "deportation judge." The position of a "deportation" judge does not exist--only immigration judges.

At the same time, the administration has been firing immigration judges. Just last week it was reported that five more judges were terminated in San Francisco.

The recruitment advertisement declares that as a "deportation judge" you will "define American for generations" and "make decisions with generational consequences."
