Citizenship Approvals Drop Under Trump

The Trump administration has drastically slowed the application process for prospective US citizens — extending wait times for immigrants hoping to vote, reunite with family, or protect themselves from deportation, Ximena Bustillo and Andrew Kreighbaum report.

US Citizenship and Immigration Services’ latest quarterly data show it decided around 71,000 cases between April and June of this year, compared to around 265,000 during the same period last year — a 73% drop. Among the cases decided, the denial rate has climbed, more than doubling in Los Angeles to 18%.
