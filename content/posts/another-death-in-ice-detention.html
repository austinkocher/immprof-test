Roberto Jimenez Gutierrez, a 43-year-old Guatemalan man, died in Otay Mesa Detention Center in San Diego on Saturday, according to a news release from ICE. The agency reports he was found “unresponsive.” Daylight San Diego.

The ICE announcement (ICE.gov) states that

"Jimenez’s criminal history includes a July 2024 conviction for selling liquor to a minor, as well as prior arrests for battery against a spouse, violation of a court order, child abuse, disorderly conduct, providing false identification to a peace officer, and being under the influence of drugs." (bold in original).
