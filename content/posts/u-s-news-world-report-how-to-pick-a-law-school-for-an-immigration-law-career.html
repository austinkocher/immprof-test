This U.S. News item from March 2025 by Anayat Durrani seemed to me to give good advice to prospective law students thinking about an immigration law career. It offers "tips on how to pick a law school for an immigration law career." It quotes an immigration attorney who said that “[w]hat matters most is finding a school that supports your interests and provides the right resources, such as immigration law courses, clinics and mentorship opportunities . . . . The key is to find a school that aligns with your passion and career goals.”
