The University of Minnesota is hosting two immigration law events.

This year’s Binger Center for New Americans Fall Forum on November 7 explores the themes of being, becoming, and belonging within the American experiment. Sessions will offer historical perspectives on citizenship, engage in debates and innovative approaches to protecting immigrant rights in our communities, and provide practical tools for discussing immigration issues with those who hold different views. A brief reception will follow. Get info here.  Register here.  There is no cost to register.  Virtual registration is still available. 

Immigration Case Update: Important Developments in Current Litigation Nov. 19
Litigation has been an important tool to protect and advance the rights of noncitizens in the United States. Cases impacting immigrants, mixed status families, and communities have become increasingly complex under the current administration. Join experts from the Binger Center for New Americans to learn about important developments in current litigation related to immigration law. Students and faculty from the Federal Immigration Litigation Clinic will discuss the most recent updates on high-profile cases, emerging trends in Eighth Circuit case law impacting Minnesotans, and important shifts in nationwide litigation strategy. Get info here.  Register here.
