Newsweek reports that President Donald Trump has lost ground on immigration, a new poll from Reuters/Ipsos has found. The poll was conducted ​online over four days and published on August 17. 1,166 U.S. adults nationwide responded and had a margin of error of 3 percent. "The poll showed that 40 percent of registered voters said that Republicans had the better approach on immigration policy, compared to 38 percent who chose Democrats."
