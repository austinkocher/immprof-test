 Philip Marcelo, Jennifer Peltz and Anthony Izaguirre for the Associated Press reports thar "[a] federal immigration officer shot a Dominican man in the neck as he drove in a car with a 5-year-old boy [yesterday], officials said. The Department of Homeland Security said it was seeking to arrest the man in a `targeted enforcement operation,' but protesters and local officials decried it as an example of the dangers of the Trump administration’s immigration crackdown."

The shooting victim survived.

President Trump's second term has been marked by an increase in migrant deaths in detention and killings by ICE officers of migrants and U.S. citizens in immigration stops and other enforcement activities. (and here).
