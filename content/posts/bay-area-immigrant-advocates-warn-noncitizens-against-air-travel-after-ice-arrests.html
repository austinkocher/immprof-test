KQED reports that local rapid response networks report that over a dozen people have been arrested in regional airports since late June. More than a dozen people have been detained at Bay Area airports since June 27. 

Following the surge in arrests, immigrants’ rights advocates are urging people without U.S. citizenship to avoid air travel.
