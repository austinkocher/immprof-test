Bad Bunny made big news with the much-watched Super Bowl half time show. Last week, NPR's Alt.Latino podcast (and here) had an episode about Bad Bunny's career and what it means for Puerto Rico and Latin music's role in the United States.  "Bad Bunny's Super Bowl halftime show was many things: a time-traveling journey through the history of Puerto Rico, a statement of pan-American unity, and a masterpiece of cultural expression."
