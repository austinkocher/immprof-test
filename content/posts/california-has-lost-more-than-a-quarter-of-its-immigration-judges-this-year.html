The Los Angeles Times reports that "More than a quarter of federal immigration judges in California have been fired, retired or quit since the start of the Trump administration. . . . The reduction follows a trend in immigration courts nationwide and constitutes, critics say, an attack on the rule of law that will lead to yet more delays in an overburdened court system."
