Deportation Theater: An Analysis of Ice’s Twitter Account by Michael Kagan

Abstract
Although the social media company formerly known as Twitter has been renamed X.com since its purchase by Elon Musk, I will continue to call it "Twitter" and individual posts as "tweets." The site is still widely known by these names, and they are also far easier to use grammatically in an Essay than any derivative of the new name.
