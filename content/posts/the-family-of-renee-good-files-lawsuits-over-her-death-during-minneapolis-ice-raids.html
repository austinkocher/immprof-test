NPR: Renee Good's family filed two civil lawsuits against the U.S. government yesterday over her death last January. Good was fatally shot by a federal agent during an immigration enforcement operation in Minnesota. The first lawsuit alleges that an ICE officer used excessive force. The second alleges that U.S. officials conspired to interfere with the civil rights of individuals in Minnesota.
