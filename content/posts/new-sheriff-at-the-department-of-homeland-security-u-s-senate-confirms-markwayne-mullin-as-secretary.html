The Senate confirmed Senator Markwayne Mullin yesterday as the new secretary of Homeland Security. The 54-45 confirmation vote puts the Oklahoma Republican. As NPR reports, "Mullin will be replacing Kristi Noem, making him the second secretary to lead the agency during this Trump administration. He assumes leadership in the midst of a shutdown that has forced 100,000 of the department’s more than a quarter-million employees to work without pay."
