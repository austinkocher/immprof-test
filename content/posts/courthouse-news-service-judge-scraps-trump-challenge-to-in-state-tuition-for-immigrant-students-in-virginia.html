 The Trump administration has challenged a number of state laws allowing undocumented residents to pay in-state tuition at public universities and colleges. Yesterday, a federal court upheld Virginia's law.

Granting summary judgment for Virginia, "Senior U.S. District Judge Robert Payne found both the domicile pathway and the tuition equity pathway of 2020’s Virginia Dream Act are not preempted by federal law."
