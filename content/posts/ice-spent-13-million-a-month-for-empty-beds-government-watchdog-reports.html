NBC News reports that "ICE spent an estimated $13 million a month for empty beds at five recently acquired detention centers and overpaid by more than 50% for nine megawarehouses — most of which the immigration enforcement agency is now trying to offload, according to a new report from the" U.S Government Accountability Office.

"In total, ICE spent more than $1 billion in taxpayer money on the warehouses. According to the report, ICE paid more than triple the assessed value for three of the warehouses."

The GAO report makes two recommendations:

"We are making the following two recommendations to ICE: The Director of ICE should ensure that agency spending plans for ICE detention funds, including those received through the reconciliation process, are updated in a timely manner in accordance with ICE policy and include all required elements. (Recommendation 1)

The Director of ICE, in coordination with the DHS Undersecretary for Management, should develop life-cycle cost estimates and evaluate alternatives to inform any significant future detention facility investments. (Recommendation 2)"
