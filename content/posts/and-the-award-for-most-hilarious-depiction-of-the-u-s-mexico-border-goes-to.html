Last night I found myself watching Fast & Furious--the 2009 film of the F&F franchise not to be confused with The Fast and the Furious (2001). I could blame the choice on one of my two young adult boys but, true confession, I was home alone. No judgment.

The movie deserves an award for The Most Hilarious Depiction of the U.S.-Mexico Border. Since it's a F&F film, you can guess what's involved... cars. going fast. But it's the border, so, cars going fast through a secret tunnel. Border Patrol is on the case but their heat seeking radars and helicopters are no match for this crew. Epic.
