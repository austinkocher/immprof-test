News from the front! The Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors yesterday declared a state of emergency over the ongoing federal immigration raids. Read the proclamation.

The vote allows the county to mobilize resources, request state and federal financial assistance to respond to the impacts of the raids, and expedite contracting to address the crisis.
