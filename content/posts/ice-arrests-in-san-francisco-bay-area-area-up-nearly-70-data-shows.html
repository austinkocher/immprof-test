The Deportation Data Project at UC Berkeley released figures showing that Immigration and Customs Enforcement arrests in the San Francisco area increased nearly 70% compared with the same period a year earlier. Data released by the project showed 2,928 ICE arrests from January through July 2025, compared with 4,970 arrests during the same period in 2026.
