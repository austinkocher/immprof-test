Born in Venezuela, William Contreras of the Milwaukee Brewers was named World Series MVP as the Brewers won the Series in five games.

Contreras has had quite a year:

"William Contreras won two championships this year. The first one came in March.

The second came Wednesday night and ended with the Milwaukee Brewers catcher holding the Willie Mays World Series MVP trophy.

Contreras hit .381 (8-for-21) with four RBIs as Milwaukee beat the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 for the first title in franchise history. Seven months after he won the World Baseball Classic with Venezuela, he became the first World Series MVP the Brewers have ever had."
