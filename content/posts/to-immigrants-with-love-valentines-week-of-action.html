This week, (February 9 to 15), communities across the country will peacefully participate in “To Immigrants With Love,” a national Valentine’s Day week of action featuring coordinated actions, vigils, digital mobilization, calls for accountability, congressional outreach, and other community-based acts of care in solidarity with immigrant communities.
