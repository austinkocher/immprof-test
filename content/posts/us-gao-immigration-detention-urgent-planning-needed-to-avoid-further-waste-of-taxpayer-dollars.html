News reports abound that U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement has wasted millions of taxpayer dollars in the Trump administration's immigration crackdown, according to a Government Accountability Office report.

Here are "fast facts" from the report:

"Since January 2025, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement has invested billions to expand detention capacity through six initiatives—including one to purchase and renovate warehouses. ICE purchased 11 warehouses for $1 billion.

We found that ICE pursued this and the other initiatives without necessary analysis and planning. ICE now intends to sell 7 of the 11 warehouses. But ICE spent over $20 million on costs it can't recover, such as security.

Developing a strategic plan for detention would help ICE better achieve its goals while also making better use of taxpayer dollars.

Our recommendation addresses this issue."
