Thousands of immigrants with legal status lose Medicaid coverage
Hundreds of thousands of immigrants living legally in the U.S. recently lost their Medicaid coverage under a provision of President Trump's signature tax and spending law. The law is projected to reduce federal Medicaid spending by $900 billion over the next decade. Deema Zein reports on what that loss of coverage means for some of those affected.
