The Associated Press reports that police arrested about 100 clergy demonstrating against immigration enforcement at a Minnesota’s airport yesterday, and several thousand gathered in Minneapolis despite Arctic temperatures to protest the Trump administration’s immigration crackdown.

"The protests are part of a broader movement against President Donald Trump’s increased immigration enforcement . . . , with labor unions, progressive organizations and clergy urging Minnesotans to stay away from work, school and even shops. The faith leaders gathered at the airport to protest deportation flights and urge airlines to call for an end to what the Department of Homeland Security has called its largest-ever immigration enforcement operation."
