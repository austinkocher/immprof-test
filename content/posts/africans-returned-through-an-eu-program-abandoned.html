The European Union has allocated hundreds of millions of dollars to help African migrants return from journeys to Europe. But The Associated Press interviewed several returnees and saw a WhatsApp group of dozens more who say they've received little or nothing. The International Organization for Migration is concerned that people are left waiting. Read the AP report.
