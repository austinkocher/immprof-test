News from Colorado. Hundreds of Colorado students walked out of school yesterday as part of a nationwide protest against Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) operations. "Organizers said students from more than 180 Colorado schools were scheduled to participate in walkouts at noon. Protests were also planned at schools across the country."
