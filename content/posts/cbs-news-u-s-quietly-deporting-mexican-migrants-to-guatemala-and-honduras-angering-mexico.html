CBS News reports that the Trump administration has been "deporting migrants from Mexico to different countries in Central America despite the Mexican government's willingness to accept the return of all its citizens, two Department of Homeland Security officials told CBS News."

U.S. immigration authorities have been sending deportees from Mexico to Guatemala and Honduras, "the DHS officials said, requesting anonymity because they were not authorized to talk to the media."

The officials said one of the objectives of the deportations is to deter illegal immigration from Mexico by sending some of its citizens far south of the U.S./Mexico border.
