 Laura Strickler and Julia Ainsley for NBC News reports that "[m]ore than a third of the roughly 220,000 people arrested by [Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE)] officers in the first nine months of the Trump administration had no criminal histories . . . . The data, which includes ICE arrests from Jan. 20 to Oct. 15, shows that nearly 75,000 people with no criminal records have been swept up in immigration operations that the president and his top officials have said would target murderers, rapists and gang members."
