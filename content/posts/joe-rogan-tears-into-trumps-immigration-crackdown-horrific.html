Are supporters of President Trump beginning to rethink his immigration approach? Here is news on that front. Joe Rogan referred to President Trump’s immigration policies during a recent episode of his podcast. “Let’s just talk about the immigration thing,” Rogan said. “The way it looks is horrific.”

Rogan is part of a group of podcasters credited with helping boost Trump's popularity, particularly with young male voters. He previously shared sharp words on his podcast about raids by Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE)

Conservative Congressmember Margaret Taylor Greene also has questioned the Trump administration's immigration enforcement policies.

Forbes has reported that the corporate executives believe that the immigration crackdown is tougher on businesses than tariffs.
