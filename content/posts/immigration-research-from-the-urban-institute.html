The Urban Institute recently released reports on topical immigration issues.

Taxes: Possible impacts of the Trump administration mass deportation campaign – Although they’re federally driven, immigration policies that may discourage tax filing, reduce federal financial support, and remove economic contributors from communities could strain the finances of many states and localities.

Data on Children of Immigrants: Explore the data and trends over time on children of immigrants and their parents, families, and households for states and the top 100 metropolitan areas. Understanding these data can inform better policies for all children and families.
