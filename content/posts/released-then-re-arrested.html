The Guardian today reports that noncitizens who have successfully fought for release from detention through habeas petitions are being re-arrested by ICE. TWENTY such cases have been identified by the news agency.

MPR also has coverage of this phenomenon--with the story of noncitizens winning habeas and being detained before even leaving the federal building.
