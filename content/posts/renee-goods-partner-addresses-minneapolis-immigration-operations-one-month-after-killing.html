NBC News reports that a month after Renee Nicole Good was killed by an immigration officer in Minneapolis, her partner released a statement yesterday, condemning the federal government’s immigration operations and urging the city to remember those whose names are never known.

In the statement, Becca Good thanked the city and said she was “so proud to call Minneapolis my home.”

“Renee was not the first person killed, and she was not the last,” she said. “You know my wife’s name and you know Alex’s name, but there are many others in this city being harmed that you don’t know — their families are hurting just like mine, even if they don’t look like mine.”

“They are neighbors, friends, coworkers, classmates. And we must also know their names,” she added. “Because this shouldn’t happen to anyone.”

Minnesota Public Radio published the full statement.
