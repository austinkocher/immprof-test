The Trump administration keeps coming, this time with the final public charge rule. Documented’s April Xu gives a deep dive into what you need to know about the rule change, and who might be impacted.

Announced last week, the new public charge rules could make it harder for many immigrants to become a lawful permanent resident if officials determine they are likely to rely on public benefits. Advocates warn that the rule could stoke fear among thousands of immigrant families, discouraging eligible immigrants from accessing health care and other services, and contributing to food insecurity and homelessness.
