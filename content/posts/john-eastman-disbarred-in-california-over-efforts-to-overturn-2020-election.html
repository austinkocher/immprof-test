News from The Hill:

"John Eastman, a lawyer who spearheaded efforts to overturn the results of the 2020 presidential election in President Trump’s favor, has been formally disbarred in California

The California Supreme Court [yesterday] declined to review his appeal of a lower court’s recommendation to strip him of his law license, instead ordering his disbarment."

Eastman, of course, was the mastermind behind President Trump's effort to curtail birthright citizenship, which is currently before the Supreme Court. Eastman formerly was a law professor and dean.
