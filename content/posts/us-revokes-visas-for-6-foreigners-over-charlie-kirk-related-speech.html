The Trump administration has revoked the visas of six foreigners deemed by U.S. officials to have made negative comments or made light of the assassination of Charlie Kirk. Read more. According to the Associated Press, "[t]he State Department said [yesterday] it had determined they should lose their visas after reviewing their online social media posts and clips about Kirk, who was killed while speaking at a Utah college campus on Sept. 10."
