From Documented:

Technology researchers have filed a lawsuit against the Trump administration, arguing that its immigration policy is being used to censor noncitizens who study social media platforms and violating their right to free speech. 

The suit, filed earlier this week by the Coalition for Independent Technology Research along with the Knight First Amendment Institute and Protect Democracy, alleges that the administration has been selectively denying visas and threatening to detain and deport individuals engaged in fact-checking, content moderation, trust and safety, and combating disinformation online, among other activities.
