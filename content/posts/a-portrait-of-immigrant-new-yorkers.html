The Center for Migration Studies has released A Portrait of Immigrant New Yorkers, a report on the city's immigrant population. New York has long been known as a city of immigrants, and today more than one in three New Yorkers (3.1 million people) are foreign-born. Almost a million (950,000 people, 1 in 9 residents) live in mixed-status households; 267,000 are children.
