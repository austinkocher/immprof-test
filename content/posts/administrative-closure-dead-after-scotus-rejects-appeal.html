The Supreme Court rejected an appeal brought by intervenors that would have preserved administrative closure.

Civ Pro nerds will love this one: Texas sued the DOJ to challenge the Biden Administration's reinstatement of administrative closure (it had been stopped under Trump 1). When Trump 2 came into office, the DOJ said they would no longer defend administrative closure and that they were okay with a permanent injunction enjoining the practice. Since this is what Texas wanted all along, it agreed. In came the intervenors: The City of Baltimore, The City of Columbus, The City of New Haven, and Centro Legal de la Raza. They appealed to the U.S. Supreme Court.

Today, the SCOTUS docket simply reads: "Application (26A500) for stay presented to Justice Alito and by him referred to the Court is denied."
