CBS News offers a troubling report. "More than 6,300 children under 18, some as young as two months old, have been detained by federal immigration authorities during President Trump's second term. . . . Ninety-seven percent had no criminal record."

Elora Mukherjee, director of the Immigrants Rights Clinic at Columbia, was interviewed for the story.

The report is less than ten minutes but chock full of information. Really, check it out. This may be worth showing in an immigration class.
