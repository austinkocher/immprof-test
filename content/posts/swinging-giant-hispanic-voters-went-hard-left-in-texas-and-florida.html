From USA Today:

"Two years after Latino voters helped Donald Trump return to the White House, they swung back toward Democrats in a major midterm upset in the Texas governor’s and congressional races that also left two longtime Miami Republicans in contests too close to call. 

The economy and the cost of living drove much of the shift, strategists on both sides of the aisle said. But they also point to immigration and President Donald Trump's enforcement campaign that reached deep into Latino neighborhoods over the past year."
