The Harvard Immigration and Refugee Clinical Program (HIRCP) at Harvard Law School is accepting applications for its 2026 Summer Internship Program. HIRCP is looking for law student interns interested in working with faculty and staff on various cases and projects. Interns will commit to at least 10 weeks, with the program running from June to August 2026. Interns can choose to work with either the Immigration and Refugee Advocacy Clinic or the Crimmigration Clinic. The deadline is December 30, 2025. Information regarding the program is here.
