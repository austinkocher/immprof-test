Nearly a year before federal immigration agents fatally shot Renee Macklin Good and Alex Pretti in incidents in Minneapolis, an Immigration and Customs Enforcement agent shot and killed 23-year-old Ruben Ray Martinez, a U.S. citizen, during a traffic encounter in Texas. Martinez is considered the first of at least six people immigration agents have killed since the start of President Trump's second term. At the time of his death, Martinez's mother, Rachel Reyes, and the public didn't know that it was an ICE officer who shot him. Reyes tells NPR that she feels betrayed by law enforcement, and that the new revelations about ICE's involvement have made grieving more painful.
