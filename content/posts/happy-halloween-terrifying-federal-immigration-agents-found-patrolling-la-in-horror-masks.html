News from the West Coast.

Federal agents in Los Angeles wearing Halloween costumes patrolled the city this week, including a mask from a horror movie and a mask related to a violent internet myth.

"Harbor Area Peace Patrols, a community group that has been protesting and documenting federal immigration activity in Los Angeles, spotted the activity earlier this week as officers left Terminal Island, a frequent staging ground for federal agents near the Port of Los Angeles. LA Taco, a local media outlet, first reported on the sightings."
