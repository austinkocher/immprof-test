I guess that Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem is not a fan of Bad Bunny, who recently was announced as the main feature of the upcoming Super Bowl half time show. As reported on the Hill, Noem said the NFL will not be “able to sleep at night” over its selecting Bad Bunny. “Well, they suck, and we’ll win, and God will bless us, and we’ll stand and be proud of ourselves at the end of the day, and they won’t be able to sleep at night because they don’t know what they believe, and they’re so weak, we’ll fix it,” Noem told a podcaster. 

The NFL has faced blowback over the selection of Bad Bunny as the halftime headliner. The artist said in an interview that he would not perform in the mainland United States over concerns his concerts could be the target of ICE raids.
