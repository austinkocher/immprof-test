As part of UC Law San Francisco's (formerly UC Hastings') Symposium on Rule of Law and Threats to Democracy, Karen Musalo will moderate a star-studded panel on the Constitutional and Statutory Rights of Non-Citizens (Friday, November 14, 2025 1:45-3pm Pacific Time).

Panelists:

  • Richard Boswell, Professor, UC Law SF
  • Lucas Guttentag, Professor of the Practice of Law, Stanford Law School; Martin R. Flug Lecturer in Law and Senior Research Scholar, Yale Law School
  • Jack Chin, Professor and Director of Clinical Legal Education, UC Davis School of Law
  • Jennifer Chacón, Professor, Stanford Law School 

Moderator:

  • Karen Musalo, Professor and Chair in International Law, Director of the Center for Gender & Refugee Studies, UC Law SF

Professors Blaine Bookey (CGRS), Ming H. Chen (RICE), Thalia González (CREJ) are co-organizing the day-long symposium on behalf of their centers. Keynote speakers include California Attorney General Rob Bonta and Berkeley Law Dean Erwin Chemerinsky. There will be an afternoon reception hosted by Chancellor and Dean David Faigman and Nicole Ozer, Executive Director from the newly-created Center for Constitutional Democracy at UC Law SF.
