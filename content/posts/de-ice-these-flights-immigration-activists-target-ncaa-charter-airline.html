Everyone loves March Madness and the NCAA basketball tournament. I was surprised to hear of the De-ICE These Flights! campaign. The campaign arises from the following:

“In 2025, GlobalX flew more than 1,700 deportation flights for ICE, while earning more than $246 million. And it’s the same airline teams have flown for March Madness. When schools fly GlobalX, they’re feeding a system that supports deportation: athletes one day, deportation routes the next. It’s the business model: profit by abusing people.”
