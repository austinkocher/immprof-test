CBS News reports that an immigration judge fired by the Trump administration is sharing the story of her dismissal and concerns about the immigration court system.

Former Judge Brea Burgie told CBS Colorado that she "tried to look at the law every day and apply the law as I saw it." She "is one of more than 100 immigration judges around the country who have been fired by DOJ."
