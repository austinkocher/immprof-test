This article ("The ever-evolving Latino vote is rapidly shifting away from Trump and Republicans") by Matt A. Barreto and Gary M. Segura got me thinking: President "Trump promised to fix the economy, and that helped increase his Latino support in 2024. But the cost of living and ICE raids may be bringing Latinos back to the Democrats in 2026."
