The Supreme Court heard oral argument last week in Trump v. Barbara, which considers President Trump's executive order restricting birthright citizenship. In a series of blog posts over the next few weeks, leading scholars will offer thoughts on the oral arguments and the case, its potential legacy, and impacts.

We begin tomorrow with posts from Professor Jack Chin (UC Davis) and Ilya Somin (George Mason Antonin Scalia Law School).
