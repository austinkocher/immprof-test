News reports on the humanitarian crisis in Venezuela are sobering. I fear that the crisis is just beginning. The Latin Times reports that, as the nation "struggles with the aftermath of the twin powerful earthquakes that devastated large portions of the country, Venezuelan Americans, immigration attorneys, and community leaders are urging the Trump administration to halt deportations and restore Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for hundreds of thousands of Venezuelans living in the United States." Expect more on this story.
