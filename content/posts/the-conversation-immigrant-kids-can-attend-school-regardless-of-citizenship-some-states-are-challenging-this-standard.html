Immigrant kids can attend school regardless of citizenship – some states are challenging this standard by Willliam McCorkle

Republican legislators in several states, including Tennessee, Oklahoma and Ohio, are trying to pass legislation that challenges Plyler v. Doe (1982). These bills would make it harder, if not impossible, for immigrant children to attend public school.
