The Associated Press reports on troubling development. "The U.S. Border Patrol is monitoring millions of American drivers nationwide in a secretive program to identify and detain people whose travel patterns it deems suspicious . . . ." The program has resulted in stops, searches, and some arrests. "A network of cameras scans and records vehicle license plate information, and an algorithm flags vehicles deemed suspicious . . . . Federal agents in turn may then flag local law enforcement."
