Since President Trump's inauguration a year ago, immigration enforcement has been in the news. Recent operations and protests in Minneapolis are the latest and perhaps the most extreme operations after ones in Los Angeles, Chicago, Washington, D.C., Charlotte, New Orleans, and elsewhere. Public opinion may be at a crossroads on the administration's enforcement measures. USA Today reports that "[m]ost voters believe President Donald Trump's administration has gone too far in its immigration enforcement efforts and view his handling of the issue as negative amid a wave of clashes between federal agents and citizens protesting against their presence, according to a new Wall Street Journal poll."
