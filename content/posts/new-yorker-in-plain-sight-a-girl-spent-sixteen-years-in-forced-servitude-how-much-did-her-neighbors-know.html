Yudhijit Bhattacharjee in the New Yorker (registration required) (In Plain Sight: A girl spent sixteen years in forced servitude. How much did her neighbors know?) tells us about Djena. In January of 2019, the Guinean couple who had enslaved her at their home in an affluent Dallas suburb were convicted in federal court. Djena had been brought from Guinea to the United States as a young child—she was barely ten when she arrived. It was hard to imagine anybody growing up as a slave in modern-day America, let alone in an upscale neighborhood in a large metropolitan area. The story asks the question how could such a thing have happened here?
