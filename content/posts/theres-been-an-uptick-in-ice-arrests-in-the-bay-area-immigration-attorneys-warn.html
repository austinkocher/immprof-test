--- title: "There’s been an uptick in ICE arrests in the Bay Area, immigration attorneys warn" date: 2026-10-23T07:34:14-04:00 slug: "theres-been-an-uptick-in-ice-arrests-in-the-bay-area-immigration-attorneys-warn" url: "/theres-been-an-uptick-in-ice-arrests-in-the-bay-area-immigration-attorneys-warn/" sections: ["Enforcement"] tags: ["Arrests and raids", "Elections", "Lawyers and legal services"] authors: ["Kevin Johnson"] ---

As election day nears, ICE operations appear to be on the rise. Several attorneys report that several ICE arrests across the Bay Area "reflect increasingly aggressive immigration enforcement."

"Bay Area `rapid response' hotlines monitoring immigration arrests have seen an uptick in reports since this weekend, said Lisa Knox, co-executive director at California Collaborative for Immigrant Justice. . . ." 

Deputy Public Defender Francisco Ugarte "called the recent enforcement a `surge,' and compared it to ICE operations in 2007 and 2008, when agents from around the country came to the Bay Area, identified targets, and then `snatched people up.'”
