The Center for Gender & Refugee Studies (CGRS) is seeking a Senior Staff Attorney/Senior Counsel who is committed to working collaboratively with directly impacted individuals and their lawyers and using litigation in combination with other advocacy strategies to promote the rights of asylum seekers. This position is focused primarily on CGRS’s affirmative litigation docket. Information about our current docket is available at here. https://cgrs.uclawsf.edu/our-work/litigation
