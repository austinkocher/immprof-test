Katie Buehler for Law360 reports that the U.S. Supreme Court today reversed a Fourth Circuit order revisin the immigration judges union's challenge to restrictions on their ability to speak publicly. The per curiam Court found that the court of appeals abused its discretion by relying on arguments not raised by the parties and ordered further proceedings.

The justices granted certiorari of a petition filed by the Executive Office for Immigration Review, and reversed the Fourth Circuit's decision reviving the National Association of Immigration Judges' free speech lawsuit.
