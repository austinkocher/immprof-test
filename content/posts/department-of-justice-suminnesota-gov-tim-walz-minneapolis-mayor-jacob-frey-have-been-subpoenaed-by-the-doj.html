The Justice Department has issued grand jury subpoenas to multiple government officials in Minnesota, including Governor Tim Walz and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey. The administration alleges that Minnesota officials conspired to impede federal immigration enforcement.  NPR reports that Minnesota officials suggest that these actions are an intimidation tactic. Officials say they pushed back against the administration's immigration operations because of what they say are the Immigration and Customs Enforcement agency’s unlawful actions.
