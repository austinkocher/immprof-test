The Los Angeles Dodgers, with its international roster of stars, open their season tonight. Yoshinobu Yamamoto is the starting pitcher for the Dodgers. Another international superstar, Shohei Ohtani, is the lead-off hitter (and designated hitter).

With all the Japanese players in Major League Baseball, baseball is huge in Japan.
