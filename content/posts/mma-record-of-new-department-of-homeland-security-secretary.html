As Kit Johnson blogged earlier today, Senator Markwayne Mullin (R-Oklahoma) is will be the Department of Homeland Security Secretary as of March 31. A member of the Cherokee Nation, Mullin will be the only member of President Trump's cabinet without a bachelor's degree.
