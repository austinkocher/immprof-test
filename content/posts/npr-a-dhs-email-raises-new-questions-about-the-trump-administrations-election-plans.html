A Department of Homeland Security email acquired by NPR, raises concerns about the agency's efforts to identify noncitizens on state voter rolls. The email included a 78-page document listing jurisdictions that provide election materials in non-English languages, along with a Department of Justice checklist for prosecuting voter-related offenses. 

"Democrats fear that the Trump administration's efforts to root out noncitizen voters will entangle or intimidate eligible voters and dampen turnout in this year's midterms. DHS has surged resources to investigate noncitizen voting, even as prior audits have found such fraud to be extremely rare."
