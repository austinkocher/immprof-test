News agencies are reporting that two immigration enforcement protesters involved in a protest at a Minnesota church have been arrested.

Attorney General Pam Bondi announced the arrest of Nekima Levy Armstrong in a post on X. The protests occurred during Sunday service at Cities Church in St. Paul, where a U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement employee serves as a pastor. Bondi later posted on X that a second person had been arrested.

Attorney General Pamela Bondi@AGPamBondi·3hMinutes ago at my direction, @HSI_HQ and @FBI agents executed an arrest in Minnesota.

So far, we have arrested Nekima Levy Armstrong, who allegedly played a key role in organizing the coordinated attack on Cities Church in St. Paul, Minnesota.

We will share more updates as they become available.
