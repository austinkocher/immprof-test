--- title: "Colorado voters reject initiative that would have forced state and local cooperation with federal immigration enforcement" date: 2026-11-06T06:13:05-05:00 slug: "colorado-voters-reject-initiative-that-would-have-forced-state-and-local-cooperation-with-federal-immigration-enforcement" url: "/colorado-voters-reject-initiative-that-would-have-forced-state-and-local-cooperation-with-federal-immigration-enforcement/" sections: ["Elections"] tags: ["State and local enforcement", "Elections", "Public opinion"] authors: ["Kevin Johnson"] ---

Earlier this week, Colorado voters rejected Amendment 81, which would have required local law enforcement to notify federal immigration authorities when a person was charged with a crime and lacked legal status, or had unknown status. See CPR, The Guardian.
