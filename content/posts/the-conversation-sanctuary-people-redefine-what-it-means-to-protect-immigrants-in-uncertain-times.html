Gina Perez for The Conversation explores the contemporary notion of faith-based sanctuary:

"I have seen faith-based advocates’ sense of sanctuary expand. The idea now encompasses efforts to assist people most affected by immigration policy, as well as other vulnerable groups, and to assure them that they are not alone."
