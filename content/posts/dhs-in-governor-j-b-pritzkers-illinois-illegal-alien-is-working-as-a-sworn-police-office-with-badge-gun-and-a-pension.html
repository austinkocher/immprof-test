Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem and the Department announced yesterday that Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) arrested "an illegal alien from Montenegro, who was working as a sworn police officer with the Hanover Park Police Department in sanctuary state Illinois." He "was encountered during a targeted enforcement action as part of Operation Midway Blitz." According to the DHS announcement, the officer "overstayed a B2 tourist visa that required him to depart the U.S. on March 31, 2015."
