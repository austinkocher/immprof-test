American Immigration Council: "The latest data from the American Immigration Council’s USCIS Immigration Filing Trends Dashboard show that the agency’s backlog of immigration petitions and applications worsened in the second quarter of fiscal year (FY) 2026, even as USCIS received dramatically fewer filings than it did one year earlier. A trend that reflects a series of policy and process changes by the Trump administration intended on restricting immigration into the United States."
