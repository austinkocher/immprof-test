J. Toby Reiner for The Conversation looks at "fundamental philosophical questions: What ethical obligations do governments have to prospective migrants? And what claims to admission do migrants have? . . . Many philosophers claim that immigration should be understood as a human right. From their perspective, governments have an important responsibility to tread carefully when denying entry to migrants and especially when deporting them."
