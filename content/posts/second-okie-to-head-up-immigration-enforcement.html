First DJT plucked OK Senator Markwayne Mullin to head DHS. Now he's picked a former OK State Trooper to head ICE: Lance Schroyer.

In fairness, NBC reports that Mullin pushed for Schroyer. Which makes sense of the OK-OK hiring. Mullin issued a statement yesterday, saying: "Once confirmed, Schroyer will expand upon President Trump’s promise to the American people to arrest and deport the worst of the worst illegal aliens and reinforce the president’s efforts to make America safe again."
