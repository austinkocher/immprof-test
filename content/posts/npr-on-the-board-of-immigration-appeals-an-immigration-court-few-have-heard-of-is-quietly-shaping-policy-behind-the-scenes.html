NPR in its "deep dive" looks at how President Trump has reshaped the Board of Immigration Appeals, part of the Executive Office for Immigration Review in the U.S. Department of Justice.

NPR's review of the data found that BIA rulings last year "backed Department of Homeland Security lawyers in 97% of publicly posted cases; that's at least 30 percentage points higher than the average from the last 16 years."
