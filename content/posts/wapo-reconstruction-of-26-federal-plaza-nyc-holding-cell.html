The Washington Post has created a 3D model of an ICE holding cell at 26 Federal Plaza in NYC. It's really quite impressive and would make a good assignment for your class on immigration detention. (Here's a second article where WaPo explains how they did it.)

The work is particularly useful given that recording devices and media are largely banned from the space.

WaPo apparently has a "graphics reporter." Cool gig! That fellow, created the model based on court documents--a floor plan, measurements and still photos--as well as leaked cellphone video of the space.
