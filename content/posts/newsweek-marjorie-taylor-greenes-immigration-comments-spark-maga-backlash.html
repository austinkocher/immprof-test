Alia Shoaib for Newsweek reports that conservative firebrand Marjorie Taylor Greene said that the mass deportation campaign has "too far, breaking with the hardline approach of the Trump administration and angering the MAGA base she was once a leading figure in."

“As a conservative and as a business owner in the construction industry, and as a realist, I can say we have to do something about labor. And that needs to be a smarter plan than just rounding up every single person and deporting them just like that,” Greene said on the Tim Dillon Show podcast. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlONOh_iUXY
