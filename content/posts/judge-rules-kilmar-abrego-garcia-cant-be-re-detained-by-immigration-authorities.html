The beat goes on.

The administration did not agree with the ruling that Kilmar Abrego Garcia could not be re-detained by U.S. immigration authorities. Newsweek published this quote: “`If this matter were actually about the law or due process, Kilmar Abrego Garcia would already be deported and would never set foot in this country again,' Tricia McLaughlin, assistant secretary for public affairs at the Department of Homeland Security, said in a statement, adding `Judge Xinis will not be satisfied until he is authorized to live in the United States forever.'"

News agencies reported today that Assistant Secretary for Public Affairs Tricia McLaughlin plans to leave the agency at the end of February.
