A Democratic wave has brought Democratic control of the U.S. House of Representatives and Senate. Now, Congress will have much to do. Will some kind of immigration reform be a priority? Time will tell. Before the election, "[a]bout 8 in 10 independent voters say U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement has used too much force in its nationwide operations, according to a poll from YouGov, which sampled 1,000 voters in late July. It's a striking increase from 2025, when polls showed independents more divided on the issue."

The Trump administration’s slowdown of the naturalization process prevented an estimated 380,000 immigrants from voting in the midterm elections. KSL, Maine Beacon.

Newly naturalized citizens headed to the polls despite fears of federal agents at voting sites and insecurities, according to a recent survey. Scripps News.

Leading up to the election and on Election Day, DHS released a flood of press releases touting ICE arrests and border security, condemning “sanctuary politicians,” and highlighting “the worst of the worst” as being swept up in its immigration crackdown. 📄DHS.gov

The Justice Department also announced yesterday the “largest monitoring effort in history,” and stated it deployed 1,000 election monitors to all 50 states. The move has drawn concerns of voter intimidation amid its crackdown on alleged alleged noncitizen voting. Democracy Docket, Gothamist, 📄Justice.gov.

A backlash to the Trump administration’s immigration crackdown has created political headwinds for Republicans in the midterms. New York Times.
