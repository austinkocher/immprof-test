The nation watched Alysa Liu skate to gold in Italy. Her relaxed, joyful performance was mesmerizing.

Today, WaPo share the amazing story of Alysa’s father: Arthur Liu. He was a Tiananmen Square protester who fled China under Operation Yellowbird. It’s a remarkable story that you can read here: https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/2026/03/20/alysa-liu-olympics-arthur-tiananmen-square-china/.
