It turns out that Renée Good and Alex Pretti are not the only U.S. citizens who have died at the hands of immigration authorities during the second Trump administration. As WaPo reports, Ruben Ray Martinez, 23, was shot and killed by an HSI agent assigned to immigration enforcement. The shooting is being investigated by the Texas Department of Public Safety and no video of the incident has been released.
