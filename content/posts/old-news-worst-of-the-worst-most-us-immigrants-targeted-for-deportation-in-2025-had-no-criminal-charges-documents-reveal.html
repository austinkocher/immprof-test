Most US immigrants targeted for deportation in 2025 had no criminal charges, documents reveal" date: 2026-02-23T05:18:06-05:00 slug: "old-news-worst-of-the-worst-most-us-immigrants-targeted-for-deportation-in-2025-had-no-criminal-charges-documents-reveal" url: "/old-news-worst-of-the-worst-most-us-immigrants-targeted-for-deportation-in-2025-had-no-criminal-charges-documents-reveal/" sections: ["Enforcement"] tags: ["Deportation", "Criminal prosecutions", "Data and trackers"] authors: ["Kevin Johnson"] image: "media/e39756902976.png" sources: - name: "The Guardian" url: "https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/feb/22/us-immigration-trump-administration" ---

A Guardian (UK) analysis of U.S. government records has found that 77% of the noncitizens placed in removal proceedings for the first time "in 2025 had no criminal conviction, exposing a stark gap between the Trump administration’s rhetoric and reality." President Donald Trump had proclaimed that the administration would remove the “the worst of the worst.”
