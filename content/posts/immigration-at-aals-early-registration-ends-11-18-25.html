The 2026 Annual Meeting of AALS is scheduled for Tuesday, January 6 – Friday, January 9, 2026 in New Orleans, LA. Discounts apply if you register by November 24.

Here are some exciting programs to look forward to:

  • Tuesday, January 6 (2:35 PM - 3:50 PM): Immigration Enforcement and the Limits of Law
  • Tuesday, January 6 (4:10 PM - 5:25 PM): Emerging Immigration Legal Scholarship
  • Wednesday, January 7 (1:00 PM - 2:15 PM): Decarceral Evidence
  • Wednesday, January 7 (4:10 PM - 5:25 PM): Immigration and Disability Law
