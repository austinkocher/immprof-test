This entire article from the BBC is worth a read. It carefully details all the ways in which the UK government is looking to change the country's immigration rules, including:

the standard length of time migrants will have to live in the UK to qualify for permanent settlement will double from five to 10 years.
... applicants must have earned at least £12,571 for five of the seven years immediately before being granted settlement.
... there will be a shorter qualifying period of five years for certain groups, including: [1] People who earned at least £50,271 for three of five years immediately before applying for settlement; [2] Care workers who earned at least £25,000 a year while meeting their visa conditions and arrived in the UK before May 2025; [3] "Skilled" public service workers in health and education, such as doctors, nurses, teachers and social workers; [4] Other health and education public service workers who fall below this skill level, for example nursing auxiliaries and teaching assistants, if they arrived in the UK before May 2025; [5] People from Hong Kong who hold British National (Overseas) status
People earning over £125,141 a year, as well as "highly skilled" migrants such as those on Global Talent or Innovator Founder visas, will qualify after three years.

In addition:

Migrants who have claimed certain benefits or breached immigration rules will only be able to settle after at least 20 years.
Adults with a criminal record will be refused settlement, with discretion to refuse cases where the offence was committed when the applicant was under 18.
To qualify for settlement, migrants will generally also have to meet other conditions, including:[1] Speak English to a high standard; [2] Owe no debt to the government, such as unpaid taxes, [3] Earn at least £12,571 a year for at least three years, aligned with the threshold for paying income tax

Refugees who arrive through official channels will qualify for permanent settlement after 5 years. Other refugees, so long as working or studying, must wait 10 years. If not working or studying, refugees must wait 20 years. Arriving by boat means a 20 year wait if working/studying or a 30 year wait otherwise.
