Rock icon Bruce Springsteen has told fans of his views about the immigration operations of the Trump administrations. As has been widely reported, he recently "denounced U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement’s `Gestapo tactics,' and dedicated a song to Renee Good, the 37-year-old woman who was killed by an ICE agent in Minneapolis earlier this month."

In an appearance this weekend in his hometown of Asbury Park, New Jersey, Springsteen made the remarks before his iconic song, “The Promised Land.”
