The Associated Press reports that the Trump administration has suspended the diversity visa lottery program. The suspect in the Brown University and MIT shootings reportedly came to the United States on a diversity visa.

Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem said in a post on X that she was ordering the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services to pause the program.
