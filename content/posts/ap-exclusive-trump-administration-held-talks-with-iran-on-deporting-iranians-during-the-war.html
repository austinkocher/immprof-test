Michael Kunzelman, Rebecca Santana and Safiyah Riddle of the Associated Press report that the Trump administration held secret talks with Iran on deporting Iranians as the war dragged on, court documents show.

"The court documents unsealed [today] show the Republican administration as recently as mid-October said it had plans to deport the Iranians through an unnamed third country, although they did not specify when that would be carried out. The judge in the case ordered the government to give the court 24-hour notice of any finalized plans to deport Iranians to Iran or to any other country and five days’ written notice before any such deportation is carried out."
