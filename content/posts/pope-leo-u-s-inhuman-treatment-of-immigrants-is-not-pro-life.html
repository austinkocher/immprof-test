--- title: "Pope Leo: U.S. \"Inhuman treatment of immigrants\" is not \"pro life\"" date: 2025-10-04T15:11:41-04:00 slug: "pope-leo-u-s-inhuman-treatment-of-immigrants-is-not-pro-life" url: "/pope-leo-u-s-inhuman-treatment-of-immigrants-is-not-pro-life/" sections: ["News"] tags: ["Race, ethnicity and religion", "Protest and advocacy", "White House and agencies"] authors: ["Kit Johnson"] image: "media/7cf714152fad.png" ---

Earlier this week, while speaking with the press, Pope Leo stated: "Someone who says 'I'm against abortion but I'm in agreement with the inhuman treatment of immigrants in the United States,' I don't know if that's pro-life." He went on to say that these are "complex issues." And he called for "greater respect for one another."
