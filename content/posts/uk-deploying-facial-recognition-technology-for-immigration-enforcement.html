The Guardian reports UK plans to deploy facial recognition technology "at transport hubs and public events as part of the biggest immigration enforcement crackdown in British history." The goal is to " identify people 'seeking to re-enter the UK unlawfully', target those subject to removal action and to crack down on broader 'immigration-related criminality'."
