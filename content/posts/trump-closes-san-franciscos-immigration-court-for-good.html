Trump Closes San Francisco’s Immigration Court for Good

San Francisco’s immigration court stopped hearing cases last week. "Once one of the busiest courts in the country, the closure of the 100 Montgomery Street courthouse comes after more than a year of firings and retirements have whittled down its bench and worsened a massive case backlog."
