TRAC has a new report out: More Immigrants Granted Bond by Immigration Judges in September 2026. Here's the opening paragraph:

In September, Immigration Judges granted bond to 2,646 individuals. According to the latest case-by-case Immigration Court records, this is the largest monthly number of bond grants in the past two years. See Figure 1. It was also the largest number of bond hearings with just under 10,000 hearings held by Immigration Judges in September 2026. The number currently detained at the end of September was 32,622 individuals, down slightly from the 32,956 individuals who were recorded as currently detained in the Court’s backlog as of the end of August 2026.
