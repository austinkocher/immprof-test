MPR reports that ICE has removed noncitizens with final orders of removal from its online detainee locator database. The news outlet reports the change went into place on September 15. Families and attorneys can no longer verify where such noncitizens are being held, with some exceptions. Individuals houses within the Adelanto facility or the temporary holding spaces in Chicago, Minneapolis, and NY, are still locatable.

ALCU Attorney My Khanh Ngo told MPR that this change makes it “nearly impossible” to challenge removals: “If you can't find your client, don't know what's happening to your client, you can't get that information to the court to stop their removal.”
