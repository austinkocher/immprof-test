The podcast series, Unsettled: Immigration in Turbulent Times, with co-hosts Alex Aleinikoff, Cristina Rodriguez, and Hiroshi Motomura, begins its second season. Here is the first episode: The Trump Administration’s War on Venezuelan Migrants. The guest is Jonathan Blitzer, staff writer for The New Yorker. He discusses his recent article “Enemies of the State: How the Trump Administration declared war on Venezuelan migrants in the U.S.”
