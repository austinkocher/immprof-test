This WaPo opinion piece is worth a read: He’s a citizen with a Real ID. ICE detained him anyway. Twice. It goes to the heart of the current moment in immigration enforcement--detention based on appearance regardless of evidence to the contrary.

This was a central theme discussed in the recent AIC webinar on workplace enforcement.

It brings to my mind Muneer Ahmad's work A Rage Shared By Law: Post-September 11 Racial Violence as Crimes of Passion, 92 California Law Review 1259 (2004), and its discussion of the "new racial construct" of the "Muslim-looking person." Worth a re-read as well.
