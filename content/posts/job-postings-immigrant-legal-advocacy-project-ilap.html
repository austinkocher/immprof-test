STAFF ATTORNEY/SENIOR STAFF ATTORNEY, DETENTION PROJECT 

Based in Portland, Maine, the Immigrant Legal Advocacy Project (ILAP) is seeking a full-time attorney to provide direct representation to individuals detained by U.S. immigration officials. The Attorney’s focus will be representing detained clients in custody and bond proceedings before the Immigration Court and, when appropriate, pursuing related litigation in federal court. Based in Portland, Maine, the Immigrant Legal Advocacy Project (ILAP) is seeking a Legal Assistant to support our growing Detention Project. The Legal Assistant’s primary responsibilities involve reviewing and screening requests for legal assistance concerning individuals detained by U.S. immigration officials.
