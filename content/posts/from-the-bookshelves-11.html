A Research Agenda for Refugee Law

Elgar Research Agendas Edited by Stephen Meili, James H. Michael Professor of International Human Rights Law, Faculty of Law, University of Minnesota, USA and Dallal Stevens, Professor of Law, Emerita, School of Law, University of Warwick, UK

Publication Date: 2026, 282 pp

This enlightening Research Agenda explores international refugee law through marginalised topics and perspectives. A diverse array of leading experts propose new research avenues for centring forgotten voices at a time of increasingly complex and rampant government-fuelled xenophobia.
