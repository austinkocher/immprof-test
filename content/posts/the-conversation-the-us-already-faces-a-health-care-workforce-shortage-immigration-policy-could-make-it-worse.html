Economist Bedassa Tadesse for The Conversation ("The US already faces a health care workforce shortage – immigration policy could make it worse") discusses how immigration influences economies, including health care systems. His view "Immigrants are a vital part of the health care workforce, especially in roles facing staffing shortages." Restrictions on immigration, in his view, threatens adequate U.S. health care staffing.
