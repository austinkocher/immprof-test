Georgetown Law's immigration clinic, the Center for Applied Legal Studies (CALS), is accepting applications for its 2027–2029 Graduate Teaching Fellowship.

The two-year fellowship is designed for immigration attorneys interested in transitioning from practice to clinical teaching. Fellows gain experience teaching and supervising law students and earn an LL.M. in Advocacy. While CALS has traditionally focused on asylum representation, we are expanding our docket to include a broader range of matters before the immigration courts, USCIS, and federal courts.

Applicants should have at least three years of immigration law practice experience, including significant removal defense experience.

The application deadline is February 7, 2027. Additional information and application instructions are available here.
