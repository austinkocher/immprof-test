The Trump administration has insisted that its removal operations have targeted the "worst of the worst," which the data does not support. The Associated Press reports the latest on this score.

The detention by U.S. immigration authorities of two teen brothers who were members of a nationally recognized mariachi ban has triggered bipartisan criticism of the Trump administration's mass deportation campaign.

Two brothers, aged 18 and 14, with their 12-year-old brother and their parents have been detained. "The family had been checking in regularly with immigration authorities, as instructed, when they were detained, [a] relative and girlfriend said."
