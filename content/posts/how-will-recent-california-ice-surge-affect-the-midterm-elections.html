Carlos De Loera analyzes the impacts of a reported Immigration and Customs Enforcement "enforcement surge" called “Operation Safe California.” "[T]here has been an uptick in ICE activity reported across the state, from San Diego to the Central Valley."

The enforcement surge came as the Trump administration has taken steps to curtail alleged noncitizen voting.
