Immigration remains a stronger issue for President Donald Trump than the economy or Iran, according to a new AP-NORC poll, even after three people were killed in encounters with Immigration and Customs Enforcement in one week in July.

The new survey from The Associated Press-NORC Center for Public Affairs Research found that only 39% of U.S. adults approve of his performance on immigration, a decline from 49% at the beginning of his second term.
