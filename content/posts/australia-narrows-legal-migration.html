The United States isn't the only nation looking to restrict legal migration. Australia is also looking to tighten its immigration pipeline, the BBC reports.

Key changes include:

  • Foreign students (other than Phd students) will no longer be able to bring their families within them while studying in Australia.
  • Foreign students completing one program of study can remain in Australia only if undertaking a more advanced degree (e.g. a Masters after a B.A.).
  • The government will cap the number of 12-month working-holiday visas allowed to extend for a second year (set at 45,000 down from 57,000) and will drastically reduce the number allowed to extend for a third year (set at 5,000 down from 31,000).

Australia's goal is to reduce net overseas migration from 292,100 (March 2025-February 2026) to 225,000 by 2028.
