In a two-year investigation, a Brazilian forensic investigator teamed up with an Associated Press journalist to identify bodies found on a drifting boat near the Amazon. The victims were among the thousands of migrants from West Africa who disappear while attempting to reach Europe. Read more.

"Tens of thousands of migrants have gone missing globally on land or sea journeys, whether crossing the Mediterranean Sea or the U.S.-Mexico border. While it is states’ responsibility to investigate and identify these victims, regardless of their nationality, they have largely failed, experts say. The U.N. migration agency has called the global crisis `one of the great political failures of modern times.'”
