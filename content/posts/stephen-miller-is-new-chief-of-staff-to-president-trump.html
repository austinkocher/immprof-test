Big news from Washington: "Top Trump adviser Stephen Miller, who is set to take over the White House chief of staff role on Monday, has driven President Trump's most controversial immigration policies . . . . . Mr. Trump announced the staffing shake-up [yesterday], calling Miller one of his `most trusted' advisers. Miller replaces Susie Wiles, who has been his chief of staff throughout his second term."
