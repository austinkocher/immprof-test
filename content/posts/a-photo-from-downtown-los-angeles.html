Last week, I visited Southwestern Law School in the Koreatown neighborhood of Los Angeles. In recent months, Immigration and Customs Enforcement had been conducting operations in the neighborhood. Worry remains in the air as arrests continue. Still, life continues. I saw street vendors open for business and small restaurants with foods from the world over. I took the above photo on display in front of a church in the neighborhood.
