Newsweek reports that House Democrat Ilhan Omar won in a landslide in Minnesota’s 5th congressional district less than a month after her Washington, D.C., home was searched by Department of Homeland Security officers. "With 99 percent of the 344,118 votes cast counted Omar led Republican challenger John Nagel with 76.5 percent of the vote against his 16.2 percent on Wednesday morning."
