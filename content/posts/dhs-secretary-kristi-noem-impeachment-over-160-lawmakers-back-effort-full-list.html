Newsweek reports that at least 140 Democrats have co-sponsored a resolution to bring articles of impeachment against Secretary of Department of Homeland Security Kristi Noem. The resolution aims to impeach the Trump administration official for 1) "Obstruction of Congress," 2) "Violation of Public Trust," and 3) "Self-Dealing." Kelly introduced the impeachment articles on January 14 against Noem for "high crimes and misdemeanors."
