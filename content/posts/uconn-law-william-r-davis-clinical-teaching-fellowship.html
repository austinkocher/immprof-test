UConn Law School is hiring for the William R. Davis Clinical Teaching Fellowship.  The fellow will co-teach and supervise students in UConn’s Asylum Clinic, starting in summer 2026.  The position announcement can be found here.

Several prior Davis Fellows have gone on to teaching positions at various law schools.  Applications are due by February 15.
