Bad Bunny is performing at half time today of the Super Bowl game between the Seattle Seahawks and New England Patriots. It has been a controversial choice in some circles. As his statement at the Grammys last weekend make clear, Bad Bunny is not a fan of ICE.

The conservative Turning Point's All American Halftime Show "celebrating faith, family and freedom" will feature performances by:

  • Kid Rock
  • Brantley Gilbert
  • Lee Brice
  • Gabby Barrett

Turning Point USA said the show will air live on YouTube, X and Rumble in addition to other conservative news outlets.
