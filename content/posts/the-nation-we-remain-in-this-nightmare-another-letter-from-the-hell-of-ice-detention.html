From The Nation:

"Last August, we published a message from behind the walls of an ICE detention center from Maryam Tahmasebi. Three months later, Maryam, her husband, and their teenage son are all still imprisoned—despite being legal permanent residents with no criminal record. (You can find more details about their case here.)

Today, we are publishing a new dispatch from Maryam, who is being held in the Dilley Immigration Processing Center in Texas. We will be publishing additional pieces by her in the months ahead."
