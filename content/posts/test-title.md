Documented's Julia Malleck interviewed Marc Rosenblum, who ran the Office of Homeland Security Statistics until he left the Department of Homeland Security in July after more than ten years there. Rosenblum says the department greatly reduced his office's public reporting and scaled back its role in keeping statistics consistent across DHS agencies. He goes on to say which immigration data he still considers reliable and what Congress could do to require regular reporting.
