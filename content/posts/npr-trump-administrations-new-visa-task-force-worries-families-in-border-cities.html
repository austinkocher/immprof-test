Families living in cities along the U.S.-Mexico border are facing new difficulties following the Trump administration’s crackdown on border crossing cards. NPR reports that

"Thousands of border families are caught up in the visa crackdown ordered in August by the Trump administration that included creating a new task force aimed at stopping foreigners from trying to gain citizenship for their U.S. born children. El Paso-based Immigration attorney Kathleen Walker said those with tourist visas should understand the rule."
