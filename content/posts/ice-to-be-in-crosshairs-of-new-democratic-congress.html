Once taking control of Congress in January, Democrats are planning to claw back the funding behind the Trump administration’s immigration operations and expand oversight over federal immigration agencies. See NPR; New York Times.

Exit polls show that 60% of voters in the midterm elections disapprove of ICE, and that 87% of those who disapprove ended up voting for Democrats.
