Harsh Legacy

How did we get to this moment in America’s immigration history?

A Film by Phyllis Coven and Yael Schacher with Turnstone Productions


How a 1996 law built the machinery of mass detention and deportation in America.

On the 30th anniversary of a little-known but highly consequential law, Harsh Legacy reveals how the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996 (IIRIRA) laid the groundwork for the enforcement system dominating headlines today—families torn apart, asylum seekers treated like criminals, and legal residents facing exile for decades-old offenses.
