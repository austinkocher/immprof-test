As Kevin noted yesterday, DHS is implementing a new rule changing the affirmative asylum process by allowing USCIS to refer cases to IJs without interviewing applicants.

The official White House Truth Social account supports the change, naturally, writing: "President Trump is working to CRUSH the immigration asylum backlog - making the government more efficient and speeding up immigration enforcement."
