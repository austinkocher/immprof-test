Los Angeles family detained at immigration center in Texas for four months" date: 2026-08-17T17:14:25-04:00 slug: "par-for-the-course-los-angeles-family-detained-at-immigration-center-in-texas-for-four-months" url: "/par-for-the-course-los-angeles-family-detained-at-immigration-center-in-texas-for-four-months/" sections: ["Enforcement"] tags: ["Detention", "Deportation", "Families"] authors: ["Kevin Johnson"] ---

Dillon Fuhrman for NBC's Los Angeles affiliate reports on a family has spent months in an immigration detention center in Texas, fighting a battle against the U.S. government because of who they are related to.

The removal case is based on the husband's mother being a spokesperson for the group that took Americans hostage during the 1979 Iran hostage crisis. "[T]he State Department is using that connection to detain the family for more than 120 days."
