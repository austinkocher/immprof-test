The backlog of Special Immigrant Juvenile Status (SIJS) applicants was 100,000 in 2023, according to a E. Tammy Kim's report in The New Yorker. Under the Biden administration, unaccompanied minors with SIJ status were eligible for a work permit while they waited for visa approval. Under the Trump administration, this relief for has been largely depleted and many teens have been deported back to their home countries.
