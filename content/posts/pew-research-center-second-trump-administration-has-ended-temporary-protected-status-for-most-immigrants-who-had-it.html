Elections matter. President Trump has ended Temporary Protected Status for most immigrants who had it. The Pew Research Center notes that "President Donald Trump’s administration has taken steps to end Temporary Protected Status for 13 countries, affecting an estimated 1 million out of 1.3 million recipients who had it as of March 2025."
