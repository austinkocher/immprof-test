CNN reports that the Trump administration is moving to reinterview "refugees who were admitted to the United States under former President Joe Biden as part of a comprehensive review of their cases, according to an internal memo and a source familiar with the plans." This would be unprecedented.

Trump officials claim that the Biden administration did bot sufficiently vet the refugees. President Trump has largely halted refugee admissions.
