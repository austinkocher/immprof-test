Edward Foley for SCOTUSBlog discusses the constitutionality of the statute that the Trump administration is relying on in demanding that California officials to set aside 20,000 ballots. The statute "makes voting by noncitizens in federal elections a crime: 18 U.S.C. § 611. There are two fundamental problems . . . with DOJ’s reliance on this statute. First, the original public meaning of the Constitution does not give Congress the authority to make voting by noncitizens a crime; rather, the Constitution leaves it to the states to decide whether or not noncitizens may vote, and this is true even for federal elections.

"
