Our Immigrant of the Day is Democratic socialist Melat Kiros has won the race to represent Colorado's 1st Congressional District in the House.

Kiros, an Ethiopian immigrant and lawyer, won in a district that includes most of Denver.

The win comes after she handily defeated a 15-term incumbent in the primary.
