In this episode of our World of Migration podcast, Migration Policy Institute Senior Policy Analyst Valerie Lacarte speaks with Cuba expert María José Espinosa Carrillo, Executive Director of the Center for Engagement and Advocacy in the Americas (CEDA), about the causes of Cuba's crisis, the dismantling of decades of preferential U.S. immigration policy, the new geography of Cuban migration, and what a regional humanitarian response could look like.
