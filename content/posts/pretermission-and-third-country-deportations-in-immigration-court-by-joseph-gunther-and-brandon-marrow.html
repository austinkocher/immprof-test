Joseph Gunther and Brandon Marrow have released a data analysis of pretermission and third-country removal orders. As they note, "[s]ince entering office in January 2025, the Trump administration has used aggressive measures to deny asylum cases in immigration court. Recently, a new strategy known as `pretermission' has emerged for ending asylum court cases abruptly. In this report, we analyze immigration court data to quantify this practice for the first time." This New York Times article cites various parts of the report.
