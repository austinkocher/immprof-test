NPR reports that immigrants are being told last minute about new in-person hearings at previously undisclosed courtroom locations. "The last-minute notifications appear to be another tactic in a broader strategy from the Trump administration to short-circuit immigrants' already limited due process options to speed up arrests and deportations."
