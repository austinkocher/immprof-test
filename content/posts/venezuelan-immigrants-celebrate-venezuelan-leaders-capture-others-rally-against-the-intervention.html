Venezuelan immigrants across the U.S. celebrate Nicolas Maduro’s capture. Others rally against the U.S. intervention in Venezuela.

Many took to the streets following news of his arrest this weekend. Worries remain about the country’s political future. See NBC, New York Times.

President Trump has sought to eliminate relief for Venezuelan migrants in the United States.
