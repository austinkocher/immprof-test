Brazilian Immigrants in the United States

By Marina Aragão Santos and Jeanne Batalova

The United States is home to the largest Brazilian community outside Brazil. Yet Brazilians account for only about 1 percent of all U.S. immigrants.

This population has grown rapidly in recent years, with nearly two-thirds of Brazilian immigrants arriving since 2010. That growth may slow, since Brazil is among the 75 countries from which the Trump administration paused immigrant visa processing.

This article offers a close look at the Brazilian immigrant population in the United States.
