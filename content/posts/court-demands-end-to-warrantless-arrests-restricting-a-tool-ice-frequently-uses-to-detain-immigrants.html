Colorado Public Radio reports on a federal court ruling prohibiting U.S. immigration officers from (1) making warrantless arrests in Colorado based on suspected immigration violations; and (2) prohibiting detentions without determining whether a noncitizen is a risk to not appear at removal hearings.

Appointed by President Barack Obama, Senior U.S. District Judge R. Brooke Jackson issued the ruling. The lawsuit was filed by the American Civil Liberties Union. Lawyers argued that Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers have routinely violated people’s federal rights by detaining them in Colorado without proof that the person is a flight risk.
