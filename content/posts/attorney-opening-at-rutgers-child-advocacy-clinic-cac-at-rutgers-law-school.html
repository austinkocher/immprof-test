The Rutgers Child Advocacy Clinic (CAC) at Rutgers Law School in Newark, New Jersey, and the Rutgers Immigrant Justice Clinic at Rutgers Law School in Camden, New Jersey, are seeking to hire a bilingual full-time attorney to begin immediately. The Staff Attorney or Senior Staff Attorney will be based in Newark, NJ, and will provide immigration representation to children in northern New Jersey who are involved with the New Jersey Division of Child Protection and Permanency. This person will join a wonderful team of attorneys who have been working on a project representing children in foster care as their immigration attorneys.  The project just celebrated its tenth anniversary and is a national model. The salary range is $85K to $95K and the position is hybrid. For information about the posting, please click here.
