Bruce Springsteen's song "American Skin (41 Shots)" is listed among the most controversial songs of the last 50 years. The song involves a Black migrant killed (with 41 shots) by police in New York. The list explains that "[i]nspired by the police shooting of Amadou Diallo, the song debuted during a concert at Madison Square Garden in 2000. In response, the New York City Police Department Patrolmen's Benevolent Association called for a public boycott of Springsteen's music."

The Boss played American Skin on his Land of Hope and Dreams tour in 2026. Here are the lyrics. Not fond of the Springsteen's politics, President Trump called on MAGA supporters to boycott the 2026 tour.
