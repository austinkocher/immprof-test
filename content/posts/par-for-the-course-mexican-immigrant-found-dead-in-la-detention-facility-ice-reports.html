Mexican immigrant found dead in LA detention facility, ICE reports" date: 2026-04-01T12:49:38-04:00 slug: "par-for-the-course-mexican-immigrant-found-dead-in-la-detention-facility-ice-reports" url: "/par-for-the-course-mexican-immigrant-found-dead-in-la-detention-facility-ice-reports/" sections: ["Enforcement"] tags: ["Detention", "Deaths in custody", "Deportation"] authors: ["Kevin Johnson"] image: "media/e39756902976.png" ---

Reuters reports that the 14th person died this year in ICE custody. Here is the ICE statement on the latest death:

"Jose Guadalupe Ramos-Solano, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico previously convicted of possession of a controlled substance and theft, passed away on March 25, 2026, at 9:29 p.m. Ramos was being held at the Adelanto ICE Processing Center when security staff discovered him unconscious and unresponsive. Staff immediately initiated life-saving procedures, and he was taken to Victor Valley Global Medical Center in Victorville, California, where he was pronounced deceased."
