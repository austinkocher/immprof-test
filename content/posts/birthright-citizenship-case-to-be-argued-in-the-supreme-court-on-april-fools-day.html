The Supreme Court will hear oral arguments in Trump v. Barbara – the birthright citizenship case – on April 1, a/k/a April Fools Day. A fair amount of commentary and the briefs can be found on the SCOTUSBlog website for the case. Expect much chatter about the case in the coming weeks.
