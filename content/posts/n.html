The Trump administration has been purging immigration judges. Dan Gooding for Newsweek reports on what some of the judges had to say. Not much good really.

While judges have lost their jobs across the country, the Trump administration is hiring new ones, with advertisements for “deportation judges” rather than “immigration judges.
