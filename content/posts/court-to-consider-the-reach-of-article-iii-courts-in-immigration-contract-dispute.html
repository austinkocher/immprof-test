As Ronald Mann encapsulates the case, oral argument tomorrow in the Supreme Court "in Department of Labor v. Sun Valley Orchards brings the justices a complex constitutional problem sure to get their focused attention: does the Constitution allow Congress to delegate to the executive branch – as opposed to the courts – the authority to adjudicate the obligation of an employer to comply with employment agreements it makes with temporary immigrant workers."
