The EOIR announced yesterday MORE new immigration judges, 27 IJs and 5 Temporary IJs.

A copy of the announcement with judicial biographies is available here.

The announcement included news that Immigration Judge Jamee E. Comans has been promoted to Chief Immigration Judge. Judge Comans formally sat in Jena, Louisiana, and gained nationwide attention when she ordered Columbia University graduate student Mahmoud Khalil deported.
