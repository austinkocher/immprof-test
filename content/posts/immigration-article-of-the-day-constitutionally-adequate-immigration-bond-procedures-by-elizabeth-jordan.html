Constitutionally Adequate Immigration Bond Procedures by Elizabeth Jordan

Abstract

Constitutionally Adequate Immigration Bond Procedures, is the first survey and analysis of federal judges' very recent assessments of immigration bond proceedings. This piece addresses an urgent issue currently requiring scholarly intervention because of a flood of federal litigation resulting in bond hearings. These hearings run the risk of violating due process because of inadequate procedures, something federal judges across the country are scrutinizing and that this piece excavates for solutions.
