As has occurred throughout history, xenophobia is sweeping the world. The New York Times reports that xenophobic violence has been mounting in South Africa, with mobs of anti-immigrant protesters march in Johannesburg and elsewhere. The protesters threaten migrants and attack foreign-owned businesses. (This link is free.) One anti-immigration group has set a deadline of June 30 for all undocumented migrants in the country to leave.
