We at the Immprof Blog are very excited to roll out a new resource library for immigration law professors.

At the top of this page you should see a new tab: Library. If you click on the triangle next to the word, you'll see a drop down menu for immigration syllabi, crimmigration syllabi, other syllabi, exams, and exercises.

Kit will be collecting materials that you're willing to share publicly and posting them to these pages. And if you have other ideas for useful materials to share, please speak up! We hope this will be a helpful resource to immprof faculty both new and seasoned.
