Stuart Anderson for Forbes writes that the Trump administration is proposing a $103,265 fee on all new H-1B visas. "The proposed immigration rule is the latest effort by Trump officials to prevent employers from hiring high-skilled foreign nationals in the United States. A presidential proclamation in Sept. 2025 imposed a $100,000 fee on the entry of new H-1B visa holders. In June, a federal judge struck down the fee, calling it an intrusion on Congress’s taxing authority."
