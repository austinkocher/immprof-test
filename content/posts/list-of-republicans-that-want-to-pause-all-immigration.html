Newsweek reports that a group of Republican lawmakers have co-sponsored a bill "that seeks to pause all immigration until certain conditions related to benefits for immigrants and different immigration pathways have been addressed."

H.R. 6225, the PAUSE Act of 2025, currently has nine signatories, including:

  • Chip Roy of Texas
  • Andy Biggs of Arizona
  • Keith Self of Texas
  • Andrew Ogles of Tennessee
  • Lauren Boebert of Colorado
  • Brandon Gill of Texas
  • Randy Fine of Florida
  • Elijah Crane of Arizona
  • Byron Donalds of Florida
