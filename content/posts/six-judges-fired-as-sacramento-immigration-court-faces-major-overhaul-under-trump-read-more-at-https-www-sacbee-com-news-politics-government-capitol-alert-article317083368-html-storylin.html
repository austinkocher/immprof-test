The Sacramento Bee reports that the Sacramento immigration court has had six of the seven judges serving there dismissed in President Donald Trump’s second term. "The overhaul is part of a broader effort by the Trump administration to reshape the nation’s immigration courts . . . . As of this week, the administration has dismissed about 125 immigration judges out of roughly 700 in place when Trump returned to office."

According to the Bee, "[e]ach [of the dismissed judges] had higher rates of granting asylum and lower rates of denying immigration relief compared to the national averages, according to Syracuse University’s Transactional Records Access Clearinghouse."
