NBC News reports that U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services has been "sending experts to its offices around the country or reassigning staff members to focus on whether some citizens processed through those offices could now be denaturalized . . . ."

The goal reportedly is 100 to 200 possible denaturalization cases a month. "Such cases have typically been very rare, involving people who concealed criminal histories or previous human rights violations during their application processes. The New York Times first reported the quota."
