--- title: "U.S. to Boot Legal Immigrants From Medicaid" date: 2026-10-07T06:25:22-04:00 slug: "u-s-to-boot-legal-immigrants-from-medicaid" url: "/u-s-to-boot-legal-immigrants-from-medicaid/" sections: ["Policy"] tags: ["Visas and legal immigration", "Public benefits and health", "Congress", "White House and agencies"] authors: ["Kevin Johnson"] ---

The Trump administration stripped Medicaid coverage from hundreds of thousands of low-income immigrants across the United States last week as part of its $1 trillion cut to social services enacted in the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. 

The Medicaid coverage cuts are just the latest move by the federal government to target immigrants who rely on social services, following the rollout of a new public charge rule in September.
