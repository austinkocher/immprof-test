This USA Today story tells one story illustrating the human costs of President Trump's immigration clampdown. Immigration application decisions now take years for decision. "[D]elays affect even the most straightforward cases, such as a U.S. citizen petitioning for a spouse who entered the country on a visa. . . . [T]hose cases were typically approved within six to eight months a few years ago. Now, that time frame is unheard of. "
