The Migration Policy Institute has a new report on U.S. immigration detention by Muzaffar Chishti and Valerie Lacarte: "U.S. Immigrant Detention Grows to Record Heights under Trump Administration."

Here are the major findings:

ICE detention had been steadily rising but has increased sharply since Trump's return
Most detainees do not have a criminal record
Private prison companies play a central role in the system
The Trump administration is relying on state partners to help detain noncitizens
