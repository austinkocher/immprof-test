Canadian travel to the United States took a huge dive in 2025. Indeed, according to this report from Canada's national statistical office, that dive was the "deepest and most sustained on record."

Canadians shifted outbound leisure-related travel plans away from the United States (-21.5%, or -3.2 million visits) in favour of overseas options...
Travel spending on visits to the United States declined by $3.3 billion ... in 2025.

As Minnesota Public Radio reports, the economic fallout from reduced Canadian travel is affecting U.S. businesses.
