On October 24, 2025, the SMU Law Review hosted a symposium titled “Immigration and the Constitution: Evolving Doctrines and Emerging Conflicts.” Although the Constitution is mostly silent on immigration, the subject has become a fashpoint for some of today’s most pressing constitutional debates. Few areas of law so vividly expose the tension between national sovereignty, individual rights, and administrative discretion. Our goal in hosting this symposium was simple but ambitious: to bring people together to think carefully and compassionately about how constitutional principles are evolving alongside the realities of our immigration system today.
