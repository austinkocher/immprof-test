From the Los Angeles Times: "[S]everal sheriffs around the country [including North Carolina] were ousted by challengers critical of their cooperation with President Trump’s push for mass deportation of people in the country illegally. Though not widespread, the results could spell trouble for other sheriffs who are seen as participants in Trump’s crackdown and possibly hinder agreements that have become a cornerstone of the administration’s efforts."
