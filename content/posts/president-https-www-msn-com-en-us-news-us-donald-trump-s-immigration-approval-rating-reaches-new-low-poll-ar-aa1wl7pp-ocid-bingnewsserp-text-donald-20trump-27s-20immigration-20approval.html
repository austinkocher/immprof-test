A poll conducted by The Washington Post, ABC News and Ipsos released last week is getting press. According to the poll, "disapproval toward Trump on immigration reached a second-term high point."

58 percent of those polled said that they disapprove of his handling of immigration, while 40 percent gave him positive marks. That marks a downward shift from October 2025.
