The Trump administration awarded a no-bid $158 million contract to the controversial anti-human trafficking group Our Rescue to provide legal services for unaccompanied migrant children. It is unclear if the organization employs immigration lawyers, though its website says it offers "legal support to help prosecute offenders." Our Rescue, previously known as Operation Underground Railroad, says it helps law enforcement perform sting operations to catch human traffickers and rescue survivors.
