Birthright citizenship: under the flag by Akhil and Vikram Amar & Samarth Desai

has been posted on SCOTUSBlog. The column is the first in a series on birthright citizenship and Trump v. Barbara, the case in which the Supreme Court will determine the legality of President Donald Trump’s order seeking to end birthright citizenship. The piece contends that the "order is egregiously unconstitutional, and we’ve now said so to the justices, in an amicus brief filed . . . by Akhil, with Vik as counsel of record and Sam as lead assistant."
