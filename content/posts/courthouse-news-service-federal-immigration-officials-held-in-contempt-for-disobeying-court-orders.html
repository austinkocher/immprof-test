A judge held DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin, U.S. Attorney General Todd Blanche, and others in civil contempt after ICE rearrested a man that a court ordered released from immigration detention. Courthouse News reports on the order. "U.S. Magistrate Judge J. Maureen Kelly said the officials also failed to comply with an order to produce body camera footage and provided inconsistent explanations and questionable sworn statements about the arrest."
