The Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR), an agency within the U.S. Department of Justice, announced yesterday the appointment of 35 new immigration judges and 3 temporary immigration judges.

This group of new hires follows previous large classes sworn in earlier in 2026 as part of an ongoing mass hiring effort by the current administration.
