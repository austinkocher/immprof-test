Its not difficult for all noncitizens to make it to the United States.

USA Today reports that Brazil’s presidential candidate Flávio Bolsonaro, an ally of President Donald Trump, fled the country after his defeat in the presidential runoff election and was headed to the United States.

"The State Department will issue visas to Flávio Bolsonaro and his family and allow them entry into the United States," a senior State Department official said.

Bolsonaro fled Brazil after he was banned from leaving the country and ordered to surrender his passport by a Brazilian Supreme Court Justice, as part of a criminal investigation into Bolsonaro’s alleged involvement in a money laundering scheme.

Bolsonaro arrived by private jet in Miami yesterday.
