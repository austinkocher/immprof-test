The Supreme Court heard oral argument earlier today in Blanche v. Lau. The Court has before it the rights of a lawful permanent resident who may have committed a crime in seeking to return to the United States after a brief trip abroad.  We blogged earlier in the week on Nancy Morawetz's analysis of the case on Just Security ("Improvidently Granted: The Sleeper Supreme Court Case Affecting the Rights of 12.8 Million Green Card Holders)."

The Justices at oral argument appeared "split," according to one assessment.
