A film in the making needs support. On the 30th anniversary of a highly consequential law, Harsh Legacy reveals how the 1996 immigration law laid the groundwork for the enforcement system dominating headlines today — families torn apart, asylum seekers treated like criminals, and legal residents facing exile for decades-old offenses.
