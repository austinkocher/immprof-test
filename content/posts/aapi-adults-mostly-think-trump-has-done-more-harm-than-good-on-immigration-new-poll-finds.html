The Associated Press reports that a new AAPI Data and The Associated Press-NORC Center for Public Affairs Research poll shows that most Asian Americans, Native Hawaiians and Pacific Islanders believe President Donald Trump has done more harm than good on the issue of immigration and border security in his second term so far.

About 6 in 10 AAPI adults say Trump has hurt immigration and border security “a lot” or “a little,” according to the survey from compared with about 4 in 10 U.S. adults in a January survey. About two-thirds of AAPI adults also say Trump has “gone too far” when it comes to deporting immigrants in the U.S., compared with about half of Americans in general.
