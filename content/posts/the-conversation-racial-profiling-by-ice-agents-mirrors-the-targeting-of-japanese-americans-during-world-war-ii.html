Anna Storti for The Conversation (Racial profiling by ICE agents mirrors the targeting of Japanese Americans during World War II) writes that "[t]argeting immigrants is a centuries-old American practice. In particular, Asian Americans have drawn parallels between the attacks on Latinos today and the forced relocation and incarceration of Japanese Americans during World War II."

Racial profiling in immigration enforcement has a long history.
