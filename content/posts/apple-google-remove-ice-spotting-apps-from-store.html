The BBC reports that Apple has pulled an ICE-spotting app called "ICEBlock" (as well as "similar apps") from its store. The app has also been removed from Google Play.

US Attorney General Pam Bondi "demanded" removal on the basis that such apps pose an ongoing threat to ICE employees. ICEBlock's developer says that Bondi has acted contrary to the 1st Amendment. Meanwhile, Google told the BBC that the app, and others like it, violated company policies.
