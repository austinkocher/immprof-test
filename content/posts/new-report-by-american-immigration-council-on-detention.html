The American Immigration Council has published a new report immigration detention in the United States.

It features three powerful stories from people currently or recently held in ICE detention.

As the report highlights, "deaths in ICE detention in 2025 were the highest ever for a non-COVID year. And for the rising number of people held in detention, the worsening conditions have led many to simply give up their cases — even if they had viable claims."
