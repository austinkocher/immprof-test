My Instagram has been filled with reels of snippets of Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem testimony before Congress. The harsh questioning of her leadership has been bipartisan.

NBC News reports that President Donald Trump has grown frustrated with Secretary Noem and has begun to consider possible replacements, lawmakers and people familiar with the discussions.

The President "has been speaking this week with Republican lawmakers about his displeasure with Noem and has made clear in those conversations that he is considering replacing her, according to two Republican lawmakers, a person familiar with White House’s thinking and three people familiar with the president’s private discussions."

The President has "told lawmakers that he is unhappy with Noem’s testimony this week before House and Senate committees."
