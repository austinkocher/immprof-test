The Trump administration most definitely is not only targeting the "worst of the worst" in a mass deportation campaign. Restricting legal immigration is among its goals. Stuart Anderson for Forbes summarizes the Trump administration's many restrictions on legal immigration, such as various family, employment, diversity visas and refugees. Analysts from the National Foundation for American Policy project a 33% to 50% reduction in legal immigration, totaling 1.5 to 2.4 million people, by the end of Trump's second term. This may harm the economy, with a potential labor loss of 19 million worker years by 2028 and a $1.9 trillion reduction in goods and services.
